The short version

This is a static informational website. It has no user accounts, no comments, no checkout, and no contact form that stores data on a server we control. We do not ask for, and you should never send us, passwords, payment details or documents.

What is collected when you visit

  • Hosting logs: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may log requests (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) for security and operation of its service. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how it handles that data.
  • On this site itself: the search and filters run entirely in your browser using a data file downloaded with the page. Your searches are not sent to us and are not stored.
  • Cookies: this site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies.

Advertising (future)

If advertising is enabled in the reserved ad spaces in future, the ad network supplying it may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure and personalise ads, under its own privacy policy. This page will be updated with the network's name and a link to its policy before or when that happens, and ads will remain in clearly separated containers.

Links to other sites

Guides may link to manufacturer websites and manuals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children
