Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code Ea

Quick answer

Error codeEa — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansEa on a Daewoo refrigerator means the ice-drop motor has malfunctioned. The manual check is to push the ice-maker test switch and watch the motor; on FRS-2011/2031 models the termination is exchanging the motor. This page also covers EA, a different code that looks identical in lowercase: EA means ice dropping was sensed by time three times with a level-sensor switch error, which pauses the ice maker.
Most common causeThe ice-drop motor itself has failed, so it cannot complete the ice-drop movement when the test switch is pushed.
Safe to check yourself?Only the test-switch observation. Push the ice-maker test switch once and watch the motor; if it does not move correctly, the motor needs exchanging, which is technician work. Do not dismantle the ice maker.
Checked againstDaewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual section 5 (Ea: ice drop motor malfunction, test-switch check; EA: ice dropping by time 3 times with level-sensor switch error, ice maker stopped); Daewoo FRS-20 User Manual section 5 (identical Ea and EA items); Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual section 6 (Ea: exchange motor; EA: pause ice maker, reset by power re-input or test switch); Daewoo IWD/KAN58 repair manual section 7.1 (Ea: ice-maker motor)

Applies to — read this first

Ea and EA look like the same code in lowercase, and they are not. Ea (small a) is the ice-drop motor failing. EA (capital A) is the level-sensor timing fault: the control sensed ice dropping by time three times while the level-sensor switch was in error, so it paused the ice maker. Because both lowercase to the same page address, this page carries both, clearly labelled, so neither fault is lost.

Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual (section 6, item 16) — EAEA Error. Cause: When sensing ice dropping 3 times with level sensor switch error. Control: Pause ice maker. Termination: With level sensor switch normal, reset the power or push the test switch.

Neither Ea nor EA appears in the ranking list pages at all, so a searcher gets nothing from them. The Ea-versus-EA distinction itself is this page's core value: the manuals place them in adjacent items precisely because they are easy to confuse and have different fixes.

What the machine does when Ea fires

With Ea, the ice-drop motor malfunctions, so ice cannot be dropped normally even when the test switch is pushed. With EA, the ice maker is paused or stopped by the control after three timed ice-drop sensings with a level-sensor switch error, until the level switch is normal and the unit is reset.

Common causes

  1. Ea: the ice-drop motor has failed and cannot move when the test switch is pushed.
  2. Ea: the motor drive or its sensing path is faulty, so the drop movement does not complete.
  3. EA: the level-sensor switch is in error while the control senses ice dropping by time three times, so the control pauses the ice maker.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm which code you have: Ea (motor) or EA (level-sensor timing). Read the case exactly as displayed, because the two faults have different fixes.
  2. For Ea: push the ice-maker test switch once and watch the motor. If it does not move correctly, the motor needs exchanging — stop there and call a technician.
  3. For EA: power-cycle once (re-input of power) or push the ice-maker test switch once, as the manuals give for reset, after checking the level switch path is clear. If EA returns, the level-sensor switch needs technician attention.

Do not swap the fixes: a new motor will not fix EA, and a reset will not fix a dead Ea motor. If either code returns after its reset step, the part behind it needs a technician.

When to call a technician

  • Ea: the ice-drop motor still does not move correctly after the test-switch check.
  • EA returns after a power re-input or test-switch reset.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or visible damage near the ice maker — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

Ea and EA are ice-maker faults, not home repairs: never dismantle the ice maker, never bypass a switch or a motor, and never open a control panel. Switch the unit off before any handling and leave motor exchange and switch work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does Ea mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

Ea means the ice-drop motor has malfunctioned. The manual check is to push the ice-maker test switch and watch the motor, and on FRS-2011/2031 models the termination is exchanging the motor.

What does EA mean, and is it the same as Ea?

No. EA means ice dropping was sensed by time three times with a level-sensor switch error, which pauses or stops the ice maker. Ea is the motor itself failing. They share a lowercase page address, which is why this page covers both, labelled separately.

How do I reset EA?

The manuals give two reset routes once the level switch is normal: re-input of power (power-cycle) or pushing the ice-maker test switch. If EA returns after reset, the level-sensor switch needs a technician.

Is Ea the same as Eu?

No. Eu is the full-ice switch failing, which limits the drop motor to 90 degrees. Ea is the drop motor itself malfunctioning. A motor that does not move at all points to Ea.

Can I fix Ea myself?

Beyond the test-switch observation, no. Motor exchange inside the ice maker is technician work.
