Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code D2

Quick answer

Error codeD2 — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansD2 = defrost-component fault on Daewoo's Spain-built no-frost combi range (ERF-364/367 generation): the defrost heater or temperature fuse is open, or there is heavy frost on the evaporator surface. It is signalled by LED blinks, not text — D2 = the freezer's red LED blinks twice (D1 = once). It is NOT the lowercase d2 forced-defrost service mode on side-by-side models (FRN-U20/FRS-U20) — same characters, different fault, different family.
Most common causeA defrost component has failed electrically — the defrost heater is open or the temperature fuse is open — or frost has built up heavily on the evaporator surface so the heater cannot clear it.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: most causes are electrical components (heater, fuse) behind the freezer back panel, which is technician work. What you can do is decode the LED blinks, confirm your model belongs to the Spain-built combi family, and check for heavy frost before calling service.
Checked againstService Manual "No-Frost Combi-Refrigerator ERF-364_367 ALL AR-EU MODELS" — Daewoo Electronics Manufacturing España S.A., 2003 (ERF-364A..EU–ERF-417A..EU and ERF-364AR–ERF-417AR) + Service Manual "No-Frost Combi-Refrigerator", Model ERF-416A — Daewoo Electronics Manufacturing España, S.A. (identical D1/D2/R1/RT LED-error table)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because D2 is an LED blink, not a display text. On the Spain-built no-frost combi family (ERF-364/367 generation) the freezer's red LED blinking twice means D2 — a genuine defrost fault. On Daewoo side-by-side models (FRN-U20/FRS-U20 family) the lowercase d2 display means something entirely different: a technician's forced-defrost service mode, not a fault at all. If you own a side-by-side, this page is not your code. And Turkish-built Daewoos (e.g. FKM367FWR1RS) use E01–E09 warnings and have no D2 at all.

D2 = freezer red LED blinks twice → defrost heater or temperature fuse open, OR heavy frost on the evaporator surface. R1 = fridge red LED blinks once → R-sensor open/short. RT = fridge red LED blinks twice → room-temperature sensor open/short.

Newer LED-combi user manuals (ERF-36.A / ERF-36.M / ERF-41.M, UK retail) — no codesGeneric troubleshooting only (power, doors, bulb). The D1/D2/R1/RT fault table exists only in the service manuals — the gap this page fills.

The #1 dedicated page on the web (removeandreplace.com) tells combi owners D2 means 'A/S forced Mode' — that the fridge is in a technician service mode. The factory service manual says the opposite: on ERF-364/367 combis, D2 is a defrost fault and the control responds with an 80-minute defrost. A user acting on the incumbent's advice would wait for a 'mode' to exit instead of checking the heater circuit. The blink table, the cause list, the 80-minute behavior, the model scope, and the D2-vs-d2 family split are what this page adds — no ranking page has them.

What the machine does when D2 fires

The freezer's red LED blinks twice, repeating. The control holds the unit in defrost for up to 80 minutes — "D2 ERROR happens when heater is off by time (80 min)" — and returns from defrost by the time limit instead of by the D-sensor.

Common causes

  1. The defrost heater is open (failed) — the manuals' own D2 cause.
  2. The temperature fuse (thermal fuse) in the defrost circuit is open — the manuals' other named D2 cause.
  3. Heavy frost has built up on the evaporator surface, beyond what the heater can clear — also a named D2 trigger in the manual.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the family first: this page is for Spain-built no-frost combis (ERF-364/367 generation, LED panel). If you own a side-by-side (FRN-U20/FRS-U20), its d2 is the forced-defrost service mode — not this fault.
  2. Read the LED on the main PCB: freezer red LED on & off twice, repeating, confirms D2. One blink = D1 (D-sensor), fridge-LED blinks are R1/RT — check the table above so you are fixing the right fault.
  3. Check for heavy frost: frost packed on the evaporator beyond what a defrost cycle clears is itself a D2 trigger. Do not chip at ice with tools — leave that to a technician.
  4. Restart the fridge once and watch the LED: if D2 returns, the cause is inside the freezer back panel — the heater circuit or the fuse — and the manuals name no user-serviceable repair for either.
  5. Do not open the freezer back panel or test the heater/fuse yourself: those are mains-connected components. Call a qualified technician for the defrost heater, the temperature fuse, and the evaporator.

The home scope is narrow by design: confirm the family, decode the blink, restart once. The manuals give no user procedure for testing or replacing the heater or fuse — that is board- and mains-level service work.

When to call a technician

  • D2 returns after one restart with the correct combi family confirmed.
  • The fridge is held in defrost for the full 80 minutes every time — the control cannot finish by the D-sensor.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other error LEDs alongside D2 — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

D2 involves mains-powered components behind the freezer back panel: never remove that panel while the fridge is connected to power, never test the heater or fuse yourself, and never bypass a thermal fuse. Frost work is also hazardous — never chip ice with sharp tools.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does D2 mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

D2 is a defrost-component fault: the defrost heater or temperature fuse is open, or there is heavy frost on the evaporator surface. It is verified in two Daewoo service manuals — the 2003 "No-Frost Combi-Refrigerator ERF-364_367 ALL AR-EU MODELS" manual and the ERF-416A manual — both of which state "D2 ERROR : FREEZER RED LED is on & off two times."

Is Daewoo D2 the same as d2?

No. On side-by-side models (FRN-U20/FRS-U20 family) lowercase d2 is a technician's forced-defrost service mode, not a fault. On the Spain-built ERF-364/367 combis uppercase D2 ERROR is a genuine defrost fault. Same characters, different family, different meaning — the case tells you which manual to follow.

How do I read the LED blinks?

On the main PCB of the Spain-built combis: freezer red LED once = D1 (D-sensor open/short), twice = D2 (defrost components), fridge red LED once = R1 (R-sensor open/short), twice = RT (room-temperature sensor open/short).

Why do some sites say D2 means forced defrost mode?

They conflate the combi fault with the side-by-side service mode — the characters look alike. The factory combi service manual documents D2 as a defrost fault and lists forced defrost as a separate LED condition of its own.

What does the 80-minute rule mean?

When D2 fires, the control holds the unit in defrost for up to 80 minutes ("D2 ERROR happens when heater is off by time (80 min)") and returns by that time limit instead of by the D-sensor.
