Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code R1

Quick answer

Error codeR1 — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansR1 = R-SENSOR (refrigerator-compartment sensor) open or short - on Daewoo's Spain-built ERF-364/367 no-frost combi range. Identification: the REFRIGERATOR red LED lamp blinks on & off ONE time. Control fallback: the system is controlled by the RT (room-temperature) sensor's condition while R1 is active; the error cancels when the R-sensor reads normally. R1 is NOT defrost-related (D1/D2 are the freezer/defrost side) and NOT the ambient sensor - the ambient sensor is RT, a separate code.
Most common causeThe R-sensor circuit is open or shorted. The manual gives no homeowner reset - the fault clears when the sensor reads normally again; identification is by LED blink.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the manual's supported diagnostic is LED-blink identification only (no multimeter or board steps for the ERF combi - the CN15/CN14 resistance checks in the old side-by-side specs do not apply). A persistent R1 is service work.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because R1 here is an LED blink, not display text: on Daewoo's Spain-built no-frost combi range (ERF-364/384/394/414 and ERF-367/387/397/417, ..EU and ..AR suffixes) the refrigerator's red LED blinking on & off ONCE means R1 - the R-sensor (refrigerator-compartment sensor) is open or short. On 88-segment models the code "R1" itself shows on the display, with R1 at the TOP of the error-priority order (R1 > D1 > D2 > RT). This page is ERF-combi-scoped: explicitly disambiguate the side-by-side-family r1 system (digital Lo/Hi display, CN15 resistance checks) - that is a different display context that lives under the side-by-side specs, not the ERF combi manual.

What the machine does when R1 fires

The refrigerator red LED blinks on & off once, repeating, and the control runs on the RT-sensor's condition until the R-sensor reads normally again.

Common causes

  1. R-sensor open circuit - the manual's own condition.
  2. R-sensor short circuit - the manual's own condition.
  3. The manual names no further sub-cause for the ERF combi.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Locate the control-panel red LEDs - freezer side vs refrigerator side.
  2. Refrigerator red LED blinking on & off once = R1 (R-sensor open/short). Twice on the same LED = RT (room-temperature sensor). Freezer red LED once = D1, twice = D2.
  3. On 88-segment models the code "R1" shows on the display itself.
  4. There is no manual reset procedure for R1 beyond the fault clearing when the sensor reads normally - do not invent multimeter or board-swap steps (the side-by-side family's CN15/CN14 checks do not apply to the ERF combi).
  5. If R1 persists, call a qualified technician.

The diagnostic the manual supports is LED-blink identification only. The CN15/CN14 resistance checks belong to the side-by-side family - this page does not import them.

When to call a technician

  • R1 persists when the sensor should read normally - the R-sensor circuit or the control board needs service.
  • Other blink codes alongside R1 - 88-segment models show the highest-priority code first (R1 > D1 > D2 > RT).

Safety first

R1 involves mains-powered electronics: never open the control panel or probe the sensor circuit yourself. Identification is by watching the LED - everything past that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does R1 mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

On the Spain-built ERF-364/367 no-frost combi range, R1 = R-sensor (refrigerator-compartment sensor) open or short - the refrigerator red LED blinks on & off once. It is verified verbatim in the 2003 factory service manual (section 44).

Is R1 the ambient temperature sensor?

No - that is ukwhitegoods.co.uk's error. On the ERF combi, R1 is the refrigerator-compartment (R) sensor; the ambient/room sensor is RT, a separate code on the same LED (blinks twice).

Is R1 a failing compressor?

No - one ranking site (forkandspoonkitchen.org) fabricates a failing-compressor r1 with "magneto switch" and "loose belt" advice. The factory manual says R1 is the R-sensor open/short with an RT-controlled fallback. Do not follow the spam.

What is the difference between R1 and RT?

Same LED, different blink count: refrigerator red LED once = R1 (compartment sensor); twice = RT (room-temperature sensor). The D-codes (D1/D2) are the freezer/defrost side; the R-codes (R1/RT) are the refrigerator-compartment side.
