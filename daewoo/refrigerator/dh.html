Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code dH

Quick answer

Error codedH — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansdH on a Daewoo refrigerator means the home-bar door switch is defective — the small home-bar door in the refrigerator door is sensed open for an hour or longer. The home bar is the small outer door set into the main refrigerator door, so you can grab drinks and snacks without opening the whole door. dH is NOT dr (the main refrigerator door switch) and NOT dF (the freezer door switch) — same family, different door. And dH only applies to dispenser models that actually have a home bar: if your Daewoo has no home bar, dH cannot be your code.
Most common causeThe home-bar door is not closing fully — blocked by an item, or its seal or hinge is worn — or the home-bar door switch itself is defective and keeps reporting the door as open even when it is shut.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the first checks: close the home-bar door firmly, remove anything blocking it, and open-and-close it deliberately so the switch senses the movement. If dH stays on with the door properly shut, the switch itself is defective and replacement is technician work.
Checked againstDaewoo FRS-20 Fridge Freezer Operating Instructions / FRS-U20 Service Manual, section 4-10-2 Dispenser Model (CLED Display of Front PCB), Error Code table (manuals.plus / scribd copies); Daewoo refrigerator error-code lists (eciftci.com.tr) for the door family's one-hour presentation

Applies to — read this first

This page is for Daewoo side-by-side dispenser refrigerators with a CLED front display and a home bar — the FRS-20 / FRS-U20 family. The door-switch family has three codes, and the door is the whole distinction: dr is the main refrigerator door, dF is the freezer door, dH is the home bar. Older Daewoo models without a home bar, and models with a simpler display, show one generic door error instead — dH does not exist on them.

ManualExact wording for dH
Daewoo FRS-20 / FRS-U20 Service Manual, section 4-10-2 (manuals.plus / scribd)'dH | Home bar Door Switch : defective' — in the same error table as 'dr | R-Door Switch : defective' and 'dF | F-Door Switch : defective'. The manual's fault entry is the switch itself.
Daewoo refrigerator error-code lists (eciftci.com.tr)'dH | When home-bar Door Open is detected for one hr. or longer' — the same family's presentation: a door switch that keeps reporting open for an hour or longer is how the fault shows itself.
The manual's general reset ruleThe service manual's diagnostic text states the general rule that all error codes reset when they return to normal — so a dH that clears after a firm close-and-open was a door that was genuinely open, not a defective switch.
dr vs dF vs dH — do not mix themdr = main refrigerator (R) door switch. dF = freezer (F) door switch. dH = home-bar door switch. Same behaviour, three doors. Replacing the wrong door's switch fixes nothing.
Models without a home barIf your Daewoo has no small outer door in the refrigerator door, it has no home bar and no dH. Older FR-530NT/590NT-style models show one generic door error instead (documented on the dr page).

No competitor page has a dH section at all: eciftci.com.tr gives dH exactly one bare table row with no explanation of what a home bar is, no dr/dF/dH comparison, no model scope and no steps, and the manual copies give one line. This page is the first dedicated dH page, and its core value is the home-bar definition, the three-door comparison, and the close-and-check steps.

What the machine does when dH fires

The display shows dH and the refrigerator believes the home-bar door has been open for an hour or longer: the door-open sensing stays active, and the code stays until the switch reports the door shut again. A genuinely open home-bar door also lets warm air in, so food near the door warms up — which is why the first check is simply to shut it properly.

Common causes

  1. The home-bar door is blocked by an item and cannot close fully.
  2. The home-bar door seal or hinge is worn, so the door does not sit shut.
  3. The home-bar door switch itself is defective and keeps reporting the door as open even when it is shut.
  4. The door was genuinely left open for an hour or longer, and the code is still showing from that event.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Find the home bar: the small outer door set into the main refrigerator door, usually with its own handle or push area.
  2. Close it firmly and check nothing is blocking it — a bottle, a carton or a tall item in the door shelf is the most common cause.
  3. Open the home-bar door and close it again deliberately, so the switch senses the full movement, then wait and see whether dH clears (the manual's general rule: codes reset when they return to normal).
  4. If dH stays on with the door properly shut and nothing blocking it, stop: the switch itself is defective, and switch replacement belongs to a qualified technician.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. A dH that clears after a firm close was a door that was genuinely open — no repair needed. A dH that stays on with a shut door is a defective switch, and switch replacement is not a home step on a dispenser model.

When to call a technician

  • dH stays on with the home-bar door properly shut and nothing blocking it.
  • The home-bar door will not stay shut, or its seal or hinge is visibly damaged.
  • Food near the home-bar door is warming, or the door-open warning keeps returning after every close.

Safety first

dH is a door-switch fault, not an electrical repair: never dismantle the door, the dispenser panel or the switch housing yourself, and never bypass a door switch. Keep hands dry, do not force a stuck door, and leave switch replacement to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does dH mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

dH means the home-bar door switch is defective — the small home-bar door in the refrigerator door is sensed open for an hour or longer. The FRS-20 / FRS-U20 service manual lists it in its dispenser-model error table, alongside dr (refrigerator door) and dF (freezer door).

What is a home bar?

A small outer door set into the main refrigerator door, so you can grab drinks and snacks without opening the whole door and letting all the cold air out. Only dispenser models with this feature have a home bar — and only those models can show dH.

What is the difference between dH, dr and dF?

Only which door: dH is the home-bar door switch, dr is the main refrigerator door switch, dF is the freezer door switch. Same behaviour — a door sensed open for an hour or longer — three different doors.

My Daewoo has no small door in the fridge door. Can it show dH?

No. Without a home bar there is no home-bar switch to fail. Older models show one generic door error instead, and a dH on such a machine would mean the display or board is misreading, not a home-bar fault.

dH cleared after I shut the door firmly. Is the switch still broken?

Probably not. The manual's general rule is that codes reset when they return to normal — a dH that clears after a firm close was a door that was genuinely open. Only a dH that stays on with a shut door points to a defective switch.
