Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code ES

Quick answer

Error codeES — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansES on a Daewoo refrigerator means the dispenser micro-switch is in error: the switch is sensed continuously for 1 minute, as if held or stuck. On FRS-2011/2031 models the control stops the dispenser and crusher functions and the related icon box goes off. This is a dispenser-switch fault, not a cooling fault: the refrigerator still cools.
Most common causeThe dispenser micro-switch is stuck or held, so it keeps sensing continuously for a full minute instead of switching on and off.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the first checks: make sure nothing is holding the dispenser lever or paddle pressed, and that the dispenser area is clean and free. Switch replacement is technician work; do not dismantle the dispenser panel.
Checked againstDaewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual section 5 (ES: micro switch error, senses 1 minute continuously, check the micro switch of the dispenser); Daewoo FRS-20 User Manual section 5 (identical ES entry); Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual section 6 (dispenser switch error: stop dispenser and crusher, icon box off, termination normal); Daewoo IWD/KAN58 repair manual section 7.1 (ES: dispenser switch)

Applies to — read this first

ES is easy to misread as a refrigeration fault, and it is not one. ES is the dispenser switch: the small switch behind the water/ice dispenser that tells the machine you are pressing for water or ice. When it senses continuously for a minute, the machine concludes the switch is stuck and shuts the dispenser functions down. Cooling is unaffected.

ManualExact wording for ES
Daewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual (section 5, item 12)ES error (micro switch error). Cause: When it senses 1 minute continuously. Check the micro switch of the dispenser.
Daewoo FRS-20 User Manual (section 5, item 12)ES in the error table as Micro switch: error, with the identical detailed item and the same 1-minute rule.
Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual (section 6, item 13)Dispenser switch error. Cause: When it senses 1 minute continuously. Control: Stop dispenser and crusher function. Display: Relative icon box is off. Termination: Normal.
Daewoo IWD / KAN58 repair manual (section 7.1)ES listed as Dispenser switch, with the repair note to test the ice/water dispenser and replace the switch. Same fault on that family.
Daewoo refrigerator EF / EI — do not mix themEF is the water-flow sensor giving no pulse, and EI is the ice-maker I-sensor open or shorted. ES is neither: it is the dispenser lever switch stuck on. Different parts, different checks.

No ranking list page carries an ES row at all: a searcher gets nothing from the all-codes lists. The 1-minute trigger, the stopped dispenser and crusher, and the icon-box-off display are what this page adds, plus the crucial reassurance that cooling is not the fault.

What the machine does when ES fires

The display shows ES and the dispenser stops working: on FRS-2011/2031 models the dispenser and crusher functions stop and the related icon box goes off. The refrigerator keeps cooling and freezing normally; only dispensing is affected.

Common causes

  1. The dispenser micro-switch is stuck in the pressed position, so it senses continuously.
  2. Something is holding the dispenser lever or paddle pressed, or debris is jamming the switch area.
  3. The micro-switch itself has failed and stays closed even when nothing is pressing it.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code is ES and that the problem is dispensing, not cooling: if the fridge is warm, that is a different fault, not ES.
  2. Release the dispenser lever or paddle fully and make sure nothing is holding it pressed; clear any visible debris around the dispenser area without dismantling anything.
  3. Power-cycle once and try the dispenser again. If ES returns with nothing holding the lever, the micro-switch itself is faulty — stop and call a technician.

An ES that clears after releasing a held lever was a stuck lever, not a broken switch. An ES that stays on with a free lever is a defective micro-switch.

When to call a technician

  • ES returns after a power-cycle with the dispenser lever free.
  • The dispenser and crusher stay dead, or the icon box stays off.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or visible damage around the dispenser — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

ES is a dispenser-switch fault, not a home repair: never dismantle the dispenser panel or the switch housing, and never bypass a switch. Switch the unit off before any handling and leave switch replacement to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does ES mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

ES means the dispenser micro-switch is in error: the switch is sensed continuously for 1 minute, as if held or stuck. The dispenser and crusher functions stop, and the related icon box goes off.

Is ES a cooling fault?

No. ES affects dispensing only. The refrigerator keeps cooling and freezing normally while ES is showing.

Why did my dispenser stop working with ES?

Because the control concluded the dispenser switch is stuck: sensing continuously for a minute is the trigger, and stopping the dispenser and crusher is the control response.

What should I check first for ES?

That nothing is holding the dispenser lever or paddle pressed, and that the dispenser area is clear. Then power-cycle once. If ES returns with a free lever, the switch itself is faulty.

Can I fix ES myself?

Beyond freeing a held lever and one power-cycle, no. Micro-switch replacement in the dispenser is technician work.
