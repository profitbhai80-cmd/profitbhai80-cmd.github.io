Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code dr

Quick answer

Error codedr — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansdr on a Daewoo refrigerator means the refrigerator (R) door has been sensed open for more than 1 hour. Check first whether the door is actually open, ajar, or blocked — if the door is closed and dr persists, the R-door switch is stuck reporting open. While dr is active the manual suspends door-switch sensing functions, and the code ends automatically once an open-and-close of the switch is sensed.
Most common causeThe refrigerator door was left open, ajar, or blocked by food or packaging for over an hour — or, if the door is closed, the R-door switch is stuck reporting open.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manual's own checks are home checks: confirm the door is really shut, clear anything blocking it, and open-and-close the door to re-trigger the switch. If dr persists with a closed door, the switch itself needs a technician.
Checked againstDaewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual §5 (door error: open sensed over 1 hour; dr = R-Door switch); Daewoo FRS20 User Manual §5 (identical door-error entry); Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual §6 (door error: deletion of door-switch sensing, termination on open-close sensing); Daewoo FR-3502 Service Manual §6 and FRS-T20DA manual §6 (same door-error entry); Daewoo IWD/KAN58 repair manual §7.1 (dR: refrigerator compartment door switch, open-close test)

Applies to — read this first

dr is the REFRIGERATOR-door code — the 'r' stands for the refrigerator compartment. Its siblings are dF (freezer door) and dH (home-bar door), and the three differ only in which door. On older FR-530NT/590NT LED-bar models there is no split: those machines show a single generic door error for the same 1-hour trigger.

The list pages give dr's trigger and then stop: no two-layer check (door actually open vs switch stuck open), no suspension behaviour, no auto-termination rule, no dH companion. The two-layer check is the diagnosis — most dr episodes end at layer one, with the door simply shut.

What the machine does when dr fires

The display shows dr and the manual suspends the functions related to door-switch sensing while it is active. In practice: the fridge has decided the refrigerator door has been open for over an hour, so it stops trusting the door switch until it senses a proper open-and-close. Once that open-close is sensed, the error terminates automatically — no reset button, no power-cycle needed.

Common causes

  1. The refrigerator door was left open for over an hour.
  2. The door is ajar or blocked by food, packaging, or an item sticking out of a shelf or door bin.
  3. The door seal is not sitting flat, so the door looks shut but never fully closes.
  4. The R-door switch is stuck reporting open even though the door is closed.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check whether the refrigerator door is actually open, ajar, or blocked: push shelves and door bins fully in, clear anything trapped in the seal, and close the door firmly until it sits flat.
  2. Open the door fully and close it again, deliberately, so the switch senses a proper open-and-close — that sensing is what terminates dr.
  3. Check the door seal all round for gaps, trapped food, or warping. If the seal cannot sit flat, the door will keep reading as open.
  4. If dr persists with a firmly closed door and a clean seal, stop: the R-door switch is stuck reporting open and needs a technician to test or replace it. Do not force the switch or tape it down.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. dr is one of the few Daewoo fridge codes with a genuine home fix — a shut door — so do not jump to the switch until the door itself has been ruled out. Never bypass a door switch.

When to call a technician

  • dr persists with the refrigerator door firmly closed and the seal clean — stuck R-door switch.
  • The door will not stay shut, or the seal is torn or warped so the door cannot sit flat.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or water near electrical parts — unplug immediately and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

dr is a door-sensing fault, not an electrical fault — but the refrigerator is still a mains appliance. Never bypass, tape down, or force a door switch, and never open the refrigerator's electrical covers. If food has warmed because the door was open for over an hour, discard anything that has warmed above safe temperatures rather than tasting it.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does dr mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

dr means the refrigerator (R) door has been sensed open for more than 1 hour. Five Daewoo manuals give the same trigger, and the service manual adds the behaviour: door-switch sensing functions are suspended while dr is active, and the code ends automatically once an open-and-close of the switch is sensed.

Is dr a faulty door sensor?

Not necessarily — that is only one cause. The manuals' trigger is the door being sensed open for over 1 hour, so a door left genuinely open or blocked by food triggers dr with a perfectly healthy switch. Check the door before blaming the switch.

What is the difference between dr, dF and dH?

Only which door: dr = refrigerator door, dF = freezer door, dH = home-bar door. Same 1-hour trigger, same suspension behaviour, same open-close termination. Older FR-530NT/590NT models do not split them and show one generic door error.

How do I clear Daewoo refrigerator dr?

Close the door firmly, then open and close it deliberately so the switch senses the open-close. That sensing terminates dr automatically. If dr persists with a closed door, the R-door switch is stuck and needs a technician.

Why do some pages say dr is a drain-pump fault?

Because a mislabelled page pasted washing-machine drain-pump language onto a fridge door code. Daewoo fridge dr has nothing to do with a drain pump — ignore that row.
