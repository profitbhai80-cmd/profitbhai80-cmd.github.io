Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code d1

Quick answer

Error coded1 — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansd1 on a Daewoo refrigerator means the defrost (D) sensor — the evaporator sensor — is disconnected or shorted ('Lo' = disconnection, 'Hi' = short). While d1 is active, defrost no longer ends on the sensor: it ends on a time limit instead (30 minutes on most families, 40 minutes on FR-530NT/590NT). The code clears automatically once the sensor reads normal.
Most common causeThe D-sensor on the evaporator has failed open or shorted, so the control can no longer tell when defrost has finished.
Safe to check yourself?Partly. The safe home checks are: power-cycle once, and watch for frost building up in the freezer and compartments warming. The manual's own check — resistance across the D-sensor terminals after separating CN15 (or CN8) — is technician work; sensor replacement is too.
Checked againstDaewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual §5 (d1: D-sensor disconnection/short, CN15 resistance check, evaporator sensor); Daewoo FRS20 User Manual §5 (same d1 entry, CN8/CN15); Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual §6 (d1: 30-min defrost-return limit); Daewoo FR-530NT/590NT Manual §6 (d1: 40-min limit); Daewoo IWD/KAN58 repair manual §7.1 (d1: defrosting sensor, 30-min defrost)

Applies to — read this first

Two traps first. d1 is NOT a door code: the door codes are dr (refrigerator door), dF (freezer door) and dH (home-bar door) — the lowercase-d family splits by what follows the d, and d1's '1' is the defrost sensor, not a door. And d1 is NOT borrowed from Haier: the meaning below comes from Daewoo manuals only.

ManualExact wording for d1
Daewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual (§5)'d1' error. Cause: D-sensor disconnection or short (full down). Check point: Measure the resistance between both terminals after separating CN15. If D-sensor is disconnected or shorted, change the D-sensor on the evaporator. How to reset: If D-sensor is normal, the error is terminated automatically.
Daewoo FRS20 User Manual (§5)Same d1 entry — disconnection or short (full down); resistance check after separating CN8 (or CN15); replace the D-sensor on the evaporator; auto-termination when normal.
Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual (§6)'d1' ERROR. Cause: D-sensor disconnection / short (pull-down). Control: Time limit (30 min.) of defrosting-return. If D-sensor is normal, the error is terminated automatically.
Daewoo FR-530NT/590NT Manual (§6) — 40-minute variant'd1' Error. Occurrence: D-Sensor open / short-circuit. Control: by limit time (40 min.) of Defrosting-return. Dissolution: if D-Sensor is in normal state, it is finished by itself.
Daewoo IWD / KAN58 repair manual (§7.1, §5.4)'d1 — Defrosting sensor — Test the control module — Replace defrosting sensor'; its defrost description adds that if the evaporator sensor is defective, defrost takes 30 min and the display shows 'd1'.
Forkandspoonkitchen's 'door latch' row — WRONGThat page calls d1 a door-latch fault and prescribes light-bulb and ice-maker steps. Every Daewoo manual above says d1 is the defrost/evaporator sensor. Do not follow the door-latch row.

The list pages get d1's label right and then stop: no evaporator location, no CN15/CN8 resistance check, no 30/40-minute defrost-return limit, no auto-clear. The time limit is the mechanism that matters — while d1 is active, defrost ends on a clock, not on the sensor, so frost can build up even though the fridge seems to run.

What the machine does when d1 fires

The display shows d1 ('Lo' for disconnection, 'Hi' for short) and defrost control changes: instead of ending defrost when the D-sensor signals completion, the control ends it on a time limit — 30 minutes on FRS-2011/2031, FR-3502, FRS-T20DA and IWD families, 40 minutes on FR-530NT/590NT. If the evaporator has not actually defrosted in that time, frost builds up and the compartments slowly warm.

Common causes

  1. The D-sensor (evaporator sensor) has failed open (disconnection) — display 'Lo'.
  2. The D-sensor has shorted — display 'Hi'.
  3. A fault in the D-sensor circuit that reads as a disconnection or a short.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code: d1 is the defrost sensor. If your display shows dr or dF, those are door codes — different pages, different checks.
  2. Power-cycle the refrigerator once: unplug it, wait a few minutes, plug it back in, and see whether d1 returns after a full defrost cycle.
  3. Check for frost buildup in the freezer and whether the compartments are holding temperature. If frost is building up or food is warming, move perishables to another cold store and do not wait.
  4. If d1 returns, stop: the manuals' checks are the D-sensor resistance after separating CN15 (FRSU21DFV) or CN8/CN15 (FRS20) and replacing the D-sensor on the evaporator — technician work on the evaporator circuit. Do not open the freezer's rear panel yourself.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. d1 has no deeper home diagnostic in any manual — code check, power-cycle, frost and temperature check, then the technician. Never open the appliance's panels or touch sensor terminals.

When to call a technician

  • d1 returns after a power-cycle and a full defrost cycle.
  • Frost building up in the freezer, or compartments warming despite the compressor running.
  • The D-sensor resistance reads open or shorted after separating CN15/CN8 — the manual's replace condition.

Safety first

d1 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics and an evaporator circuit: never open the freezer's rear panel or touch sensor terminals while investigating it. Unplug the refrigerator before any visible check. Food that has warmed above safe temperatures should be discarded, not tasted to check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does d1 mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

d1 means the defrost (D) sensor on the evaporator is disconnected ('Lo') or shorted ('Hi'). Five Daewoo manuals agree, and the FRSU21DFV/FRS20 manuals give the same check: resistance across the D-sensor terminals after separating CN15 (or CN8).

Is Daewoo d1 a door code?

No. The door codes are dr (refrigerator door), dF (freezer door) and dH (home-bar door). d1 is the defrost sensor. The mislabelled forkandspoonkitchen page calls d1 a door-latch fault — that row is wrong in full.

Why does the defrost time matter for d1?

While d1 is active, defrost ends on a time limit (30 minutes on most families, 40 minutes on FR-530NT/590NT) instead of on the sensor. If the evaporator has not defrosted in that time, frost builds up and compartments warm.

How do I clear Daewoo refrigerator d1?

Power-cycle once and watch whether d1 returns after a full defrost cycle. If it returns, the D-sensor fault is genuine: the manuals' check is the sensor resistance, and replacement is technician work.

Is d1 30 minutes or 40 minutes?

Both, by family: 30 minutes on FRS-2011/2031, FR-3502, FRS-T20DA and IWD families; 40 minutes on FR-530NT/590NT. The fault is identical — only the clock differs.
