Daewoo Refrigerator Error Code F3

Quick answer

Error codeF3 — Daewoo Refrigerator
What it meansF3 on a Daewoo refrigerator means the defrost cycle ended on its time limit instead of on the D (defrost) sensor — the defrost timed out, and the control deletes Pre-cool mode in defrosting mode. The time limit is 80 minutes on most families (FRS-2011/2031, FRS20, FR-530NT/590NT) and 50 minutes on FRSU21DFV. This is not a sensor open/short code: it is a defrost that never got its sensor signal to finish.
Most common causeThe defrost cycle ran to its time limit without the D-sensor signalling completion — most often because the defrost heater or its thermal fuse is not doing its job, so the evaporator never reaches the temperature the sensor waits for.
Safe to check yourself?Partly. The safe home checks are: power-cycle once and watch whether F3 returns after a full defrost cycle, and note whether frost is building up in the freezer. The manual's own checks — defrost heater resistance and the thermal fuse — are technician work.
Checked againstDaewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual section 6 (F3: defrosting-return by 80-min time limit, Pre-cool deleted); Daewoo FRS20 User Manual section 5 (F3: 80-min limit, defrost heater resistance check); Daewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual section 5 (F3: 50-min limit); Daewoo IWD/KAN58 repair manual section 7.1 (F3: last defrost reset over time, not via freezer sensor; check heater and thermal fuse)

Applies to — read this first

F3 is one fault with two clocks: the defrost timed out instead of ending on the D-sensor, and the only difference between families is how long the clock runs. The mechanism, the consequence and the manual's checks are identical everywhere else — so the first thing to establish is which minute limit your family uses.

ManualExact wording for F3
Daewoo FRS-2011/2031 Service Manual (§6)'F3' ERROR. Cause: in case defrosting-return is done by time limit of 80 min. Control: deletion of Pre-cool mode in defrosting mode. If defrosting-return is done by D-sensor, it is terminated.
Daewoo FRS20 User Manual (§5)Same 80-minute cause, with the check point: measure the resistance between both terminals of the defrost heater. If the resistance is infinite (disconnected) or zero (short), change the heater.
Daewoo FRSU21DFV User Manual (§5)'F3' error. Cause: in case defrosting return is done by time limit of 50 min. Check point: measure the resistance between both terminals of the defrost heater.
Daewoo FR-530NT/590NT Manual (§6, LED-bar system)The same fault as a bar-LED pattern: 'Defrosting, when it returns to Time (80 min.), not D-Sensor' with 'Deletion of Pre-cool Mode in Defrosting Mode' — confirming the 80-minute variant on a fourth family.
Daewoo IWD / KAN58 repair manual (§7.1)'F3 — Last defrost reset occurred over time, not via the freezer compartment sensor — Test the defrosting heater, thermal fuse — Replace defrosting heater, thermal fuse.' No minute figure, same fault.

The list pages get F3 wrong by omission: one presents the 50-minute figure as universal, hiding the 80-minute majority variant, and none of them explains what 'return by time limit' means, mentions the Pre-cool deletion, or gives the heater-resistance check. This page carries the split with the manual named for each variant.

What the machine does when F3 fires

The display shows F3 and the control deletes Pre-cool mode in defrosting mode: because the defrost ended on the clock rather than on the D-sensor, the evaporator may not have fully defrosted. Over repeated cycles that can show up as frost building up in the freezer and the compartments slowly warming — the reason a one-off F3 after a power cut is less worrying than an F3 that keeps returning.

Common causes

  1. The defrost heater has failed (open or shorted), so the evaporator never reaches the temperature the D-sensor waits for.
  2. The thermal fuse in the defrost circuit has failed (named on the IWD family).
  3. The D-sensor itself is not signalling completion, so the cycle runs to its time limit instead.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Power-cycle the refrigerator once: unplug it, wait a few minutes, plug it back in, and see whether F3 returns after a full defrost cycle. A one-off F3 that never returns may have been a transient timing glitch.
  2. Check for frost buildup in the freezer and whether the compartments are holding temperature. If frost is building up or food is warming, do not wait — move perishables to another cold store.
  3. If F3 returns, stop: the manuals' checks are the defrost heater resistance (infinite or zero means replace the heater) and the thermal fuse — both technician work involving the defrost circuit. Do not open the freezer's rear panel or test heater terminals yourself.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. F3 has no deeper home diagnostic in any manual — power-cycle, frost and temperature check, then the technician's heater and fuse checks. Never open the appliance's panels or touch heater terminals.

When to call a technician

  • F3 returns after a power-cycle and a full defrost cycle.
  • Frost building up in the freezer, or compartments warming despite the compressor running.
  • Defrost heater resistance infinite (disconnected) or zero (short) — the manual's replace condition.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or water near electrical parts — unplug immediately and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

F3 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics and a heater circuit: never open the freezer's rear panel or touch heater or fuse terminals while investigating it. Unplug the refrigerator before any visible check. Heater-resistance and fuse checks belong to a qualified technician — food that has warmed above safe temperatures should be discarded, not tasted to check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F3 mean on a Daewoo refrigerator?

F3 means the defrost cycle ended on its time limit instead of on the D (defrost) sensor — the defrost timed out without the sensor signalling completion — and the control deletes Pre-cool mode in defrosting mode.

Is Daewoo refrigerator F3 80 minutes or 50 minutes?

Both, by family: 80 minutes on FRS-2011/2031, FRS20 and FR-530NT/590NT, and 50 minutes on FRSU21DFV. The list pages that present 50 minutes as universal hide the 80-minute majority variant.

Does F3 mean my defrost heater is broken?

Not necessarily — F3 means the defrost timed out, and the heater is the manual's first check, not a proven verdict. The manual's check is the heater resistance: infinite (disconnected) or zero (short) means replace the heater; the IWD family adds the thermal fuse. Both checks are technician work.

Is Daewoo refrigerator F3 the same as Haier refrigerator F3?

No. Haier fridge F3 is an RT (ambient) sensor fault. Same letter, different brand, different fault — never carry a code meaning across brands.

How do I clear Daewoo refrigerator F3?

Power-cycle once and watch whether F3 returns after a full defrost cycle. If it returns, or if frost is building up, the defrost heater or thermal fuse needs a technician — that is the manual's own check path.
