Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code H5

Quick answer

Error codeH5 — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansH5 = water temperature error in the wool/delicate course: the water temperature in a wool or delicate course is 45 °C or higher. The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes and clears when power is turned off and on. Front-loader families only.
Most common causeThe wash thermistor reading over 45 °C in a wool/delicate course — the troubleshooting guide names the thermistor wash (inferior or broken down) with 'change the thermistor wash' as the remedy.
Safe to check yourself?Limited. The manual's alarm behaviour and clear method are user-side (power off/on); the thermistor wash remedy is technician work.
Checked againstDaewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33 (H5 flowchart); Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34; Daewoo error-mode flowchart list

Applies to — read this first

H5 is a temperature error with a precise definition, and one competitor contradicts the service manual outright. H5 means: water temperature error in the wool/delicate course — water temperature in those courses is 45 °C or higher. The DWC-1011W service manual's flowchart and the front-loader troubleshooting guide agree, and the guide adds the remedy: change the thermistor wash (inferior or broken down). Scope: front-loader families only. The competitor error: one site's H5 page frames it as a maintenance warning — 'need for internal cleaning with water at 60 °C' — which the service manual does not say, and which, if followed on a wool course, would risk the very load the course protects.

ManualExact wording for H5
Daewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33 (ManualsLib)H5 error, water temperature error in wool/delicate course: (1) water temperature in wool/delicate course is 45 °C or higher; (2) buzzer alarm 10 seconds per every 10 minutes; (3) display cleared when turning off/on power.
Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34 (via Scribd)H5: water temp. error; water temp. over 45 °C in delicate & wool course; change the thermistor wash (thermistor wash inferior/broke down).
Daewoo error-mode flowchart list (via jimcontent)H5: water temperature error in wool/delicate course; 45 °C or higher (continuation of the same list that gives H6/H8).

The H5 page wins on the manual's terms: the 45 °C threshold, the wool/delicate scope, the thermistor-wash cause, and the alarm/clear behaviour (10 s per 10 min; power off/on). The 60 °C cleaning instruction on one competitor page is invented and contradicts the service manual.

What the machine does when H5 fires

The machine reports the temperature error and sounds the buzzer alarm for 10 seconds every 10 minutes while the fault stands. The display clears when the power is turned off and on.

Common causes

  1. Water temperature over 45 °C in a wool or delicate course (the manuals' definition).
  2. The thermistor wash being inferior or broken down (the troubleshooting guide's named cause and remedy).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the course: H5 is defined for wool/delicate courses — note whether the error appeared on one of those programmes.
  2. Clear the display by turning the power off and on — the manuals' own clear method.
  3. If H5 returns on wool/delicate courses, stop and call a technician: the troubleshooting guide's remedy is to change the thermistor wash, which is service work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not run a 60 °C cleaning cycle for H5: that instruction is not in the service manual, and hot water on a wool course risks the load the course protects.

When to call a technician

  • H5 returns on wool/delicate courses after a power off/on.
  • Water in a wool/delicate course feels excessively hot — stop the machine and call a technician.

Safety first

H5 involves the heating/temperature system: never open the machine's panels to reach the thermistor or heater, and never bypass temperature protection. The manuals' user steps end at power off/on.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does H5 mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

H5 is a water temperature error in the wool/delicate course: the water temperature in those courses is 45 °C or higher. The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes and clears on power off/on. Front-loader families.

Should I run a 60 °C cleaning cycle for Daewoo H5?

No. That instruction comes from one competitor page, not the service manual — the manual defines H5 as a 45 °C+ temperature error in wool/delicate courses with a thermistor-wash remedy. A 60 °C cycle on a wool course risks the load.

How do I clear Daewoo H5?

Turn the power off and on — that is the manuals' clear method. If H5 returns on wool/delicate courses, the troubleshooting guide's remedy is changing the thermistor wash: technician work.

Is Daewoo H5 a cleaning reminder?

No. The service manual says H5 is a water temperature error (45 °C or higher in wool/delicate), not a maintenance warning. The cleaning-reminder framing contradicts the manual.
