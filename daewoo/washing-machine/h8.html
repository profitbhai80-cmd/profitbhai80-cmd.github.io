Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code H8

Quick answer

Error codeH8 — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansH8 = heater wash overheating error: the washing heater temperature increases by 5 °C or more within 30 seconds when there is no water in the tank. Check the water level and the heater wash. The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes and clears on power off/on. Front-loader families only.
Most common causeThe heater working without water in the tank — the troubleshooting guide's checks are the water level and the heater wash.
Safe to check yourself?Limited. The manuals give the trigger and the alarm behaviour; the heater-wash remedy is technician work. The user-side step is the power off/on clear.
Checked againstDaewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33 (H8 flowchart: 5 °C or more within 30 seconds, no water in tank); Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34

Applies to — read this first

H8 is the opposite heater fault to H6, and the manual gives it a trigger no competitor states: the washing heater temperature increases by 5 °C or more within 30 seconds when there is no water in the tank. The DWC-1011W service manual's flowchart and the front-loader troubleshooting guide agree, and the guide adds the checks: check the water level and the heater wash. Scope: front-loader families only.

ManualExact wording for H8
Daewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33H8 error, washing heater overheating: heater temperature increases by 5 °C or more within 30 seconds when there is no water in tank, etc.; buzzer 10 s per 10 min; cleared on power off/on.
Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34H8: heater wash overheating; heater worked without water in the tub; check water level and heater wash.

Competitors give H8 as generic 'overheating without water' and omit the 5 °C/30 s trigger that distinguishes H8 from a generic overheat. The H8-vs-H6 split (overheating vs not heating) and H8-vs-H4 split (no-water rapid rise vs 95 °C wash overheating) keep the heater family straight.

What the machine does when H8 fires

The machine detects the heater temperature rising 5 °C or more within 30 seconds with no water in the tank and reports H8, sounding the buzzer alarm for 10 seconds every 10 minutes while the fault stands. The display clears when the power is turned off and on.

Common causes

  1. The heater worked without water in the tank (the manuals' definition).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Turn the power off and on to clear the display — the manuals' own clear method.
  2. Do not run heated washes until H8 is resolved: the fault means the heater was heating with no water present.
  3. Call a technician: the troubleshooting guide's checks are the water level and the heater wash, which are service work.

Work through the steps in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not repeatedly retry heated programmes with H8 showing — the heater was overheating without water.

When to call a technician

  • H8 returns after a power off/on.
  • Any burning smell — unplug immediately and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

An overheating heater with no water is a fire-risk fault: unplug the machine, do not run heated cycles, and never open the panels to reach the heater. Call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does H8 mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

H8 is a heater wash overheating error: the heater temperature increases by 5 °C or more within 30 seconds when there is no water in the tank. The alarm sounds 10 seconds every 10 minutes; it clears on power off/on.

Is Daewoo H8 the same as H6?

No — opposite faults. H8 = the heater is OVERHEATING with no water (5 °C/30 s trigger). H6 = the heater is NOT heating (fails the 15-minute rise test).

Is Daewoo H8 the same as H4?

No. H8 = heater overheating with no water in the tank. H4 = washing temperature sensor overheating at 95 °C or higher. Different faults, different codes.

Can I keep using the machine with H8?

No. The manuals' outcome is a service call: H8 means the heater was heating without water. Do not run heated washes until it is resolved.
