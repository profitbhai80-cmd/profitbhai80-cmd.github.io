Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code E7

Quick answer

Error codeE7 — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansE7 on a Daewoo DWD/DWC drum washing machine means Direction error: the Hall signal IC reading differs from the predicted signal for the drum's direction of rotation, so the motor is stopped and 'E7' is displayed. The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes and the display clears when the power is turned off and on. This is a front-loader / washer-dryer code only — it does not exist on DWF top-loaders, and it is not a pressure-sensor code (that is E9).
Most common causeMotor-direction feedback fault — the Hall signal IC reading does not match the predicted signal for the drum's direction of rotation.
Safe to check yourself?Only in part. Switching the power off and on once to clear the display is safe user action. Everything the service manuals prescribe beyond that — checking the motor connector and changing the Hall IC — is technician work inside a mains-powered machine. Do not remove covers or probe motor wiring.
Checked againstDaewoo Washing Machine Error Codes PDF, DD front-loader platform — E7 (Direction) definition, alarm and display-clear behaviour; Daewoo DWC-ED1213 Drum Washing Machine Service Manual (Dec 2005) — E7 Direction Error troubleshooting table; Daewoo DWD-E1211R / DWD-E1211W Drum Washing Machine Service Manual — E7 Direction Error

Applies to — read this first

E7 exists only on Daewoo drum (front-loader / washer-dryer) platforms — DWD and DWC models with a direct-drive motor and Hall-sensor feedback. What you must NOT do is borrow this meaning for a DWF top-loader: no DWF top-loader manual lists E7 (the DWF-9001Q series Auto Alarm table stops at E6), and E7 is not a pressure-sensor code either — the pressure-sensor code is E9.

Daewoo DWF top-loaders — GUARDRAIL, do not borrowNo DWF top-loader manual lists E7. The DWF-9001Q series Auto Alarm table stops at E6 and the DWF-7002FL manual lists E1 to E6 only. E7 on a top-loader display belongs to a different platform's documentation.

The DD front-loader error-code document gives the trigger precisely: the Hall signal IC reading differs from the predicted signal for the direction of rotation. The DWC-ED1213 service manual's two causes are a drum moving in the opposite direction and a broken motor Hall IC (check the connector and change it). If E7 appears on a machine whose manual does not list E7, the code belongs to a different platform — do not apply this page to it.

What the machine does when E7 fires

The motor (engine) is stopped and 'E7' is displayed in the display window. The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes, and the error display is cleared when the power is turned off and on — the DD front-loader document's exact behaviour.

Common causes

  1. The Hall signal IC reading differs from the predicted signal for the drum's direction of rotation — the DD front-loader document's exact trigger. The control stops the motor and shows E7.
  2. The drum is moving in the opposite direction to the commanded direction — the DWC-ED1213 service manual's first E7 cause.
  3. The motor Hall IC is broken — the DWC-ED1213 and DWD-E1211R/W service manuals' second cause. Their remedy is to check the connector and change the Hall IC, which is technician work.
  4. A connector fault in the motor feedback circuit — the service manuals route E7 diagnosis through the motor connector before any part is changed.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Note when E7 appeared — at the start of drum movement, at a change of rotation direction, or at the start of spin — and whether the drum moved at all. That timing helps the technician and costs nothing.
  2. Switch the machine off and back on once. The DD front-loader document states the error display is cleared when the power is turned off and on.
  3. If E7 returns after that single power cycle, stop. The service manuals' remedies are to check the motor connector and change the Hall IC (and, if those do not resolve it, the motor or PCB) — all technician work. Do not keep restarting the machine to push a cycle through.
  4. When you call for service, give the full model number, the exact display (E7), and the stage of the cycle where the drum stopped. The manuals' diagnosis depends on the exact model and platform.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. The power cycle clears the display (per the DD document) but does not repair a broken Hall IC or a bad connector — a returning E7 is a technician matter, not a reason for repeated restarts.

When to call a technician

  • E7 returns after a single power off/on cycle — the Hall IC or its connector needs testing.
  • The drum does not turn, turns only one way, or judders when it should reverse — motor feedback failure for service.
  • Any burning smell, smoke, sparking, or water near electrical parts — switch off immediately and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E7 involves the motor and its feedback circuit in a mains-powered machine — never remove covers, touch motor connectors, or probe wiring. If you notice a burning smell, smoke, sparking, or water near electrical parts, switch the machine off and call a qualified technician. Do not repeatedly restart the machine to force a cycle through; a motor running without reliable direction feedback can be damaged further.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E7 mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

On Daewoo DWD/DWC drum (front-loader / washer-dryer) machines E7 is a Direction error: the Hall signal IC reading differs from the predicted signal for the direction of rotation, so the motor is stopped and E7 is displayed. The DWC-ED1213 service manual's causes are a drum moving in the opposite direction and a broken motor Hall IC.

How do I clear the E7 code on my Daewoo washer?

The DD front-loader document states the error display is cleared when the power is turned off and on. Switch the machine off and on once and note whether E7 returns. Clearing the display does not repair a broken Hall IC or a bad connector — if E7 returns, the machine needs a technician.

Is Daewoo washer E7 a pressure-sensor error?

No. E7 is a motor-direction / Hall-feedback error. The pressure-sensor (water-level sensor) code on these platforms is E9. Any table that calls E7 a pressure-sensor error is wrong.

Does E7 apply to Daewoo top-loaders?

No. No DWF top-loader manual lists E7 — the DWF-9001Q series Auto Alarm table stops at E6 and the DWF-7002FL manual lists E1 to E6 only. E7 is a drum front-loader / washer-dryer code only.
