Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code PFE

Quick answer

Error codePFE — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansPFE = pump filter error: the drain pump filter is clogged, the drain pump does not work during spin, too much or unsuitable detergent was used, or the drain hose is placed higher than 1 m above the floor. If PFE shows: turn power off, turn it on with the Power button, press SPIN, then press Start/Pause. Front-loader families only. Daewoo manuals spell it PFE — some blogs write PEF, but it is the same code.
Most common causeA clogged drain pump filter is the first named cause, followed by a non-working drain pump, detergent overdose/wrong type, or a drain hose routed too high.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals give four home checkpoints plus a specific reset sequence. Beyond those, it is technician work.
Checked againstDaewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide p.34 (PFE 4 checkpoints); Daewoo DWC-ED1232 operation table (PFE checkpoints); Daewoo DWD-HC1011 / DWD-HD9414 service manuals p.31 (PFE + reset sequence)

Applies to — read this first

PFE is the pump filter error, and Daewoo gives it both a checklist and a reset sequence — two things no competitor has together. The troubleshooting guide names four checkpoints: the drain pump filter is clogged (clean it); the drain pump does not work during spin (change it); a large amount of detergent was used (use the proper amount); the drain hose is placed higher than 1 m above the floor (do not place it above 1 m). And the DWD-HC1011/HD9414 service manuals give the reset: if PFE was displayed, turn power off, turn it on pressing the Power button, press SPIN, then press Start/Pause. Spelling note: Daewoo manuals spell it PFE; some blogs write PEF — same code, not a different one.

What the machine does when PFE fires

The washer stops during spinning or spins insufficiently: PFE is a spin-stage fault, not a general drain fault. The machine will not complete the spin while the pump-filter/detergent/hose-height condition stands.

Common causes

  1. The drain pump filter is clogged.
  2. The drain pump does not work during spin.
  3. Too much or unsuitable detergent was used.
  4. The drain hose is placed higher than 1 m above the floor.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off and unplug, then clean the drain pump filter (towels and a shallow container ready — water will come out).
  2. Check the detergent: use the proper amount of a suitable detergent — too much or the wrong type triggers PFE.
  3. Check the drain hose routing: it must not be placed higher than 1 m above the floor.
  4. Run the manual's reset sequence: turn power off, turn it on pressing the Power button, press SPIN, then press Start/Pause.
  5. If PFE returns after the checkpoints and reset, stop and call a technician — the drain pump itself may need changing, which is service work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. The reset sequence is the manuals' own procedure — use it exactly: Power button, then SPIN, then Start/Pause.

When to call a technician

  • PFE returns after the four checkpoints and the reset sequence.
  • The drain pump makes no sound or hums without pumping during spin — report it, do not open the machine.

Safety first

Drain water can be hot: let it cool before opening the pump filter, keep towels and a shallow container ready, and unplug the machine before any check. Never open the machine's panels to reach the pump itself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does PFE mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

PFE is a pump filter error: the drain pump filter is clogged, the drain pump does not work during spin, too much or unsuitable detergent was used, or the drain hose is placed higher than 1 m above the floor. Front-loader families.

How do I reset Daewoo PFE?

The service manuals' sequence: turn the power off, turn it on pressing the Power button, press SPIN, then press Start/Pause. Run the four checkpoints first (filter, pump, detergent, hose height).

Is Daewoo PFE the same as OE?

No. PFE = pump filter error at the spin stage (filter/detergent/hose-height). OE = the drain error. Different codes, different faults.

Is it PFE or PEF?

Daewoo manuals spell it PFE. Some blogs write PEF — it is the same code, not a different one. This page uses the manuals' spelling.
