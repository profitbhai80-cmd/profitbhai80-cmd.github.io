Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code H6

Quick answer

Error codeH6 — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansH6 = heater wash error: the washing heater is not working — within 15 minutes of heater operation the temperature does not rise by 2 °C or more (when the standard temperature is 42 °C or lower) or by 1 °C or more (when it is higher than 42 °C). The alarm sounds for 10 seconds every 10 minutes and clears on power off/on. Front-loader families only.
Most common causeThe heater wash is not raising the water temperature by the manual's 15-minute test — the troubleshooting guide's remedy is to change the heater wash.
Safe to check yourself?Limited. The manuals give the test logic and the alarm behaviour; the heater-wash remedy is technician work. The user-side step is the power off/on clear.
Checked againstDaewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33 (H6 flowchart with 42 °C branches); Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34

Applies to — read this first

H6 is the manual's heater test written as a code: within 15 minutes after heater operation begins, if the standard temperature is 42 °C or lower and the temperature does not increase by 2 °C or more — or if the standard is higher than 42 °C and it does not increase by 1 °C or more — the machine flags H6. And if the temperature falls below the standard by 2 °C or more due to re-supply of water, the standard resets and the 15-minute check resets with it. That full test logic, including the re-supply reset rule, is what no competitor states.

ManualExact wording for H6
Daewoo DWC-1011W service manual p.33H6 error, abnormality in washing heater: within 15 minutes after heater operation begins — if standard temperature is 42 °C or lower and temperature does not increase by 2 °C or more, or if standard is higher than 42 °C and it does not increase by 1 °C or more; if temperature falls below standard by 2 °C or more due to re-supply of water, the standard resets and the 15-minute check resets. Buzzer 10 s per 10 min; cleared on power off/on.
Daewoo front-loader troubleshooting guide pp.33-34H6: heater wash error; heater wash does not work; water temp. does not rise over 2 °C during 15 min; change the heater wash.

Competitors give H6 as a generic 'heater failure' and omit the 15-minute / 2 °C / 1 °C test logic and the re-supply reset — the only diagnostic content the manual gives. The H6-vs-H8 split (heater not heating vs heater overheating without water) and H6-vs-H4 split (heater failure vs 95 °C overheating) keep the heater family straight.

What the machine does when H6 fires

The machine detects that the heater is not raising the temperature by the manual's test and reports H6, sounding the buzzer alarm for 10 seconds every 10 minutes while the fault stands. The display clears when the power is turned off and on.

Common causes

  1. The heater wash is not working — temperature not rising by the manual's 15-minute test (the manuals' definition).
  2. A re-supply of water dropping the temperature below standard by 2 °C or more (the manual's reset condition, not a fault).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Note the conditions: H6's test only applies within 15 minutes of heater operation — a re-supply of cold water resets the check, which is normal behaviour, not a fault.
  2. Clear the display by turning the power off and on — the manuals' own clear method.
  3. If H6 returns on heated washes, stop and call a technician: the troubleshooting guide's remedy is to change the heater wash, which is service work.

Work through the steps in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not open the machine to test the heater: the manuals' user steps end at power off/on.

When to call a technician

  • H6 returns on heated washes after a power off/on.
  • Any burning smell or visible damage near the heater area — unplug and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

The heater is a mains-powered internal component. Never open the machine's panels or probe heater wiring; the manuals' user steps end at power off/on.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does H6 mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

H6 is a heater wash error: within 15 minutes of heater operation, the temperature does not rise by 2 °C or more (standard 42 °C or lower) or by 1 °C or more (standard above 42 °C). The alarm sounds 10 seconds every 10 minutes; it clears on power off/on.

What is the 42 °C branch in Daewoo H6?

The manual's test has two branches: at or below 42 °C standard, the temperature must rise by 2 °C or more in 15 minutes; above 42 °C, by 1 °C or more. If re-supplied water drops the temperature 2 °C or more below standard, the check resets — normal behaviour, not a fault.

Is Daewoo H6 the same as H8?

No — opposite heater faults. H6 = the heater is NOT heating (temperature fails the 15-minute rise test). H8 = the heater is OVERHEATING with no water in the tank (5 °C or more within 30 seconds). Competitors often blur both into 'heater problem.'

Is Daewoo H6 the same as H4?

No. H6 = heater wash error (not heating). H4 = washing temperature sensor overheating at 95 °C or higher. Different faults, different codes.
