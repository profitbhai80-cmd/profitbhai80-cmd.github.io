Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code OE

Quick answer

Error codeOE — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansDrain fault — the water is not draining. The Daewoo service manual puts it precisely: the water level did not reach the reset point after drainage started.
Most common causeA clogged drain pump filter or a kinked/blocked drain hose.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the filter and hose checks below, with the machine switched off and unplugged. Have towels ready; water will come out.
Checked againstDaewoo D-CV701PC series service manual (OE = failure in water drainage); Daewoo D-MU123 user manual troubleshooting table (OE = DRAIN ERROR); Daewoo washing machine service error-code document (QE = water does not drain for more than 15 minutes — the same fault on other model families)

Applies to — read this first

Daewoo uses two different display codes for the same drain fault depending on the model family. Check your manual for which one your machine uses.

Code on your displayModel documentationMeaning
OEDaewoo D-CV701PC series service manual; Daewoo D-MU123 user manualFailure in water drainage — the water level did not reach the reset point after drainage started.
QEDaewoo washing machine service error-code document"This error occurs when water does not drain more than 15 minutes."

Same fault, same fixes. If your machine shows QE instead of OE, everything below still applies.

Common causes

  1. The drain pump filter (behind the small flap at the bottom front) is clogged with lint, coins, hair or debris.
  2. The drain hose is kinked, crushed behind the machine, or pushed too deep into the standpipe so it seals it shut.
  3. The standpipe or sink drain the hose feeds into is blocked.
  4. Excessive foam from too much detergent is slowing the drain (the machine cannot pump foam away properly).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the machine off and unplug it. If the drum is full of water, bail out what you can with a jug first — the filter flap will release the rest.
  2. Open the drain pump filter flap at the bottom front. Put a towel and a shallow tray under it, unscrew the filter slowly, and remove lint, coins, buttons and debris.
  3. Rinse the filter under a tap, check its housing for stuck objects, then screw it back in firmly.
  4. Pull the machine out only if it moves freely; check the full length of the drain hose for kinks, crushing or a bend sharper than a gentle curve.
  5. Check the hose end is not jammed airtight into the standpipe — it needs an air gap to drain freely.
  6. Plug back in and run a rinse and spin cycle, watching the hose area and the filter flap for drips.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • The code returns immediately with a clean filter and a clear hose — the drain pump itself may have failed.
  • You hear the pump humming or straining but no water moves.
  • Water leaks from the pump area or the filter housing when the machine drains.
  • There is a burning smell or the machine trips the circuit breaker during draining.

Safety first

Always switch off and unplug the washing machine before cleaning the filter or touching hoses. Water will pour out of the filter housing — keep towels down and never work with wet hands on a machine that is still connected to power. Never run the machine with the filter flap removed.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does OE mean on a Daewoo washing machine?

OE is a drain fault: the water is not draining properly. The Daewoo D-CV701PC series service manual defines it as a failure in water drainage where the water level did not reach the reset point after drainage started. The Daewoo D-MU123 user manual lists OE as "DRAIN ERROR" in its display error table.

What is the difference between OE and QE on a Daewoo washing machine?

They are the same drain problem on different model families. Some Daewoo families display OE for the drain fault, while the Daewoo service error-code document uses QE for it: "This error occurs when water does not drain more than 15 minutes." If your manual lists QE instead of OE, the causes and fixes are identical.

How do I clear Daewoo washing machine error OE?

Switch the machine off, open the drain pump filter flap (with a towel and a shallow tray ready), remove any lint, coins or debris, and refit it firmly. Check the drain hose for kinks and that it is not pushed too deep into the standpipe. Then run a rinse and spin cycle.

Can I keep washing with OE showing?

No. With a drain fault the cycle cannot finish: dirty water stays in the drum, the spin is skipped, and clothes come out soaking wet. Fix the cause before running another full cycle.
