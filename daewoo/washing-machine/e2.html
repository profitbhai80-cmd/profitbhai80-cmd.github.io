Daewoo Washing Machine Error Code E2

Quick answer

Error codeE2 — Daewoo Washing Machine
What it meansE2 means the lid is open on Daewoo DWF/DQ top-loaders. Both Daewoo manuals define it identically, and the manual's tactic is specific: open the lid, put the clothes evenly and symmetrically, then close the lid. It is a safety-stop code, not a parts fault — and it is not the overflow code that E2 means on Daewoo front-loaders.
Most common causeThe lid is open or was opened during operation; the machine stops and waits for the lid to be closed properly.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — fully. E2's fix is opening/closing the lid and arranging laundry: no tools, no panels, no water or electrical contact.
Checked againstDaewoo Washing Machine User Manual — DWF-9001Q, DWF-1401Q, DWF-1801Q, DWF-2001Q (Daewoo Electronics Sales & Services (M) Sdn Bhd); DAEMA DWF-2028ZD Fully Automatic Washing Machine User Manual (Model DWF-2028ZD)

Applies to — read this first

E2 belongs to Daewoo's DWF/DQ top-loader families — two manufacturer user manuals print the identical Auto Alarm table with E2 = 'The lid is open'. Do NOT borrow meanings from other Daewoo platforms: on DWD front-loaders E2 is an overflow alert (drum service manual), and on the Turkish Vestal platform E01 is the door fault. This page is the top-loader counterpart to the published LE page (LE = door opened on DWD front-loaders; E2 = lid opened on DWF top-loaders).

The top-loader lid-open counterpart of front-loader LE is E2.

The manual's E2 tactic is unusually specific — 'Open the lid and put the clothes evenly and symmetrically, then close the lid' — pairing the lid check with a redistribution, since a lid opened mid-spin usually means the user intervened on a shaking machine. Note on sources: two manuals.plus renderings of the DWF-9001Q document split E0's wrapped problem cell across rows, shifting that rendering's Problem column down one row; the clean rendering (E0's problem kept in one cell) reads E2 = 'The lid is open', consistent with the published LE research.

What the machine does when E2 fires

The machine stops the cycle and shows E2 with its alarm when the lid is open or opened mid-cycle. It waits — the manual's tactic implies the cycle resumes after the lid-and-load check and the lid is closed properly. This is a safety interlock response, not a component fault.

Common causes

  1. The lid was opened during the wash or spin process — the manual's safety section assumes the lid stays shut while running.
  2. The lid was not closed properly before START/PAUSE was pressed — the manual's troubleshooting asks: 'Is the top cover of the washing machine close?'
  3. Laundry piled above the rim or the detergent box preventing the lid from seating fully.
  4. The lid was opened to redistribute an unbalanced load (E1) and not closed again before resuming.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Open the lid fully and check nothing — bunched laundry, the detergent box, a stray item — is stopping it from seating properly.
  2. Put the clothes evenly and symmetrically around the tub (the manual's verbatim tactic), keeping the highest point of the load below the balance ring.
  3. Close the lid properly until it seats fully — the manual's troubleshooting asks: 'Is the top cover of the washing machine close?'
  4. Press START/PAUSE to resume the cycle; the machine continues once the lid is registered closed.
  5. If E2 appears the moment any cycle starts even with the lid firmly closed, the lid switch/sensor itself may be faulty — stop and call a technician rather than forcing the lid.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. E2 clears by closing the lid — do not let any page talk you into testing inlet valves or pressure switches for it; those are front-loader E2 parts, not top-loader E2 parts. If a check needs the lid switch removed or wiring tested, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E2 stays on with the lid firmly closed and properly seated — the lid switch or its wiring is suspect.
  • The lid feels loose, misaligned, or will not stay shut on its own.
  • E2 alternating with E6 (child-lock lid alarm) on every cycle — an interlock fault rather than a user error.

Safety first

Never open the lid or put your hand in the tub while the machine is washing or spinning — wait until all motion stops; the manual's own warning stands. If you opened the lid because the machine was shaking violently, redistribute the load before resuming rather than just slamming the lid shut.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E2 mean on a Daewoo top-load washing machine?

E2 means the lid is open. Both Daewoo DWF manuals define it identically: 'The lid is open' — and the tactic is to open the lid, put the clothes evenly and symmetrically, then close the lid. This applies to DWF/DQ top-loader families.

How do I clear Daewoo top-loader E2?

Follow the manual verbatim: open the lid, arrange the clothes evenly and symmetrically in the tub, close the lid properly, then press START/PAUSE to resume. Check nothing — laundry or the detergent box — is stopping the lid from seating fully.

Some sites say Daewoo E2 means overflow or a water-intake problem. Is that right?

Not on a DWF/DQ top-loader. Overflow (E2) and water-intake faults belong to Daewoo's DWD front-loader platform and its service manual — on the DWF top-loader platform both manufacturer manuals define E2 as 'The lid is open'. Code meanings do not transfer across platforms.

One site told me to inspect the rubber door seal for E2. Should I?

No — that advice is front-loader-only. DWF top-loaders have no door rubber seal; the manual's E2 tactic is about the lid and the load distribution, not a seal. Generic lists that mention seals are not written for top-loaders.

Is Daewoo top-loader E2 the same as LE?

They are the platform counterparts: LE = door opened on Daewoo DWD front-loaders, E2 = lid opened on DWF/DQ top-loaders. Same user action, different code per platform.
