Daewoo Dishwasher Error Code E1

Quick answer

Error codeE1 — Daewoo Dishwasher
What it meansE1 = water filling exceeded the preset time ("Longer inlet time") — the inlet valve was open for 4 minutes without the water level/quantity reaching its target. On EGSP-family models the buzzer sounds 30 seconds and the INTENSIVE program LED flickers fast; on MQ-family models "E1" shows on the digital display. It is NOT a door-latch code — that reading on some websites contradicts both Daewoo service manuals. Do not confuse it with Daewoo washer E1 (the DWC-UD121DC manual is a WASHING MACHINE manual), or with F5 (insufficient water — a different code system on the UK line).
Most common causeThe water tap is not fully open, the inlet hose/filter is restricted, or household water pressure is too low — the DDWMJ1411W manual's own checks. The MQ manual adds a drain-pump causality note: if water is not draining out, the pressure switch acts first and cuts the inlet valve, so no water gets in and E1 fires.
Safe to check yourself?Partially: the manuals' own user-side checks (open the tap fully, check the inlet hose and filter for restriction, confirm water pressure) are homeowner-safe. The manuals' supplementary-inlet rule means E1 can also fire on the second fill window, not just the first. Let the drain run finish after E1 (EGSP: until the pressure switch resets + 60 seconds; MQ: until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes) — do not interrupt it.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E1 is a dishwasher fill-timeout code on two Daewoo DW platforms — the EGSP/GSP pressure-switch family (LED-signal models) and the MQ flowmeter family (digital-display models). The meaning is identical on both: the inlet valve was open for 4 minutes without the fill target being reached. The indication differs (INTENSIVE LED vs digital "E1") — match your model family. Do NOT merge E1 with Daewoo washer E1 (the DWC-UD121DC manual is a WASHING MACHINE manual; the DWF-7002FL washer E1 is a 20-minute inlet fault — washer-only), with F5 (insufficient water — different code system, UK Vestel line), or with "1E" threads (a different code string).

What the machine does when E1 fires

EGSP family: the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds, the INTENSIVE program LED flickers quickly, all other loads are cut immediately, and the drain pump runs until the pressure switch resets and continues draining for 60 seconds more. MQ family: the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds, "E1" shows on the digital display, all other components stop immediately, and the drain pump runs until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes.

Common causes

  1. The water tap is not opened, or water intake is restricted, or water pressure is too low — the DDWMJ1411W manual's own cause row.
  2. The supplementary water inlet time exceeded 4 minutes — the EGSP manual's own second-fill rule: E1 can fire on the second fill window, not just the first.
  3. The drain pump has failed — the MQ manual's own causality note: if water has not drained out, the pressure switch acts first to cut the inlet valve in the next inflow, so no water gets in and E1 occurs.
  4. The inlet valve, pressure switch (EGSP) or flowmeter (MQ) itself is faulty — technician diagnosis.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Make sure the water tap is fully open, the inlet hose is not kinked or restricted, and household water pressure is normal — the DDWMJ1411W manual's own user checks.
  2. Clean the inlet hose filter if flow looks restricted.
  3. Power off and on to clear the display; let the drain run finish (EGSP: until the pressure-switch reset + 60 seconds; MQ: until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes) — do not interrupt it.
  4. If E1 returns with a good supply, the MQ manual's causality note applies: check whether the drain pump is actually draining — a drain failure can present as E1.
  5. If E1 keeps returning, call a qualified technician — inlet-valve, pressure-switch/flowmeter, and drain-pump diagnosis is service work.

The manuals' user-side scope is the water supply (tap, hose, filter, pressure). The inlet-valve, pressure-switch, flowmeter, and drain-pump diagnostics belong to a technician. There is no invented reset flow here — the steps above are the manuals' own checks and reaction rules.

When to call a technician

  • E1 returns immediately with the tap fully open and normal household pressure.
  • E1 fires on the supplementary (second) fill window — the EGSP rule, not a new fault.
  • The machine drains poorly or not at all — the MQ manual's own drain-pump-causes-E1 note.
  • Other error codes appear alongside E1 — then the machine has a fault beyond the fill path.

Safety first

E1 involves water and mains-powered electronics together: switch the machine off before touching the inlet hose or its filter, and never dismantle the inlet valve or drain pump yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E1 mean on a Daewoo dishwasher?

E1 means water filling exceeded the preset time — "Longer inlet time". The inlet valve was open for 4 minutes without the water level/quantity reaching its target. It is verified verbatim in two Daewoo DW technical service manuals: EGSP-130E/13E/90E/9E (p.49) and DDW-MQ1214S (pp.28–30).

Why does E1 appear on the second fill too?

The EGSP manual's supplementary-inlet rule: "When the supplementary water inlet time is longer than 4 minutes, E1 also comes on." E1 is a 4-minute-per-fill-window rule, not a first-fill-only rule.

What is the difference between E1 and F5?

Different platforms, different code systems. E1 (EGSP/MQ platforms) = fill exceeded the 4-minute preset time. F5 (UK Vestel-platform line, DA1D5/DA1E6) = insufficient water. Do not cross-read them.

What do the 30-second buzzer and the flickering LED mean?

On EGSP/GSP pressure-switch models: the buzzer alarms 30 seconds and the INTENSIVE program LED flickers fast while the drain pump runs. On MQ digital models: "E1" shows on the display with the same 30-second buzzer. Same fault, different indication — match your model family.

Can a bad drain pump cause E1?

Yes — the DDW-MQ1214S manual says so verbatim: "failure of drain pump may cause E1, because if the water has not drained out, in next inflow process the pressure switch will act first to cut down the inlet valve, thus no water will get in and E1 error will occur."
