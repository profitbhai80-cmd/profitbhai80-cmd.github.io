Daewoo Dishwasher Error Code E8

Quick answer

Error codeE8 — Daewoo Dishwasher
What it meansE8 = diverter valve fault — the diverter (alternative-washing / distribution) valve failed to signal, or failed to reach its commanded position. On MQ models (DDW-MQ1214S): no diverter feeding signal within 15 seconds, or the valve not positioned accurately within 60 seconds. Reaction: the drain pump runs until the flowmeter stays motionless for 2 minutes, all other components stop immediately, and the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds. E8 exists ONLY on diverter-equipped models (alternative washing / dual-zone wash).
Most common causeThe diverter valve circuit is faulty — the MQ manual's first named check is whether the diverter valve circuit is correct, and to reconnect the wire of the diverter valve.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: no manual reset procedure exists for E8. Check first whether your model even has a diverter (an Alt / dual-zone button means it does). Power-cycle once; if E8 returns, the diverter valve circuit or the valve itself needs a technician.
Checked againstDaewoo DDW-MQ1214S Technical Service Manual, Error code pp.28-30 + Daewoo DDW-M1215 Instruction Manual, p.19 ("Error codes")

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E8 is a diverter-only code: it can occur only on models fitted with a diverter (alternative-washing / dual-zone) valve. The meaning is identical on both named Daewoo documents — the MQ service manual (DDW-MQ1214S, digital "error 8") and the M1215 user manual (DDW-M1215, "Divider vale failed") — so there is no meaning split, only a scope split: if your model has no Alt / dual-zone wash function, E8 cannot occur on it, and the display means something else. Do NOT merge E8 with Daewoo WASHER E8 (motor / Hall-sensor error, a different appliance) or with the G-platform W60B row ("Water diversion system") — that is the same diverter fault under a third name, on a Midea-branded document, not a separate Daewoo code.

What the machine does when E8 fires

The drain pump runs until the flowmeter stays motionless for 2 minutes, all other components stop immediately, and the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds. In a wash program the diverter valve stops and E8 is set off after the program ends. In the test program the MQ manual lists E8 among the valid codes (E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E8).

Common causes

  1. Diverter valve circuit fault — the MQ manual's first named check: "Check whether the Diverter Valve circuit is correct or not. Reconnect the wire of Diverter Valve and solve the problem."
  2. Detection failure, not a confirmed dead valve: no feeding signal within 15 seconds, or the valve not positioned accurately within 60 seconds.
  3. Wrong-code reading — Daewoo washer E8 is a motor / Hall-sensor error (different appliance); repairserviceuae's dishwasher table has no E8 row at all, while its E4 ("Overload error") is wrong on every platform.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check whether your model has a diverter / alternative-wash function (an Alt / dual-zone button on the panel means it does). If it has none, E8 cannot occur on it — the display means something else.
  2. Power-cycle the machine once; if E8 clears, it may have been a transient read.
  3. If E8 persists after power-cycle, the diverter valve circuit or the valve itself is suspect — call a qualified technician (per the manuals).

The steps above are the manuals' own routing. This page does not invent multimeter or valve-replacement DIY steps — the MQ manual's only E8 check is the diverter-valve circuit and its wiring. The M1215 user manual's generic overflow warning ("If overflow occurs, turn off the main water supply before calling a service") is not E8-specific and is not presented as an E8 fix.

When to call a technician

  • E8 persists after one power-cycle — the diverter valve circuit or the valve needs service.
  • There is water in the base pan — turn off the main water supply before calling a service (M1215 generic overflow warning).

Safety first

E8 involves the diverter-valve circuit in a mains-powered dishwasher: never open the machine or probe the valve circuit yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E8 mean on a Daewoo dishwasher?

E8 = diverter valve fault. On MQ models (DDW-MQ1214S) the controller sets E8 when it detects no diverter feeding signal within 15 seconds, or the diverter valve is not positioned accurately within 60 seconds. The M1215 user manual prints the same fault as "Divider vale failed". It is verified in two named Daewoo DW manuals.

Does E8 mean the diverter valve is dead?

No - E8 means the controller could not confirm the valve: no feeding signal within 15 seconds, or no accurate positioning within 60 seconds. The MQ manual's first check is the diverter valve circuit and its wiring, not the valve itself. Only a technician can confirm whether the valve has failed.

Why does the drain pump run when E8 shows?

That is the documented E8 reaction in the MQ manual: the drain pump runs until the flowmeter stays motionless for 2 minutes, all other components stop immediately, and the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds.

Does my Daewoo dishwasher have E8?

Only if it has a diverter / alternative-washing (dual-zone) function - look for an Alt or dual-zone button. EGSP models, the DDW-M1212 and the whole G platform have no E8. And Daewoo washer E8 is a different code (motor / Hall-sensor error) - do not apply it to a dishwasher.
