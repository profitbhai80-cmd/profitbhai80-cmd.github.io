Frequently asked questions

What does FE mean on a Daewoo dishwasher?

FE means a faulty electronic card on Daewoo's UK-line dishwashers (DA1D5 / DA1E6 series, Vestel platform). It is verified verbatim in two Daewoo user manuals: the DA1D5SI0ES/DA1D5SW0ES and DA1E6FI0ES/DA1E6FW0ES manuals, both reading "FE | Faulty electronic card | Contact for service."

What is the "electronic card"?

The main control board — the manual's term for the appliance's central electronics.

How is FE different from F6 or F8?

F6 = faulty heater sensor and F8 = heater error — component-level heater faults. FE = the control card itself is faulty — a board-level fault. The manuals separate them.
