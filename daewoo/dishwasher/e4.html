Daewoo Dishwasher Error Code E4

Quick answer

Error codeE4 — Daewoo Dishwasher
What it meansE4 = Overflow - water has reached the overflow level (leak into the base or overfill) and held the overflow switch for more than 2 seconds. The drain pump runs automatically, all other components stop, the buzzer alarms for 30 seconds, and E4 carries the HIGHEST priority - once E4's operation completes, every other error operation is invalid. E4 is NOT an "overload" error - that claim on repairserviceuae.com is fabricated.
Most common causeWater in the base (leak) or overfill/over-foaming; the MQ manual adds a stuck-open inlet valve ("The valve pole is rusted or locked by dreg, so the valve can't open or close. It will cause the E1 or E4 error").
Safe to check yourself?Limited: no manual reset procedure exists for E4. The manuals' stated reactions run automatically; the manual-grounded leads are finding the water source (leak, stuck-open inlet valve, overfill). If E4 recurs immediately, the overflow switch circuit or PCB is suspect - technician work.
Checked againstDaewoo DDW-MQ1214S Technical Service Manual, Error code pp.28-30 + Daewoo EGSP-130E / EGSP-13E / EGSP-90E / EGSP-9E Technical & Service Manual, second error-code block (manualslib p.50)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E4 is a dishwasher overflow code on two Daewoo DW platforms - the MQ flowmeter family (DDW-MQ1214S, digital display) and the EGSP pressure-switch family (EGSP-130E/13E/90E/9E, LED-signal models). The meaning is identical on both: the overflow switch held for more than 2 seconds. The sensor differs (microswitch on MQ; pressure switch 140/120 on EGSP) and the indication differs ("error 4" on the MQ display; INTENSIVE + ECO LEDs flickering on EGSP). Do NOT merge E4 with the DDW-G1211F 2011 UK line, where E4 = temperature sensor malfunction - a different Daewoo DW code system entirely.

What the machine does when E4 fires

MQ family: the drain pump runs until the flowmeter stays motionless for 2 minutes, all other components stop immediately, the buzzer alarms 30 seconds, and "error 4" shows. EGSP family: the appliance activates the drain pump, cuts all other loads, the buzzer alarms 30 seconds, INTENSIVE + ECO LEDs flicker, and the drain pump runs until the pressure switch resets (+ 60 seconds), then re-checks the switch repeatedly.

Common causes

  1. Water has leaked into the base and reached the overflow level - the manual's own condition.
  2. The inlet valve is stuck open ("The valve pole is rusted or locked by dreg, so the valve can't open or close. It will cause the E1 or E4 error" - the MQ manual's theory of parts).
  3. Overfill or over-foaming - water reaching the overflow level without a leak.
  4. The overflow switch circuit itself is faulty - if E4 recurs immediately with no water source found.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Let the documented reaction finish: the drain pump runs automatically (MQ: until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes; EGSP: until the pressure switch resets + 60 seconds) - do not interrupt it.
  2. Find and fix the water source: a leak into the base, a stuck-open inlet valve (the MQ manual's named E4 cause), or overfill/over-foaming.
  3. The code clears when the overflow switch resets after the water source is fixed - no manual reset procedure exists.
  4. If E4 recurs immediately, the overflow switch circuit or PCB is suspect - call a qualified technician (per the manuals' inspection sections).

The steps above are the manuals' own reaction rules and diagnostic leads. This page does not invent multimeter or float-switch DIY steps - neither manual gives a homeowner reset procedure for E4.

When to call a technician

  • E4 recurs immediately after the base is dry - the overflow switch circuit or PCB needs service.
  • E4 appears with no water in the base and a closing inlet valve - the pressure switch (EGSP) or microswitch (MQ) itself is suspect.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E4 means water where it should not be, in a mains-powered dishwasher: switch it off before any inspection, and never dismantle the inlet valve, overflow switch or PCB yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E4 mean on a Daewoo dishwasher?

E4 = Overflow - the overflow switch held for more than 2 seconds. It is verified verbatim in two named Daewoo DW manuals: DDW-MQ1214S (pp.28-30) and EGSP-130E/13E/90E/9E (second error block, manualslib p.50).

Does E4 always mean a leak?

No - overfill/over-foaming or a rusted/stuck-open inlet valve also trips it (the MQ manual's own defeat points). The code clears when the overflow switch resets after the water source is fixed.

Can I reset Daewoo dishwasher E4?

No manual reset procedure exists. The drain pump runs its documented cycle automatically; the code clears when the overflow switch resets. If it recurs immediately, the switch circuit or PCB needs service.

Why does the drain pump keep running with E4?

That is the documented E4 reaction: MQ runs the pump until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes; EGSP runs it until the pressure switch resets + 60 seconds, then re-checks the switch repeatedly. Do not interrupt it.

Is E4 an overload error?

No - repairserviceuae.com's "Overload error - too many items loaded or a jammed spray arm" is fabricated. Both Daewoo manuals define E4 as Overflow with highest priority; overloading a dishwasher cannot produce E4.
