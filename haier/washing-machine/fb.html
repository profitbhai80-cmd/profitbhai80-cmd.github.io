Haier Washing Machine Error Code Fb

Quick answer

Error codeFb — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansFb = water overflow: a leak has reached the base pan and the float has tripped the water-overflow protection. The machine shuts down all functions automatically except the drain pump, which keeps running for safety. The manuals' first instruction is immediate and simple: close the tap. Do NOT confuse Fb with E8 — E8 is the water-protective-level error (water signal above the protective level, often a self-syphoning drain hose), a different mechanism with a different fix.
Most common causeA leak inside the machine — hose, seal, dispenser or pump — letting water reach the base pan where the overflow float sits.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — close the tap immediately (the manuals' own first step), then stop and call after-sales service. The protection logic runs the drain pump automatically; your step is the tap.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Fb is an overflow-protection code, not a fill code. Across eight Haier front-load manual families (HW70B1426S, INTELIUS 500/HW80BD, HW80-BD16266, HW90B14266A, HWF75DW1/HWF85DW1, HWD100-BD1499U1, HW08-CP1439S, HW100/HW110-BD14397U1S), the Fb row is the same: water overflow — close the tap immediately — contact after-sales service. Front-load families only. And keep Fb separate from E8: both codes sit in the same manuals, but E8 is "Water protective level - error" (water signal above the protective level — the manuals' named user-checkable cause is a self-syphoning drain hose), while Fb is a base-pan leak float. Different mechanisms, different first steps.

What the machine does when Fb fires

The machine shuts down all functions automatically except the drain pump, which is activated for safety reasons — the overflow protection is on the deepest part of the appliance and trips when leakage reaches it.

Common causes

  1. A leaking hose, door seal, dispenser or pump letting water reach the base pan.
  2. Water collecting at the deepest part of the appliance, where the overflow float sits.
  3. Not an inlet-valve problem: Fb is the base-pan leak float, not the solenoid valve — do not chase the valve.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Close the tap immediately — the manuals' own first step, on all eight families.
  2. On the HW100/HW110-BD14397U1S family the manual adds: open the drain pump to drain, shut down the machine.
  3. Do not open the machine or reset the electronics first — the protection logic runs the drain pump automatically; your step is the tap.
  4. Then contact the after-sales service — the leak that tripped the float needs a technician to find and fix.

The home scope is one action: the tap. The drain pump runs itself for safety; everything after the tap is after-sales service work.

When to call a technician

  • Fb shows at all — the base-pan float has tripped, and the leak source needs a technician.
  • Water visibly pooling under or inside the machine.
  • Fb returns after service — the leak was not found the first time.

Safety first

Fb involves water and mains-powered electronics together: switch off at the outlet before touching anything wet, never bypass the overflow protection, and never open the machine's panels yourself — that is service work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does Fb mean on a Haier washing machine?

Fb means water overflow: a leak has reached the base pan and tripped the water-overflow protection. It is verified in eight Haier front-load manual families with the same row: "Fb | Water overflow. | Close tap immediately. Contact the After sales service."

What should I do first when Fb appears?

Close the tap immediately — the manuals' own first step. The drain pump will run automatically for safety; then contact after-sales service.

Is Fb the same as E8?

No. E8 is the water-protective-level error (water signal above the protective level, often a self-syphoning drain hose — "Auto release. Otherwise contact the After sales service."). Fb is the base-pan leak float ("Close tap immediately"). Same manuals, different rows, different mechanisms.

Why does the drain pump keep running with Fb?

The manuals' warning note: "In case of leakage all functions will be shut down automatically except drain pump which will be activated for safety reasons." The pump running is the protection working, not a second fault.

Why do some sites say Fb is a solenoid valve problem?

An aggregator claims suspicion falls on a solenoid valve not closing — but the manuals define Fb as the base-pan leak float, a leak-protection code. Chasing the inlet valve misses the actual mechanism.
