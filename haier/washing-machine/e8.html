Haier Washing Machine Error Code E8

Quick answer

Error codeE8 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansE8 = water protective level error: the water has exceeded the protective level. On B14979 families a self-syphoning drain hose is a named cause — check the drain hose installation; otherwise contact after-sales service. Front-load families only; twin-tub code tables were not used for this page.
Most common causeA self-syphoning drain hose letting the level rise wrongly (the B14979 named cause), or water genuinely above the protective level.
Safe to check yourself?Partly. The B14979 manual's own home check is the drain hose installation. Beyond that the manuals say: contact after-sales service — E8 is not a user-serviceable diagnosis.
Checked againstHaier HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 user manual §9.2 (self-syphoning hose + protective level); Haier HW70B1426S user manual; Haier HW70-B14929-S user manual; Haier HW7014829 user manual; Haier HLC1700AXS manual (above warning level)

Applies to — read this first

E8 is the protective-level code, and it must not be confused with the level sensor (that is FA). Five front-load and washer-dryer manuals agree: the water has exceeded the protective level. The B14979 manual adds a cause no competitor states — a self-syphoning drain hose — with 'check drain hose installation' as the home check. Scope: front-load families only. Twin-tub tables were not used, and their E8 meanings must not be imported.

The key competitor error is aysonline's table, which calls E8 a water level SENSOR error — that is FA, not E8. E8 is the protective level being exceeded; FA is the sensor circuit. This page keeps the manual's split.

What the machine does when E8 fires

The machine reports that the water has risen above its permitted protection level and will not continue normally: B14979 pairs it with a self-syphoning hose check, HW70B1426S notes an auto-release behaviour, and the HLC1700AXS manual says to stop running. It is a risk-condition warning, not a confirmed parts diagnosis.

Common causes

  1. A self-syphoning drain hose (the B14979 named cause) — check the drain hose installation.
  2. Water genuinely above the protective level during fill or operation.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Stop the cycle and check the drain hose installation: it must not be self-syphoning — the B14979 manual names this as the cause to check.
  2. If the hose installation looks correct, or E8 returns after the check, contact after-sales service — every manual's solution for E8 ends there.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not run more cycles to see whether the warning goes away: the manuals treat E8 as an active risk condition.

When to call a technician

  • E8 returns with the drain hose installation correct.
  • Water visibly high in the drum or continuing to enter — unplug and call service rather than retrying.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E8 involves water above a safety threshold: do not keep restarting the machine to test it. If water is visibly overfull, unplug the machine and keep the area dry while you wait for service.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E8 mean on a Haier washing machine?

On front-load families E8 is a water protective level error: the water has exceeded the protective level. The B14979 manual names a self-syphoning drain hose as a cause and says to check the drain hose installation; otherwise contact after-sales service.

Is Haier E8 a water level sensor fault?

No — that is FA. E8 = protective level exceeded; FA = water level sensor (circuit) error. One competitor table prints E8 as the sensor fault, which is backwards against every manual.

What is a self-syphoning drain hose?

The B14979 manual's named E8 cause: a drain hose installation that lets water siphon out or back wrongly, disturbing the level the machine expects. Checking the drain hose installation is the manual's home step for E8.

Can I keep using the machine with E8 showing?

No. The manuals say to stop and contact after-sales service if the hose check does not resolve it. E8 marks a risk condition, not a message to ignore.
