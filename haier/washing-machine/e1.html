Haier Washing Machine Error Code E1

Quick answer

Error codeE1 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansDrainage failure — the machine cannot empty the water. Exact manual wording differs slightly by family (see the series table).
Most common causeA blocked or badly installed drain hose (top-loaders); a clogged pump filter (front-loaders).
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the external hose and filter checks below. Do not open the machine's body.
Checked againstHaier HWM90DD user manual; Haier HWM120-1678ES5 manual; Haier HWM170-B278S6 manual; Haier AU HWT09/HWT10/HWT12 top-loader guide; Haier official EG/AE top-load manuals; Haier HW70B1426, HW90B14266A, HW90BP14636 front-load manuals; Haier HWF85ANB1/HWD80-B14979 manuals

Applies to — read this first

Haier's manuals are unusually consistent about E1: every family that uses it means it the same way — a drainage fault. But the fixes differ between top-loaders and front-loaders, and some families don't use E1 at all.

Model familyWhat E1 means here
Haier top-loaders — HWM90DD, HWM120-1678ES5, HWM170-B278S6, HWT09/HWT10/HWT12 and similar (Haier AU, EG and AE manuals)Drainage failure: the drain hose is blocked; the machine does not drain or drains slowly (drainage time over about 8 minutes).
Haier European/UK front-loaders — HW70B1426, HW70B1426S, HW80-B14266A, HW90B14266A, HW90BP14636, HW80BD (Intelius 500), HW08-CP1439SDrainage error: the water was not emptied within about 4 minutes.
HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 washer-dryer and HWF85ANB1/HWF90ANB1 (NZ/AU) front-loadersThis series does not use E1. A drainage fault shows as CLR FLTR (water not fully drained within 6 minutes) instead.

E1 vs E2 vs E4 — the confusion checker

Many websites mix these three codes up, so check you are chasing the right one before you start:

If your model is…E1E2E4
Haier top-loader (HWM90DD, HWM120-1678ES5, HWM170-B278S6, HWT-series)Drainage failure — drain hose blockedTop lid not closedWater inlet failure — fill time exceeded
Haier front-loader (HW70B1426 family, HW90B14266A, HW90BP14636, HW80BD)Drainage error — water not emptied in ~4 minutesDoor lock errorWater level not reached (inlet), ~8 minutes

If your manual lists none of these E-codes and your display shows CLR FLTR, you have the B14979/HWF85ANB1 family — treat it exactly like the E1 drainage fault above.

Common causes

  1. The drain hose is blocked or kinked behind the machine (top-loaders).
  2. The drain hose end is installed wrong — lying flat on the floor, pushed too far down the waste pipe, or set below the minimum height, so the machine cannot push water out.
  3. The pump filter is clogged with lint, coins, buttons or hair (front-loaders).
  4. A partial blockage that lets water out slowly, so the machine times out before the drum is empty.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the machine off and unplug it at the socket before touching the filter or the hose. Water and electricity are a dangerous mix.
  2. Pull the machine out only if it moves freely. Check the full length of the drain hose for kinks and blockages, and straighten any bend. Do not force a built-in unit.
  3. Check the drain hose installation: the end must not be lying flat on the floor or pushed deep into the standpipe. On top-loaders the manuals require the end to sit at least about 10 cm above floor level and properly installed; on front-loaders the height must be between 80 and 100 cm.
  4. On front-loaders, clean the pump filter: open the service flap at the front bottom, place a shallow tray and towels down, open the emergency drain hose cap and let the water out, then unscrew the filter, clean it under running water, and refit it firmly.
  5. On top-loaders, after clearing any blockage the manuals say to press the Start/Pause button once more to continue.
  6. Run a short cycle and watch whether the machine drains normally and the code stays away.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E1 returns immediately even with a clear, correctly installed hose and a clean pump filter.
  • You can hear no drain pump sound at all, or the filter chamber was completely clear yet water stays in the drum.
  • There is water leaking under or behind the machine.
  • There is any electrical burning smell, or the machine trips the breaker.

Safety first

Always switch off and unplug the washing machine before moving it or disconnecting hoses, and keep the floor dry while you work. Water and electricity are a dangerous mix — never work on a machine that is connected to power with wet hands or a wet floor.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E1 mean on a Haier washing machine?

On every Haier washing machine family that uses it, E1 is a drainage fault: the machine cannot empty the water. On top-loaders (HWM90DD, HWM120-1678ES5, HWM170-B278S6, HWT-series) the manuals describe it as a blocked drain hose; on the European HW-B1426 front-load family it means water was not emptied within about 4 minutes. Some families, like the HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 washer-dryer and the HWF85ANB1/HWF90ANB1 series, do not use E1 at all — their drainage code is CLR FLTR.

Why do other websites say E1 is a water inlet or door fault?

Because they copy a single universal meaning without checking any manual. The Haier manuals put inlet failures at E4 and lid or door-lock faults at E2 on these families; E1 is the drainage code in every manual that lists it. A site that says E1 is a fill fault or a door fault is wrong.

How do I clear Haier washing machine E1?

Unplug the machine first. On top-loaders, unblock and straighten the drain hose and check its end height, then press Start/Pause once more. On front-loaders, open the service flap at the front bottom, drain the emergency hose into a tray, unscrew the pump filter, clean it under running water, refit it firmly, and run a short cycle to confirm.

My Haier manual has no E1. What should I look for?

You probably have one of the families that does not use E1, such as the HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 washer-dryer series or the HWF85ANB1/HWF90ANB1 front-load series. On those machines the drainage fault shows as CLR FLTR (water not fully drained within 6 minutes), not E1. Check for that code on your display instead.

Does E1 mean my drain pump is broken?

Usually not. In the manuals the first suspects are a blocked or badly installed drain hose and, on front-loaders, a clogged pump filter — both external checks. If the code returns immediately after a clear hose and a clean filter, then the pump or its wiring is the next suspect and that is technician territory.
