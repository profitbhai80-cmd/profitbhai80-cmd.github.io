Haier Washing Machine Error Code E5

Quick answer

Error codeE5 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansE5 means three different things depending on your model family. On HLT364XXQ and GWT900AW/GWT700AW/GWT800AW models it is the water level sensor fault — the manuals say to call service. On HWM70-A models it is water temperature too high (over 60°C): the digital tube displays E5 and rings, and after cooling you press Start/Pause to resume. On HW70-B12929-S and HWD80/100/120-B14979 washer-dryer models it is a drainage error: water not fully drained within 1 minute on the Refresh (i-Refresh) programme — clean the drain-pump filter and check the drain hose (HW150-BP14986E variant: 3-minute window, select no spin speed and run Spin to drain).
Most common causeDepends on the family: a failed water level sensor (HLT/GWT families — call service), incoming water that is too hot, over 60°C on HWM70-A (let it cool, then press Start/Pause), or water not fully drained within 1 minute on the Refresh programme (HW70-B12929-S / HWD-B14979 — clean the drain-pump filter, check the drain hose).
Safe to check yourself?The temperature branch and the drainage branch, partly: letting the water cool, checking hose connections, and pressing Start/Pause are user-safe, and so are cleaning the drain-pump filter and checking the drain hose for kinks. The level-sensor branch has no safe home fix — the manuals' remedy is after-sales service.

Applies to — read this first

E5 is a split-personality code: it means three different things on three Haier washer families, and the remedy flips with the meaning. On HLT364XXQ and GWT900AW/GWT700AW/GWT800AW models, E5 is the water level sensor fault — and the manuals' only remedy is to call service. On HWM70-A models, E5 is water temperature too high (over 60°C) — and that one IS user-fixable: let the water cool, then press Start/Pause. On HW70-B12929-S and HWD80/100/120-B14979 washer-dryer models, E5 is a drainage error on the Refresh (i-Refresh) programme — water not fully drained within 1 minute — and that one is also user-fixable: clean the drain-pump filter and check the drain hose. Update (2026-11-08): this third branch was added today; the overflow/drainage FAQ and the B14979 "no E5" note were corrected at the same time. Check your model family on the rating label before you do anything — see how to find your model number.

The same fault wears different codes on other families, which is why the model check matters: the water level sensor fault is E6 on the template top-load families (HWM70-0588H and relatives) and FA on washer-only B14979 front-loads — the washer-only B14979 family has no E5 row, but the washer-dryer HWD-B14979 family DOES (Refresh drainage). The HLTW500AXW temperature branch is a variant of the temperature meaning (hot water too cold, or cold water too hot, with a hose-connection check), not the HWM70-A over-60°C definition. Do not confuse E5 with Haier dishwasher E5 (a water-supply code on a different appliance).

What the machine does when E5 fires

HLT/GWT branch: the display shows E5 for a water level sensor fault and the manuals give no user remedy — the machine is telling you to call service. HWM70-A branch: the digital tube displays E5 and rings while the water temperature is over 60°C; the machine waits until the water cools, then resumes when you press Start/Pause. Refresh branch: on HW70-B12929-S / HWD-B14979 models the display shows E5 when the water is not fully drained within 1 minute of the Refresh programme; the manual's fix is to clean the drain-pump filter and check the drain hose (HW150-BP14986E: 3-minute window, select no spin speed and run Spin to drain).

Common causes

  1. HLT/GWT families: the water level sensor has failed — the manuals state no other cause and no home check for this branch.
  2. HWM70-A family: the incoming water is too hot — over 60°C, per the HWM70-A manual. The same manual's safety section warns not to use water hotter than 50°C inside the tub.
  3. HW70-B12929-S / HWD-B14979 families: the water was not fully drained within 1 minute on the Refresh programme — the drain-pump filter screen is dirty or the drain hose is kinked or blocked (the manuals' own causes).
  4. HLTW500AXW temperature branch: the hot and cold hoses are swapped (hot to cold, cold to hot), or the hot-water heater is not supplying hot water — the manual's own checks for its E5 water-temperature problem.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Identify your model family on the rating label first — HLT/GWT, HWM70-A, or something else. See our guide to finding your model number. The fix depends entirely on this step.
  2. If your model is HLT364XXQ or GWT900AW/GWT700AW/GWT800AW (level-sensor branch): power-cycle once — unplug, wait a few minutes, replug — to rule out a glitch. If E5 returns, stop: the manuals' remedy is after-sales service, and they forbid user repair. Do not open the machine.
  3. If your model is HWM70-A (temperature branch): turn the hot tap down or off and let the water in the tub cool. Check that the hot and cold hoses are connected to the proper valves (hot to hot, cold to cold) — swapped hoses are the HLTW500AXW manual's own check for its temperature branch.
  4. After cooling, press the Start/Pause knob to resume — the HWM70-A manual's own remedy. If E5 returns with cool water and correctly connected hoses, the temperature sensing needs a technician.
  5. If your model is HW70-B12929-S or HWD80/100/120-B14979 (Refresh drainage branch): clean the filter screen of the drainage pump, then check the drain-hose installation for kinks or obstructions. On HW150-BP14986E models, select no spin speed and run a Spin cycle to drain the water. If E5 returns after that, switch the machine off, unplug it, and contact customer service.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. The three branches must not be mixed: never apply the temperature-branch fix (cool + restart) to a level-sensor E5, never apply the drainage-branch fix to a level-sensor E5, and never call a technician for a temperature E5 before letting the water cool. If a check needs the machine opened or a part tested, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E5 returns after a power cycle on an HLT/GWT model — the water level sensor fault is confirmed as a service matter.
  • E5 returns with cool water and correctly connected hoses on an HWM70-A model — the temperature sensing itself is faulty.
  • Any water leaking near electrical parts, burning smell, or sparking — switch off immediately and call a qualified technician.
  • E5 returns after cleaning the pump filter and checking the drain hose on a HW70-B12929-S / HWD-B14979 model — the drainage fault needs service.
  • E5 on a washer-only B14979 front-load or a template top-load model — those models have no E5, so check the code again against its own manual rather than applying this page. (The washer-dryer HWD-B14979 DOES have E5 — Refresh drainage, covered above.)

Safety first

Unplug the machine before touching hoses or moving it, and close the tap before disconnecting any hose — water and electricity are a dangerous mix. Never use water hotter than 50°C inside the tub (HWM70-A safety section) and never open the machine or attempt sensor repairs yourself: both manuals forbid user repair. If you see water leaking near electrics, switch the machine off and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E5 mean on a Haier washing machine?

It depends on the family. On HLT364XXQ and GWT900AW/GWT700AW/GWT800AW models, E5 means the water level sensor fault — call service. On HWM70-A models, E5 means the water temperature is too high (over 60°C): the digital tube displays E5 and rings, and after cooling you press Start/Pause. On HW70-B12929-S and HWD80/100/120-B14979 washer-dryer models, E5 means a drainage error: water not fully drained within 1 minute on the Refresh (i-Refresh) programme — clean the drain-pump filter and check the drain hose (HW150-BP14986E variant: 3-minute window, select no spin speed and run Spin to drain). Same code, three different faults — check your rating label.

How do I fix Haier washer E5 on an HLT or GWT model?

You don't — not at home. Both manuals' only remedy for the water level sensor fault is 'Call service,' and both manuals forbid user repair ('Do not repair it by yourself'). Power-cycle once to rule out a glitch; if E5 returns, call after-sales service.

How do I fix Haier washer E5 on an HWM70-A model?

Let the water cool down, check that the hot and cold hoses are connected to the proper valves (hot to hot, cold to cold), then press the Start/Pause knob to resume — the manual's own remedy. If E5 returns with cool water and correctly connected hoses, the temperature sensing needs a technician.

How do I fix Haier washer E5 on a Refresh programme?

Clean the drain-pump filter screen, then check the drain-hose installation for kinks or obstructions — the manuals' own fix for HW70-B12929-S and HWD80/100/120-B14979 models. On HW150-BP14986E models, select no spin speed and run a Spin cycle to drain the water. If E5 returns after that, switch the machine off, unplug it, and contact customer service.

Some sites say Haier E5 is a water overflow or drainage error. Is that right?

Partly. Overflow is still wrong everywhere — overflow protection is F2 on the HLT-family table, and overflow territory is E8/Fb, not E5. But drainage IS correct on one branch: on HW70-B12929-S and HWD80/100/120-B14979 models E5 is a Refresh-programme drainage error (water not fully drained within 1 minute). Drainage is E1 only on Haier top-loads. The three verified E5 meanings are the water level sensor fault (HLT/GWT), water temperature too high (HWM70-A), and Refresh drainage (front-load / washer-dryer BP platform).
