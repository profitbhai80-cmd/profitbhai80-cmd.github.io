Haier Washing Machine Error Code EC3

Quick answer

Error codeEC3 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansEC3 = water level sensor fault — the water level sensor circuit is short-cut or open. The manual prescribes no user fix: "Warning and stop running" — technician-only. This code exists only on the XQG50 220V international washer-dryer models — the HWD1000 table has NO EC1, EC2 or EC3 rows. Do not merge EC3 with: digitron top-load E6 ("The water level sensor is bad" — different code, different platform); front-load B14979 FA ("Water level sensor error" — different code, different platform); B14929-S E8 ("Water level protection error"); UK Err8 (water level); or EC2 (temperature sensor).
Most common causeThe water level sensor circuit is short or open — the manuals give the fault class, not a cause tree, and document no user fix.
Safe to check yourself?No user fix is documented. Call a technician (likely the pressure/level sensor, its hose or wiring, or the control board). Do not invent checks the manual does not give.

What the machine does when EC3 fires

The display shows EC3 and the machine warns and stops running — the water level sensor circuit is short or open.

Common causes

  1. The water level sensor circuit is short-cut or open — the manual's fault class (likely the pressure/level sensor, its hose or wiring, or the control board; the manuals name no cause).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Call a technician — the manual's own and only prescription ("Warning and stop running"; no user fix documented). Likely the pressure/level sensor, its hose or wiring, or the control board.

Stating this honestly: the manuals document no user fix for EC3, so there is no invented fix flow here.

When to call a technician

  • EC3 shows at all — the sensor circuit needs a technician (the manuals document no user fix).

Safety first

EC3 involves the sensor circuit and mains-powered electronics: never open the machine or touch sensors, hoses or wiring yourself — the repair is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does EC3 mean on a Haier washing machine?

EC3 means a water level sensor fault — the water level sensor circuit is short-cut or open. It is verified verbatim in two Haier manuals: the XQG50-S1216 user manual and the XQG50-S1016 operation manual, with the action "Warning and stop running".

What is the difference between EC3 and E13?

EC3 = the water LEVEL SENSOR is faulty (circuit short/open) — a sensor fault. E13 = the water level is actually ABOVE the warning level (overfill) — a level fault. Different rows, different faults.

Is EC3 the same as the E6/FA "water level sensor" codes?

No. Haier reuses the water-level-sensor meaning across platforms with different codes: combo EC3, top-load E6, front-load B14979 FA, B14929-S E8, UK Err8. Match your platform and your code — this page is combo EC3.

Does my HWD1000 have EC3?

No — the HWD1000 table has no EC1, EC2 or EC3 rows. EC3 exists only on the XQG50 220V international models.
