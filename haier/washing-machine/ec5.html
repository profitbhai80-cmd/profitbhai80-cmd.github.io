Haier Washing Machine Error Code EC5

Quick answer

Error codeEC5 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansEC5 = dry temperature sensor fault — the drying sensor circuit is short or open. The manual prescribes no user fix: "Warning and stop running" / "Ask the technician to repair it." This is a washer-dryer COMBO platform code only (XQG50 / HWD1000 families). Phrase honesty: the HWD1000's labeled rows are definitive — "The drying sensor circuit short or open" belongs to EC5, NOT to EC2, at whose position the XQG50 hosts' OCR misplaces it. Do not merge EC5 with EC2 (water temperature sensor), EC1 (heater), EC4 (air vent temperature), or justanswer's AI "heating system fault" reading of EA3.
Most common causeThe drying sensor circuit is short or open — the manuals give the fault class, not a cause tree, and document no user fix.
Safe to check yourself?No user fix is documented. Call a technician (likely the dry thermistor, its wiring, or the control board). Do not invent checks the manual does not give.

What the machine does when EC5 fires

The display shows EC5 and the machine warns and stops running — the drying sensor circuit is short or open.

Common causes

  1. The drying sensor circuit is short or open — the manual's fault class (likely the dry thermistor, its wiring, or the control board; the manuals name no cause).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Call a technician — the manual's own and only prescription ("Warning and stop running"; "Ask the technician to repair it"). Likely the dry thermistor, its wiring, or the control board.

Stating this honestly: the manuals document no user fix for EC5, so there is no invented fix flow here.

When to call a technician

  • EC5 shows at all — the drying sensor circuit needs a technician (the manuals document no user fix).

Safety first

EC5 involves the drying circuit and mains-powered electronics: never open the machine or touch sensors or wiring yourself — the repair is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does EC5 mean on a Haier washing machine?

EC5 means a dry temperature sensor fault — the drying sensor circuit is short or open. It is verified verbatim in the Haier XQG50-S1216 user manual and the HWD1000 front-load washer & dryer user manual ("EC5 The dry temperature sensor | The drying sensor circuit short or open | Warning and stop running | Ask the technician to repair it").

What is the difference between EC5, EC2 and EC1?

The sensor trio: EC5 = dry temperature sensor (drying circuit); EC2 = water temperature sensor (wash circuit); EC1 = heater (water temp not changing in 10 min, wash continues cold). Three different rows, three different faults.

What is the difference between EC5 and EC4?

EC5 = the dry temperature SENSOR circuit is short or open (a sensor fault). EC4 = the air-vent TEMPERATURE is not rising during the dry phase (a temperature condition). Different rows, different faults.

Does my top-load Haier have EC5?

No — EC5 exists only on the XQG50/HWD1000 washer-dryer combo platform.
