Haier Washing Machine Error Code F7

Quick answer

Error codeF7 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansF7 = motor error (motor circuit error). Contact after-sales service. Front-load families only. Not drainage (that is E1/CLR FLTR) and not communication (that is FC).
Most common causeThe machine is detecting a motor error. The manuals name no cause beyond the motor fault itself and prescribe no user diagnosis.
Safe to check yourself?No user repair step exists: five manuals print contact-service as the solution. Confirm the code, power-cycle once, then call service.
Checked againstHaier HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 §9.2; Haier HW70B1426S; Haier HW70-B14929-S; Haier HW7014829; Haier HLC1700AXS manual (motor circuit error)

Applies to — read this first

F7 is motor-only across five manuals — four front-load user manuals print 'motor error' and the HLC1700AXS washer-dryer manual prints 'motor circuit error,' all with contact-service as the solution. The competitor noise is two AI-generated forum summaries that add communication or drainage meanings the manuals do not contain. F7 is the motor; FC is communication; E1/CLR FLTR is drainage. This page keeps all three apart.

ManualExact wording for F7
Haier HW80/HW100/HW120-B14979 §9.2; HW70B1426S; HW70-B14929-S; HW7014829F7: motor error. Contact after-sales service / customer support / maintenance personnel. No user step prescribed.
Haier HLC1700AXS manual (p.31 error codes)F7: motor circuit error; stop running; service needed.

The two forum errors this page corrects: drainage advice for F7 (drainage is E1/CLR FLTR territory) and communication advice for F7 (communication is FC territory). The manuals give F7 a motor-only meaning.

What the machine does when F7 fires

The control reports a motor error and the manuals give no further machine behaviour: F7 is a motor-circuit fault, and the prescribed outcome is to stop and arrange service.

Common causes

  1. The machine has detected a motor error / motor circuit error (the manuals' full extent of the diagnosis).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the display reads F7 (not FC, not E1/CLR FLTR): F7 = motor; FC = communication; E1/CLR FLTR = drainage.
  2. Power-cycle once: switch off, unplug for a few minutes, plug back in and retry. If F7 returns, the motor fault is genuine.
  3. Contact after-sales service — that is the manual's own solution, and there is no user step beyond it.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not open the machine to reach the motor or its wiring: the manuals prescribe contact-service only.

When to call a technician

  • F7 returns after a power-cycle — the motor fault is genuine; call after-sales service.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

The motor is a mains-powered internal component. Never open the machine's panels, never probe motor wiring, and never bypass safety devices to force a spin; the manuals say to contact after-sales service.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F7 mean on a Haier washing machine?

F7 is a motor error (motor circuit error) on front-load families. Five manuals agree, all prescribing contact after-sales service.

Is Haier F7 a drainage problem?

No. Drainage faults are E1/CLR FLTR in the manuals; F7 is the motor. The drainage framing comes from AI-generated forum summaries, not the manuals.

Is Haier F7 a communication error?

No — communication errors are FC/FC0/FC1/FC2. F7 is the motor; FC is board-to-board communication. Different faults, different codes.
