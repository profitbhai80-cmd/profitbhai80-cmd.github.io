Haier Washing Machine Error Code F2

Quick answer

Error codeF2 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansF2 = water overflow / overflow error — the machine's overflow protection has tripped (water level above the safe limit). This is a TOP-LOAD platform code only: the HWM90/HWM200/HWM100-316/GWT top-load families. Front-loaders use Fb (base-pan flood float) and E8 (water protective level) instead — do not merge F2 with Fb or E8. All five manuals route F2 straight to service: "Please contact Customer Care." There is no user repair step in any manual — and this page invents none.
Most common causeWater is entering or standing abnormally — all five manuals give the meaning and the service prescription, but no cause tree. The adjacent rows' only user-checkable items are the inlet hose and the tap.
Safe to check yourself?The manuals give no homeowner fix: "Please contact Customer Care." The safe moves are: switch the machine off, close the water tap (do not leave it filling unattended), and call service.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F2 is a top-load-platform code, not a universal Haier code. The HWM90DD, HWM200-M1990DD/1678, HWM100-316S6/S8 and GWT950AW top-load manuals all define F2 as overflow — and all route it straight to service. The HW front-load manuals carry NO F2 row: front-loaders use Fb (base-pan leak float — "Close tap immediately") and E8 (water protective level — "Auto release") instead. Do NOT merge F2 with Fb or E8 — separate codes, separate platforms, separate first steps. Do NOT import the portable-washer F2 (drain fault — different platform, different code set).

What the machine does when F2 fires

The display shows F2 — the overflow protection has tripped. All five manuals' action is the same: contact Customer Care.

Common causes

  1. Water is standing or entering above the safe level — the overflow condition itself.
  2. The inlet hose is loose or the tap is leaking in — from the same manuals' adjacent rows, general context only (not F2 steps).
  3. A level-sensing or valve fault the manuals do not detail — technician diagnosis.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the machine off and close the water tap — do not leave a machine showing F2 filling unattended.
  2. If the water inlet hose is visibly loose, fasten it — from the same manuals' adjacent rows, general context only (not an F2 fix).
  3. Contact Customer Care — the manuals' own and only instruction for F2. Do not open the machine.

Stating this honestly: all five manuals route F2 straight to service and give NO user repair steps, so there is no invented fix flow here. The steps above are safety moves (stop the water, stop the machine, call service) — the diagnosis and repair are technician work.

When to call a technician

  • F2 shows at all — the manuals' uniform instruction is "Please contact Customer Care."
  • Water keeps entering with the machine off — turn the tap off and call service.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F2 is an overflow fault: water and mains-powered electronics together. Switch the machine off before touching anything wet, never leave it filling unattended, and never open the machine's panels — the repair is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F2 mean on a Haier washing machine?

F2 means water overflow — the machine's overflow protection has tripped. It is verified verbatim in five Haier top-load manuals: HWM90DD ("The protection of water overflow"), HWM200-M1990DD and HWM200-1678 ("Over flow Error"), HWM100-316S6/S8 via Haier's own download server ("OVERFLOW ERROR"), and GWT950AW ("Water overflow protection").

Is F2 the same as Fb or E8?

No. F2 is the top-load overflow code. Front-loaders use Fb (base-pan leak float — "Close tap immediately") and E8 (water protective level — "Auto release") instead. Separate codes, separate platforms, separate first steps — the HWM200-M1990DD manual lists F2 and E8 as separate rows.

Why does this page give no fix steps?

Because the manuals give none. All five manuals route F2 straight to "Please contact Customer Care." Pages that invent multimeter tests or valve fixes for F2 are guessing — this page does not.

My portable Haier washer shows F2 — same code?

No. Portable washers use a different code set, and their F2 is a drain fault — wrong platform. This page covers the HWM90/HWM200/HWM100-316/GWT top-load families.

What is "overflow protection" on a top-loader?

The machine's safety trip when water rises above the safe level — the control flags it as F2 and the manuals send you to Customer Care rather than a homeowner reset.
