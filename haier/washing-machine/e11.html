Haier Washing Machine Error Code E11

Quick answer

Error codeE11 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansE11 = water-supply fault — the water level cannot reach the reset water level within 5 minutes (a fill timeout at the start of the cycle). The machine warns and stops running. This is a washer-dryer COMBO platform code only (XQG50 and HWD1000 families) — it does not exist on Haier top-loads or HW front-loads. Do not import the Haier heat-pump E11 (zone/pool/DHW config) or other brands' E11 (Hoover, Frigidaire, Electrolux, Bosch) — wrong appliances, wrong meanings.
Most common causeThe water tap is closed or only partially open, household water pressure or supply is low, the inlet hose is kinked, or the inlet-valve mesh filter is clogged with grit — the manual's own check.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manual's own check is "Check if the water pressure or water supply is low": confirm the tap is fully open, pressure is normal, the hose is unkinked and the inlet filter is clean.
Checked againstHaier XQG50-S1216 user manual ("Warning code" table) + Haier HWD1000 (Front Load Clothes Washer & Dryer) user manual (explicitly labeled E11 row) + Haier XQG50-S1016 operation manual (same table, third model)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E11 exists ONLY on Haier's XQG50 / HWD1000 washer-dryer combo platform — the 5-minute fill-timeout code. It is absent from the digitron top-load tables (E0–E9) and from the HW front-load E1/E2/E4/E8/F-series tables. Do NOT merge E11 with E1 (drainage or fill depending on family), with the Haier heat-pump E11 (zone/pool/DHW DIP-switch configuration — different appliance), or with Hoover/Frigidaire/Electrolux/Bosch E11 (other brands, other meanings).

What the machine does when E11 fires

The display shows E11 and the machine warns and stops running — the fill phase has timed out at 5 minutes without reaching the reset water level.

Common causes

  1. The water tap is closed or only partially open — the simplest cause, check first.
  2. Household water pressure or supply is low — the manual's own named check.
  3. The inlet hose is kinked or crushed along its visible length.
  4. The inlet-valve mesh filter is clogged with grit or sediment.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Make sure the water tap is fully open.
  2. Check that household water pressure / supply is normal — the manual's 5-minute fill window fails on weak supply.
  3. Check the inlet hose for kinks and the inlet-valve mesh filter for grit.
  4. Restart the machine; if E11 returns immediately on fill, it is a real supply fault, not a glitch.

The home scope is the tap, pressure, hose and filter — all within the manual's own check ("Check if the water pressure or water supply is low"). If E11 returns immediately on fill with a good supply, the inlet valve or level sensing needs a technician.

When to call a technician

  • E11 returns immediately on fill with the tap fully open and normal household pressure.
  • Water enters slowly or not at all despite a clear filter and unkinked hose — the inlet valve is suspect.

Safety first

E11 involves water and mains-powered electronics together: switch the machine off before touching the inlet hose or filter, and never dismantle the inlet valve yourself — that is service work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E11 mean on a Haier washing machine?

E11 means a water-supply fault — the water level cannot reach the reset water level within 5 minutes (a fill timeout). It is verified verbatim in the Haier XQG50-S1216 user manual and the HWD1000 washer & dryer user manual.

What is the difference between E11, E12 and E13?

On the same warning-code table: E11 = water supply fill timeout (water level not reached in 5 minutes), E12 = drainage timeout (8 minutes), E13 = water-supply overfill (water above the warning level). Different rows, different faults.

What is the "reset water level"?

The manual's phrase for the target water level the machine must reach to proceed — E11 fires when the level cannot reach it within the 5-minute fill window.

Is E11 a heat-pump code?

No. A Haier heat-pump "E11" (zone/pool/DHW configuration) is a different code on a different appliance. For a washing machine, E11 is the fill-timeout code on the XQG50/HWD1000 washer-dryer combo platform.

Does my top-load Haier have E11?

No — E11 exists only on the XQG50/HWD1000 washer-dryer combo platform. Digitron top-loads and HW front-loads have no E11 row in their manuals.
