Haier Washing Machine Error Code E4

Quick answer

Error codeE4 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansE4 means the water level was not reached in time — a water-fill failure. The timeout differs by family (12 minutes on the B14979 front-load, about 15 on most top-loads, 30 on the XQSB60-JN, 8 in Haier's generic list) but the meaning is the same on every family we checked. Distinguish from FA (water-level SENSOR failure) and E8 (drain-hose self-syphoning).
Most common causeThe tap is closed or partly open, water pressure is too low, or the inlet valve/filter is blocked.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own checklist (tap, pressure, inlet valve filter, lid open/close) is all user-safe.

Applies to — read this first

E4 is refreshingly consistent: every Haier family we checked defines it as the fill timeout — only the timeout value moves (12 minutes front-load B14979, about 15 minutes most top-loads, 30 minutes XQSB60-JN, 8 minutes Haier's generic list). The diagnostic triangle the competitors never draw: E4 = water not arriving (supply problem, your checks below); FA = the water-level SENSOR is bad (technician); E8 = the drain hose is self-syphoning the water away (installation).

Do not confuse: FA = water level sensor failure (sensor fault, technician); E8 = drain-hose self-syphoning on the B14979 family (installation fault). Several ranking pages assign E4 to 'door lock' or 'unbalanced load' — every Haier manual contradicts both.

What the machine does when E4 fires

The machine tries to fill for the family's timeout period, then gives up and displays E4 — the wash never starts. On most models the display rings or buzzes with the code.

Common causes

  1. The water tap is closed or only partly open.
  2. The water supply is interrupted or the pressure is too low.
  3. The inlet valve or its mesh filter is blocked.
  4. The inlet hose is kinked.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the tap is fully open and water flows from it.
  2. Straighten the inlet hose; check for kinks.
  3. Close the tap, disconnect the inlet hose, and clean the inlet valve mesh filter; reconnect.
  4. Open and close the lid/door once, then restart — the manuals' own retry step.
  5. If E4 persists with good supply and a clean filter, the inlet valve itself may have failed — technician territory.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. E4 is a supply problem in nearly every case — the manual's checklist resolves it without parts. If a check needs the machine opened or the valve tested electrically, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

E4 vs FA vs E8 — which one do you actually have?

Three Haier codes look alike and are routinely swapped on ranking pages, so use this decision guide before you act. E4 means water is not arriving in time — a supply problem, and the checks below are yours. FA means the water-level sensor itself is bad — that is a technician fault, not a supply problem. E8, on the B14979 front-load family, means the drain hose is self-syphoning the water away — an installation fault, not a supply fault either.

When to call a technician

  • E4 with a confirmed-good supply, straight hose, and clean inlet filter.
  • No water entering at all (inlet valve failure).
  • Water entering then stopping (valve or pressure-switch fault).
  • Any FA code alongside E4 — a sensor fault needs a technician.

Safety first

Close the tap before disconnecting the inlet hose and unplug the machine before moving it. Water and electricity are a dangerous mix — mop up spills and never work on a powered machine with wet hands. Inlet-valve electrical tests are service-staff work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E4 mean on a Haier washing machine?

E4 means the water level was not reached in time — a water-fill failure. The timeout is 12 minutes on the B14979 front-load, about 15 on most top-loads. The meaning is the same on every Haier family we checked.

How do I fix Haier washer E4?

Open the tap fully, check water pressure, straighten the inlet hose, clean the inlet valve mesh filter, then open and close the lid/door once and restart — the manuals' own checklist.

Is Haier E4 a door lock error?

No — one ranking page claims E4 = door lock error, but every Haier manual defines E4 as the fill failure. The door/lock code on the B14979 front-load is E2.

What is the difference between Haier E4, FA and E8?

E4 = water not arriving in time (supply problem — your checks); FA = the water-level sensor itself is bad (technician); E8 = the drain hose is self-syphoning water away (installation). Different causes, different fixes.

E4 keeps coming back after I check the tap — what next?

If the supply is confirmed good, the hose is straight and the inlet filter is clean, yet E4 returns, the inlet valve itself may have failed — that is technician territory. Water entering then stopping points to a valve or pressure-switch fault, and any FA code alongside E4 means a sensor fault, which also needs a technician.

Is Haier E4 an unbalanced-load error?

No. Unbalanced load is Unb on the B14979 front-load family, and one city repair page's claim that E4 means unbalanced load is wrong — every Haier manual defines E4 as the fill timeout. The same page is wrong on E1 and E2 as well, so do not use it for any Haier code.
