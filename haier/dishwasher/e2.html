Haier Dishwasher Error Code E2

Quick answer

Error codeE2 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE2 on a Haier XS dishwasher means the dishwasher does not fill with water. With a display it shows as ERROR E2 with a short audio signal; models without a display show it as 2 LED flashes with a 5-second pause, repeating. This fill meaning belongs to the XS family only — on DW9, DW12 and HDW12 families E2 has no fault meaning at all and the manual simply says to contact the nearest authorised service centre, so DW/HDW owners must not follow the XS fill diagnosis.
Most common causeOn XS models: the water supply is closed, the supply hose is bent or crushed, the drain pipe is at the wrong height, or the inlet ('sand') filter is clogged.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, on XS models — the manual's own four checks (supply open, hose straight, drain-pipe height, sand filter) are safe home checks. On DW9/DW12/HDW12 models there is no home diagnosis for E2: contact the authorised service centre.
Checked againstHaier XS 6B0M3PDB User Manual §19 (manua.ls, p.45); Haier XS 6B0S3FSB User Manual §19 (manua.ls); Haier DW9-UFE3 User's Manual (manualzilla, E2/E6 service line); Haier DW12-TFE1/DW12-TFE2 User Manual (manualsdump, E2/E6 service line)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Haier dishwasher E2 flips between families and every blog list gets it wrong. On the newer XS family E2 is a fill fault with four manual checks. On the DW9, DW12 and HDW12 families E2 is paired with E6 as a 'contact the nearest authorised service centre' line with no fault meaning at all. Check which family you own before you touch anything.

Haier washing-machine E2 — do not borrowHaier washing-machine E2 is a door/lid fault (door or lid not closed mid-cycle), a different appliance with a different code system. It must never appear on a dishwasher page except as this warning.

The trap on the web is total: every blog list we checked calls E2 a drainage code, which is wrong on both families — XS E2 is a fill fault, DW E2 is service-only, and drainage on XS models is E3. The XS fill checks overlap the DW family's E5 supply checks in substance, but the codes are not interchangeable across families.

What the machine does when E2 fires

On XS models the dishwasher fails to fill: with a display it shows ERROR E2 with a short audio signal, and on display-less models the LEDs flash twice, pause 5 seconds, and repeat. The wash cannot proceed without water, so the machine waits on the fill fault rather than running a dry cycle.

Common causes

  1. The water supply tap is closed or only partly open.
  2. The water supply hose is bent or crushed behind the machine.
  3. The drain pipe is installed at the wrong height, so water cannot stay in the tub.
  4. The inlet ('sand') filter is clogged and blocking the fill.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Make sure the water supply tap is fully open and water flows from it.
  2. Pull the machine out just enough to check the supply hose is not bent or crushed, then push it back without kinking the hose.
  3. Check the drain pipe is at the proper height given in the installation section of your manual.
  4. Close the water supply, unscrew the drain pipe from the back of the dishwasher and check that the 'sand' (inlet) filter is not clogged; clean it if it is, then reconnect.
  5. If the supply is open, the hose is straight, the height is correct and the sand filter is clean, stop: contact the Technical Assistance Centre with your model and serial number — that is the manual's own next step.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. On DW9, DW12 and HDW12 models none of these steps is a diagnosis for E2 — if your machine is one of those families, skip straight to the authorised service centre.

When to call a technician

  • E2 on a DW9, DW12 or HDW12 machine — the manual gives no home diagnosis for these families.
  • XS E2 persists with the supply open, the hose straight, the drain-pipe height correct and the sand filter clean.
  • Any sign of water leaking from the machine — switch off, close the tap and call the service centre; do not run cycles on a leaking dishwasher.

Safety first

E2 is a water fault, not an electrical fault — but the dishwasher is still a mains appliance with water inside it. Close the water supply before disconnecting any hose, mop up spills, and never work on a powered machine with wet hands. Never open the machine's panels or touch internal valves or wiring.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E2 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

On XS models E2 means the dishwasher does not fill with water — with a display it shows as ERROR E2 with a short audio signal, and on display-less models as 2 LED flashes with a 5-second pause. On DW9, DW12 and HDW12 families E2 has no fault meaning: the manual says to contact the nearest authorised service centre.

Is Haier dishwasher E2 a drainage problem?

No — not on either family, even though every blog list we checked says so. XS E2 is a fill fault; drainage on XS models is E3. On DW9/DW12/HDW12 models E2 is a service-only code. Following drainage advice for E2 sends you to the wrong hose.

My dishwasher is a DW9 and shows E2 — do I follow the fill checks?

No. The DW9, DW12 and HDW12 manuals pair E2 with E6 as a 'contact the nearest authorised service centre' line. You may still check that the tap is open, but the manual gives you no fill meaning for E2 on those families, so do not treat the XS fill steps as your diagnosis.

How do I read E2 on an XS model with no display?

Count the LED flashes: E2 is 2 flashes, then a 5-second pause, repeating. That flash pattern is how display-less XS models report the fill fault.

What if all four XS checks pass and E2 is still there?

Stop and contact the Technical Assistance Centre with your model and serial number — that is the manual's own next step once the supply, hose, drain-pipe height and sand filter have all been checked.
