Haier Dishwasher Error Code E60

Quick answer

Error codeE60 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE60 = heating fault: the water is not reaching temperature / the heating system is faulty. On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A60 - NOT F60: no F60 appears in any Haier dishwasher document. This is the HEATER fault; the sensor fault is E70/A70. The official guidance is book-a-service only.
Most common causeThe heating system is faulty - "Heating fault" (Haier) / "Water temperature error" (F&P) describe the same failure. The manuals name no component-level sub-cause for A60.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the official article gives no homeowner steps ("Please book a service."); the F&P manual's only user action is one OFF/ON cycle. No manual documents heater-resistance or high-limit-thermostat DIY for A60.

What the machine does when E60 fires

The display shows E60 (or A60 on newer panels) with the water not reaching temperature - the heating system has failed its task.

Common causes

  1. Heating fault - the official article's own row.
  2. Water temperature error - the F&P manual's own wording for the same failure.
  3. The manuals name no further sub-cause for A60.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again (the F&P manual's only user action); if the fault persists, call Customer Care.
  2. The official article's routing: "Please book a service."

The steps above are the manuals' full response - nothing more exists in either document. This page does not invent heater-resistance or high-limit-thermostat DIY steps (appliancemode suggests them; no manual documents them for A60 - his resistance table is his data, not repeated here).

When to call a technician

  • E60 persists after one OFF/ON cycle - the heater assembly, high-limit thermostat, or their wiring needs service.

Safety first

E60 involves the heater and mains-powered electronics together: never open the machine, never probe heater terminals, and never replace a heater, thermostat or sensor yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E60 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E60 = heating fault - the water is not reaching temperature ("A60 Heating fault" in Haier's official article; "A60, Water temperature error" in the F&P DW24U6I1 guide). On newer displays it shows as A60.

Is E60 the same as F60?

No. There is no F60 in any Haier dishwasher document - appliancemode's "Also seen as F60" is wrong. The verified service code is A60.

Is E60 a sensor fault?

No - that is E70/A70 (NTC thermistor fault). E60/A60 is the heating-system fault. Different components, different codes.

Is E60 the same as single-digit E3 or E8 heating codes?

No. Those belong to the older single-digit platforms; HDW E60/A60 is the two-digit platform's heating fault. Different code systems - do not cross-read them.
