Haier Dishwasher Error Code FC0

Quick answer

Error codeFC0 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansFC0 = wash-pump / motor fault - the wash pump motor circuit is faulty, so the pump that circulates water through the spray arms cannot run. Haier names the component (wash pump); F&P names the failure class (motor fault). Compatible wordings, not identical. Spelling: Haier spells it "FC0" (zero); F&P spells it "FCO" (letter O). F&P scopes the row "(DW24U6I only)".
Most common causeThe wash pump motor circuit is faulty. Do not claim which motor part failed - neither document says.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the F&P manual's only user action is switch OFF at the power supply, wait a few moments, switch ON again. Haier's article says book a service. No manual documents opening the machine or testing the wash-pump motor.
Checked againstHaier official support article "Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes" (support.haier.com.au, AU/NZ) + Fisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 User Guide (p.29)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because FC0 is a THIN code with three flags: the two named families word the fault differently - Haier's official article pins it to the wash pump ("FC0 Wash Pump Error"); Fisher & Paykel's DW24U6I1 manual says only the generic "Motor fault" and scopes the row "(DW24U6I only)". The meanings are compatible (the wash pump IS motor-driven; a wash-pump error is a motor fault), not identical. Second flag: F&P spells the code "FCO" (letter O); the Haier article spells it "FC0" (zero) - the page title uses FC0 and the FCO spelling is noted, not hidden. Third flag: do not confuse dishwasher FC0 with Haier washer/dryer "FC" (HLC1700AXS, "no feedback signal from driver unit") - different appliance, different fault.

What the machine does when FC0 fires

The display shows FC0 (FCO) and the wash pump cannot run, so water does not circulate through the spray arms. The wash pump is not the drain pump: the drain pump pumps dirty water out (E20/A20 is the drain fault).

Common causes

  1. Wash pump error (Haier wording) / motor fault (F&P wording) - the wash pump motor circuit is faulty; neither document says which motor part failed.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher OFF at the power supply, wait a few moments, and switch ON again (F&P DW24U6I1 - the stronger power-cycle wording: off AT THE POWER SUPPLY, not just the control panel).
  2. If the fault persists, turn the water off, disconnect from the power supply, and call Customer Care (F&P). Haier's article says: please book a service.
  3. Do not invent pump-access, impeller, or multimeter steps. No manual documents opening the machine or testing the wash-pump motor. Top-appliance-repair's "Power off for 5 minutes, then restart" is competitor advice, not manual wording (the manual says "wait a few moments").

The steps above are the manuals' own response, in order. This page does not invent pump-access, impeller, or multimeter steps - no manual documents them for FC0.

When to call a technician

  • FC0 persists after one power-cycle - turn the water off, disconnect from power, and call Customer Care / book a service.
  • Any other fault code alongside FC0 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

FC0 is a motor fault in a mains-powered dishwasher: switch it off at the power supply before any check, and never open the machine or test the wash-pump motor yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

Is FC0 the same as FCO?

Yes - the same code. Haier's official article spells it "FC0" (zero) and Fisher & Paykel's manual spells it "FCO" (letter O). On the display the two look alike.

Is the wash pump the same as the drain pump?

No. The wash pump circulates water through the spray arms during washing; the drain pump pumps dirty water out (E20/A20 is the drain fault). FC0 is the wash pump - do not follow drain-pump advice for it.

Can I reset FC0 myself?

The F&P manual's only user action: switch the dishwasher OFF at the power supply, wait a few moments, switch ON again. If it persists, turn the water off, disconnect from power and call Customer Care. Haier's article says book a service.

Why does Fisher & Paykel call it just "Motor fault"?

F&P's fault table uses generic failure classes where Haier's article names the component. The wash pump is driven by a motor, so "motor fault" and "wash pump error" describe the same failure from different angles - the page states both wordings rather than picking one.

My Haier washing machine shows FC - is it the same code?

No. "FC" on Haier washer/dryer combos (e.g. HLC1700AXS) means "no feedback signal from the driver unit" - a motor-control fault on a different appliance. Dishwasher FC0 (wash pump error) is a different code on a different machine.
