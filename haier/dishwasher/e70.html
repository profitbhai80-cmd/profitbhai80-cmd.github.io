Haier Dishwasher Error Code E70

Quick answer

Error codeE70 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE70 = temperature-sensing (NTC thermistor) fault - the sensor that measures wash-water temperature is faulty, open/short, or its harness is damaged. On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A70 ("Temperature sensing Fault" in Haier's official article; "Temperature sensing Alarm error" in the F&P guide). This is the SENSOR fault, not the heater fault - E60/A60 is the heater fault. Note: appliancemode labels it "Also seen as F70" - the F-variant is unverified (Haier's article says A70).
Most common causeThe NTC thermistor - the sensor that measures the water temperature - is faulty, open, or shorted, or its harness is damaged.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the F&P manual's only user action is one OFF/ON cycle (plus its generic clear-fault routine); the HDW15U3B1 manual adds the CANCEL-reset procedure. No manual documents multimeter or harness DIY beyond that.
Checked againstHaier official "Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes" support article (support.haier.com.au, AU/NZ) + Fisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 User Guide, p.29 "FAULT CODES AND DESCRIPTIONS" + Haier HDW15U3B1 User Manual (CANCEL-reset procedure, via manua.ls FAQ)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E70 is the HDW two-digit platform's temperature-SENSOR fault (newer displays show A70) - and it must be kept apart from E60 (the heater fault). The official article's row is "A70 Temperature sensing Fault"; the F&P guide's row is "A70 / Temperature sensing / Alarm error". appliancemode ties the HDW display code E70 to this fault as "NTC Thermistor Error" but labels it "Also seen as F70" - the F-variant is unverified; Haier's article says A70. Caveat: F&P is Haier Group-owned, but the DW24U6I1 is a distinct named model whose A-code table matches Haier's official article row-for-row.

What the machine does when E70 fires

The display shows E70 (or A70 on newer panels); the temperature-sensing circuit has failed, so the machine cannot measure wash-water temperature and the programme cannot proceed safely.

Common causes

  1. NTC thermistor faulty - the sensor that measures the water temperature (appliancemode's wording, corroborated by the official "Temperature sensing Fault" row).
  2. Sensor open or shorted, or its harness damaged - the failure modes behind the official row.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again (the F&P manual's own action); if the fault persists, call Customer Care.
  2. HDW15U3B1 manual's own reset: press and hold CANCEL to stop the beeping, then again to clear the fault code; turn the dishwasher off, then on again. If the issue persists, note the fault code and contact Customer Care.
  3. The official article's routing: "Please book a service."

The steps above are the manuals' own actions, in order. This page does not invent multimeter steps beyond citing appliancemode's published table (his values: 10000 ohms at 25 C, 635 ohms at 100 C) - and his table is his data, not a manual.

When to call a technician

  • E70 persists after the CANCEL-reset and one OFF/ON cycle - the NTC sensor, its harness, or the board input needs service.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E70 involves the sensor circuit in a mains-powered dishwasher: never open the machine or unplug sensor connectors yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E70 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E70 = temperature-sensing (NTC thermistor) fault - the sensor that measures wash-water temperature is faulty ("A70 Temperature sensing Fault" in Haier's official article; "A70 / Temperature sensing / Alarm error" in the F&P DW24U6I1 guide). On newer displays it shows as A70.

Is E70 a heater fault?

No. E70/A70 is the temperature-SENSOR fault; E60/A60 is the heater fault. Different components, different codes.

Is E70 the same as F70?

The F70 variant is unverified: appliancemode labels it "Also seen as F70", but Haier's official article says A70. Trust A70.

Can I keep using the dishwasher with E70?

No manual gives a verified safe-use claim for E70 - both sources route to service ("Please book a service" / "If fault persists call Customer Care").
