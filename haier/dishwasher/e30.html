Haier Dishwasher Error Code E30

Quick answer

Error codeE30 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE30 = overflow / anti-flood alarm - water detected in the base, flood switch activated. On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as F30. Causes per the manuals: leak in the dishwasher, inlet valve open and blocked, blocked drain. The official response: switch off at the wall, turn off the tap, book a service.
Most common causeA leak in the dishwasher, an inlet valve open and blocked, or a blocked drain - the HDW15U3S1 manual's own cause row for F30.
Safe to check yourself?Partially: the manuals' own response is switch off at the wall, turn off the water tap, and book a service - there is no manual-backed DIY beyond that (tilt-forward tricks from forum threads are not in any manual).
Checked againstHaier official "Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes" support article (support.haier.com.au, AU/NZ) + Haier HDW15U3S1 User Manual, FAULT CODES table + Fisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 User Guide, p.29 "FAULT CODES AND DESCRIPTIONS" + Haier HDW15F4B1 User Manual (p.32)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E30 is the HDW two-digit platform's overflow alarm (newer displays show F30) - the anti-flood device in the base has tripped. The official article's wording is "F30 Overflow alarm. Ask the customer to switch off the dishwasher at the wall. Turn off the tap for the water supply. Please book a service." Do not merge it with single-digit E4 (different platform), with dishwasherusa's F30 guide token (adjacent code, separate page), or with invented "tilt and forget" resets.

What the machine does when E30 fires

The display shows E30 (or F30 on newer panels) with an acoustic alarm; the anti-flood device is on - water is in the base and the flood switch has activated (HDW14G2X: "Leak: The drain pump runs permanently!").

Common causes

  1. There is a leak in the dishwasher - the HDW15U3S1 manual's own cause row.
  2. The water inlet valve is open and blocked - the manual's own cause row.
  3. The drain is blocked - the manual's own cause row.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off the dishwasher at the wall (the official article's own instruction).
  2. Turn off the tap for the water supply (the official article's own instruction).
  3. Book a service (Haier AUS / Haier NZ - the official article's own routing).
  4. F&P DW24U6I1 variant: turn the dishwasher OFF and ON again; if the fault persists, turn the water off, disconnect from the power supply, call Customer Care.
  5. HDW15F4B1 reset procedure: press and hold CANCEL to stop the beeping, then press and hold CANCEL again to clear the fault code; turn the dishwasher off, then on again (same procedure in the HDW15U3B1 manual).

The steps above are the manuals' own response, in order. This page does not invent tilt-forward tricks or float-switch cleaning - forum threads suggest tilting, but no manual documents it (mentioned here only as third-party advice, not a manual step).

When to call a technician

  • E30 returns after the base is dry and the supply is sound - the inlet valve, flood switch, or drain path needs service.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E30 means water where it should not be, in a mains-powered appliance: switch it off at the wall and turn off the water tap before any check. Never dismantle the inlet valve, drain pump or base components yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E30 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E30 = overflow / anti-flood alarm - the flood switch in the base has activated ("F30 Overflow alarm" in Haier's official article). It is verified verbatim in Haier's official AU/NZ support article, the HDW15U3S1 manual, the Fisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 guide, and the HDW15F4B1 manual (p.32).

Can I just tilt the dishwasher to clear E30?

No manual documents a tilt-forward reset - it is forum advice only. The manuals' own response is: switch off at the wall, turn off the tap, book a service. Drying the base may stop the alarm, but the leak source still needs finding.

Is Haier E30 the same as dishwasherusa's F30 guide?

Same fault family (F30 is the newer display code for E30), but this page is the manual-grounded E30 guide with verbatim rows from three named documents; dishwasherusa's page is an adjacent, separate guide.

Is E30 the same as single-digit E4?

No. Single-digit E4 is the older platform; HDW E30/F30 is the two-digit platform's overflow alarm. Different code systems - do not cross-read them.
