Haier Dishwasher Error Code E20

Quick answer

Error codeE20 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE20 = drainage fault - the dishwasher does not drain sufficiently. On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A20. Causes per the manuals: clogged filters, blocked drain connection or piping, kinked drain hose, and an undrilled sink spigot on new installs. Not the single-digit E2 of the older platform, and not Bosch E20 - different code systems.
Most common causeThe filters are clogged, the drain connection or piping is blocked, the drain hose is kinked, or (new installs) the sink spigot was never drilled out - the manuals' own cause set.
Safe to check yourself?Yes - the manuals' remedy is homeowner-scope: scoop out water, clean the filters, check the drain hose for kinks, and (new installs) check the sink spigot; continue the programme with START/PAUSE.
Checked againstHaier official "Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes" support article (support.haier.com.au, AU/NZ) + Haier HDW14G2X User Manual (HDW14G2, HDW15G2P, HDW15G3), "Error codes on the display"

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E20 is the HDW two-digit platform's drain fault (newer displays show A20) - the companion to E10's fill fault. The official article's wording is "A20 Draining water alarm: the dishwasher does not drain sufficiently." Do not merge it with single-digit E2 (different platform), with Bosch E20 (different brand and code system), or with any turbidity-sensor claim (see below).

What the machine does when E20 fires

The display shows E20 (or A20 on newer panels) with an acoustic alarm, and the dishwasher does not drain sufficiently - water stays in the tub at the end of (or during) the cycle.

Common causes

  1. Clogged filters - the HDW14G2X manual's own first cause.
  2. Blocked drain connection or piping - the manual's own second cause.
  3. Kinked drain hose - the official article's own check.
  4. Undrilled sink spigot on new installs - the official article's own check.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Scoop out as much water as possible, then clean the filters (HDW14G2X).
  2. Check the drain hose is not kinked or bent; check the drain connection and piping are correct and not blocked.
  3. New installs: confirm the sink spigot has been drilled out (min 15 mm hole) and the drain hose rises 400-850 mm above the appliance bottom.
  4. Continue the programme by pressing the START/PAUSE button (HDW14G2X).
  5. If the fault persists, contact customer care - the manual's own routing.

The steps above are the manuals' own checks and reset procedure, in order. This page does not invent drain-pump multimeter or impeller steps - no manual documents them for E20 (appliancemode's 160-195 ohm drain-pump figure is cited on his page, not in any manual, and is not repeated here).

When to call a technician

  • E20 persists with clean filters, an unkinked correctly-routed hose and a drilled-out spigot - the drain pump or its control needs service.

Safety first

E20 involves standing water and mains-powered electronics together: switch the machine off before removing and cleaning the filters or touching the drain hose, and never dismantle the drain pump yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E20 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E20 = the dishwasher does not drain sufficiently (drainage fault). On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A20. It is verified verbatim in Haier's official AU/NZ support article and the HDW14G2X user manual.

Does the turbidity sensor cause E20?

No manual says so - appliancemode's claim that a turbidity-sensor problem "can impact drain cycle timing" is invented. Turbidity faults are E80/E81/E82, a separate code family.

Why does E20 appear right after installation?

The official article's own check: new installs must have the sink spigot drilled out (min 15 mm), or the machine cannot drain. The HDW14G2X manual adds the drain-hose height rule: 400-850 mm above the appliance bottom at one point.

Is Haier dishwasher E20 the same as single-digit E2?

No. Single-digit E2 is the older platform; HDW E20/A20 is the two-digit platform's drain fault. Different code systems - do not cross-read them.
