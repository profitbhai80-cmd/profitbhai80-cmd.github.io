Haier Dishwasher Error Code F90

Quick answer

Error codeF90 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansF90 = data/electronics error - a control/data fault on the dishwasher's electronics, cleared (if transient) by switching the dishwasher OFF and ON again; if it persists, call Customer Care. No manual names a component, a cause, or any further home check. Scope: the '6' (top-tier) variants only - DW24U6I / DW60FC6 / DW60U6I.
Most common causeNo manual gives any F90-specific cause - the guides name only the fault (data/electronics error) and the remedy (power cycle, then Customer Care). This page states that honestly and invents no cause.
Safe to check yourself?Yes - one power cycle only: switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again. If the fault persists, call Customer Care. No manual gives any further home check for F90.
Checked againstFisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 User Guide (p.29) + Fisher & Paykel DW60FC models User Guide 591216C (pp.20-21, official fisherpaykel.com PDF) + Fisher & Paykel DW60U2I1 User Manual (p.31)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Haier F90 is a short, exact code with a narrow scope: it exists ONLY on the '6' (top-tier) variants of each range - DW24U6I, DW60FC6 and DW60U6I. DW60FC1/FC2/FC4 and DW60U2I do NOT show F90 (their tables skip it). The Haier official support article ('Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes', support.haier.com.au) lists NO F90 row, and no Haier-branded HDW/XS user manual lists F90 - F90 lives only in the Fisher & Paykel guides on the shared Haier/F&P platform. Do NOT merge F90 with F50 (electronics error - a different, broader row present on ALL variants) or with FA0 (electronics/power supply error - frequency/power-supply, a different fault). Do not import F&P DishDrawer F9 (different code, different platform) or any washer/dryer F90.

What the machine does when F90 fires

The display shows F90 and the dishwasher stops - a data/electronics error on the control. The manuals give no further behaviour detail for F90.

Common causes

  1. No manual gives any F90-specific cause - the guides name only the fault (data/electronics error) and the remedy. This page invents no board, sensor, or wiring claim.
  2. Wrong-code reading - F50 (electronics error, all variants) and FA0 (electronics/power supply error) are different faults; one ranking page lumps F40/F50/F90/FA0 into a single row, which is wrong for all four.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again - the manuals' full remedy, identical in all three guides.
  2. If the fault persists, call Customer Care - the manuals' own routing.
  3. The DW24U6I1 guide's generic clear routine: touch twice to clear the fault; touch and hold again to turn the dishwasher off and wait a few minutes; open the door to turn the dishwasher back on.
  4. Do not invent causes or further checks - no manual gives any F90-specific cause, component check, or home fix beyond the power cycle.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy, printed exactly. The manuals give no further diagnosis for F90 - this is a call-Customer-Care code, not a DIY fix.

When to call a technician

  • F90 persists after the OFF/ON cycle - call Customer Care; the data/electronics fault needs service.
  • Any other fault code alongside F90 - note the exact code and call Customer Care.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F90 is an electronics fault in a mains-powered dishwasher: never open the machine or probe the control board yourself. The power cycle is the only homeowner action the manuals give.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F90 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

Data/electronics error - a control/data fault on the dishwasher's electronics. Verified verbatim in three Fisher & Paykel user guides (DW24U6I1 p.29, DW60FC 591216C pp.20-21, DW60U2I1 p.31) on the shared Haier/F&P platform.

How do I reset Haier dishwasher F90?

Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again. If the fault persists, call Customer Care - that is the manuals' full remedy, identical in all three guides. The DW24U6I1 guide's generic clear routine is: touch twice to clear the fault, touch and hold to turn the dishwasher off and wait a few minutes, then open the door to turn it back on.

Which Haier dishwashers show F90?

Only the '6' (top-tier) variants: DW24U6I, DW60FC6 and DW60U6I. DW60FC1/FC2/FC4 and DW60U2I do not show F90 - their tables skip it. The Haier official support article lists no F90 row at all.

Is F90 the same as F50 or FA0?

No. F50 is an electronics error (a broader row, present on all variants); FA0 is an electronics/power supply error (frequency/power-supply). F90 is a data/electronics error. One ranking page lumps F40, F50, F90 and FA0 into one row - that is wrong for all four (F40 is a water-flow error).

Why won't my dishwasher tell me more about F90?

Because the manuals do not say more: no manual names a component, a cause, or any further home check for F90. It is a call-Customer-Care code, not a DIY fix - do not follow repair blogs that invent a board, sensor, or wiring cause for it.
