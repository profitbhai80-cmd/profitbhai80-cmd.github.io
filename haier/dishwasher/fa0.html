Haier Dishwasher Error Code FA0

Quick answer

Error codeFA0 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansFA0 = power-supply / frequency fault - the dishwasher's electronics detect that the mains power supply frequency is out of synchronisation, or the power supply itself is faulty. Haier's official article calls it a "Frequency synchronisation consistency check" (environmental / dirty power supply); Fisher & Paykel's DW24U6I1 manual calls it "Electronics/power supply error". Compatible wordings, not identical - the page carries the difference. Spelling: Haier's article spells it "FAO" (letter O); F&P spells it "FA0" (zero).
Most common causeA "dirty" power supply - the mains frequency is out of synchronisation. Both sources point at the incoming power, not at any wash component.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: check whether other appliances in the home are misbehaving (Haier article's only diagnostic). F&P's only user action: switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again. No multimeter or frequency-meter steps exist in the manuals.
Checked againstHaier official support article "Haier Dishwasher Fault Codes" (support.haier.com.au, AU/NZ) + Fisher & Paykel DW24U6I1 User Guide (p.29)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because FA0 is a THIN code with three flags: the two named families word the fault differently - Haier's official article calls it a "Frequency synchronisation consistency check" (environmental / dirty power supply); Fisher & Paykel's DW24U6I1 manual calls it "Electronics/power supply error". The meanings are compatible (a frequency-sync check IS a power-supply fault), not identical. Second flag: Haier spells the code "FAO" (letter O); F&P spells it "FA0" (zero) - the page title uses FA0 and the FAO spelling is noted, not hidden. Third flag: appliancemode's "EA0" display alias appears in NO named manual - do not present EA0 as a verified alias.

What the machine does when FA0 fires

The display shows FA0 (FAO) and the dishwasher reports a power-supply / frequency fault. The fault is about the incoming mains power, not a wash component.

Common causes

  1. Dirty power supply - the mains frequency is out of synchronisation (Haier article's environmental framing).
  2. Electronics/power supply error (F&P wording) - the power supply itself is faulty.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check whether any other appliances in the home are not working correctly - the Haier article's only diagnostic. If yes, call your power service provider; if no, please book a service.
  2. Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again (F&P DW24U6I1). If the fault persists, call Customer Care.
  3. General HDW fault-clear procedure (Haier HDW15U3B1 user manual - the U3B1 manual does not list FA0 itself): press and hold 'CANCEL' to stop the beeping, then again to clear the fault code; turn the dishwasher off, then on again. If the issue persists, note the fault code and contact Customer Care.
  4. Do not invent multimeter/frequency-meter steps. Appliancemode says verifying outlet frequency "usually requires an electrician or a specific meter" - competitor advice, usable as attributed reference only, not manual-backed.

The steps above are the manuals' own checks and reset procedures, in order. This page does not invent meter or electrician steps - no manual documents them for FA0.

When to call a technician

  • FA0 persists after one OFF/ON cycle - call Customer Care / book a service.
  • Other appliances in the home are also misbehaving - call your power service provider (Haier article).
  • Any other fault code alongside FA0 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

FA0 is a power-supply fault in a mains-powered dishwasher: switch it off at the power supply before any check, and never open the machine or probe the power-supply electronics yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

Is FA0 the same as EA0?

No verified EA0 code exists in any named manual. Haier's official article spells the code "FAO" and Fisher & Paykel's manual spells it "FA0" - the same code, O/zero spelling variant. "EA0" is appliancemode's label only.

What causes FA0 - is it my dishwasher or my power supply?

Per Haier's official article it can be environmental: a "dirty" power supply. Check whether other appliances in the home are also misbehaving - if yes, call your power service provider; if no, book a service.

Can I reset FA0 myself?

The F&P manual says switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again; if the fault persists, call Customer Care. Haier's article gives no homeowner reset - book a service if no other appliances are affected.

FA0 vs F50 - what's the difference?

F50 is a communication failure between the dishwasher's own electronic boards (Haier article: "F50 E52 Communication Failure"). FA0 is about the incoming mains power supply / frequency synchronisation - an external-power fault, not a board-to-board fault.

Does FA0 mean I need an electrician?

Not necessarily. The official article's first step is checking other appliances, not calling an electrician. If other appliances are affected, the article says call your power service provider; otherwise book a dishwasher service.
