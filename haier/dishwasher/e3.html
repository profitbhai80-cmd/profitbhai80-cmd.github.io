Haier Dishwasher Error Code E3

Quick answer

Error codeE3 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE3 on a Haier DW9/DW12/HDW12 dishwasher means the drain hose is too low; on the newer XS family it means the dishwasher does not drain water. The DW families signal it with 10 short buzzer signals every 30 seconds. Set the hose's free end within the 40–100 cm installation range (DW families) or clear the drain pipe, filter and siphon (XS), power-cycle, and contact the authorised service centre if E3 persists.
Most common causeOn DW/HDW families the drain hose is routed too low, so water siphons out instead of draining properly; on XS families the drain path is bent, trapped, blocked or the filter/siphon is clogged.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — re-routing the drain hose, clearing the filter and checking the siphon are safe home checks. Do not disconnect a hose while water stands in the tub.
Checked againstHaier DW9-UFE3 User's Manual — E3: drain hose too low (10 short buzzer signals every 30 seconds); Haier DW12-TFE3-F / DW12-TFE5 User Manuals — identical E3 wording; Haier HDW12-TFE3WH/SS User Manual — identical E3 wording; Haier XS 6B0M3PDB User Manual (§19) — ERROR E3: dishwasher does not drain water; check drain pipe, filter, siphon; Haier XS 6B0S3FSB User Manual (§19) — identical XS E3 row; Haier DW12-EBE4TIS User Manual — drain hose free end between 40 and 100 cm, not immersed in water

Applies to — read this first

E3 is the mirror image of the live E4 page: on DW9/DW12/HDW12 families too HIGH or crimped is E4, too LOW is E3 — and on the newer XS family E3 is the general does-not-drain code. The page leads with that series table, because the DW and XS meanings are different faults that share one code, and because the ranking blog lists get E3 wrong by calling it a heating fault.

No Haier dishwasher manual gives E3 as a heating fault — the XS heating code is E8, not E3. The blog list that calls E3 a heating-element fault is wrong on E1, E2, E6 and E8 as well.

What the machine does when E3 fires

On DW/HDW families the machine announces E3 with 10 short buzzer signals every 30 seconds and 'E3' on the time-remaining indicator, and water cannot drain correctly — on a too-low hose it siphons away instead of draining in the machine's own sequence. On XS models E3 shows on the display (or as 3 flashes with a 5-second pause on display-less models) with a short audio signal, and water remains in the tub because the drain path is blocked.

Common causes

  1. The drain hose's free end sits below the 40 cm installation minimum (DW/HDW families) — the hose siphons and the machine cannot drain in sequence.
  2. The drain hose is bent, trapped or blocked somewhere along its run (XS families, and any family with a kinked hose).
  3. The dishwasher filter is clogged (XS E3 check) — food debris blocking the drain path.
  4. The siphon (trap) the hose drains into is clogged (XS E3 check).
  5. The hose is immersed in standing water at the drain point, defeating the air gap the installation range exists to preserve.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher off. If water is standing in the tub, do not disconnect any hose — leave the water where it is until the fault is found.
  2. Trace the full drain-hose run from the back of the machine to the drain point. On DW/HDW families, measure the free end: it must sit between 40 and 100 cm high and must not be immersed in water. Re-route a too-low hose into that range.
  3. Straighten any bend or kink and free any trapped section of hose, pushing the machine back carefully without re-trapping it.
  4. On XS models (and any model with a filter check in its E3 row), remove and clean the dishwasher filter, and check the siphon/trap for a clog.
  5. Power the dishwasher off, wait a few minutes, power it on and try a short cycle. If E3 persists with a correctly-routed, clear drain path, contact the nearest authorised service centre with your model and serial number.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. E3 has no deeper home diagnostic in any manual — hose height and route, filter, siphon, power-cycle, then the service centre. Never run repeated cycles on standing water.

When to call a technician

  • E3 persists with the hose in the 40–100 cm range, straight and unblocked, and the filter and siphon clear.
  • Water has leaked onto the floor or near electrics — switch off at the wall and call the service centre.
  • The drain hose is damaged, split or perished — it needs replacement by the service centre.
  • The drain pump itself is suspected — that is beyond the manuals' E3 checks and is service-centre work.

Safety first

E3 is a plumbing fault, not an electrical fault — but the dishwasher is still a mains appliance with water inside it. Switch it off before inspecting the hose, never disconnect a drain hose while the tub holds water, and never open the machine's panels or touch pump electrics. If water has leaked onto the floor or into electrics, switch off at the wall, mop up, and call the authorised service centre.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E3 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

On DW9/DW12/HDW12 families E3 means the drain hose is too low. On the newer XS family E3 means the dishwasher does not drain water (bent, trapped or blocked drain pipe, clogged filter, or clogged siphon). Check which family you own first — the fix differs.

What height should the drain hose be?

On the DW12 family the installation manual specifies the drain hose's free end at a height between 40 and 100 cm, not immersed in water. Below 40 cm the hose siphons and you get E3; above 100 cm the pump cannot lift the water and you get E4.

Is Haier dishwasher E3 a heating problem?

No. No Haier dishwasher manual gives E3 as a heating fault — the XS heating code is E8. E3 is a drain fault on every family checked: too-low hose on DW/HDW, does-not-drain on XS.

How is E3 different from E4?

They are a mirror pair on DW/HDW families: E3 is the hose too LOW, E4 is the hose too HIGH or crimped. On XS families the pair is different: E3 is does-not-drain and E4 is a leak code. Read the code and the family together.

How do I clear Haier dishwasher E3?

Switch off, re-route the drain hose so its free end sits at 40–100 cm and is not immersed, straighten any bend, clean the filter and check the siphon (XS), then power off, wait a few minutes, power on and retry. If E3 persists, contact the authorised service centre.
