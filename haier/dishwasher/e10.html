Haier Dishwasher Error Code E10

Quick answer

Error codeE10 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE10 = water inlet / fill fault - the dishwasher does not fill with water. On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A10. Sub-codes fold into this page: E11 = water tap closed during the first fill; E12 = water tap closed during refill. Root causes per the manuals: tap closed, kinked inlet hose, clogged inlet-hose filter, low water pressure. Not the single-digit E1 of the older Haier DW platform - a different code system.
Most common causeThe water tap is closed, the inlet hose is kinked, the inlet-hose filter is clogged, or household water pressure is too low - the manuals' own cause set. E11/E12 name the tap-closed case explicitly.
Safe to check yourself?Yes - the manuals' remedy is homeowner-scope: open the water tap, check for inlet-hose kinks, clean the inlet-hose filter, confirm water pressure, then switch off/on and restart the programme.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Haier runs two dishwasher code systems: the single-digit E1-E5 platform (already live on this site) and the HDW two-digit platform (E10, E20, E30 ...) where displays show E10 or, on newer panels, A10. This page is the HDW E10/A10 page - water inlet / fill fault, with E11 (tap closed on first fill) and E12 (tap closed on refill) folded in. Do not merge it with single-digit E1 (door/not-filling on the old platform - different code system) or with other brands' E10.

What the machine does when E10 fires

The display shows E10 (or A10 on newer panels) with an acoustic alarm, and the dishwasher does not fill with water - the fill is blocked or the water supply is off.

Common causes

  1. Water tap is closed - the E11/E12 sub-codes name this explicitly.
  2. Kinked or bent inlet hose - the official article's own check.
  3. Clogged inlet-hose filter (at the tap connection or the rear of the appliance) - the manuals' own cause.
  4. Water pressure below the installation minimum - the official article's own check (HDW14G2X installation data: 0.3-10 bar).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Open the water tap fully - the manuals' own first step.
  2. Check the inlet hose is not kinked or bent; check water flows from the tap.
  3. Clean the inlet-hose filter at the tap connection and at the rear of the appliance.
  4. If the aqua-stop marker is red, the hose must be replaced (HDW14G2X).
  5. To reset the fault, switch the dishwasher off and on, then restart the programme (HDW15U3S1); HDW14G2X adds: after solving "Error 10", switch off and restart.
  6. If water pressure is too low, contact the local water authority (the manuals' own routing).

The steps above are the manuals' own checks and reset procedures, in order. This page does not invent inlet-valve replacement or multimeter checks - no manual documents them for E10 (appliancemode's 3.2k-4k ohm inlet-valve figure is cited on his page, not in any manual, and is not repeated here).

When to call a technician

  • E10 returns with the tap open, the hose unkinked, the filter clean and normal pressure - the inlet valve or pressure switch needs service.
  • E11/E12 persist on a known-good supply - technician diagnosis.

Safety first

E10 involves the water supply and mains-powered electronics together: switch the machine off before touching the inlet hose or its filter, and never dismantle the inlet valve yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E10 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E10 = the dishwasher does not fill with water (water inlet / fill fault). On newer HDW displays the same fault shows as A10. It is verified verbatim in Haier's official AU/NZ support article and three HDW user manuals (HDW15U3S1, HDW14G2X, HDW15F4B1 p.32).

What are Haier dishwasher codes E11 and E12?

Inlet-fill sub-codes of E10: E11 = water tap closed during the first fill; E12 = water tap closed during refill. Same fault family, same remedy (open the tap, check the hose and pressure).

Is Haier dishwasher E10 the same as E1?

No. E1 belongs to the older single-digit Haier DW platform (door/not-filling); E10/A10 belongs to the HDW two-digit platform (inlet-fill fault). Different code systems - do not cross-read them.

How do I reset Haier dishwasher E10?

The HDW15U3S1 manual's own procedure: fix the supply problem (open the tap, unkink the hose, clean the filter), then switch the dishwasher off and on and restart the programme.

The inlet hose has an aqua-stop with a red marker. What now?

The HDW14G2X manual says a red aqua-stop marker means the hose must be replaced - the safety device has tripped.
