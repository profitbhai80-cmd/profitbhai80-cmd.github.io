Haier Dishwasher Error Code E81

Quick answer

Error codeE81 — Haier Dishwasher
What it meansE81 = turbidity sensor fault. Haier's official article lists it directly ("A80 E81 Turbidity Sensor Fault") - the same display form as the HDW panel. Per appliancemode's undocumented sub-claim, E81 specifically flags the turbidity sensor's calibration being out of range - competitor-documented, NOT manual-verified. The official article gives no calibration detail; this page presents both wordings honestly.
Most common causeThe turbidity sensor's calibration is out of range - per appliancemode's undocumented sub-claim; the official article confirms only "Turbidity Sensor Fault".
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the parent-fault manuals' only user actions are one OFF/ON cycle (F&P) and the CANCEL-reset procedure (HDW15U3B1). No source gives any calibration procedure.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E81 is THIN-documented: the official article names it directly as a turbidity-sensor fault, but the calibration-out-of-range detail comes only from appliancemode - no named manual gives it. This page states both, and flags which is which. The official row is "A80 E81 Turbidity Sensor Fault. Please book a service."; appliancemode's unattributed sub-bullet is "E81: Turbidity Sensor Calibration out of Range." Do not merge E81 with dishwasherusa's "E7 - Turbidity Sensor Fault" - the HDW platform has no E7.

What the machine does when E81 fires

The display shows E81; the turbidity-sensing circuit has faulted (official wording: "Turbidity Sensor Fault").

Common causes

  1. Turbidity sensor fault - the official article's own row.
  2. Calibration out of range - appliancemode's competitor-documented sub-claim; no manual confirms the calibration detail.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher OFF and ON again (the F&P manual's own parent-fault action); if the fault persists, call Customer Care.
  2. HDW15U3B1 manual's own reset: press and hold CANCEL to stop the beeping, then again to clear the fault code; turn the dishwasher off, then on again. If the issue persists, note the fault code and contact Customer Care.
  3. The official article's routing: "Please book a service."

The steps above are the parent-fault manuals' own actions. No source gives any calibration procedure - this page does not invent one.

When to call a technician

  • E81 persists after the CANCEL-reset and one OFF/ON cycle - the turbidity sensor or its circuit needs service.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E81 involves the sensor circuit in a mains-powered dishwasher: never open the machine or unplug sensor connectors yourself - that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E81 mean on a Haier dishwasher?

E81 = turbidity sensor fault, named directly in Haier's official article ("A80 E81 Turbidity Sensor Fault"). Per appliancemode's competitor-documented sub-claim, E81 specifically flags the turbidity sensor's calibration being out of range - the official article gives no calibration detail.

How is E81 different from E80?

The official article groups A80/E81 in one "Turbidity Sensor Fault" row - E81 is the sub-code of the E80 family. The calibration-out-of-range distinction is competitor-documented, not manual-verified.

What does calibration-out-of-range mean for the wash?

Reasoned from appliancemode's definition (not a manual claim): the sensor cannot establish a clean-water reference, so cycles cannot be soil-adjusted.

Is E81 the same as dishwasherusa's E7?

No - that is the wrong-code trap. The HDW platform has no E7; turbidity faults are E80/E81/E82.
