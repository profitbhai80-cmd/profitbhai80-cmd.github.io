Haier Split Air Conditioner Error Code F42

Quick answer

Error codeF42 — Haier Split Air Conditioner
What it meansF42 = low system pressure protection — the system pressure is too low, detected as the system temperature falling below -45°C: undercharged / low refrigerant, low defrosting temperature, heat-exchange problems, or a malfunctioning fan motor. The outdoor error number is 45. On boards with two LEDs this reads as LED1 4 flashes + LED2 5 flashes (M×10+N rule, M3 p.29). Do NOT read it as a flat LED1 count, and do NOT confuse it with outdoor error 42 — that is F39.
Most common causeOperating temperature too low, heat exchange problems, or low refrigerant (M3 Table A row 45); the FlexFit/cassette cause lists say the same thing as undercharged refrigerant, low defrosting temperature, or a malfunctioning fan motor.
Safe to check yourself?No — there is no owner fix in any manual. Switch the unit off, check that filters and the outdoor unit are not visibly blocked (only if safely accessible), and call a technician. Refrigerant charging, restriction checks and airflow diagnosis are technician work.
Checked againstHaier European HVAC Technical Book 2024-25, DIAGNOSTIC Table A (Mono Residential and Multi Units), p.31; Haier 3U24MS2VHB FlexFit Series Multi Zone Split System Service Manual, Console Technical Overview p.112 + p.35; Haier FlexFit 17400 BTU Dual-Zone Mini Split Service Manual, §"Outdoor Unit Error Codes", p.129; haier-ac.pt cassette/duct PDF (Cod.-Erros-Condutas-Alta-Pest.pdf); Haier 4U36MS2VHB Mid-Static Ducted Service Manual, p.92; Haier 1U105S2SS1FA outdoor troubleshooting (haier-ac.pt, Cod.-Erros-Consolas-Chao-Tecto.pdf)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F42 is a split-wall/ductless Table A code — it means low system pressure protection on the EU mono/multi range, the US FlexFit multi-zone/console families, and the cassette/convertible/duct families (M3/FlexFit/cassette/4U36/1U105, identical outdoor error number 45). It is NOT listed in M7 (the AU/NZ R32 installation manual jumps F35 → F43) or in M2 (the US ductless manual lists only 22 codes). The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) carries the same wording under F45 — never merge cabinet F45 with Table A F42. The number-42 trap runs both ways: FlexFit/ducted numeric 42 = high-pressure switch open = F39, NOT F42; Table A F42 is outdoor error 45. Check the model and series, not just the letters.

What the machine does when F42 fires

The indoor display shows F42, and the low system pressure protection has tripped. Per the 1U105 outdoor troubleshooting table, the minimum temperature value of indoor pipe Tm and outdoor Ts is lower than -45°C in cooling mode, or the minimum temperature value of outdoor Tc and outdoor Te is lower than -45°C in heating mode. The error is Non-resumable — it will not clear until the fault is fixed and the power is cut and restored.

Common causes

  1. Operating temperature too low (M3 Table A row 45).
  2. Heat exchange problems (M3 Table A row 45).
  3. Low refrigerant / undercharged (M3 Table A row 45; FlexFit p.129).
  4. Low defrosting temperature (FlexFit p.129).
  5. Malfunction of fan motor (FlexFit p.129).
  6. In cooling mode: lack of charge, low heat on indoor coil, restrictions, airflow, or dirt, or low indoor load (FlexFit p.129); in heating mode: cold outdoor air, lack of charge, or a restriction.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off.
  2. Check only that filters and the outdoor unit are not visibly blocked (only if safely accessible), then call a technician — there is no owner fix in any manual for F42.
  3. The technician works from the manual's cause list (FlexFit p.129): in cooling mode — lack of charge, low heat on indoor coil, restrictions, airflow, or dirt, or low indoor load; in heating mode — cold outdoor air, lack of charge, or a restriction.

No power-cycle reset time and no pressure value in bar/psi exist in any manual for F42. Do NOT present F40's switch-continuity step as the F42 fix — F42 is a system-pressure protection, not a switch alarm — and do NOT present E5's indoor-ice steps as F42's. No flowchart exists for F42 in M2 (it is not listed there at all).

When to call a technician

  • F42 returns after a restart — the charge, airflow, a restriction, or the fan motor needs diagnosis.
  • The error is Non-resumable — it will not clear until the fault is fixed and the power is cut and restored (1U105).
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F42 diagnosis involves refrigerant charging, restriction checks and airflow diagnosis: that is qualified-technician work. Never open the outdoor unit while it is connected to power, and never attempt refrigerant work yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F42 mean on a Haier split air conditioner?

F42 means the low system pressure protection has tripped — the system pressure is too low. The manuals detect it as the system temperature falling below -45°C (indoor pipe/outdoor suction in cooling, outdoor TC/Te in heating), caused by low refrigerant, low defrosting temperature, heat-exchange problems, or a malfunctioning fan motor. The outdoor error number is 45 (M3 Table A p.31; 1U105 troubleshooting).

How is F42 detected?

Per the 1U105 outdoor troubleshooting table, the minimum temperature value of indoor pipe Tm and outdoor Ts is lower than -45°C in cooling mode, or the minimum of outdoor Tc and Te is lower than -45°C in heating mode. The error is Non-resumable — it will not clear until the fault is fixed and the power is cut and restored.

Can I fix Haier F42 myself?

The manual's own cause list is technician work: lack of charge, low heat on the indoor coil, restrictions, airflow or dirt, low indoor load (cooling); cold outdoor air, lack of charge, restriction (heating). Switch the unit off, check only that filters and the outdoor unit are not visibly blocked, and call a technician.

Is F42 the same as F40?

No. F40 is the low-pressure SWITCH alarm (the switch itself is open). F42 is LOW SYSTEM pressure protection (the system temperature/pressure is genuinely too low). Different faults, different codes (43 vs 45).

My ducted manual says 42 is the high-pressure switch — is that F42?

No, and this is the trap in both directions. In the ducted/FlexFit numeric tables, outdoor error 42 is the high-pressure switch open — that is Table A F39. Table A F42 is outdoor error 45 (low system pressure protection). And on a cabinet unit, the same low-system-pressure wording sits under F45. Always match the code to your unit type, not just the number.
