Haier Split Air Conditioner Error Code F43

Quick answer

Error codeF43 — Haier Split Air Conditioner
What it meansF43 = indoor–outdoor unit communication protocol incorrect — the indoor and outdoor units do not match (model/protocol mismatch), so their communication protocol is incorrect; check indoor–outdoor unit (OU-IU) compatibility. The outdoor error number is 46, and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 46 times (M7 p.25) — the highest flash count in the M3 Table A outdoor set. M7 lists F43 with "/" (no indoor-display indication given in its table). M7 calls the same code "Model matching abnormality" and FlexFit calls it "Incorrect match between indoor & outdoor" — one fault, three labels, never three faults.
Most common causeIndoor–outdoor unit communication problem — the indoor and outdoor units are not a matched, compatible pair (M3 Table A row 46).
Safe to check yourself?No — no fix step exists in any manual. Switch the unit off and call a technician/installer to check that the indoor and outdoor units are a compatible matched pair (model and protocol) and that the interconnect wiring is correct.
Checked againstHaier European HVAC Technical Book 2024-25, DIAGNOSTIC Table A (Mono Residential and Multi Units), p.31; Haier 1U26MADFRA Room Air Conditioner Installation Manual (Sept 2024, R32, AU/NZ), Outdoor Unit Troubleshooting, p.25; Haier 3U24MS2VHB FlexFit Series Multi Zone Split System Service Manual, Console Technical Overview p.112

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F43 is a split-wall/ductless Table A code — it means the indoor–outdoor communication protocol is incorrect on the EU mono/multi range, the AU/NZ R32 split families and the US FlexFit multi-zone/console families (M3/M7/FlexFit, identical outdoor error number 46). It is NOT listed in M2 (the US ductless manual lists only 22 codes), and the 1U105, 4U36 and cassette/duct numeric tables all END at outdoor 45/F42 — none has a 46/F43 row. The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) has NO F43-as-matching-fault — cabinet F43 is the LOW pressure switch alarm (Table A F40's meaning), a completely different fault. Check the model and series, not just the letters.

What the machine does when F43 fires

The indoor and outdoor units do not match, so their communication protocol does not agree. F43 means the two units are talking but do not MATCH (wrong protocol / incompatible pair) — not that communication has failed altogether. M7 lists F43 with "/" (no indoor-display indication given in its table); the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 46 times.

Common causes

  1. Indoor–outdoor unit communication problem — check OU-IU compatibility (M3 Table A row 46).
  2. The indoor and outdoor units are not a matched pair (model/protocol mismatch) — M7's "Model matching abnormality" / FlexFit's "Incorrect match between indoor & outdoor".

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off.
  2. Call your installer or a technician to check that the indoor and outdoor units are a compatible matched pair (model and protocol) and that the interconnect wiring is correct.
  3. The only manual instruction is M3's "check OU-IU compatibility" — confirming the indoor and outdoor units are a matched, compatible pair is installer/technician work (and may mean the wrong indoor unit was fitted).

No power-cycle reset time, no wiring check, no "re-pair the units" step, no compatibility list and no reset procedure exists in any manual for F43 — the page says so honestly and stays short rather than padding with invented diagnostics. Do NOT present E7's 4-minute rule or wiring steps as F43 steps — E7 is a different fault (no communication at all) — and do NOT present the JustAnswer thread's power-cycling anecdote as a manual step (the user there reported needing to power-cycle repeatedly; that is a symptom report, not a fix). Do NOT present F3's module–PCB steps as F43 steps. There is no F43 flowchart in any manual.

When to call a technician

  • F43 shows on a newly installed or recently serviced unit — the indoor and outdoor units may not be a matched pair.
  • The outdoor LED1 flashes 46 times — model/protocol mismatch (E7 is the no-communication fault, F3 is the module–PCB fault).
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

F43 diagnosis means checking unit compatibility and interconnect wiring: that is installer/technician work. Never open the indoor or outdoor unit while it is connected to power, and do not keep power-cycling the unit — repeated restarts are a symptom, not a fix.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F43 mean on a Haier split air conditioner?

F43 means the indoor–outdoor communication protocol is incorrect — the indoor and outdoor units do not match (model/protocol mismatch). The manuals say to check indoor–outdoor unit (OU-IU) compatibility. The outdoor error number is 46, and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 46 times (M3 Table A p.31; M7 p.25).

My manual says F43 is "Model matching abnormality" — is that the same fault?

Yes. The AU/NZ installation manual (M7) and the HVA103-era table call F43 "Model matching abnormality"; the European Technical Book (M3) calls it "communication protocol incorrect"; FlexFit calls it "Incorrect match between indoor & outdoor". One fault, three labels: the indoor and outdoor units are not a matched pair.

Is F43 the same as E7?

No. E7 is a communication FAULT — no communication between the indoor and outdoor units for more than 4 minutes. F43 means the units are connected but do not MATCH — the wrong indoor unit paired with the outdoor unit, or an incompatible protocol. Different faults, different fixes: E7 points at wiring/boards, F43 points at unit compatibility.

Can I fix Haier F43 myself?

No fix step exists in any manual read. The only manual instruction is M3's "check OU-IU compatibility" — confirming the indoor and outdoor units are a matched, compatible pair, which is installer/technician work (and may mean the wrong indoor unit was fitted). Switch the unit off and call your installer or a technician; do not keep power-cycling it — one owner thread reports needing repeated restarts, which is a symptom, not a fix.

Is F43 the same on a Haier cabinet air conditioner?

No — the cabinet trap is total here. The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) uses F43 for the LOW pressure switch alarm, a completely different fault, and has no matching/protocol code at all. Check whether your unit is a split or a cabinet before using any F-code meaning.
