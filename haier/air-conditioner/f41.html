Haier Split Air Conditioner Error Code F41

Quick answer

Error codeF41 — Haier Split Air Conditioner
What it meansF41 = temperature protection of the outdoor heat exchanger TC too high — the outdoor condenser temperature (TC/Te) has risen too high, i.e. the system high-pressure protection has tripped: overcharged refrigerant, high condensing temperature, heat-exchange problems, or a malfunctioning fan motor. The outdoor error number is 44. On boards with two LEDs this reads as LED1 4 flashes + LED2 4 flashes (M×10+N rule, M3 p.29). Do NOT read it as a flat LED1 count. The four non-M3 sources call outdoor 44 "system high pressure protection" while M3 calls F41 "temperature protection of outdoor heat exchanger TC too high" — one fault, two labels, never two codes.
Most common causeOperating temperature too high, heat exchange problems, or excessive refrigerant (M3 Table A row 44); the FlexFit/cassette cause lists say the same thing as overcharged refrigerant, high condensing temperature, or a malfunctioning fan motor.
Safe to check yourself?No — there is no owner fix in any manual. Switch the unit off, check that the outdoor coil/airflow is not visibly blocked (only if safely accessible), and call a technician. Refrigerant charging (weight method only), restriction checks and pressure-switch replacement are technician work.
Checked againstHaier European HVAC Technical Book 2024-25, DIAGNOSTIC Table A (Mono Residential and Multi Units), p.31; Haier 3U24MS2VHB FlexFit Series Multi Zone Split System Service Manual, Console Technical Overview p.112 + p.35; Haier FlexFit 17400 BTU Dual-Zone Mini Split Service Manual, §"Outdoor Unit Error Codes", p.129; haier-ac.pt cassette/duct PDF (Cod.-Erros-Condutas-Alta-Pest.pdf); Haier 4U36MS2VHB Mid-Static Ducted Service Manual, p.92; Haier 1U105S2SS1FA outdoor troubleshooting (haier-ac.pt, Cod.-Erros-Consolas-Chao-Tecto.pdf)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F41 is a split-wall/ductless Table A code — it means the outdoor heat exchanger TC temperature is too high on the EU mono/multi range, the US FlexFit multi-zone/console families, and the cassette/convertible/duct families (M3/FlexFit/cassette/4U36/1U105, identical outdoor error number 44). It is NOT listed in M7 (the AU/NZ R32 installation manual jumps F35 → F43) or in M2 (the US ductless manual lists only 22 codes). The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) carries the same wording under F44 — never merge cabinet F44 with Table A F41. There is NO Table A F44 — do not let the number 44 become a code. Check the model and series, not just the letters.

What the machine does when F41 fires

The indoor display shows F41, and the system high-pressure protection has tripped. Per the 1U105 outdoor troubleshooting table, the maximum temperature value of Tc and Te is over 65°C; the error locks if it occurs 3 times in 30 minutes, and it is Non-resumable — it will not clear until the fault is fixed and the power is cut and restored.

Common causes

  1. Operating temperature too high (M3 Table A row 44).
  2. Heat exchange problems (M3 Table A row 44).
  3. Excessive refrigerant / overcharged (M3 Table A row 44; FlexFit p.129).
  4. High condensing temperature (FlexFit p.129).
  5. Malfunction of fan motor (FlexFit p.129).
  6. Dirty outdoor coil or a restriction — typical causes of high pressure in cooling mode (FlexFit p.129); in heating mode: overcharge, undersized refrigerant lines or excessive length, or a restriction.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off.
  2. Check only that the outdoor coil/airflow is not visibly blocked (only if safely accessible), then call a technician — there is no owner fix in any manual for F41.
  3. The technician works from the manual's cause list (FlexFit p.129): excessive refrigerant pressure — if the system is a new installation, the charge is likely too high, and the Weight Method is the ONLY way to charge this system.
  4. If the refrigerant pressures are correct, yet the system does not close the error reporting pressure switch, the technician replaces the defective pressure switch (FlexFit p.129).

No power-cycle reset time and no pressure value in bar/psi exist in any manual for F41. Do NOT present F39's switch-continuity step as the F41 fix — F41 is a temperature/pressure protection, not a switch alarm — and do NOT present F36's sensor-replacement cause as F41's (in F41 the TC sensor is reporting a genuinely high temperature). No flowchart exists for F41 in M2 (it is not listed there at all).

When to call a technician

  • F41 returns after a restart — the charge, the outdoor coil, a restriction, or the fan motor needs diagnosis.
  • The error locks after 3 occurrences in 30 minutes — the fault factor must be cleaned out and the power cut and re-offered (1U105).
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F41 diagnosis involves refrigerant charging (weight method only), restriction checks and pressure-switch replacement: that is qualified-technician work. Never open the outdoor unit while it is connected to power, and never attempt refrigerant work yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F41 mean on a Haier split air conditioner?

F41 means the outdoor heat exchanger TC temperature is too high — the system high-pressure protection has tripped. The outdoor condenser temperature (the maximum of Tc and Te) is over 65°C, caused by excessive refrigerant, high condensing temperature, heat-exchange problems, or a malfunctioning fan motor. The outdoor error number is 44 (M3 Table A p.31; 1U105 troubleshooting).

How is F41 detected?

Per the 1U105 outdoor troubleshooting table, the maximum temperature value of Tc and Te is over 65°C. The error locks if it occurs 3 times in 30 minutes, and it is Non-resumable — it will not clear until the fault is fixed and the power is cut and restored.

Can I fix Haier F41 myself?

The manual's own cause list is technician work: overcharge (the weight method is the only way to charge the system), a dirty outdoor coil, a restriction, or undersized/excessively long refrigerant lines in heating mode. Switch the unit off, check only that the outdoor coil/airflow is not visibly blocked, and call a technician.

Is F41 the same as F39?

No. F39 is the high-pressure SWITCH alarm (the switch itself is open). F41 is the system high-pressure PROTECTION — the condenser temperature is genuinely too high. A faulty switch and an overheating condenser are different faults with different codes (42 vs 44).

Is F41 the same as F36?

No. F36 is a SENSOR fault — the piping TC probe has failed. F41 means the TC temperature is genuinely too high — the sensor is working and reporting a real over-temperature. And on a cabinet unit the same wording sits under F44, not F41 — check whether your unit is a split or a cabinet.
