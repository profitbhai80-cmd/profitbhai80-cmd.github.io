Haier Split Air Conditioner Error Code F13

Quick answer

Error codeF13 — Haier Split Air Conditioner
What it meansF13 = lack of refrigerant / clogging of the refrigerant delivery tube (discharging pipe blocked). The outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 16 times. Detection (M3 Table A): the unit reports the error and stops when it detects Td-Tci >= 25°C for 1 minute after the compressor has been running in cooling mode for 10 minutes; the alarm goes out after 3 occurrences in an hour and locks the machine. A ducted manual uses a different threshold (Td-Ts >= 80°C) — that variant applies to ducted units, not split-wall Table A.
Most common causeLack of refrigerant, or clogging of the refrigerant delivery tube (M3 Table A p.30).
Safe to check yourself?No — no owner fix step exists in any manual. Switch the unit off and call a technician; refrigerant handling is technician-only work.
Checked againstHaier European HVAC Technical Book 2024-25 (haierhvac.eu), DIAGNOSTIC Table A p.30; Haier 1U26MADFRA Room Air Conditioner Installation Manual (Sept 2024, R32, AU/NZ), Outdoor Unit Troubleshooting p.25; Haier FlexFit Series Multi Zone Split System Heat Pump Service Manual (3U24MS2VHB), Console Technical Overview p.112; Haier 4U36MS2VHB Service Manual, Mid-Static Ducted Technical Overview p.92; haier-ac.pt Outdoor Unit Trouble Shooting (1U105S2SS1FA etc.), ducted manual

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F13 is a split-wall/ductless Table A code — it means lack of refrigerant / clogged refrigerant delivery tube on the EU mono residential / multi range, the AU/NZ R32 split families and the US FlexFit multi-zone consoles (M3/M7/FlexFit, identical LED count of 16), with numeric corroboration from the 4U36 ducted table and haier-ac.pt. The US ductless split manual (M2) does NOT list F13. The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) uses F13 for a different fault — the compressor delivery temperature probe (Td) — and puts the lack-of-refrigerant wording under F16. The old fixed-speed lamp table has no F13 at all. Check the model and series, not just the letters.

The ranking pages get F13 wrong. acerrorcode, the #1 list page, says F13 is "Communication failure between outdoor PCB and module" — wrong: that is F3's meaning (communication fault between the IPM/SPDU and outdoor PCB, LED1 4). Arlington gets the meaning and LED1 16 right but omits the second half of the manual meaning (clogging of the refrigerant delivery tube), omits the M3 detection rule and the 3-times-in-an-hour lockout, and invents a fix step ("Clean the condenser coils and fans") that is NOT a manual F13 step. Do NOT repeat the EC code's refrigerant-leakage wording as F13 — EC is an acerrorcode-only code with no manual support; F13 is the manual-backed refrigerant-shortage code.

What the machine does when F13 fires

The unit reports the error and stops when it detects Td-Tci >= 25°C for 1 minute after the compressor has been running in cooling mode for 10 minutes, and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 16 times. If the alarm goes out 3 times in an hour, the machine locks (M3 Table A p.30). M7 marks F13 "Trouble record" — logged in the unit's trouble history rather than shown straight on the indoor panel.

Common causes

  1. Lack of refrigerant (M3 Table A p.30; M7 p.25; FlexFit p.112).
  2. Clogging of the refrigerant delivery tube / discharging pipe blocked (M3 Table A p.30; haier-ac.pt).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off.
  2. Call a technician — refrigerant charge and delivery-tube checks are technician work; refrigerant handling, leak-finding and recharging need a qualified technician. Do not try to top up the refrigerant yourself.

No "top up the refrigerant yourself" step, no leak-detection procedure, no pressure value, no "clean the condenser" step and no power-cycle reset time exists in any manual for F13. There is no flowchart for F13 anywhere in M2 pp.44-50. Do NOT present Arlington's "clean the condenser coils and fans" as a manual F13 step — it is Arlington's own addition. The FlexFit hidden-code viewing procedure (hold the Emergency button for 15 seconds to view the code indoors) is a FlexFit procedure, not a split-wall procedure.

When to call a technician

  • F13 returns after a restart — the refrigerant charge and delivery tube need diagnosis by a technician.
  • The outdoor LED1 flashes 16 times — lack of refrigerant (F3, the communication fault, is 4).
  • Poor cooling or heating with F13 showing — the refrigerant shortage needs a technician, not a coil-cleaning DIY step.

Safety first

Refrigerant handling, leak-finding and recharging are qualified-technician work. Never open the outdoor unit while it is connected to power, and never handle refrigerant yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F13 mean on a Haier split air conditioner?

F13 means lack of refrigerant, or clogging of the refrigerant delivery tube. The outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 16 times (M3 Table A p.30; M7 p.25; FlexFit 3U24MS2VHB p.112).

Is F13 a communication fault?

No. One list page (acerrorcode) calls F13 "Communication failure between outdoor PCB and module", but that is F3's meaning — the communication fault between the power module (IPM/SPDU) and the outdoor PCB, LED1 4 flashes. F13 is a refrigerant fault, not a communication fault.

How does the unit detect F13?

Per the European Technical Book (M3 Table A): the unit reports the error and stops if it detects Td-Tci >= 25°C for 1 minute after the compressor starts in cooling mode for 10 minutes. If the alarm goes out 3 times in an hour, the machine locks. (A ducted manual uses a different threshold, Td-Ts >= 80°C — that variant applies to ducted units, not split-wall Table A.)

Can I fix Haier F13 myself?

No fix step exists in any manual read. Refrigerant charge and delivery-tube checks are technician work — refrigerant handling, leak-finding and recharging need a qualified technician. Switch the unit off and call one. Do not follow guides that tell you to clean the condenser coils for F13: that is not a manual F13 step.

Is F13 the same on a Haier cabinet air conditioner?

No. The cabinet 14kW table (M3 Table B) uses F13 for the compressor delivery temperature probe (Td) fault — a sensor fault — and puts the lack-of-refrigerant wording under F16. Check whether your unit is a split or a cabinet before using any F-code meaning.
