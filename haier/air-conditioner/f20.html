Haier Split Air Conditioner Error Code F20

Quick answer

Error codeF20 — Haier Split Air Conditioner
What it meansF20 = compressor overcurrent / high-pressure protection — the outdoor unit has tripped its high-pressure protection (with compressor overcurrent as M3's second half). The indoor display shows F20 (M7 marks it "Trouble record") and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 5 times. The alarm goes out 3 times in an hour and locks the machine (M3). Wording split, stated honestly: M3 alone says "Compressor over current / high pressure"; M7 and Haier India say only "High pressure protection".
Most common causeHigh-pressure protection trip (M7 p.25; Haier India official page) — M3 adds compressor overcurrent as the second half of the meaning. No cause list beyond the headline exists in any manual.
Safe to check yourself?No — Haier India's official page says these codes "only can be solved by after-sale service engineers". Switch the unit off and call the Haier hotline / a technician.
Checked againstHaier European HVAC Technical Book 2024-25 (haierhvac.eu), DIAGNOSTIC Table A p.30; Haier 1U26MADFRA Room Air Conditioner Installation Manual (Sept 2024, R32, AU/NZ), Outdoor Unit Troubleshooting p.25; Haier India official self-service page, 'How to do if indoor display error code?' (haier.com/in)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F20 is a split-wall/ductless Table A code — it means compressor overcurrent / high-pressure protection on the EU mono residential / multi range and the AU/NZ R32 split families, with the same high-pressure wording on Haier India's official self-service page (M3/M7/Haier India). The US ductless split manual (M2) does NOT list F20. The commercial and cabinet tables use F20 for a DIFFERENT fault — "Temperature protection of indoor pipe too high" (alarm 40) — the sharpest trap for this code. The old fixed-speed lamp table has no F20 at all. Check the model and series, not just the letters.

Every ranking competitor page omits F20 entirely. acerrorcode has no F20 row at all — its table jumps F19 to F21 — while putting the high-pressure meaning on the wrong code (F8, "High pressure abnormal"). Arlington has no F20 section (its sections run F19 to F21), decorexpro has no F20 content, and lioncityaircon uses a different Haier E-code system with no F-codes. F2 is "Failed to start compressor / Overcurrent" (LED1 24) — a different code for a different overcurrent event; do not follow any guide that treats F2's fix as F20's. F39 is "High pressure switch alarm" — a pressure SWITCH fault, not the F20 protection trip. F19 is voltage too high/low (LED1 6); F22 is AC overcurrent protection (LED1 3).

What the machine does when F20 fires

The outdoor unit's high-pressure protection has tripped, and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 5 times (M7, p.25; consistent with M3 Table A alarm 25). The indoor display shows F20 — M7 marks it "Trouble record" rather than "Directly display". The alarm goes out 3 times in an hour and locks the machine (M3).

Common causes

  1. Compressor overcurrent (M3 Table A p.30 — M3-only half of the meaning).
  2. High pressure (M3 Table A p.30; M7 p.25; Haier India official page).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off.
  2. Call a technician — Haier India's official page says these codes "only can be solved by after-sale service engineers" and asks you to "turn off the unit and call our Haier hotline for help". No fix step exists in any manual for F20.

No power-cycle reset time, no pressure threshold, no "clean the coils" step, no fan check and no refrigerant-charge step exists in any manual for F20. The commercial table's F20 cause ("Check heat exchange / refrigerant charge / sensors / electronic board") belongs to the commercial F20, indoor-pipe-too-high, and must NOT be presented as Table A F20 steps. M7's general warnings (auto-restart after power recovery, energise the crankcase heater 12 hours before a test run) are general installation warnings, NOT F20 steps. Do NOT present appliancepartspros' E3 "clean the vents/coils/fan" fix as an F20 fix — that page misassigns F20's meaning to E3. F20 is a one-row code in every manual — the page stays short rather than padding with invented diagnostics.

When to call a technician

  • F20 returns after a restart — the high-pressure / compressor circuit needs diagnosis by a technician.
  • The outdoor LED1 flashes 5 times — high-pressure protection (F2 is 24, F39 is the pressure switch alarm).
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

High-pressure protection is a compressor / outdoor-unit fault. Never open the outdoor unit while it is connected to power, and never attempt high-pressure or refrigerant work yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F20 mean on a Haier split air conditioner?

F20 means compressor overcurrent / high-pressure protection has tripped: the outdoor unit's high-pressure protection has activated. The indoor display shows F20 (a "Trouble record" code in the AU/NZ manual) and the outdoor PCB LED1 flashes 5 times (M3 Table A p.30; M7 p.25; Haier India official page).

Is F20 the same as F2?

No. F2 is "Failed to start compressor / Overcurrent" (LED1 24 flashes) — a failed compressor start. F20 is the high-pressure protection trip (LED1 5 flashes). Different codes, different flash counts, different faults — never merge them, even though both mention overcurrent.

Can I fix Haier F20 myself?

No fix step exists in any manual read. Haier India's official page says these codes "only can be solved by after-sale service engineers" and asks you to "turn off the unit and call our Haier hotline for help". Switch the unit off and call a technician; do not clean coils or check refrigerant on the strength of a guide that borrowed F20's meaning for E3.

Is F20 the same on a Haier cabinet or ducted air conditioner?

No. On cabinet 14kW and mono commercial units, F20 means "Temperature protection of indoor pipe too high" — a pipe-temperature fault, not high-pressure protection. Check whether your unit is a wall split (Table A) or a cabinet/ducted unit before using any F-code meaning.

How is F20 different from F39?

F39 is a "High pressure switch alarm" — the high-pressure SWITCH is unplugged, faulty, or seeing excessive refrigerant. F20 is the high-pressure PROTECTION trip itself. A switch fault and a protection trip are different faults on the same pressure circuit.
