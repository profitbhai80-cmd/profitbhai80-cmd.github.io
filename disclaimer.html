General guidance only

All content on Appliance Error Codes & Troubleshooting is provided for general information and education. It is not professional repair advice for your specific appliance, your specific fault, or your specific home.

Meanings vary by model — this matters

Error codes are not standardised. Manufacturers reuse the same short code with different meanings across model families, regions and years. Every guide here names the model families and the manual it was checked against; if your model is not in that list, your code may mean something else. Always consult the official documentation for your exact model before acting.

Safety warnings

  • Electricity: never open, test inside, or work on an appliance that is connected to power. Unplug it or isolate it at the breaker first, with dry hands. Some appliances (notably microwaves) remain dangerous even unplugged.
  • Water: close the water supply before disconnecting hoses, and keep water away from plugs, sockets and wiring.
  • Heat: heating elements, ovens and dryers can burn long after a cycle ends. Let things cool.
  • Gas: never open gas lines, adjust gas valves, or bypass flame-failure devices. Gas work requires a qualified, registered professional.
  • Refrigerant: sealed refrigerant systems (air conditioners, fridges) must only be handled by certified technicians with recovery equipment.
  • Safety devices: never bypass, tape down, or short out door locks, float switches, thermal cut-outs or any other safety device to make a code disappear.
