What to Check When a Dishwasher Will Not Drain

A dishwasher that finishes with water standing in the bottom has a drainage restriction until proven otherwise. The good news: the most common causes are in the tub, not inside the machine, and need no tools beyond a cloth and a jug.

First, safety and expectations

Switch the machine off before reaching inside. A thin film of water over the filter is normal; water covering the whole floor of the tub is not. Never put your hand into standing dishwasher water without checking for broken glass first — look, then reach.

1. Clean the filters

Remove the filter assembly in the floor of the tub (usually a twist-and-lift cylinder plus a flat mesh). Rinse it under the tap and scrub off grease and food residue. Blocked filters are the number one cause of poor drainage and of drainage error codes.

2. Check the drain hose

The hose runs from the machine to the sink trap or a standpipe. Look for kinks or a squashed section where the machine was pushed back. If the machine was recently installed or the sink plumbing changed, a hose pushed too far into a standpipe, or a sealed connection with no air break, can also stop drainage.

3. Check the sink trap and air gap

If the dishwasher drains through the sink trap, a blocked trap blocks the dishwasher too. Run the tap: if the sink drains slowly, clear the trap (a bucket underneath, gloves on). Homes with a countertop air gap should pop its cap and clear any debris inside — it clogs quietly and often.

4. The garbage disposal trap (new installations)

If a dishwasher was newly connected to a garbage disposal and has never drained, the disposal's dishwasher inlet often still has its factory knockout plug in place. That is an installer-level fix — if you are not confident with plumbing, this is a fair job for a professional.

When it is the pump

If filters, hose, trap and air gap are all clear and the machine hums but will not empty — or shows a drainage code again — the drain pump or its impeller may be jammed or failed. That is internal work and belongs to a technician.
