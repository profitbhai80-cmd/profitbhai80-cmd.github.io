Haier Washing Machine Error Code E2

Quick answer

Error codeE2 — Haier Washing Machine
What it meansDepends on the machine type: a door lock error on the HW80-B14979 front-load, or the top lid not closed during the wash on the HWM90DD top-load.
Most common causeThe door/lid is not fully shut, something is trapped in the seal, or the lock itself has failed.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — for the close-and-check steps below. A failed lock is a replacement part job.
Checked againstHaier HW80-B14979 manual; Haier HWM90DD manual

Applies to — read this first

Warning: some error-code lists say Haier E2 is a drainage fault. Neither manual we checked says that — the HW80-B14979 front-load manual says door lock error, and the HWM90DD top-load manual says the top lid is not closed during washing.

Model familyWhat E2 means here
HW80-B14979 (front-load)Door lock error — the door is not locked properly; shut the door firmly.
HWM90DD (top-load)The top lid is not closed during the wash cycle.

Find your model number on the rating label if you are not sure which family your machine belongs to — see how to find your model number.

Common causes

  1. The door or lid simply was not pushed fully shut.
  2. Clothing trapped in the door seal or lid edge, preventing closure.
  3. An overloaded drum pushing the door outward (front-load).
  4. A worn or failed door/lid lock or its sensor switch.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine.
  2. Open the door/lid fully, remove any clothing caught in the seal or lid edge, and redistribute an overloaded load.
  3. Close the door firmly until you hear/feel the latch engage (front-load), or close the lid fully (top-load).
  4. Check the rubber door seal for small trapped items (coins, socks) that stop it sealing.
  5. Reconnect power and start a short cycle; the lock should engage within seconds and the wash should begin.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E2 returns with the door/lid definitely shut and nothing trapped — the lock mechanism has likely failed.
  • The door will not unlock at the end of a cycle, or locks/unlocks randomly.
  • Visible damage to the latch, hinge or lid switch.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Never force a locked front-load door open mid-cycle and never bypass the door lock — it keeps hot water and a spinning drum safely inside.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E2 mean on a Haier washing machine?

On the HW80-B14979 front-load it is a door lock error. On the HWM90DD top-load it means the top lid is not closed during the wash.

Is Haier E2 a drainage fault?

Not in the manuals we checked. Both manuals tie E2 to the door/lid, not drainage — the drainage claim on some lists appears to be a copying error.

The door looks shut. Why E2?

Check for clothing in the seal, reduce an overloaded drum, and push the door firmly until the latch clicks. If it still shows E2, the lock itself may have failed.

Can I bypass the door lock to finish a wash?

No. The lock is a safety device. Get it repaired instead.
