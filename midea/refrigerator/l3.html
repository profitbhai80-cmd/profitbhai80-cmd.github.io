Midea Refrigerator Error Code L3

Quick answer

Error codeL3 — Midea Refrigerator
What it meansL3 = freezer fan fault — the freezer fan has stopped working. The fan inside the freezer that circulates cold air is not running. Check L4 too: that is the same fan running at the wrong speed, a different code. L-codes appear only on the smart side-by-side families; French-door and bottom-freezer families use E-codes.
Most common causeA dead or stalled freezer fan motor, or loose connection terminals on the fan.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L3 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. The home scope is: confirm the code, listen for the fan, restart once. Opening the back panel or replacing the motor is technician work.

Applies to — read this first

The L-codes are the smart side-by-side families' fan codes — MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P and MRS25I7BST — and they do not appear on the French-door or bottom-freezer E-code families. The manuals list the four as separate rows in one block: L3 freezer fan fault, L4 freezer fan speed abnormal, L5 condenser fan fault, L6 condenser fan speed abnormal. Do not merge them: L3 means the freezer fan is not working at all.

What the machine does when L3 fires

The display shows L3 and the fault stays: it does not self-clear. With the freezer fan stopped, cold air stops circulating in the freezer — the manuals say cooling or freezing may continue for a while, but temperatures will drift as the fan stays dead.

Common causes

  1. The freezer fan motor has died and is not running at all.
  2. The fan's connection terminals are loose, corroded or unplugged.
  3. The fan is stalled by heavy ice build-up or a physical obstruction.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the exact code and your model: L3 is a smart side-by-side family code (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P, MRS25I7BST).
  2. Listen at the freezer with the door open: silence from the fan area supports L3 (a running-but-odd fan points to L4 instead).
  3. Restart the fridge once and see whether L3 returns.
  4. Do not chip ice off the evaporator and do not open the back panel — the fan, its terminals and the motor replacement belong to a technician.
  5. If L3 persists, contact maintenance personnel as the manuals instruct.

The manuals name no user-serviceable fix for L3 beyond the restart. The service manual's fan steps — terminal reseat, fan motor replacement — are qualified-person work that means opening the machine.

When to call a technician

  • L3 persists after one restart.
  • The freezer is audibly silent at the fan and temperatures are drifting up.
  • Heavy frost is building on the freezer evaporator while L3 shows — the dead fan and the frost feed each other.
  • Any burning smell or damaged wiring — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

L3 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics and a fan behind a panel: never open the back panel or touch fan wiring while investigating it. Switch the fridge off at the outlet before any check, and leave fan and board work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does L3 mean on a Midea refrigerator?

L3 means the freezer fan has faulted — the fan inside the freezer that circulates cold air is not working. It is verified in three Midea manuals, two of them on midea.com itself (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P), plus the MRS25I7BST manual.

What is the difference between L3 and L4?

The manuals' own distinction: L3 = freezer fan fault (the fan is not working); L4 = freezer fan speed is abnormal (the fan is turning but at the wrong speed). If the fan is spinning oddly, your code is L4, not L3.

Is the freezer fan the same as the condenser fan?

No. The freezer fan (L3/L4) is inside the freezer circulating cold air; the condenser fan (L5/L6) is near the compressor at the back/bottom cooling the condenser. The manuals keep them as four separate rows — do not confuse the two fans.

Can the fridge keep running with L3 showing?

The manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L3 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. It is a degrading state: with the fan dead, freezer temperatures drift over time.

How is the freezer fan actually fixed?

The Midea service manual's fan checks are: verify whether the freezer fan stops working, plug and unplug its connection terminals, and replace the fan motor if it is dead. All of that is qualified-person work — it means opening the machine.
