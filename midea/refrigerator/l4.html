Midea Refrigerator Error Code L4

Quick answer

Error codeL4 — Midea Refrigerator
What it meansL4 = freezer fan speed is abnormal — the freezer fan is turning, but at the wrong speed. Different from L3, which means the fan has stopped entirely. The manuals list them as two separate rows: do not merge them. L-codes appear only on the smart side-by-side families; French-door and bottom-freezer families use E-codes.
Most common causeA stalled or obstructed freezer fan, bad connection terminals, or a failing fan motor — the fan turns, but outside its expected speed.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L4 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. The home scope is: confirm the code, check for visible obstructions without opening anything, restart once. The rest is technician work.

Applies to — read this first

The L-codes are the smart side-by-side families' fan codes — MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P and MRS25I7BST — and they do not appear on the French-door or bottom-freezer E-code families. L4 sits directly below L3 in the manuals' fan-code block, and the two rows must not be merged: L3 is a dead fan, L4 is a fan running at the wrong speed. If your fan is silent, your code is L3; if it is spinning oddly, it is L4.

What the machine does when L4 fires

The display shows L4 and the fault stays: it does not self-clear. The freezer fan is turning, but outside its expected speed — so freezer airflow is wrong and temperatures drift even though the fan is audibly alive.

Common causes

  1. The freezer fan is stalled or obstructed — heavy ice or a foreign object slowing it.
  2. Bad connection terminals on the fan, corrupting its speed feedback or drive.
  3. A failing fan motor or bearing: it turns, but cannot hold its expected speed.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the exact code and your model: L4 is a smart side-by-side family code (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P, MRS25I7BST).
  2. Listen at the freezer: a fan that is running oddly — slow, pulsing, noisy — supports L4 over L3. Do not open anything to look at it.
  3. Restart the fridge once and see whether L4 returns.
  4. Do not chip ice off the evaporator and do not open the back panel — terminal checks and motor replacement are technician work.
  5. If L4 persists, contact maintenance personnel as the manuals instruct.

The manuals name no user-serviceable fix for L4 beyond the restart. The service manual's fan steps — terminal reseat, fan motor replacement — are qualified-person work that means opening the machine.

When to call a technician

  • L4 persists after one restart.
  • The freezer fan is audibly struggling — slow, pulsing or grinding — while L4 shows.
  • Freezer temperatures are drifting despite the fan running.
  • Any burning smell or damaged wiring — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

L4 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics and a fan behind a panel: never open the back panel or touch fan wiring while investigating it. Switch the fridge off at the outlet before any check, and leave fan and board work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does L4 mean on a Midea refrigerator?

L4 means the freezer fan speed is abnormal — the freezer fan is turning, but outside its expected speed. It is verified in three Midea manuals, two of them on midea.com itself (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P), plus the MRS25I7BST manual.

The fan is spinning — why is there an error?

Because the control expects a specific speed: L4 fires when the fan turns but not at the speed the board expects — stalled by ice, a failing bearing, or bad terminal feedback. That is exactly what separates L4 from L3 (fan not working at all).

What is the difference between L4 and L3?

The manuals' own distinction: L3 = freezer fan fault (the fan is not working); L4 = freezer fan speed abnormal (the fan is turning but at the wrong speed). Silent fan: L3. Spinning-but-odd fan: L4.

Can the fridge keep running with L4 showing?

The manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L4 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. Wrong-speed airflow still lets temperatures drift.

How is a wrong-speed freezer fan actually fixed?

The Midea service manual's fan steps are: plug and unplug the connection terminals, and replace the fan motor if it is failing. All of that is qualified-person work — it means opening the machine.
