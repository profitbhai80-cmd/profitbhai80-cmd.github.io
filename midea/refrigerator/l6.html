Midea Refrigerator Error Code L6

Quick answer

Error codeL6 — Midea Refrigerator
What it meansL6 = condenser fan speed is abnormal — the condenser fan near the compressor is turning, but at the wrong speed. Different from L5, which means the fan has stopped. It mirrors the L3/L4 freezer-fan pair: L5/L6 are the condenser-fan pair. L-codes appear only on the smart side-by-side families; French-door and bottom-freezer families use E-codes.
Most common causeA stalled or obstructed condenser fan, bad connection terminals, or a failing fan motor — the fan turns, but outside its expected speed.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L6 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. The home scope is: confirm the code, check the back/bottom airflow is clear from the outside, restart once. The rest is technician work.

Applies to — read this first

The L-codes are the smart side-by-side families' fan codes — MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P and MRS25I7BST — and they do not appear on the French-door or bottom-freezer E-code families. L6 is the last row of the manuals' fan-code block, and it mirrors the L3/L4 pair on the other fan: L5 is the condenser fan fault (not working), L6 is the condenser fan running at the wrong speed. Two different fans, four different rows — keep them separate.

What the machine does when L6 fires

The display shows L6 and the fault stays: it does not self-clear. The condenser fan is turning, but outside its expected speed — so condenser cooling is wrong and the compressor works harder while the fridge may keep running for a while.

Common causes

  1. The condenser fan is stalled or obstructed by dust build-up or a foreign object, so it cannot reach its expected speed.
  2. Bad connection terminals on the fan, corrupting its speed feedback or drive.
  3. A failing fan motor or bearing: it turns, but cannot hold its expected speed.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the exact code and your model: L6 is a smart side-by-side family code (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P, MRS25I7BST).
  2. From the outside only, check the back/bottom area is clear of dust blankets and obstructions — a choked condenser area is the one home-checkable contributor. Do not reach into the fan.
  3. Restart the fridge once and see whether L6 returns.
  4. Do not open the back panel or touch the fan or its wiring — terminal checks and motor replacement are technician territory.
  5. If L6 persists, contact maintenance personnel as the manuals instruct.

The manuals name no user-serviceable fix for L6 beyond the restart and clear outside airflow. The fan, its terminals and any motor replacement are qualified-person work that means opening the machine.

When to call a technician

  • L6 persists after one restart with clear outside airflow.
  • The compressor area runs hot or the fridge is struggling to hold temperature while L6 shows.
  • The condenser fan is audibly struggling — slow, pulsing or grinding — while L6 shows.
  • Any burning smell or damaged wiring — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

L6 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics at the back of the unit: never open the back panel, remove the rear cover, or touch fan wiring while investigating it. Switch the fridge off at the outlet before any visible check, and leave fan and board work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does L6 mean on a Midea refrigerator?

L6 means the condenser fan speed is abnormal — the condenser fan near the compressor is turning, but at the wrong speed. It is verified in three Midea manuals, two of them on midea.com itself (MDRS925CIE46M, MDRB600MIC46P), plus the MRS25I7BST manual.

The condenser fan is spinning — why is there an error?

Because the control expects a specific speed: L6 fires when the fan turns but not at the speed the board expects — stalled by dust, a failing bearing, or bad terminal feedback. That is exactly what separates L6 from L5 (fan not working at all).

What is the difference between L6 and L5?

The manuals' own distinction, mirroring the L3/L4 pair: L5 = condenser fan fault (the fan is not working); L6 = condenser fan speed abnormal (the fan is turning but at the wrong speed).

Is the condenser fan the same as the freezer fan?

No. The condenser fan (L5/L6) sits near the compressor at the back/bottom and cools the condenser; the freezer fan (L3/L4) is inside the freezer circulating cold air. The manuals keep them as four separate rows.

Can the fridge keep running with L6 showing?

The manuals say the refrigerator may still provide cooling or freezing functions with L6 showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. A wrong-speed condenser fan still lets the compressor run hot over time.
