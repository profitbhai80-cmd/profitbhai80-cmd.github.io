Midea Refrigerator Error Code E0

Quick answer

Error codeE0 — Midea Refrigerator
What it meansE0 = ice maker fault ('Ice machine fault' in the US manual) on ice-maker-equipped Midea side-by-side fridges. DO NOT MIX THEM UP: the Midea air-conditioner E0 is an EEPROM parameter error — a completely different fault on a different appliance — and air-conditioner E0 pages dominate this query's search results. Applies to — read this first

E0 is an ice-maker code on ice-maker-equipped side-by-side families — the UK MDRS925CIE46M and the US MRS26D5AST. If your fridge has no ice maker, or your unit is an air conditioner, this page is not your code. The single most important thing on this page is the disambiguation below, because air-conditioner E0 (EEPROM) pages own half of this query's search results and will tell you to check chips that have nothing to do with your fridge.

What the machine does when E0 fires

The display shows E0 and ice production stops: the ice maker is faulted. The refrigerator itself keeps refrigerating — E0 is an ice-maker code, not a cooling code.

Common causes

  1. The connection terminals on the ice maker and the main PCB are loose, unplugged, or fouled with foreign matter — the service manual's own first check.
  2. A faulty ice-maker module.
  3. A fault in the main PCB's ice-making circuit.
  4. Note the split: E0 is the ice maker fault; EE is the ice maker SENSOR fault; EP is the ice maker off-ice (drop) fault. Match your code to the right row.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the appliance: this page is for Midea refrigerators with an ice maker. If your unit is an air conditioner, stop — its E0 is an EEPROM fault, a different diagnosis entirely.
  2. Note any sibling codes: EE (ice maker sensor) or EP (off-ice) point to different faults in the same ice-maker family.
  3. Power-cycle the fridge once and see whether E0 returns.
  4. Do not follow air-conditioner E0 guides — EEPROM checks have no place in a refrigerator diagnosis.
  5. If E0 persists, follow the US manual's prescribed response: contact the place of purchase or Midea customer service (1-866-646-4332). The service manual's terminal reseat is qualified-person work — do not open the machine.

The home scope is: confirm the appliance, confirm the code, restart once, and then call the seller or Midea service as the manual says. The terminal check in the service manual tree belongs to a qualified person — it means opening the machine.

When to call a technician

  • E0 persists after one restart.
  • EE or EP appear alongside E0 — the ice-maker family has faults beyond the terminals.
  • The water or ice dispenser is dead too, or there are signs of water damage near the ice maker — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E0 involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics: never open the machine's panels or touch ice-maker wiring while investigating it. Switch the fridge off at the outlet before any visible check, and leave terminal and board work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E0 mean on a Midea refrigerator?

E0 means the ice maker has faulted — 'Ice maker fault' in the UK MDRS925CIE46M manual, 'Ice machine fault' in the US MRS26D5AST manual. It appears only on ice-maker-equipped side-by-side families.

Is fridge E0 the same as air-conditioner E0?

No. Midea air-conditioner E0 is an EEPROM parameter error on indoor/outdoor PCBs — a completely different fault on a different appliance. AC E0 pages dominate this query's search results, so check you are on a refrigerator page before following any steps.

What is the difference between E0, EE and EP?

The manuals list three separate ice-maker rows: E0 = ice maker fault, EE = ice maker sensor fault, EP = ice maker off-ice fault (it fails to drop the ice). Each needs its own diagnosis — do not treat them as one 'ice maker error'.

What does the Midea service manual say to check for E0?

The REF MDRE411FGE01 BMF No Frost service manual's E0 tree starts with the connection terminals on the ice maker and the main PCB: check they are plugged in place and free of foreign matter, then clean and re-plug them. That check is qualified-person work — it means opening the machine.

Can I clear E0 by unplugging the fridge?

One restart is fine and is the only home step the manuals support. If E0 returns, the US manual's prescribed response is to contact the place of purchase or Midea customer service (1-866-646-4332).
