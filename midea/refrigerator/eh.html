Midea Refrigerator Error Code EH

Quick answer

Error codeEH — Midea Refrigerator
What it meansEH = the humidity (moisture) sensor has failed. It is the same fault with two wordings: 'humidity sensor error' on some models, 'moisture sensor error' on others. The manuals say the fridge may still cool or freeze with EH showing, but you should contact maintenance personnel.
Most common causeThe humidity/moisture sensor itself has failed, or its connection to the control board is bad.
Safe to check yourself?Limited: the manuals' own instruction is that the refrigerator may still cool or freeze, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. What you can do is confirm the code and your model, restart once, and stop there. The manuals say nothing about a user-serviceable fix.

Applies to — read this first

EH appears in the French-door and built-in Midea families; it is absent from the bottom-freezer and side-by-side tables checked (MDRB521MIE02, MDRS619FIE, MDRS791MIE, MDRS925CIE46M). If your side-by-side or bottom-freezer shows an H-flavoured code, check the display again — EH is not in those families' manuals.

What the machine does when EH fires

The display shows EH and the fault stays: it does not self-clear. The manuals' preamble is the same as for the other sensor codes — the refrigerator may still cool or freeze with EH showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel.

Common causes

  1. The humidity (moisture) sensor has failed.
  2. A bad connection between the humidity sensor and the main control board.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the exact code: EH. Then check your model — EH is a French-door/built-in family code; if your side-by-side or bottom-freezer shows it, re-read the display.
  2. Note which wording your manual uses — 'humidity sensor' or 'moisture sensor' — so you know you are looking at the same fault, not a different code.
  3. Restart the fridge once and see whether EH returns.
  4. If EH returns, stop: the manuals name no user-serviceable step beyond the restart. Contact maintenance personnel as the manuals instruct.

The manuals say only that the humidity sensor has failed — they do not say where it sits, what it controls, or how to test it. Any page that tells you it lives in the crisper drawer or hands you a voltage chart is filling gaps the manuals leave empty.

When to call a technician

  • EH persists after one restart.
  • Other fault codes appear alongside EH — then the machine has faults beyond the humidity sensor.
  • Any burning smell or visibly damaged wiring — switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

EH involves mains-powered refrigerator electronics: never open the back panel or touch sensor wiring while investigating it. Switch the fridge off at the outlet before any visible check, and leave board and sensor work to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does EH mean on a Midea refrigerator?

EH means the humidity (moisture) sensor has failed. It is verified in 6 Midea manuals across French-door (US), built-in and UK/EU families, including the official midea.com manual for the MRQ23P4AST.

My manual says 'moisture sensor error' — is that a different code?

No. It is the same EH fault with different wording: the MDRF632FGF46APD and MERF402FIE manuals say 'moisture sensor error' where the others say 'humidity sensor error'. Same code, same fault.

Does EH stop the fridge from cooling?

The manuals say the refrigerator may still cool or freeze with EH showing, but the user should contact maintenance personnel. Treat it as a degrading state, not a stable one.

Where is the humidity sensor — the crisper drawer?

No Midea manual says where the sensor sits or what it controls — only that it has failed. The 'crisper drawers' claim on one ranking page is invented; this page does not repeat it.

Can I test the humidity sensor with a multimeter?

No Midea manual gives a user procedure or a per-model voltage chart for the EH sensor. The manuals' instruction is to contact maintenance personnel.
