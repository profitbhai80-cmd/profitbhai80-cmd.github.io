Midea Dishwasher Error Code Ed

Quick answer

Error codeEd — Midea Dishwasher
What it meansEd on a Midea dishwasher means a communication failure between the main circuit board and the display circuit board: communication data anomalies, or signal loss, detected on the display board for 20 seconds. The manual fix order is to check the display-board connections, reinsert the connecting line, and power on again. The cause in the user manuals is an open circuit or broken communication wiring.
Most common causeThe communication wiring between the main board and the display board is open, broken, or loose, so the display board receives no valid signal for 20 seconds.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the manual steps: check the display-board connections and reinsert the connecting line, then power on again. If Ed persists, the wiring or a board is faulty, which is technician work.
Checked againstMidea Dishwasher Technical Service Manual (manuals.plus, 7601S valid-code family: Ed communication failure, 20-second anomaly trigger, connections and reinsert fix); Midea Smart Dishwasher with WiFi User Manual model 340607 (Ed: main-to-display communication failure, open circuit or break wiring); Midea MDC22P2ASS Dishwasher User Manual (Ed: communication exception over 20 seconds); Midea SV 5.14K14C User Manual (Ed: communication failure, check wiring); Midea MDT24P6CST User Manual FAQ (Ed: signal loss over 20 seconds)

Applies to — read this first

A dishwasher has two boards that must talk: the main circuit board runs the wash, and the display circuit board shows the program and reads your button presses. Ed means they stopped talking. It is not a wash fault, not a pump fault, and not a water fault: it is the link between the two boards failing for 20 seconds.

Ed means communication failure: if communication data anomalies are detected on the display board for 20 seconds, Ed occurs. Trap warning — 1Ed and 2Ed are not this EdOne ranking page lists 1Ed and 2Ed as discharge-pipe temperature sensors of systems A and B. Those are air-conditioner system codes pasted onto a dishwasher page, not dishwasher Ed. Dishwasher Ed is a board-to-board communication failure.

No ranking page states the 20-second trigger, explains the two boards in plain English, or gives the manual fix order. One ranking page does not contain a dishwasher Ed at all and instead contaminates its table with air-conditioner codes. The 20-second rule and the two-board explanation are what this page adds.

What the machine does when Ed fires

The display shows Ed and the dishwasher cannot run a program normally: with the main board and the display board out of communication, the machine cannot be reliably controlled or reported, so it warns Ed instead of washing on a broken link.

Common causes

  1. The communication wiring between the main board and the display board is open or broken.
  2. A display-board connector is loose, so the signal is lost for 20 seconds.
  3. The display board or the main board communication circuit has failed, so no valid signal is exchanged.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code is Ed on a dishwasher, not 1Ed or 2Ed: those are air-conditioner system codes, not this fault.
  2. Switch the dishwasher off, check the display-board connections are seated, reinsert the connecting line firmly, and power on again, in that order, as the service manual gives.
  3. If Ed returns after reseating and a power-on, stop. A broken wire or a failed board circuit is technician work; do not replace boards yourself.

An Ed that clears after reseating was a loose connection. An Ed that stays on is a wiring or board fault, and the manuals say to contact customer service at that point.

When to call a technician

  • Ed returns after reseating the connections and powering on again.
  • The display is dead, frozen, or unresponsive alongside Ed.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or water leak — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

Ed is a board-communication fault, not a home board repair: never open the control panel housing beyond reseating a connector with the power off, never touch a PCB, and never bypass a safety device. Switch the unit off before any handling and leave wiring and board replacement to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does Ed mean on a Midea dishwasher?

Ed means a communication failure between the main circuit board and the display circuit board: communication data anomalies, or signal loss, detected on the display board for 20 seconds.

What are the two boards?

The main circuit board runs the wash, and the display circuit board shows the program and reads your button presses. Ed means they stopped talking to each other.

How do I fix Midea dishwasher Ed?

In the manual order: check the display-board connections, reinsert the connecting line, and power on again. If Ed persists, the wiring or a board is faulty and needs a technician.

Is Ed the same as EC?

No. Ed is the two boards not communicating. EC is a BLDC wash-pump fault with L-subcodes. Different faults, different fixes.

What are 1Ed and 2Ed?

Air-conditioner system codes (discharge-pipe temperature sensors) that one ranking page pastes onto its dishwasher table. They are not dishwasher Ed and must not be followed on a dishwasher.
