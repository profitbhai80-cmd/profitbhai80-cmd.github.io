Midea Dishwasher Error Code EC

Quick answer

Error codeEC — Midea Dishwasher
What it meansEC on a Midea dishwasher means a BLDC wash-pump fault: the washing pump does not work well. The manuals give a subcode procedure: close the door and hold the Program button for 5 seconds (MDWEF1433DW) or Delay plus Strwash for 5 seconds (MDWEB1403MB), and a subcode L0 to L6 appears, telling whether the fault is the BLDC motor, the PCB, or a supply or overheating condition. EC is scoped to BLDC wash-pump models only, and it is not the Midea air-conditioner EC refrigerant-leak code.
Most common causeThe BLDC wash pump or its control has failed: motor failure, PCB failure, a supply-voltage excursion, a blocked motor, IPM overheating, or a sampling error, as the subcode shows.
Safe to check yourself?Only the subcode reveal and one restart. Reading the subcode is safe; replacing a BLDC motor or a PCB is technician work, and a machine unused for a long time may simply have a pump that fails to start.
Checked againstMidea Dishwasher Service Manual MDWEF1433DW-SS-WA (midea.com official PDF: EC wash pump does not work well, 5-second Program-button subcode reveal, L1/L4/L2-L5-L6 mapping); Midea Dishwasher Service Manual MDWEB1403MB-WA (midea.com official PDF: same EC analysis, Delay plus Strwash reveal); Midea Dishwasher Technical Service Manual, Troubleshooting and Repair Guide (manuals.plus: EC BLDC fault, L-condition triggers)

Applies to — read this first

EC without its subcode is only half a diagnosis, and that is exactly what the manuals refuse to give you: EC is the headline, and the L-subcode is the verdict. Close the door, hold the reveal buttons for 5 seconds, and the subcode tells you whether you are looking at a motor, a board, or a supply condition. Read the subcode before you order any part.

Subcode L0 — no failureL0 means no failure was found by the subcode check. If EC still shows with L0, the fault is intermittent or the pump simply failed to start after long disuse.
Subcode L1 — BLDC motor or PCB failureL1 means a BLDC motor or PCB failure: replace the motor or the PCB. The subcode alone does not choose between them; a technician must.
Subcode L4 — BLDC motor failureL4 means a BLDC motor failure: replace the motor. Trigger detail from the technical manual: motor blocked or phase loss, with cumulative restarts reaching 15 times.
Subcodes L2, L3, L5, L6 — PCB or supply faultsL2, L3, L5 and L6 mean a PCB failure: replace the PCB. Triggers from the technical manual: L1 also follows motor phase-winding current above 3A with 5 cumulative restarts; L2 follows input voltage above 285 plus or minus 10 volts for 7 minutes; L3 follows input voltage below 170 plus or minus 10 volts for 7 minutes; L5 follows IPM module overheating; L6 follows a BLDC circuit AD sampling error.
Scope warning — BLDC models onlyEC appears on BLDC wash-pump models (the MDWEF1433DW and MDWEB1403MB service families). The older 7601S valid-code list (E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E8, Ed) does not include EC, so EC must not be presented as universal to all Midea dishwashers. And Midea mini-split EC is a refrigerant-leak code on a different appliance — never merge the two.

What the machine does when EC fires

The display shows EC and the wash pump does not work well: washing cannot proceed normally because the pump that circulates wash water is faulty or uncontrolled. The machine stops the wash rather than running a cycle with no circulation.

Common causes

  1. The BLDC wash-pump motor has failed, or is blocked, or has lost a phase (subcodes L1, L4).
  2. The control PCB has failed, or its BLDC drive circuit has a sampling error (subcodes L1, L2, L3, L5, L6).
  3. The supply voltage went out of range for 7 minutes (above about 285 volts for L2, below about 170 volts for L3), or the IPM module overheated (L5).
  4. The machine was unused for a long time and the wash pump simply fails to start (noted in the service manual; subcode may show L0).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code is EC on a BLDC wash-pump dishwasher, not the air-conditioner EC: if your appliance is an air conditioner, that EC is a refrigerant-leak code on a different page.
  2. Reveal the subcode: close the door and hold the Program button for 5 seconds (MDWEF1433DW) or Delay plus Strwash for 5 seconds (MDWEB1403MB). Note the subcode before cutting power, because it disappears when power is cut.
  3. If the subcode is L0, or the machine was unused for a long time, restart once (press Power) and run a short cycle to see whether the pump starts. If EC returns, or the subcode is L1 to L6, stop: motor and PCB replacement are technician work.

Never order a motor or a board from EC alone: the subcode decides which part the manuals blame, and L1 still leaves motor-versus-PCB to a technician. A subcode read is a diagnosis, not a repair.

When to call a technician

  • EC returns after a restart, with any subcode from L1 to L6.
  • The wash pump does not run, or the machine stops mid-wash with EC.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or water leak — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

EC is a wash-pump and board fault, not a home repair: never open the dishwasher base or the pump housing, never touch the PCB, and never bypass a safety device. Switch the unit off and shut the water supply before any handling, and leave motor and PCB replacement to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does EC mean on a Midea dishwasher?

EC means a BLDC wash-pump fault: the washing pump does not work well. The manuals add a subcode (L0 to L6) that tells whether the fault is the motor, the PCB, or a supply or overheating condition.

How do I see the EC subcode?

Close the door and hold the Program button for 5 seconds (MDWEF1433DW models) or Delay plus Strwash for 5 seconds (MDWEB1403MB models). The subcode disappears when power is cut, so note it before switching off; press Power to restart to re-display it.

What do the EC subcodes mean?

L0 means no failure found. L1 means a BLDC motor or PCB failure. L4 means a BLDC motor failure. L2, L3, L5 and L6 mean a PCB failure, with triggers including supply-voltage excursions, IPM overheating and a sampling error.

Is dishwasher EC the same as air-conditioner EC?

No. Midea dishwasher EC is a BLDC wash-pump fault. Midea mini-split EC is a refrigerant-leak detection code. Same letters, different appliances, different repairs — never merge them.

Can I fix dishwasher EC myself?

Only the subcode reveal and one restart. Motor and PCB replacement are technician work, and L1 still leaves motor-versus-PCB to a technician.
