Midea Dishwasher Error Code E9

Quick answer

Error codeE9 — Midea Dishwasher
What it meansE9 on a Midea dishwasher means a button or touch key has been pressed for more than 30 seconds — a stuck touch key. The service manual's fix is: check for foreign objects on the button, clean it, then power on again; the quick-start guide adds: contact professional service if it persists. E9 is SCOPED to touch-key platforms: the 7601S platform's valid-code list (E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E8, Ed) has no E9, so 7601S owners must not use this page.
Most common causeSomething pressing or stuck on a touch key — a foreign object on the button, or a key held down continuously for over 30 seconds.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manual's own fix is a home fix: check the key for foreign objects, clean it, and power on again. If E9 persists after cleaning, the key or panel needs professional service.

Applies to — read this first

E9 is a stuck key, not a heating fault and not a leak — and the two ranking pages get it wrong in opposite directions, one calling it heating (that is E3's territory) and the other calling it leakage (that is E4). Both pages also mislabel E4 in the same sections, which is why neither can be trusted for E9. The platform scope matters just as much: E9 belongs to touch-key panels, not to the 7601S push-button platform.

A page that jumps straight to 'contact service' hides the home fix; a page that never mentions service hides the fallback.

What the machine does when E9 fires

The dishwasher reports E9 and waits: a button or touch key has been continuously pressed for over 30 seconds, so the control treats the key as stuck rather than as a command. Nothing is heating wrongly and nothing is leaking — the machine is refusing to run on a key it believes is jammed.

Common causes

  1. A foreign object resting on a touch key — the service manual's first check.
  2. A touch key held down continuously for over 30 seconds, by an object or by a stuck key mechanism.
  3. A faulty touch key or panel that reads as continuously pressed even when clean — the professional-service case.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Look at the control panel: is anything resting on a key, pressing it, or stuck against it? Remove it.
  2. Clean the key and the panel around it — wipe away anything that could hold the key down — then power the dishwasher off and on again, as the service manual instructs.
  3. Press each key once, briefly, and check the display: if E9 clears, the key was simply held or fouled. Do not hold any key down for 30 seconds while testing.
  4. If E9 persists with a clean, unobstructed panel, stop: the quick-start guide's instruction applies — contact professional service. The key or panel itself is faulty, and panel work is not a home repair.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. E9 is one of the few Midea dishwasher codes with a genuine home fix — a clean key — so do not jump to service until the panel has been checked. Never pry keys off the panel or open the control housing.

When to call a technician

  • E9 persists with a clean, unobstructed touch panel after a power-cycle — faulty key or panel.
  • A key that feels physically stuck, sunken, or unresponsive alongside E9.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or water near electrical parts — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

E9 is a control-panel fault, not a water or heating fault — but the dishwasher is still a mains appliance with water inside it. Never pry keys off the panel, never open the control housing, and never work on a powered machine with wet hands. If the panel itself is faulty, leave it to professional service.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E9 mean on a Midea dishwasher?

E9 means a button or touch key has been pressed for more than 30 seconds — a stuck touch key. The service manual's fix is to check for foreign objects on the button, clean it, and power on again; the WQP8 quick-start guide adds professional service if it persists.

Is Midea dishwasher E9 a heating problem?

No. Heating is E3's territory (heating exceeded pre-set time). The troubleshootinglab page reassigns E3's fault to E9 — and mislabels E4 as drainage in the same section — so its error-code section cannot be trusted.

Is Midea dishwasher E9 a leak?

No. Leakage/overflow is E4. The vectorlinux page calls E9 a water-leakage problem and contradicts itself on E4 across sections, while also inventing EA as a communication code.

Does every Midea dishwasher have an E9 code?

No. E9 is scoped to touch-key platforms. The 7601S platform's valid-code list (E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E8, Ed) has no E9 — 7601S owners must not use this page.

How do I clear Midea dishwasher E9?

Remove anything pressing the key, clean the key and panel, then power off and on again. If E9 persists with a clean panel, the key or panel is faulty — contact professional service, as the quick-start guide instructs.
