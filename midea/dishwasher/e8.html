Midea Dishwasher Error Code E8

Quick answer

Error codeE8 — Midea Dishwasher
What it meansE8 on a Midea dishwasher means the diverter (distributary) valve is faulty: the machine does not detect the diverter valve signal for 15 seconds, or accurate diverter positioning within 60 seconds. E8 is valid ONLY on models equipped with a diverter valve for the alternative-washing function — models without a diverter have no E8. During a washing program E8 is set off only after the program ends, which is why the code can appear when the wash itself seemed to finish normally.
Most common causeThe diverter valve's signal or positioning fails: the valve cannot be detected for 15 seconds, or it does not reach accurate positioning within 60 seconds, so the machine cannot aim wash water correctly between the spray arms.
Safe to check yourself?Partly. The safe home checks are: let the program finish and the drain pump stop before acting, then power-cycle once and run a short program to see whether E8 returns. The diverter valve circuit, flowmeter circuit and valve replacement are technician work under the service manual's inspection tree.
Checked againstMidea Technical Service Manual MDWEB1403MB-WA (midea.com, official, diverter-validity note); Midea Dishwasher Technical Service Manual, 7601S platform (E8 definition, 15s/60s thresholds, machine reaction, inspection tree); Midea standard-version display-code dishwasher user manuals (E8: open circuit or break of distributary valve)

Applies to — read this first

This page is only for Midea dishwashers equipped with a diverter valve (the alternative-washing function). The diverter switches wash water between the upper and lower spray arms; when its signal or positioning fails, the machine cannot aim water correctly. That scope is the whole page: the service manuals say explicitly that E8 is valid for diverter-equipped models only.

ManualExact wording for E8
Midea Technical Service Manual MDWEB1403MB-WA (midea.com, official)Error Code section note: 'E8 is valid for models equipped with a diverter valve (alternative washing function).' A second service-manual variant lists valid codes E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E8, E9, Ed, Ec with the same diverter note.
Midea Dishwasher Technical Service Manual — 7601S platform'E8 Diverter valve fault (For models equipped with a diverter for alternative washing function). During the test program, if the machine does not detect the diverter valve signal for 15 seconds or the diverter valve positioning for 60 seconds, E8 occurs. During the washing program, if the diverter valve signal is not detected for 15 seconds or positioning is not accurate for 60 seconds, the diverter valve will stop, and E8 will be set off after the washing program ends. When E8 occurs, the drain pump runs until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes, and all other components stop. The buzzer alarms for 30 seconds, and the error code is shown.' Inspection tree: diverter valve circuit, then flowmeter circuit, then replace the valve.
Midea standard-version display-code dishwasher user manualsERROR CODES tables list E8 as open circuit or break of distributary valve — 'distributary valve' is the user manuals' term for the diverter valve, consistent with the service definition.
Other brands' E8 — do not borrowGE E8 is a high-level pressure-switch fault; generic forum E8 advice talks about control panels and power supplies. Neither is Midea dishwasher E8. Midea refrigerator tables have no E8 at all — any 'refrigerator E8' row is invented.

The blog page we checked calls E8 a 'water level problem' and tells readers to check the water-level sensor — flatly wrong. Water-level sensing on this platform belongs to the flowmeter and pressure-switch items under E1 and E4, not E8. A reader following that page would check a sensor their machine does not report for E8, and would never check the diverter valve circuit.

What the machine does when E8 fires

When E8 occurs, the drain pump runs until the flowmeter is motionless for 2 minutes, and all other components stop. The buzzer alarms for 30 seconds and the error code is shown. During a washing program the behaviour is special: the diverter valve stops, but E8 itself is set off only after the washing program ends — so the wash may look finished before the code appears.

Common causes

  1. The diverter valve signal is not detected for 15 seconds.
  2. The diverter valve does not reach accurate positioning within 60 seconds.
  3. A fault in the diverter valve circuit (open circuit or break of the distributary valve).
  4. A fault in the flowmeter circuit, which the service manual checks second in its inspection tree.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Let the program finish and the drain pump run until it stops on its own — do not interrupt the drain cycle that E8 triggers.
  2. Once the machine is quiet, power-cycle it once: switch it off, wait a few minutes, switch it on, and run a short program to see whether E8 returns.
  3. If E8 returns, stop: the service manual's inspection tree is the diverter valve circuit first, then the flowmeter circuit, then valve replacement — all technician work. Do not open the machine or test valve circuits yourself.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. E8 has no deeper home diagnostic in any manual — finish, power-cycle, one test program, then the technician's circuit tree. Never open the machine's panels or touch valve wiring.

When to call a technician

  • E8 returns after a power-cycle and a test program.
  • The diverter valve circuit or flowmeter circuit needs checking — the service manual's inspection order.
  • Any sign of water leaking from the machine — switch off, close the tap and call the service centre; do not run cycles on a leaking dishwasher.

Safety first

E8 is a valve-circuit fault, not a fault to open up: never open the dishwasher's panels or touch valve or flowmeter wiring, and never work on a powered machine with wet hands. The diverter valve circuit, flowmeter circuit and valve replacement all belong to a qualified technician. Close the water supply before disconnecting any hose.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E8 mean on a Midea dishwasher?

E8 means the diverter (distributary) valve is faulty: the machine does not detect the diverter valve signal for 15 seconds, or accurate diverter positioning within 60 seconds. The official midea.com service manual MDWEB1403MB-WA and the 7601S service manual agree on this definition.

Does every Midea dishwasher have an E8 code?

No. E8 is valid only on models equipped with a diverter valve for the alternative-washing function. Models without a diverter have no E8 — the service manuals say so explicitly.

Why does E8 appear only after the wash ends?

Because the service manual defines it that way: during a washing program, if the diverter signal is not detected for 15 seconds or positioning is not accurate for 60 seconds, the diverter valve stops — and E8 is set off only after the washing program ends. The wash can look finished before the code appears.

Is Midea dishwasher E8 a water-level problem?

No. One blog page claims exactly that, but it is flatly wrong: the manual's E8 is a diverter signal and positioning fault, and water-level sensing on this platform belongs to the flowmeter and pressure-switch items under E1 and E4, not E8.

How do I clear Midea dishwasher E8?

Let the program finish and the drain pump stop, then power-cycle once and run a short program. If E8 returns, the diverter valve circuit, flowmeter circuit or valve itself needs a technician — that is the service manual's inspection order.
