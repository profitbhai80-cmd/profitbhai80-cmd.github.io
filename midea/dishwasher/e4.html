Midea Dishwasher Error Code E4

Quick answer

Error codeE4 — Midea Dishwasher
What it meansOverflow / leak protection activated: too much water in the tub or a leak detected in the base, and the drain pump runs to empty the machine.
Most common causeA leak into the base tray (hose, pump or door seal), too much water entering, or excessive foam from the wrong detergent.
Safe to check yourself?Partly — you can stop the water, check for foam and obvious leaks. Finding and fixing an internal leak is technician work.
Checked againstMidea dishwasher manuals, including US model MDT24P4AST manual

Applies to — read this first

Warning: some ranking pages claim Midea E4 is a temperature sensor fault. Midea's own manuals (including the US MDT24P4AST) say E4 is overflow/leak protection. Trust the manual.

Find your model number on the rating label if you are not sure which family your machine belongs to — see how to find your model number.

Common causes

  1. Water leaking into the base tray from a hose, the pump, or a worn door seal — the float switch triggers protection.
  2. The inlet valve letting in too much water.
  3. Excessive foam from using hand-wash detergent or too much rinse aid, which can fool the level system.
  4. A tilted machine or a blocked drain causing water to back up.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the dishwasher off and close the water tap immediately — E4 means water is somewhere it should not be.
  2. If the drain pump is running on its own, that is the protection working; let it finish, then keep the power off.
  3. Open the door slowly: a tub full of foam points to wrong detergent — scoop out foam and water, and expect a long clean-up; foam alone can trigger this code.
  4. Look under and around the machine (without moving a built-in unit) for visible water; dry the area and do not run the machine again until the source is known.
  5. Check the filters and drain for blockages that could back water up.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E4 returns on the next cycle — there is a real leak or a faulty inlet valve/float switch.
  • Water appears under the machine again after drying.
  • You cannot see where the water came from — internal leaks need the base opened by a technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Water plus mains electricity: switch off at the wall, keep hands dry, and never tip the machine or open its base yourself. If water has reached sockets or wiring, keep the power off and call a professional.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E4 mean on a Midea dishwasher?

Per Midea's manuals, E4 is overflow/leak protection: too much water in the tub or water detected in the base, with the drain pump running.

Is E4 a temperature sensor fault?

No. That claim appears on some websites, but Midea's manuals — including the US MDT24P4AST — define E4 as overflow/leak protection.

Why does the pump run with the door open?

That is the leak-protection system emptying the machine. It is doing its job; switch off the water and find the cause.

Can too much foam cause E4?

Yes. Hand-wash detergent or excess foam can overflow the tub and trigger protection. Use only dishwasher detergent.
