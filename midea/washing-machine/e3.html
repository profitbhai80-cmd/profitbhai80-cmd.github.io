Midea Top-Load Washing Machine Error Code E3

Quick answer

Error codeE3 — Midea Top-Load Washing Machine
What it meansE3 = lid open — when the washing machine is in the spin program and the safety switch is not closed, the machine alarms (M4 §6). TOP-LOAD ONLY: front-load Midea washers have no E3 — their door fault is E30.
Most common causeLid open; lid closed but the safety-switch contact is bad; safety switch or power cable loose; or PCB failure.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — close the lid properly and press Start (MA100 p.20). Switch, cable and PCB work is technician work.
Checked againstMidea MAS70/MAS80/MAS120 Top-Load Service Manual (manuals.plus), §6 'ERORR CODES AND EXPLANATIONS' (§6 + §4 + §8 + §7.6); Midea MA100W75/85/95 Owner's Manual (manualspro.net), troubleshooting table p.20; Midea MAE70-504TPS/01FM-AU-S1 User Manual (manuals.plus), troubleshooting table

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E3 is a TOP-LOAD code — it means lid open on the MAS70/MAS80/MAS120 US top-load families and the MA100/MAE70 top-load families (M4 §6/§4/§8; MA100 p.20; MAE70 troubleshooting table). The front-load family has NO E3 — its door fault is E30 (door can't be locked after 3 failed attempts). Never write 'E3 or E30'. E3 is NOT a front-load door-lock fault: the top-load machine has a lid + safety switch, not a front-load door lock. E2 is the drain fault. E4 is unbalance — the SAME safety switch, hit by the tub (M4 §8). E5 has NO manual support in M4 or MA100.

The ranking pages miss the spin-program trigger entirely. appliancemode's top-load hub calls E3 a 'Lid Open Error' with a lid-lock solenoid of ~65 Ohm — but gives no spin-program trigger, no 'cover closed but contact undesirable' branch, no M4 §8 safety-switch mechanism and no MA100 owner step, and treats FD as a separate lid-lock code (M4 has no FD). codigodeerror's dedicated E3 page is front-load framed throughout: 'clothing trapped between the rubber seal and the door' — a top-load washer has NO rubber door seal — and it never distinguishes E3 from E30. iFixit's E3 guide is the strongest practical one (part numbers, CN4/CN12, magnet test) but its 30-60 Ohm / 50-80 Ohm values are video-derived, conflict with appliancemode's ~65 Ohm, and frame E3 purely as a lid-lock hardware fault, missing M4's simpler branches. removeandreplace's 2016 hub gives one line ('Lid not closed. Close lid before it begins to spin') and also lists F0 and HU — neither in M4 or MA100. Other pages get E3 flatly wrong: homegeargeek says 'E3: Overheating issue', theblackurbantimes maps E3 to an unbalanced load, and manua.ls says 'E3, E4, or E5 typically indicate... an unbalanced load' — E3 is lid open and E5 has no manual support.

What the machine does when E3 fires

When the washing machine is in the 'spin' program and the safety switch is not closed, the machine alarms (M4 §6). The safety switch cuts off the motor power when the cover is lifted during spinning (M4 §8).

Common causes

  1. Cover is opened — 'Check that the lid is on or not' (M4 §6).
  2. Cover is closed but 'Safety switch contact undesirable' (M4 §6).
  3. 'Safety switch and power cable loose' (M4 §6).
  4. PCB failure (M4 §6).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Close the lid, Press 'Start' button (MA100 p.20, verbatim owner step).
  2. Check that the lid is on or not (M4 §6).
  3. Close the lid, check safety switch contact undesirable or not (M4 §6).
  4. Reconnect the power cord to check whether it can return to normal (M4 §6).
  5. Technician work (M4 §6, §7.6, §8): test the lid/safety switch with an Ohm meter for continuity; check whether the lever of the safety switch touches the outer tub if touching noise happens; check contact defect of the safety switch, defect of connection parts and whether the lid is open if a door error is displayed; replace the PCB if the function is not OK.

Lid-lock confusion: iFixit's E3 guide and appliancemode describe a lid LOCK assembly with a magnetic switch and resistance values (iFixit: blue wires 30-60 Ohm, black wires 50-80 Ohm pressed; appliancemode: lid-lock solenoid ~65 Ohm). Those values conflict with each other and NEITHER appears in M4 as read — M4's E3 is a SAFETY SWITCH (lever + contact), tested for continuity, with no resistance value given. Present M4's safety-switch framing and flag the lid-lock values as uncited, not manual values. Do NOT invent: no power-cycle reset time and no lock-replacement procedure exist in any manual for E3. Owner action is: close the lid properly and press Start. Switch, cable and PCB work is technician work.

When to call a technician

  • E3 returns even though the lid is closed — the safety-switch contact, cable or PCB needs diagnosis.
  • Touching noise from the safety switch lever against the outer tub (M4 §8).
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E3's owner checks are external only — close the lid and press Start. Testing or replacing the safety switch means opening the machine: that is technician work. Never open the machine while it is connected to power.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E3 mean on a Midea top-load washing machine?

E3 means the lid is open: when the washing machine is in the spin program and the safety switch is not closed, the machine alarms (M4 §6).

What causes Midea top-load E3?

Per the MAS70/80/120 service manual: the lid is open; the lid is closed but the safety-switch contact is bad; the safety switch or power cable is loose; or the PCB has failed.

What should I do first for E3?

The owner manual (MA100 p.20) says: 'Close the lid, Press 'Start' button.' Make sure nothing is trapped under the lid and it sits fully closed, then press Start. Switch, cable and PCB work is technician work.

Is E3 the same on a Midea front-load washer?

No. Front-load Midea washers (MF100/MF110/MF200/MF210) have NO E3 code — their door fault is E30, which means the door can't be locked after 3 failed attempts. Do not apply this page to a front-load machine, and do not confuse E3 with a front-load door lock.

The lid is closed but E3 still shows — what now?

M4 §6 covers exactly this: with the cover closed, the safety-switch contact itself may be bad, or the switch/power cable may be loose. A technician tests the switch for continuity (M4 §7.6) and checks the switch lever (M4 §8). Do not replace the lid lock on a guess — the manual's first checks are the lid, the switch contact, and the cable.
