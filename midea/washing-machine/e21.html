Midea Front-Load Washing Machine Error Code E21

Quick answer

Error codeE21 — Midea Front-Load Washing Machine
What it meansE21 = overtime water draining — the drain phase runs over time because the water level doesn't change within 6 minutes (M1 §6; M5 §5 p.22). E21 is a drain timeout, not a 'drain pump failure': the pump is only one of the possible causes the check procedure covers.
Most common causeA kinked or blocked drain hose, a blocked drain pump filter, a drain hose run higher than 1 m, or a faulty drain pump (150–250 Ω check, M5 pp.22–25).
Safe to check yourself?Yes — check the drain hose for blockage, check for kinks, clean the drain pump filter, and check the hose height is under 1 m. Pump resistance testing (150–250 Ω) is technician work.
Checked againstMidea MF210 Series Service Manual (midea.com), §6 'ERORR CODES AND EXPLANATIONS' (§6, E21 row); Midea MF110W60D Service Manual (manua.ls, 71 pp.), §5 'MALFUNCTION CODES AND EXPLANATIONS' (§5 pp.22–25); Midea MF200W120WB User Manual (manuals.plus), user error table; Midea MFE50/60/75 Owner's Manual (AU, manuals.plus), user error table; Midea WBMF310 Washer-Dryer User Manual (manua.ls), user error table; Midea Drum Washing Machine Service Manual (guidespdf), §3.2 (§3.2, 7-minute variant)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E21 is a FRONT-LOAD code — it means overtime water draining on the MF210, MF110, MF200, MFE and WBMF310 front-load families (M1/M2/M3/M5/M6, identical 6-minute rule). E20 (appliancemode's 'Drain Pump Error' / 'general drainage issue') has NO manual support in M1/M5 — never merge E20 into E21 and never present E20 as a real code. E10 is the water-supply (inlet) timeout — the opposite end of the cycle. E30 is the door-lock fault, unrelated to draining. One per-model timing difference: the drum service manual (M7 §3.2) gives 7 minutes instead of 6 — record both, never harmonise them into one number.

The ranking pages give E21 no precision. appliancemode merges 'E20, E21: Drain Pump Errors' — E20 is invented ('general drainage issue', no M1/M5 support) — and gives the drain pump resistance as '30 Ω for 110 Vac or 180 Ω for 220 Vac', WRONG vs M5's 150–250 Ω (a healthy 200 Ω pump would be wrongly condemned against those values); it gives no 6-minute rule, no 1 m hose-height rule, no monthly filter cleaning and no named manual. crownclassiccleaners maps 'E21 / Inverter Fault' to a heavy-load motor hum — WRONG, E21 is a drain timeout, not an inverter fault. The two JustAnswer threads jump straight to 'pump clogged or faulty, will need replacement', skipping the manual's check order (hose kink, filter, height) and the 150–250 Ω pump test.

What the machine does when E21 fires

The machine's drain phase runs over time: the water level does not change within 6 minutes of draining, so the board raises E21 — the drain phase is not completing as expected.

Common causes

  1. Drain hose kinked (M5 pp.22–25).
  2. Drain pump filter blocked — clean the drain pump filter once a month (M5 pp.22–25).
  3. Drain hose height beyond 1 m (M5 pp.22–25).
  4. Drain pump faulty — resistance should read 150–250 Ω (M5 pp.22–25, technician check).
  5. Water level sensor faulty — resistance should read 10–50 Ω (M5 pp.22–25, technician check).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the drain hose for blockage (M2/M3/M6).
  2. Check whether the drain hose is kinked (M5 pp.22–25).
  3. Check whether the drain pump filter is blocked — clean the drain pump filter once a month (M5 pp.22–25).
  4. Check whether the drain hose height is beyond 1 m (M5 pp.22–25).
  5. Technician checks (M5 pp.22–25, not owner steps): drain pump resistance 150–250 Ω OK; water level sensor 10–50 Ω OK. Do not open the machine to test these yourself.

Do NOT use appliancemode's 'typical 30 Ω for 110 Vac or 180 Ω for 220 Vac' — WRONG vs M5's 150–250 Ω. Do NOT invent: no power-cycle reset time, no pump-replacement procedure and no other resistance value exists in any manual for E21. If the checks above do not clear the fault, the page's only further advice is to call a technician; do not present the JustAnswer threads' 'pump clogged or faulty, will need replacement' jump as a manual step — the manual checks hose, filter and height first and tests the pump before condemning it. Note the per-model timing: the drum service manual (M7 §3.2) gives 7 minutes instead of 6 — check your model's manual for its figure.

When to call a technician

  • E21 returns after the hose, filter and height checks — the drain pump or level sensor needs diagnosis.
  • Water is not draining at all, or the drain pump resistance is outside 150–250 Ω (technician check).
  • Any leak, burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E21's owner checks are external only — drain hose and pump filter. Testing or replacing the drain pump means opening the machine: that is technician work. Never open the machine while it is connected to power.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E21 mean on a Midea front-load washing machine?

E21 means overtime water draining: the drain phase runs over time because the water level doesn't change within 6 minutes (M1 §6; M5 §5 p.22). It is a drain timeout, not a 'drain pump failure' — the pump is only one possible cause.

What should I check first when my Midea washer shows E21?

The user manuals say: check the drain hose for blockage (M2/M3/M6). The MF110 service manual adds: check whether the drain hose is kinked, whether the drain pump filter is blocked, and whether the drain hose height is beyond 1 m (M5 pp.22–25).

How often should I clean the drain pump filter?

Once a month, per the M5 service manual (M5 §5 pp.22–25). A blocked filter is one of the causes the manual lists for E21.

What resistance should the drain pump show?

150–250 Ω is OK per the M5 service manual. Do not use the 30 Ω / 180 Ω figures printed by appliancemode — they contradict the manual and would make a healthy pump look faulty.

Is E21 the same as E20?

No — E20 ('Drain Pump Error') has no support in the Midea service manuals read (M1, M5). It appears only on the appliancemode hub, merged with E21. Treat E21 as the real drain-timeout code and ignore E20. Also note the per-model timing: the drum service manual (M7 §3.2) gives 7 minutes instead of 6 — check your model's manual for its figure.
