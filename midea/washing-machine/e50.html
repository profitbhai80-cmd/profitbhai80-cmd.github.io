Midea Front-Load Washing Machine Error Code E50

Quick answer

Error codeE50 — Midea Front-Load Washing Machine
What it meansE50 = motor inverter PCB abnormal — the motor inverter PCB detects abnormal signals and shows the error (M1 §6; M5 §5 p.30). BLDC/inverter models.
Most common causeOne of four categories (M5 p.30): external voltage too high or too low; inverter PCB overloaded because motor rotation is blocked; inverter PCB damaged (excessive current or abnormal IPM temperature sampling); or a main-PCB issue.
Safe to check yourself?Limited — check the supply voltage against the rating label and check for motor obstruction. Motor and board replacement is technician work.
Checked againstMidea MF210 Series Service Manual (midea.com), §6 'ERORR CODES AND EXPLANATIONS' (§6, E50 row); Midea MF110W60D Service Manual (manua.ls, 71 pp.), §5 'MALFUNCTION CODES AND EXPLANATIONS' (§5 p.30, pp.32–34); Midea MF110W70BA10 manual FAQ (manua.ls) — E50 motor inverter PCB corroboration

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E50 is a FRONT-LOAD code for BLDC/inverter models — it means the motor inverter PCB is abnormal on the MF210 and MF110 front-load families (M1/M5, identical wording). E50 is NOT 'Multiple Errors' (removeandreplace) and NOT a flood-switch/drainage fault (JustAnswer Chester) and NOT a motor-interface fault (JustAnswer James) — those three claims contradict each other and the manuals. Westinghouse E50 ('Motor system problems', suds/overload) is a DIFFERENT BRAND — same code, different appliance family, never import. The MLH52S7AGS is a US top-load variant whose E50 meaning is not in any manual read — noted as a model-specific caveat; the manual meaning wins. E52/E53/E55–E58/E5A–E5D (appliancemode) have NO manual support in M1/M5 — never present them as verified.

The ranking pages give E50 no diagnosis. appliancemode's section 'E50, E52, E53, E55, E56, E57, E58, E5A, E5B, E5C, E5D, E64' calls E50 a 'BLDC motor inverter board error: General communication or internal fault' — vague, with no 4-category structure, no voltage thresholds (165/275 V, 83/158 V), a guessed BLDC resistance ('typical ~5 Ω U-V/W — example', no manual value), and E52/E53/E55–E58/E5A–E5D listed with NO manual support in M1/M5. removeandreplace calls E50 'Multiple Errors' — WRONG, it contradicts M1/M5. The two JustAnswer threads about the MLH52S7AGS contradict each other on the SAME model (flood switch vs motor interface) — forum claims, not adopted. The wsimg PDF calls E50 'a common mistake' with no diagnosis at all.

What the machine does when E50 fires

The motor inverter PCB detects abnormal signals and shows E50 (M1 §6; M5 §5 p.30) — the inverter board itself reports the fault, in one of four categories the service manual defines.

Common causes

  1. (a) External voltage abnormal — the inverter PCB judges the voltage too high or too low (M5 p.30).
  2. (b) Inverter PCB overloaded — motor rotation blocked (M5 p.30).
  3. (c) Inverter PCB damaged — excessive current or abnormal IPM temperature sampling (M5 p.30).
  4. (d) Inverter PCB abnormal due to a main PCB issue — (1) motor speed signal cannot be detected, (2) main PCB abnormality means the motor inverter PCB temperature is abnormal / motor temperature is misjudged too high, (3) motor inverter reads Flash information incorrectly due to a defective inverter PCB (M5 p.30).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the supply voltage against the rating label (M5 pp.32–34). Voltage thresholds: 220–240 V rating-label models — fault if supply is below 165 V or above 275 V; 110–127 V models — fault if below 83 V or above 158 V.
  2. Ensure there is no obstruction in the motor causing it to overload (MF110W70BA10 FAQ).
  3. Then check the motor: if the motor is defective, replace the motor (M5 pp.32–34, technician step).
  4. Then check the main PCB: if the main PCB is at fault, replace it (M5 pp.32–34, technician step).

Do NOT invent: no BLDC motor resistance value is given for E50 in M1/M5 as read (appliancemode's 'typical ~5 Ω U-V/W — example' is a hedged guess). No power-cycle reset time is given for E50. Motor and board replacement is technician work; the owner-checkable step is the supply voltage vs the rating label and checking for motor obstruction.

When to call a technician

  • E50 returns after the supply-voltage and obstruction checks — the motor, inverter PCB or main PCB needs diagnosis.
  • The motor is blocked or cannot turn freely.
  • Any burning smell or visible damage — switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E50's owner steps are the supply voltage vs the rating label and a motor-obstruction check. Motor and board replacement is technician work. Never open the machine while it is connected to power.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E50 mean on a Midea front-load washing machine?

E50 means the motor inverter PCB is abnormal: the motor inverter PCB detects abnormal signals and shows the error (M1 §6; M5 §5 p.30).

What causes Midea E50?

Per the MF110W60D service manual, four categories: (a) external voltage too high or too low; (b) inverter PCB overloaded because motor rotation is blocked; (c) inverter PCB damaged (excessive current or abnormal IPM temperature sampling); (d) a main-PCB issue (motor speed signal not detected, temperature misjudged too high, or Flash information read incorrectly).

What voltage triggers E50?

For 220–240 V rating-label models, a fault is raised if supply is below 165 V or above 275 V; for 110–127 V models, below 83 V or above 158 V (M5 pp.32–34). Check the supply against your machine's rating label first.

Is E50 a 'multiple errors' code?

No. One 2016 guide calls E50 'Multiple Errors', but the official MF210 and MF110W60D service manuals give it a specific meaning — motor inverter PCB abnormal — with a specific diagnosis. It is not a catch-all.

My Midea washer shows E50 — is it a flood or drainage problem?

No. Two forum threads about the US top-load model MLH52S7AGS claim flood switch and motor interface respectively — they contradict each other, and neither matches the manuals. Per M1/M5, E50 is the motor inverter PCB. Check supply voltage and motor obstruction; motor and board replacement is technician work.
