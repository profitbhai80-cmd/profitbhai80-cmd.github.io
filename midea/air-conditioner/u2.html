Midea Air Conditioner Error Code U2

Quick answer

Error codeU2 — Midea Air Conditioner
What it meansU2 = DC bus voltage level dropping malfunction — a voltage dip, caused by unstable supply voltage. Unlike U1/U3 (board faults), U2 points at MAINS POWER QUALITY: the first check is the electrical supply, not the board. SCOPE WARNING: the Seeley Braemar KDHA owner's manual (Midea-made, different platform) defines U2 as "Compressor Phase Loss/Phase Reversal/Out of Phase" — a different fault. U2 = DC-bus-voltage-drop is scoped to the Blueridge/STEALTH Midea-platform mini-split family only. Do not merge with Seeley U2.
Most common causeUnstable supply voltage — the manuals' "Possible Causes" column lists only this.
Safe to check yourself?Manual-derived homeowner-safe guidance: have the supply voltage checked (electrician); power-cycle after the supply is verified stable. No board-swap or multimeter steps in the manuals — do not invent them.

What the machine does when U2 fires

The display shows U2 — the control has flagged the DC bus voltage dropping below its level; the manuals' only named cause is unstable supply voltage.

Common causes

  1. Unstable supply voltage — the manuals' "Possible Causes" column lists only this.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Have the supply voltage checked by an electrician — the manual's own cause (unstable supply voltage).
  2. Power-cycle the unit after the supply is verified stable.
  3. If U2 returns on a verified-stable supply, contact a qualified technician — the manuals give no further procedure.

The manuals' cause column is one line — unstable supply voltage. The guidance above stays inside that scope plus power-cycling; no board-swap or multimeter steps exist in any manual, so this page invents none.

When to call a technician

  • U2 returns with the supply verified stable by an electrician — deeper diagnosis is technician work.

Safety first

U2 is a supply-voltage fault: never measure mains voltage yourself and never open the outdoor unit — voltage checks are electrician/technician work. Power-cycle only from the breaker or remote, then call a qualified professional.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U2 mean on a Midea air conditioner?

U2 means a DC bus voltage level dropping malfunction — a voltage dip — on the Blueridge/STEALTH Midea-platform mini-split family. Both manuals name the single cause: unstable supply voltage.

What is the difference between U2 and P1?

U2 is the DC-bus-voltage-drop FAULT on this platform's diagnostic table; P1-class codes are the low-voltage PROTECTION family on other Midea platforms. Do not cross-read platforms.

Do I need an electrician?

The manual's own cause is unstable supply voltage — so yes, an electrician checking the supply is the manual's first-line answer, before any board work.

Is the Seeley KDHA U2 the same?

No. The Seeley Braemar KDHA manual defines U2 as "Compressor Phase Loss/Phase Reversal/Out of Phase" — a different fault on a different Midea-made platform. Match your platform.
