Midea Air Conditioner Error Code U3

Quick answer

Error codeU3 — Midea Air Conditioner
What it meansU3 = current detection malfunction — the outdoor control board is the suspect. Distinct from U1 (the compressor phase-current circuit specifically): U3 is the GENERAL current-detection fault. Both are board-level; neither manual gives DIY steps. SCOPE WARNING: the Seeley Braemar KDHA owner's manual (Midea-made, different platform) defines U3 as "DC Bus Voltage Drop Error" — the Blueridge/STEALTH U2 meaning. Do not merge Seeley U3 with this page's U3.
Most common causeOutdoor control board malfunction — the manuals' "Possible Causes" column lists only this.

What the machine does when U3 fires

The display shows U3 — the control has flagged the general current-detection fault; the manuals route it to the outdoor control board.

Common causes

  1. Outdoor control board malfunction — the manuals' "Possible Causes" column lists only this.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Power-cycle the unit — the only manual-derived homeowner step.
  2. If U3 returns, contact a qualified technician — the outdoor control board is the suspect per both manuals.

Stating this honestly: the manuals give no homeowner procedure for U3 beyond power-cycling, so there is no invented fix flow here. No multimeter or board-swap steps exist in any manual — this page invents none.

When to call a technician

  • U3 returns after a power-cycle — the outdoor control board is the suspect (the manuals' own conclusion).

Safety first

U3 is an outdoor-unit board fault: the outdoor unit is mains-powered and refrigerant-connected. Never open it, never probe terminals, and never replace a control board yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U3 mean on a Midea air conditioner?

U3 means a current detection malfunction on the Blueridge/STEALTH Midea-platform mini-split family. Both manuals name the single cause: outdoor control board malfunction.

What is the difference between U1 and U3?

U1 = compressor phase-current CIRCUIT malfunction (the compressor's current-sensing circuit specifically). U3 = current detection malfunction (general). Both are board-level on this platform — the manuals distinguish them.

Is it the compressor or the board?

The manuals say the board: "Possible Causes" lists only "Outdoor control board malfunction."

Is the Seeley KDHA U3 the same?

No. The Seeley Braemar KDHA manual defines U3 as "DC Bus Voltage Drop Error" — the Blueridge/STEALTH U2 meaning. Match your platform.
