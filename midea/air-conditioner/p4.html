Midea Air Conditioner Error Code P4

Quick answer

Error codeP4 — Midea Air Conditioner
What it meansP4 means an inverter compressor drive error on Midea mini-splits: an abnormal inverter compressor drive signal was calculated by the detection circuit. The service document's causes: wiring mistakes, IPM malfunction, faulty outdoor fan motor, compressor malfunction, faulty outdoor PCB, or a surge — and, uniquely, bad commissioning (a lack of vacuum drying can cause P4). The indoor display shows P4; the outdoor display may show it as inverter module protection.
Most common causeA wiring mistake between the compressor and the PCB, an IPM (intelligent power module) fault — or, on newly installed units, poor commissioning without proper vacuum drying.
Safe to check yourself?Only the power-cycle and the external checks. Everything from the IPM continuity check onward is technician work.
Checked againstMidea High Wall System Error Codes and Fault Diagnosis Guide (Troubleshooting Type A 230VAC); Midea MSV1-12HRFN1-MT0W service manual (Vertu series); Midea MSV1I09HRDN1 service manual; Midea Cassette/Duct/Ceiling & Floor owner's manual (document 16122500000082, 20150328)

Applies to — read this first

P4 is the inverter-drive code, and it has the most actionable install-day angle of any Midea code: the Midea technical document states outright that P4 can occur on badly commissioned systems — a lack of vacuum drying can cause it. If your unit is newly installed and shows P4, the installer should return before any parts are touched. On older units the causes run through wiring, the IPM module, the outdoor fan motor, the compressor, the outdoor PCB, or a surge.

Midea MSV1I09HRDN1 service manualIndoor-unit LED display table: "P4 | Inverter compressor drive protection." (The same manual's outdoor-unit display table maps P4 to inverter module protection.)

Display mapping: indoor display P4 = inverter compressor drive protection/error; outdoor display P4 = inverter module protection; PC04 is the 4-character variant. Do not confuse with P1 (voltage) — one content-farm page assigns P4 the voltage meaning, which is P1's.

What the machine does when P4 fires

When the abnormal drive signal is calculated, the unit stops and displays P4 — the inverter drive is protecting the compressor from a drive fault.

Common causes

  1. A wiring mistake between the compressor and the PCB (including the wrong wiring sequence).
  2. An IPM (intelligent power module) malfunction.
  3. A faulty outdoor fan motor.
  4. A compressor malfunction.
  5. A faulty outdoor PCB.
  6. A power surge.
  7. Bad commissioning — no or inadequate vacuum drying on installation.

Safe checks, in order

  1. If the unit was recently installed: stop and call the installer back — the technical document names bad commissioning (lack of vacuum drying) as a P4 cause, and re-commissioning is the installer's responsibility.
  2. Otherwise: shut off the isolator, wait a few seconds, restore power — one clean restart.
  3. With the power off, do a visual-only check of the outdoor unit: clear airflow blockages and confirm the fan spins freely by hand.
  4. If P4 returns, stop: the service document's next steps are the wiring check (control PCB ↔ module PCB ↔ compressor), the IPM continuity check, and the compressor coil resistance check — all technician work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. The commissioning angle is this page's signature: a brand-new unit with P4 should go back to the installer, not to a parts catalogue. If a check needs an electrical cover removed, continuity measured, or wiring touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • P4 on a newly installed unit (installer callback — vacuum drying/commissioning).
  • P4 returning after restart on an older unit (IPM, wiring, compressor, or PCB fault).
  • The compressor will not start or trips immediately.
  • Any burning smell or scorch marks at the outdoor unit.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Never remove the outdoor unit's electrical covers — the inverter drive section carries high DC voltage that persists after switch-off. IPM continuity and compressor resistance measurements are technician work. If the unit shows distress signs, switch it off at the isolator and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does P4 mean on a Midea mini split?

P4 is an inverter compressor drive error: an abnormal inverter compressor drive signal was calculated by the detection circuit. Causes per the Midea technical document: wiring mistakes, IPM malfunction, faulty outdoor fan motor, compressor malfunction, faulty outdoor PCB, surge — or bad commissioning.

My new Midea mini split shows P4 right after installation. What should I do?

Call the installer back. The Midea technical document states P4 can occur on badly commissioned systems and a lack of vacuum drying can cause it — re-commissioning is the installer's responsibility, not a parts problem.

Is Midea P4 a voltage problem?

No — one content-farm page assigns P4 the voltage meaning, but voltage protection is P1. P4 is the inverter compressor drive error. The two codes protect different parts of the drive.

What is the IPM check for Midea P4?

The service document's diagnostic sequence includes an IPM (intelligent power module) continuity check after the wiring check — uniform terminal resistance is expected. It is a technician test with the covers off; not a home check.
