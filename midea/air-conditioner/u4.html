Midea Air Conditioner Error Code U4

Quick answer

Error codeU4 — Midea Air Conditioner
What it meansU4 = reversing valve malfunction. The manuals' three ordered causes: (1) voltage to the reversing valve below 175V; (2) loose connections between the valve and the control board; (3) reversing valve solenoid malfunction. Plain-terms: the reversing valve switches the system between heating and cooling — when it fails, the mode won't change (or heat fails). Technician-level. SCOPE WARNING: the Seeley Braemar KDHA owner's manual (Midea-made, different platform) defines U4 as "Heating: Indoor Coil, Outdoor Coil, Ambient Error" — a sensor fault. Do not merge Seeley U4 with this page's U4.

What the machine does when U4 fires

The display shows U4 — the control has flagged the reversing valve; expect the unit to fail to switch between heating and cooling (or heat to fail outright).

Common causes

  1. Voltage to the reversing valve is less than 175V — the manuals' cause 1.
  2. Loose connections between the reversing valve and the control board — the manuals' cause 2.
  3. Reversing valve solenoid malfunction — the manuals' cause 3.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Power-cycle the unit — the only manual-derived homeowner step.
  2. The manuals' cause list, in order, as the technician's checklist: check the valve supply voltage (must be ≥175V); check the valve↔board connections; check the solenoid.
  3. If U4 returns, contact a qualified technician — the manuals give no homeowner valve procedure.

The manuals' three causes are presented in their order as a technician checklist — not homeowner DIY. Do not invent multimeter procedures beyond what the manuals state.

When to call a technician

  • U4 returns after a power-cycle — the valve supply, its connections, or the solenoid needs a technician (the manuals' own cause list).
  • The unit will not switch between heating and cooling — the plain-terms signature of a reversing-valve fault.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

U4 is an outdoor-unit valve fault: the outdoor unit is mains-powered and refrigerant-connected. Never open it, never probe terminals, and never touch the reversing valve or its solenoid yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U4 mean on a Midea air conditioner?

U4 means a reversing valve malfunction on the Blueridge/STEALTH Midea-platform mini-split family. Both manuals give the same three ordered causes: (1) voltage to the reversing valve below 175V; (2) loose connections between the valve and the control board; (3) reversing valve solenoid malfunction.

What does the reversing valve do?

Plain-terms: it switches the system between heating and cooling. When it fails, the mode won't change — or heat fails outright.

Is it the valve or the board?

The manuals list both: the valve's supply voltage and the valve↔board connections, then the solenoid. The technician checks them in the manuals' order.

Is the Seeley KDHA U4 the same?

No. The Seeley Braemar KDHA manual defines U4 as a heating sensor fault ("Heating: Indoor Coil, Outdoor Coil, Ambient Error"). Match your platform.
