Midea Air Conditioner Error Code P3

Quick answer

Error codeP3 — Midea Air Conditioner
What it meansP3 on a Midea air conditioner (indoor display, timer flashing, running lamp 4 flashes) means outdoor ambient temperature too low protection: in heating mode the unit stops when the outdoor temperature stays below its family threshold for 1 hour, and resumes on its own when the recovery conditions are met. The threshold splits by family: below minus 25 degrees Celsius on one service-manual family, below minus 15 degrees on the Luna family. P3 is a protection state, not a component failure. The outdoor-unit display P3 and chiller P3 (low pressure, system B) are different codes and must not be borrowed.
Most common causeExtreme outdoor cold in heating mode: the outdoor temperature stayed below the family threshold for 1 hour, so the protection stopped the unit.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, in the sense that there is nothing to fix: confirm the code is P3 on the indoor display, confirm the outdoor temperature is genuinely extreme, and wait for the recovery conditions. Do not open the outdoor unit or attempt refrigerant work.
Checked againstMidea service manual (Scribd 411737552, section 9.1 indoor error display and section 9.3 P3 note: outdoor below minus 25 degrees for 1 hour, recovery above minus 22 degrees for 10 minutes with compressor stopped 1 hour, or above minus 5 degrees for 10 minutes); Midea Luna Series DC Inverter installation/service manual (Scribd 137093612, section 9.1: outdoor below minus 15 degrees for 1 hour, recovery above minus 12 degrees for 10 minutes with compressor stopped over 1 hour, or above 5 degrees for 10 minutes); Midea Air Conditioner Owner's Manual (manuals.plus, error table row 23: outdoor low temperature protection, timer flashing, running 4); Midea R410A Middle Static Pressure Duct Type Owner's Manual (manualspro, row 20: same P3 meaning, timer flashing)

Applies to — read this first

P3 is the unit protecting itself from extreme cold, and the most important sentence on this page is that the unit is supposed to stop. In heating mode, when the outdoor temperature stays below the family threshold for an hour, P3 appears and the machine stops. It is not a broken sensor, not a failed board, and not a fault to chase with parts: it is a weather condition with a recovery rule.

Display-side warning — outdoor P3 and chiller P3The outdoor-unit display P3 on some tables is a different code, and chiller P3 means low pressure protection in system B with manual recovery. Neither belongs on an indoor-display mini-split P3 page, and borrowing either would misdiagnose a cold-weather protection as a pressure fault.

No ranking page gives the thresholds, the recovery conditions, or the display-side warning: one gives the label only, one contradicts itself between its indoor and outdoor tables, one presents the chiller row alongside the indoor table with no separation, and two omit P3 entirely. The thresholds with family labels, the recovery rules, and the exclusions are what this page adds.

What the machine does when P3 fires

In heating mode the unit stops when the outdoor temperature stays below the family threshold for 1 hour, and the indoor display shows P3. The unit then waits for its recovery conditions: the outdoor temperature rising above the recovery threshold for 10 minutes (with the compressor stopped for an hour on the stricter route, or above the warmer recovery temperature for 10 minutes on the other route). When recovery is met, the unit resumes on its own.

Common causes

  1. Outdoor temperature below minus 25 degrees Celsius for 1 hour (service-manual family) during heating.
  2. Outdoor temperature below minus 15 degrees Celsius for 1 hour (Luna family) during heating.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code and the display side: P3 on the indoor display, with the timer lamp flashing and the running lamp flashing 4 times, is this page. An outdoor-display P3 or a chiller P3 is a different code.
  2. Confirm the outdoor temperature is genuinely extreme for your family threshold (minus 25 or minus 15 degrees Celsius sustained for an hour). If it is, there is nothing to fix: wait for the recovery conditions and let the unit resume on its own.
  3. If P3 shows in mild weather, or the unit never resumes after the outdoor temperature has clearly recovered, stop: a temperature-sensor or board fault is then possible, and sensor and board work belong to a qualified technician. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself.

Do not present P3 as a sensor to replace: on a genuinely cold day it is the protection working as designed, and the correct home action is waiting, not parts. Only a P3 in mild weather, or a P3 that never clears after recovery, earns a technician call.

When to call a technician

  • P3 shows in mild weather, far above either family threshold.
  • The unit never resumes after the outdoor temperature has clearly recovered past the recovery conditions.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or refrigerant smell near the outdoor unit — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

P3 involves mains-powered air-conditioner electronics and an outdoor unit: never open the outdoor unit's covers or touch sensor terminals while investigating it, and never attempt refrigerant work — refrigerant handling belongs to a qualified technician. Switch the unit off at the isolator before any visible check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does P3 mean on a Midea air conditioner?

P3 on the indoor display means outdoor ambient temperature too low protection: in heating mode the unit stops when the outdoor temperature stays below its family threshold for 1 hour, and resumes when the recovery conditions are met.

What temperature triggers Midea P3?

It splits by family: below minus 25 degrees Celsius for 1 hour on the service-manual family, and below minus 15 degrees Celsius for 1 hour on the Luna family. The page gives both thresholds with family labels rather than picking one.

How does P3 clear?

On its own, when recovery is met: outdoor above minus 22 degrees for 10 minutes with the compressor stopped 1 hour, or above minus 5 degrees for 10 minutes (service-manual family); or above minus 12 degrees for 10 minutes with the compressor stopped over 1 hour, or above 5 degrees for 10 minutes (Luna family).

Is P3 a broken sensor?

No, not on a genuinely cold day. P3 is a protection state: the unit is supposed to stop in extreme cold. Only a P3 in mild weather, or one that never clears after recovery, points at a sensor or board fault.

Is chiller P3 the same?

No. Chiller P3 means low pressure protection in system B with manual recovery, and the outdoor-unit display P3 is a different code again. Neither belongs on an indoor-display mini-split P3 diagnosis.
