Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E3

Quick answer

Error codeE3 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE3 splits by model family: on ES-HK800G / ES-F120G / ES-F100G / ES-G80G it means the spin does not balance; on ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT / ES-M1108T it is a drain-hose fault. Check the model number first — there is no single E3 meaning.
Most common causeOn the HK/F/G family: laundry bunched to one side during spin. On the W/M family: the drain hose position is too high, its end is blocked, or the hose is folded, crumpled or tied.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — redistribute the laundry (HK/F/G) or adjust/clean the drain hose (W/M), then press START/PAUSE. If E3 persists, the machine needs a technician.
Checked againstSharp ES-HK800G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-F120G / ES-F100G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-G80G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT Operation Manual, Abnormal display (printed p.17); Sharp ES-M1108T Operation Manual, Abnormal display (printed p.6)

Applies to — read this first

E3 has NO single meaning — it splits by model family, and the split table below is the page's lead element. On ES-HK800G, ES-F120G / ES-F100G and ES-G80G, E3 means the spin does not balance. On ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT and ES-M1108T, E3 is a drain-hose fault: the drain position is relatively high, the end of the drain hose is blocked, or the drain hose is folded, crumpled or tied. Check the model number first — never state one E3 meaning without the family check. E3 is also not the same code as front-load E03, which is a pump/filter fault.

The ranking pages get E3 wrong by leading with one meaning. knowledgebase.bison.co.in (ranks #1) leads with the MINORITY meaning — "On many Sharp top-load models, E3 is associated with a drainage problem" — while the majority meaning (spin imbalance, 3 of 5 families) appears NOWHERE in its E3 section. Its "platform difference" — "certain top-load documentation assigns E3 to a lid condition" — is INVENTED: no manual read assigns E3 to the lid. Its drain-pump checks (impeller, winding, connector, supply voltage) and drain-outlet/standpipe checks are not in any E3 manual row (the W/M settlement is hose-only). Its "one top-load family" list (E2 abnormal water level, E3 lid, E4 imbalance, E5 drainage) matches NO single manual. primerepair.co.uk covers E01–E04 only and has NO E3 section at all. What the machine does when E3 fires

On the HK/F/G family, E3 fires when the spin does not balance — the machine stops the spin until the laundry is redistributed. On the W/M family, E3 fires when the drain hose cannot drain — the drain position is too high, the hose end is blocked, or the hose is folded, crumpled or tied.

Common causes

  1. Spin imbalance (ES-HK800G / ES-F120G / ES-G80G): the laundry is not placed equally around the tub.
  2. Drain-hose fault (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT / ES-M1108T): the drain position is relatively high.
  3. Drain-hose fault (W/M family): the end of the drain hose is blocked.
  4. Drain-hose fault (W/M family): the drain hose is folded, crumpled or tied.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Imbalance side (HK/F/G): open the top lid, place the laundry equally around the tub, close the lid and clear up the alarm.
  2. Drain side (W/M): check the drain hose — if its position is too high, adjust it properly, then press START/PAUSE.
  3. Drain side (W/M): if the end of the drain hose is blocked, clean it, then press START/PAUSE.
  4. Drain side (W/M): if the hose is folded, crumpled or tied, straighten it, then press START/PAUSE.
  5. If E3 persists after the manual's own steps, stop — the machine needs a technician.

The manuals name no other owner step: no drain-pump impeller/winding check, no drain-outlet/standpipe check, no 3–5 minute drain timeout, and no control-module replacement exists in any E3 manual row. Side A's only owner action is redistributing the laundry; side B's only owner actions are adjusting/cleaning the drain hose.

When to call a technician

  • E3 persists after redistributing the laundry (HK/F/G) or after adjusting/cleaning the drain hose (W/M).
  • The machine vibrates violently or moves during spin even with a balanced load.
  • Water is leaking from the drain hose or around the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

E3 is a load or drainage fault, not an electrical fault, but never reach into the machine while the tub is spinning and never open the machine while it is connected to power. If water is leaking around the machine, turn off the tap and unplug the machine before touching anything wet.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E3 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

It depends on the model family. On ES-HK800G, ES-F120G/F100G and ES-G80G, E3 means the spin does not balance. On ES-W95TWXT/ES-W85TWXT and ES-M1108T, E3 is a drain-hose fault. Check the model number first.

How do I fix Sharp E3 (spin imbalance)?

On the HK/F/G family: open the top lid, place the laundry equally around the tub, close the lid and clear up the alarm.

How do I fix Sharp E3 (drain hose)?

On the W/M family: check the drain hose — if its position is too high, adjust it properly; if the end is blocked, clean it; if it is folded, crumpled or tied, straighten it — then press START/PAUSE.

Is Sharp E3 the same as E03?

No. E3 on top-load machines is spin imbalance or a drain-hose fault (by family); E03 on front-load machines is a pump/filter fault. Different code, different platform.

Does E3 mean the lid is open?

No. Some guides suggest E3 can mean a lid condition, but no Sharp manual read assigns E3 to the lid — the lid codes on these machines are E0, E2 and E5/U9. If the lid is open during spin, the machine shows E2, not E3.
