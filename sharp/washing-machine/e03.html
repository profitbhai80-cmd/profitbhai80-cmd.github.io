Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E03

Quick answer

Error codeE03 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE03 means the pump is faulty, or the pump filter is clogged, or the electrical connection of the pump is faulty. The manual's only owner action is to clean the pump filter (per the maintenance/cleaning chapter); if the problem persists, contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Most common causeThe pump filter is clogged — the manual's owner fix is to clean the pump filter.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — clean the pump filter, following the maintenance and cleaning chapter of your model's manual. If E03 persists, contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Checked againstSharp ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-NFB914AWC User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-HFB8143WD-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.27)

Applies to — read this first

E03 is a FRONT-LOAD code only. On Sharp front-load washers E03 means the pump is faulty, or the pump filter is clogged, or the electrical connection of the pump is faulty. Do not confuse it with top-load E3 (no zero), which differs by family: on ES-HK800G / ES-F120G, E3 means "Spin does not balance"; on ES-W95TWXT, E3 is a drain-hose fault (hose position too high, end blocked, or hose folded/crumpled/tied). Neither is "pump/filter".

The ranking pages do not carry the E03-vs-E3 split. primerepair.co.uk describes E03 as "not draining… within the designated time period" — the manual never says "not draining" and never gives a time period — and invents "around 3–5 minutes before giving up", a number no Sharp manual states. It also tells owners to "replace the drain pump" on a humming symptom and blames the control module, while the manual says only "clean the pump filter… contact the nearest authorised service agent". Its sump-pipe/waste-pipe checks are generic drain advice, not in the E03 row. knowledgebase.bison.co.in gives E03 a one-line summary entry only ("E03 – pump/filter fault") — no dedicated section, no fix steps, no manual quote — and its separate top-load E3 section says drainage on "many Sharp top-load models" while ES-HK800G E3 is spin imbalance, its own sections disagreeing without model scoping. theblackurbantimes.com has no E03 coverage; its generic drainage advice carries no code and must not be attached to E03, and its E1 = drainage claim is wrong on every manual read.

What the machine does when E03 fires

The machine reports a pump fault: the pump is faulty, the pump filter is clogged, or the pump's electrical connection is faulty (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10). The manual's only owner action is to clean the pump filter, per the maintenance and cleaning chapter; if the problem persists, contact the nearest authorised service agent.

Common causes

  1. The pump is faulty (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  2. The pump filter is clogged (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  3. The electrical connection of the pump is faulty (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Clean the pump filter, following the maintenance and cleaning chapter of your model's manual (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  2. If the problem persists after cleaning the filter, contact the nearest authorised service agent.

The manuals name no other owner step: no drain timeout (no "3–5 minutes"), no pump-replacement-on-humming rule, no sump-pipe check, and no control-module replacement exists in them. The page's only owner action is the manual's own: clean the pump filter per the maintenance chapter; if E03 persists, contact the nearest authorised service agent.

When to call a technician

  • E03 persists after the pump filter has been cleaned.
  • The pump makes no sound at all, or makes a loud humming/grinding noise — do not keep running the machine.
  • Water is leaking from the pump filter area — switch off, unplug, and mop up before touching anything.

Safety first

Before cleaning the pump filter, switch the machine off and unplug it, and have towels and a shallow tray ready — water left in the machine will come out when the filter is opened. Never open the machine while it is connected to power.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E03 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp front-load washers, E03 means the pump is faulty, or the pump filter is clogged, or the electrical connection of the pump is faulty.

How do I fix Sharp E03?

Clean the pump filter, following the maintenance and cleaning chapter of your model's manual. If E03 persists after cleaning the filter, contact the nearest authorised service agent.

Is Sharp E03 the same as E3?

No. E03 on front-load machines is a pump/filter fault; E3 on top-load machines is spin imbalance (ES-HK800G) or a drain-hose fault (ES-W95TWXT), depending on the family. Check the model number.

Does E03 mean the machine will not drain?

A faulty or clogged pump can stop the machine draining, but the manual defines E03 by the pump/filter/connection — not by a drain time. No Sharp manual gives a 3–5 minute drain timeout for E03.

Should I replace the drain pump if E03 keeps showing?

The manual does not tell owners to replace the pump. Clean the filter first; if E03 persists, the pump, its connection, or the machine needs an authorised service agent.
