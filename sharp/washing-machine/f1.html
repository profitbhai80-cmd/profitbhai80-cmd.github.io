Sharp Washing Machine Error Code F1

Quick answer

Error codeF1 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansF1 means the water level sensor is wrong. The manual's only owner action is a 1-minute power-off reset: turn the power off, leave it for 1 minute, turn it on again. If F1 still shows, cut the power supply and call a technician.
Most common causeThe water level sensor is reporting wrong — the manual names no component cause for owners to check.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the 1-minute power-off reset only. If F1 persists, cut the power and call a technician.
Checked againstSharp ES-HK800G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-F120G / ES-F100G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-G80G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display)

Applies to — read this first

F1 is a TOP-LOAD code only. On Sharp top-load washers (ES-HK800G, ES-F120G / ES-F100G, ES-G80G), F1 means the water level sensor is wrong. The W/M top-load family (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT, ES-M1108T) has NO F1 and reports the same sensor fault as C2 — ES-M1108T p.6: "C2 — Water level sensor shows an abnormal level" — so check the model family, not just the code. Front-load Sharp machines do not use F1 at all (Er 01–Er 04 on ES-NDB8144SD-EN §11). F1 is brand-specific: other brands' F1 (Whirlpool control-board short, Hotpoint motor fault, IFB F01, Sharp dishwasher F1 drainage) are different faults on different machines — never import them.

What the machine does when F1 fires

When F1 fires, the machine reports the water level sensor as wrong and will not run normally. The manual's only owner action is the 1-minute power-off reset; if F1 still shows after that, cut the power supply and call a technician.

Common causes

  1. The water level sensor is reporting wrong — the manual names no component cause for owners to check.
  2. The sensor fault can also appear on the W/M family as C2 ("Water level sensor shows an abnormal level") — same sensor fault, different code letter.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Turn the power off and leave it for 1 minute.
  2. Turn the power on again and check whether F1 still shows.
  3. If F1 still shows, cut the power supply and call a technician — that is the manual's instruction.

The manuals name no other owner step: no pressure-hose kink check, no multimeter continuity test, no pressure-switch replacement, no inlet-valve check, no control-board replacement exists in any F1 manual row. (The M1108T C2 settlement — pull out the plug and entrust the maintenance department — is a different family's instruction and must not be merged into F1.)

When to call a technician

  • F1 still shows after the 1-minute power-off reset.
  • F1 appears together with other fault codes.
  • The machine will not start, or water is leaking — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

F1 is a sensor fault inside a mains-powered machine. Never open the machine, never test or replace the sensor yourself, and never bypass a safety device. If F1 persists after the 1-minute reset, cut the power supply and call a technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F1 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp top-load washers (ES-HK800G, ES-F120G/F100G, ES-G80G), F1 means the water level sensor is wrong.

How do I fix Sharp F1?

Turn the power off and leave it for 1 minute, then turn the power on again. If F1 still shows, cut the power supply and call a technician — that is the manual's instruction.

Is Sharp washer F1 the same as the F1 on other brands?

No. F1 is brand-specific: on Whirlpool it can mean a control-board short and on Hotpoint a motor fault. On Sharp top-load washers it is a water-level-sensor fault. Always match the code to the brand and model.

My Sharp washer shows C2, not F1 — is that the same fault?

Closely related but a different code on a different family: the ES-M1108T family reports the sensor fault as C2 ("Water level sensor shows an abnormal level") and has no F1. Its settlement is different too — pull out the plug and entrust the maintenance department.

Should I test or replace the pressure switch if F1 keeps showing?

The Sharp manuals do not tell owners to test or replace anything for F1. Do the 1-minute power-off reset once; if F1 persists, cut the power and call a technician.
