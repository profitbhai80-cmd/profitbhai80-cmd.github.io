Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E02

Quick answer

Error codeE02 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE02 means the water pressure or the water level inside the machine may be low. Manual owner actions only: check the tap is fully turned on; the mains water may be cut off; if the problem continues the machine stops automatically after a while — then unplug, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Most common causeThe tap is not fully turned on, or the mains water supply is cut off.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — check the tap is fully turned on. If E02 persists, unplug the machine, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Checked againstSharp ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-NFB914AWC User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-HFB8143WD-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.27)

Applies to — read this first

E02 is a FRONT-LOAD code only. On Sharp front-load washers E02 means the water pressure or the water level inside the machine may be low. Do not confuse it with top-load E2 (no zero), which on ES-HK800G / ES-F120G / ES-W95TWXT means the top lid is open when spinning — a different code on a different platform. And do not confuse E02 (too little water/pressure) with E04 (too much water) on the same front-load table.

The ranking pages do not carry the E02-vs-E2 split. primerepair.co.uk gets E02 directionally right ("water pressure too low") but drops the manual's "or the water level" half, invents a humming-solenoid-valve diagnosis (not in any Sharp manual), claims the "control module has failed and will need to be replaced" (the manual says unplug/turn off tap/contact service agent), and omits both the manual's "machine will stop after a while automatically" behaviour and the "mains water may be cut-off" cause. knowledgebase.bison.co.in gives E02 a one-line summary entry only ("E02 – low water pressure/level") — no dedicated section, no fix steps, no manual quote — and its top-load summary table (E2 abnormal water level, E3 lid, E4 imbalance, E5 drainage) matches no single manual read. theblackurbantimes.com has no E02 coverage; its E2 = door claim is the trap (front-load door is E01; front-load E02 is water pressure/level; top-load E2 is the lid).

What the machine does when E02 fires

If the problem still continues, the machine will stop after a while automatically (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10). The manual then says: unplug the machine, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

Common causes

  1. The tap is not fully turned on (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  2. The mains water may be cut off (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the tap is fully turned on (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  2. Check whether the mains water is cut off — if the supply is off, E02 is expected.
  3. If the problem still continues, the machine will stop after a while automatically. Then unplug the machine, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

The manuals name no other owner steps: no solenoid-valve test, no humming-valve diagnosis, and no control-module replacement exists in them. The page's only owner actions are the manuals' own: check the tap is fully turned on (mains water may be cut off); if E02 continues, the machine stops by itself — then unplug, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

When to call a technician

  • E02 persists even though the tap is fully on and the mains water supply is normal.
  • The machine stopped by itself after E02 appeared.
  • Water is leaking from the inlet hose or around the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Never open the machine while it is connected to power. If water is leaking around the machine, turn off the tap and unplug the machine before touching anything wet.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E02 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp front-load washers, E02 means the water pressure or the water level inside the machine may be low. Check that the tap is fully turned on — the mains water may be cut off.

Is Sharp E02 the same as E2?

No. E02 on front-load machines is low water pressure/level; E2 on top-load machines is usually the top lid open when spinning. Different code, different platform.

Will E02 clear itself?

The manual says if the problem continues, the machine will stop after a while automatically. Then unplug it, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

What is the difference between E02 and E04?

Opposite faults: E02 is too little water or pressure; E04 is too much water (excess water). Check the tap first for E02.

Should I replace the inlet valve if E02 keeps showing?

The manual does not name the valve or any part for E02. If the tap is fully on and the mains supply is normal but E02 persists, the machine needs an authorised service agent — do not replace parts on a code alone.
