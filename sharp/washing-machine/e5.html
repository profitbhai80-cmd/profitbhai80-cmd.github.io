Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E5

Quick answer

Error codeE5 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE5 has no single meaning — it splits by model family. On ES-HK800G / ES-F120G / ES-F100G / ES-G80G it means the top lid was opened after setting child-lock; on ES-X155 / ES-X751 / ES-X851 it means a drainage fault. Check the model number first.
Most common causeOn the HK/F/G family: the top lid was opened after child-lock was set. On the X family: the drain hose is too high, frozen, blocked, bent, squeezed or tangled.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — close the top lid within 5 seconds (HK/F/G) or fix the drain hose (X series), then press START/PAUSE. If E5 persists, the machine needs a technician.

Applies to — read this first

E5 has NO single meaning — it splits by model family, and the split table below is the page's lead element. On ES-HK800G, ES-F120G / ES-F100G and ES-G80G, E5 means the top lid was opened after setting child-lock (child-lock light flashing synchronously). On ES-X155, ES-X751 and ES-X851, E5 means a drainage fault. Never state one E5 meaning without the family check. E5 is also not the same code as U9 (the W/M family's child-lock lid code) or E7 (the X family's child-lock lid code).

The ranking pages get E5 wrong by giving only one meaning. knowledgebase.bison.co.in (ranks #3) mentions E5 exactly once in the whole page — "E5 – drainage" in its "one top-load family" list — with no E5 section, no quick-table row and no FAQ question. That list (E1 water supply, E2 abnormal water level, E3 lid, E4 imbalance, E5 drainage) DOES match a single manual — the ES-X155 troubleshooting table — but bison never names ES-X155, and the list is wrong as a general top-load statement: on HK/F/G, E5 is the child-lock lid code, E2 is the lid, E3 is imbalance and E4 is a 20-minute filling timeout. Bison gives no child-lock E5 meaning anywhere. usro.net's J-Tech E5 ("outdoor temperature sensor error") belongs to Sharp inverter fridges/air conditioners, never to washers. manuals.plus's own ES-G80G FAQ block garbles settlements across E1/E2/E4 — a manuals.plus FAQ error, not the manual text; never quote it as the manual.

What the machine does when E5 fires

On the HK/F/G family, E5 fires when the top lid is opened after child-lock is set: the washing stops and an alarm sounds. If you close the lid within 5 seconds, the alarm stops and the machine continues; after 5 seconds it begins draining if the water in the inner tub is 5 cm higher, with an alarm every 10 minutes. On the X family, E5 fires when the machine cannot drain: the drain position is too high, or the hose is frozen, blocked, bent, squeezed or tangled.

Common causes

  1. Child-lock side (HK/F/G): the top lid was opened after setting child-lock.
  2. Drainage side (X series): the draining position is relatively high.
  3. Drainage side (X series): the drainage hose is frozen or blocked.
  4. Drainage side (X series): the drainage hose is bent, squeezed or tangled.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Child-lock side (HK/F/G): close the top lid and clear up the alarm — within 5 seconds if possible, so the machine continues without draining.
  2. Drainage side (X series): lower down the drain hose, then press START/PAUSE.
  3. Drainage side (X series): defreeze or unblock the drainage hose, then press START/PAUSE.
  4. Drainage side (X series): smooth out the drainage hose if it is bent, squeezed or tangled, then press START/PAUSE.
  5. If E5 persists after the manual's own steps, stop — the machine needs a technician.

The manuals name no other owner step: no lid-switch test, no drain-pump impeller/winding check, no pressure-hose or PCB step exists in any E5 manual row. Side A's only owner action is closing the lid within 5 seconds; side B's only owner actions are the three hose fixes. Do not replace parts on the code alone.

When to call a technician

  • E5 persists after closing the lid (HK/F/G) or fixing the drain hose (X series).
  • E5 appears even though the lid is closed and child lock is off.
  • Water is leaking from the drain hose or around the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

E5 is a lid or drainage fault, not an electrical fault, but never reach into the machine while the tub is spinning and never open the machine while it is connected to power. If water is leaking around the machine, turn off the tap and unplug the machine before touching anything wet.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E5 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

It depends on the model family. On ES-HK800G, ES-F120G/F100G and ES-G80G, E5 means the top lid was opened after setting child-lock. On ES-X155, ES-X751 and ES-X851, E5 means a drainage fault (drain hose too high, frozen, blocked, bent or tangled). Check the model number first.

How do I fix Sharp E5 (child lock)?

On the HK/F/G family: close the top lid and clear up the alarm. If you close the lid within 5 seconds of opening it, the alarm stops and the machine continues; after 5 seconds it starts draining.

How do I fix Sharp E5 (drainage)?

On the X family: lower the drain hose, defreeze or unblock it, or smooth it out if bent, squeezed or tangled — then press START/PAUSE.

Is Sharp E5 the same as U9?

Same trigger, different code on a different family. E5 is the child-lock lid code on the ES-HK800G / ES-F120G / ES-G80G family; U9 is the child-lock lid code on the ES-W95TWXT / ES-M1108T / ES-W110DS-S family. Their settlements differ too.

Why do some guides say E5 means drainage?

Because on the ES-X155 family it does. But on the HK/F/G family E5 means the lid was opened after setting child-lock. One code, two meanings — the model number decides which one applies.
