Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E04

Quick answer

Error codeE04 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE04 means the machine has an excessive amount of water. The manual's behaviour note: the machine will discharge the water by itself. Owner actions only: once the water has drained, turn off the machine, unplug it, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Most common causeThe machine has an excessive amount of water — the manual names no component cause; the machine discharges the water by itself.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — let the machine discharge the water by itself. Once the water has drained, turn the machine off, unplug it, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.
Checked againstSharp ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-NFB914AWC User Manual §10 (EN p.28); Sharp ES-HFB8143WD-DE User Manual §10 (EN p.27)

Applies to — read this first

E04 is a FRONT-LOAD code only. On Sharp front-load washers E04 means the machine has an excessive amount of water. Do not confuse it with top-load E4 (no zero), which is the OPPOSITE fault and differs by family: on ES-W95TWXT, E4 means imbalance ("Washing machine inclines or is shaky… clothing is lopsided"); on ES-HK800G / ES-F120G, E4 means "After 20 minutes of water filling time and still not reaching the water level setting" — a filling timeout, too little water, not too much. Neither is "excess water".

What the machine does when E04 fires

The machine will discharge the water by itself (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10). Once the water has drained, the manual says: turn off the machine, unplug it, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

Common causes

  1. The machine has an excessive amount of water (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10). The Sharp manuals name no component cause for E04.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Let the machine discharge the water by itself (ES-NFA814BW1NA-DE User Manual §10).
  2. Once the water has drained, turn off your machine and unplug it.
  3. Turn off the tap and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

The manuals name no other owner step: no pressure-switch check, no inlet-valve-stuck-open cause, no waste-pipe backflow cause, and no control-module replacement exists in them. The page's only owner actions are the manuals' own: let the machine discharge the water by itself; once drained, turn off, unplug, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

When to call a technician

  • E04 appears again on the next wash after the machine has discharged the water.
  • The machine does not discharge the water by itself.
  • Water is leaking from the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

Do not open the door while the machine holds excess water — water will spill out. Let the machine discharge the water by itself, then turn it off, unplug it, and turn off the tap. If water is leaking onto the floor, unplug the machine before stepping in it.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E04 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp front-load washers, E04 means the machine has an excessive amount of water. The machine will discharge the water by itself.

What should I do when E04 appears?

Let the machine discharge the water by itself. Once the water has drained, turn the machine off, unplug it, turn off the tap, and contact the nearest authorised service agent.

Is Sharp E04 the same as E4?

No. E04 on front-load machines is excess water; E4 on top-load machines is load imbalance (ES-W95TWXT) or a 20-minute filling timeout (ES-HK800G) — the opposite kind of fault. Check the model number.

What is the difference between E04 and E02?

Opposite faults on the same front-load table: E02 is too little water or pressure; E04 is too much water.

What causes E04?

The Sharp manuals do not name a component cause for E04 — they say only that the machine has an excessive amount of water and will discharge it by itself. Do not trust lists of pressure-switch or valve causes; those are not in the manuals.
