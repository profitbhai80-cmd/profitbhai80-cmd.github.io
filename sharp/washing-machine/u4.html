Sharp Washing Machine Error Code U4

Quick answer

Error codeU4 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansU4 means the door is not closed. Close the door until the 'locked' light is on, confirm it, then press START/PAUSE to reoperate.
Most common causeThe door was not closed until the 'locked' light came on.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — close the door until the 'locked' light is on, confirm it, then press START/PAUSE. If U4 persists with the door closed and the light on, the door lock needs a technician.
Checked againstSharp ES-FH10AT-W Manual, Troubleshooting Guide; Sharp ES-FH105BV-B Operation Manual, Troubleshooting Guide

Applies to — read this first

U4 is a FRONT-LOAD code only, on the FH family (ES-FH10AT-W, ES-FH105BV-B). On these machines U4 means the door is not closed. U4 is not the same code as E01 on the front-load E01–E04 family (door not shut — same symptom, different code, different family, different settlement) or H on the same FH family (door can't be opened — the opposite direction: after the wash, door won't open; U4 is before the wash, door won't shut).

No ranking page states Sharp washer U4 at all — not even the two FH manuals rank in the query's top 10. The entire top 10 is E01 / Er 01 front-load manuals: "The door of your machine is not shut, properly. Shut the door properly until you hear a click. If your machine persists to indicate fault, turn off your machine, unplug it and contact the nearest authorised service agent immediately." A searcher landing on any of these gets the E01 door fault and can mistake it for U4 — same symptom, different code and family, and the E01 settlement (click, then unplug + service agent) differs from the FH U4 settlement ('locked' light, confirm, press START/PAUSE). knowledgebase.bison.co.in has no U4 content (its only U-code is U9).

What the machine does when U4 fires

When U4 fires, the door is not closed and the machine will not start. The manual's settlement names the 'locked' light: close the door until the 'locked' light is on, confirm it, then press START/PAUSE to reoperate.

Common causes

  1. The door was not closed fully.
  2. The door was closed but the 'locked' light never came on.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Close the door until the 'locked' light is on.
  2. Confirm the light is on.
  3. Press the START/PAUSE button to reoperate.
  4. If U4 stays on with the door closed and the 'locked' light on, stop — the door lock needs a technician.

The manuals name no other owner step: no door-lock replacement, no "turn off, unplug and contact the nearest authorised service agent" step (that is the E01 settlement, not the FH U4 settlement), and no latch or hinge check exists in any U4 manual row. Do not replace parts on the code alone.

When to call a technician

  • U4 persists with the door closed and the 'locked' light on.
  • The 'locked' light never comes on with the door closed.
  • U4 appears together with other fault codes.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

U4 is a door fault. Never force the door shut, and never open the machine while it is connected to power. If the door lock is faulty, the machine needs a technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U4 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp front-load washers ES-FH10AT-W and ES-FH105BV-B, U4 means the door is not closed. Close the door until the 'locked' light is on, confirm it, then press START/PAUSE.

Is Sharp U4 the same as E01?

Same symptom, different code on a different family. E01 is the door-not-shut code on the front-load E01–E04 family (close until you hear a click, then unplug + service agent if it persists). U4 is the door-not-closed code on the FH family (close until the 'locked' light is on, confirm, press START/PAUSE). Check the model number first.

What is the 'locked' light?

The FH manual's own detail: close the door until the 'locked' light is on, then confirm it before pressing START/PAUSE. If the light never comes on with the door closed, the door lock needs a technician.

Is Sharp U4 the same as H?

No. U4 on the same FH family means the door is not closed (before the wash). H means the door can't be opened (after the wash). Same family, opposite directions.

What if U4 stays on with the door closed?

The manuals give no further owner step beyond closing until the 'locked' light is on and pressing START/PAUSE. If U4 persists with the door closed and the light on, the door lock needs a technician — do not replace parts on the code alone.
