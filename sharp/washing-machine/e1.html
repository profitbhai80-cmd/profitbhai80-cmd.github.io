Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E1

Quick answer

Error codeE1 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE1 means the washer has not reached the set water level — a water-inlet / water-supply fault. The manuals' causes are a closed faucet, low flow at the faucet, or a blocked water-inlet filter net. The 15-minute check is Sharp's own wording, carried identically by the ES-HK800G, ES-G80G and ES-F120G manuals.
Most common causeA closed faucet, low flow at the faucet, or a blocked water-inlet filter net — the ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual's own three causes.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manual's own owner checks only: turn the faucet on, increase the water supply, clean the inlet filter net, then press START/PAUSE. If E1 persists, the machine needs a technician.
Checked againstSharp ES-HK800G Operation Manual §1 (Abnormal display); Sharp ES-F120G / ES-F100G Operation Manual (Abnormal display table); Sharp ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT Operation Manual (Common non-fault phenomenon); Sharp ES-G80G Operation Manual (Abnormal display table)

Applies to — read this first

E1 is a TOP-LOAD code only, and it is not the same code as front-load E01. On Sharp top-load washers E1 means the machine has not reached the set water level — a water-inlet / water-supply fault. On Sharp front-load washers there is no E1 at all: the front-load code is E01, and it means the door is not shut properly. Check the model number and the platform first, not just the letters. On the same top-load platform, E4 is a separate filling-timeout code (the 20-minute check) — E1 (15-minute check) vs E4 (20-minute check) are two distinct codes.

The ranking pages do not carry this split. knowledgebase.bison.co.in gets E1 directionally right ("not receiving sufficient water") but its "one top-load family" summary table (E2 abnormal water level, E3 lid, E4 imbalance, E5 drainage) matches NO single manual read — ES-HK800G has E2 lid / E3 imbalance / E4 filling timeout, and ES-W95TWXT has E2 lid / E3 drain / E4 imbalance. Its E3 section says drainage on "many top-load models" while ES-HK800G E3 is spin imbalance, and its E4 section says imbalance while ES-HK800G E4 is a filling timeout. theblackurbantimes.com says E1 is a drainage problem — WRONG on all four manuals read; drain-hose advice belongs to a drain fault, not E1. It also assigns the door meaning to E2 (wrong on both platforms) and gives an "open and close the door six times within 12 seconds" reset that is not a Sharp manual instruction. webtech360's 1E ("Water does not enter the machine") is a digit-first code from Vietnamese Sharp models — a different code format, never import it onto E1.

What the machine does when E1 fires

The machine has not reached the set water level after 15 minutes of draining time (ES-HK800G / ES-F120G wording). The ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual adds a timing note: when water is not supplied enough to operate the machine, this error code will be displayed only after six hours.

Common causes

  1. The faucet is closed (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual).
  2. Low flow at the faucet (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual).
  3. The water inlet filter net is blocked (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Turn the faucet fully on, then press START/PAUSE (ES-W95TWXT / ES-W85TWXT manual).
  2. If flow is low at the faucet, increase the water supply, then press START/PAUSE.
  3. Clean the water inlet filter net, then press START/PAUSE.
  4. On ES-HK800G / ES-F120G models: open the top lid and refer to the "before using" procedures and "common non fault phenomenon" in the manual to settle it, then close the top lid and clear up the alarm.

The manuals name no other owner steps: no solenoid-valve test, no multimeter step, and no control-module replacement exists in them. If E1 persists after the water-supply checks, the machine needs a technician — inlet valve, water-level sensor and control faults are not owner steps in these manuals.

When to call a technician

  • E1 persists after the faucet is fully on, the water supply is normal, and the inlet filter net is clean.
  • E1 appears even though water is clearly entering the machine normally.
  • Water is leaking from the inlet hose or around the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

E1 is a water-supply fault, not an electrical fault, but keep water away from the machine's electrical parts and never open the machine while it is connected to power. If water is leaking around the machine, turn off the tap and unplug the machine before touching anything wet.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E1 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp top-load washers, E1 means the machine has not reached the set water level — a water-inlet / water-supply fault. The manual's causes are a closed faucet, low flow at the faucet, or a blocked water-inlet filter net.

Is Sharp E1 the same as E01?

No. E1 on top-load machines is a water-inlet fault; E01 on front-load machines means the door is not shut properly. Same brand, different platform, different fault — check the model number, not just the letters.

How do I fix Sharp E1?

Turn the faucet fully on, increase the water supply if flow is low, and clean the water-inlet filter net; then press START/PAUSE. The ES-W95TWXT manual notes E1 may only appear after six hours if water is not supplied enough.

Is E1 a drainage problem?

No. Some blogs say E1 means drainage, but every Sharp top-load manual read defines E1 as a water-level-not-reached (water inlet) fault. Drainage is a different code on these platforms (E3 on the ES-W95TWXT family).

What is the difference between E1 and E4 on a Sharp top-load washer?

Both are water-level-not-reached faults with different timers: E1 is the 15-minute check, E4 is the 20-minute water-filling check (ES-HK800G / ES-F120G). Fix the water supply first for either.
