Sharp Washing Machine Error Code E11

Quick answer

Error codeE11 — Sharp Washing Machine
What it meansE11 means no water — the machine is not receiving water. Check the tap, a frozen pipe, a blocked filter, or whether the water has stopped, then press START/PAUSE.
Most common causeThe tap is off, the water pipe is frozen, the filter is blocked, or the water has stopped.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manual's own four checks only: tap, frozen pipe, blocked filter, water stopped — then press START/PAUSE. If E11 persists with water flowing, the machine needs a technician.
Checked againstSharp ES-FH10AT-W Manual, Troubleshooting Guide; Sharp ES-FH105BV-B Operation Manual, Troubleshooting Guide

Applies to — read this first

E11 is a FRONT-LOAD code only, on the FH family (ES-FH10AT-W, ES-FH105BV-B). On these machines E11 means no water — the machine is not receiving water. E11 is not the same code as top-load E1 (a water-inlet fault with a 15-minute check) or front-load E01 (door not shut, on the E01–E04 family). E12 on the same FH family is the opposite water direction — undrained, not no water.

What the machine does when E11 fires

When E11 fires, the machine is not receiving water. The manuals list four inspection points — tap on/off, frozen water pipe, blocked filter, water stopped — and the settlement is to press START/PAUSE.

Common causes

  1. The tap is turned off.
  2. The water pipe is frozen.
  3. The filter is blocked.
  4. The water supply has stopped.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the tap is turned on.
  2. Check the water pipe has not frozen.
  3. Check the filter is not blocked.
  4. Check whether the water has stopped.
  5. Press the START/PAUSE button.

The manuals name no other owner step: no inlet-valve resistance test, no pressure-switch or water-level-sensor step, and no "replace the main control board" step exists in any E11 manual row. JustAnswer's Whirlpool E11 advice ("Overflow Recovery Mode… replace the main control board") is for Whirlpool, not Sharp. Do not replace parts on the code alone.

When to call a technician

  • E11 persists with the tap open and water flowing.
  • E11 appears even though water is clearly entering the machine normally.
  • Water is leaking from the inlet hose or around the machine — turn off the tap and unplug the machine first.

Safety first

E11 is a water-supply fault, not an electrical fault, but keep water away from the machine's electrical parts and never open the machine while it is connected to power. If water is leaking around the machine, turn off the tap and unplug the machine before touching anything wet.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E11 mean on a Sharp washing machine?

On Sharp front-load washers ES-FH10AT-W and ES-FH105BV-B, E11 means no water — the machine is not receiving water. Check the tap, a frozen pipe, a blocked filter, or whether the water has stopped, then press START/PAUSE.

Is Sharp E11 the same as E1?

Same direction, different code on a different platform. E1 is the water-inlet fault on top-load machines (15-minute check); E11 is the no-water code on the FH front-load family. Check the model number, not just the letters.

Is Sharp E11 the same as E01?

No. E01 on the front-load E01–E04 family means the door is not shut properly. E11 on the FH family means no water. Different code, different family.

What is the difference between E11 and E12?

Opposite water directions on the same FH family. E11 = no water coming in; E12 = water not draining out. Their inspection points differ.

What if E11 stays on with the tap open?

The manuals give no further owner step beyond the four checks and pressing START/PAUSE. If E11 persists with water flowing, the inlet valve or sensor needs a technician — do not replace parts on the code alone.
