Beko Washing Machine Error Code E12

Quick answer

Error codeE12 — Beko Washing Machine
What it meansE12 = water-level aquastop error: water has been detected in or under the machine. Unplug the machine and clear any water under it before calling service. This is not a drain fault (that is E05) and not E27 — E27 is a separate newer-platform code.
Most common causeWater detected in or under the machine from a leak — inlet hoses, drain hose, door seal, pump seal, dispenser or the sump hose area.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the external checks: unplug, look for and mop up water under and around the machine, and check hoses visually. Finding the leak source itself is often technician work.
Checked againstArçelik Beko B13 Washing Machine Service Handbook §11 (water level aquastop level error); Beko user manual BM3WFSU48415WPBB2 (E12: water inside/under the machine, unplug)

Applies to — read this first

E12 is the aquastop code, and the manuals give you the exact action to take. The B13 service handbook names it 'water level aquastop level error' — the anti-flood system has detected water in or under the machine. A Beko user manual (BM3WFSU48415WPBB2) prints the E12 row in the same form owners see it: 'There may be water inside the machine. Unplug the machine. There may be water under the machine. Clean the water under the machine.' Two manufacturer documents, two families — a service handbook row and a user-manual row.

E12 is aquastop water detection: unplug first, clear water, then investigate.

What the machine does when E12 fires

The machine stops and will not run normally while the aquastop system senses water where it should not be. You may find a wet floor under or behind the machine — in one owner case, a hot wash was left running and the owner returned to a wet floor with E12 on the screen.

Common causes

  1. Water leaking from an inlet hose or its seals.
  2. Water from the drain hose connection or the sump hose area.
  3. Water from the detergent drawer area or the door seal during filling.
  4. A pump seal or internal hose leak letting water reach the base tray.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Unplug the machine immediately — the manual's first instruction.
  2. Check under and around the machine for water; mop up any water under the machine.
  3. Visually check the inlet hoses, drain hose and the area around the detergent drawer for obvious leaks or loose connections. Do not open any panels.
  4. Plug the machine back in and try running a short cycle; watch the base area for fresh water.
  5. If water reappears, or you see water leaking from one of the hoses, close the water valves and call the service — that is the manual's own next step.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the situation is safe. Do not run repeated cycles on a machine that keeps producing water underneath it.

When to call a technician

  • Water keeps reappearing under the machine after you have cleaned it up.
  • You can see a hose leaking, split or disconnected.
  • E12 persists with no visible water — the aquastop sensor or wiring may be at fault, which is technician work.

Safety first

Water plus a mains appliance is the dangerous combination: unplug before you touch anything wet, and mop with dry hands on a dry footing. Never open the machine's panels to hunt for the leak yourself — internal hoses and the aquastop device are technician territory.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E12 mean on a Beko washing machine?

E12 is the water-level aquastop error: the anti-flood system has detected water in or under the machine. The user manual's own instruction is: unplug the machine, clean the water under the machine, plug it back in, and try a short cycle.

Is Beko E12 a drainage fault?

No — that is E05's territory (pump filter clogged). E12 means the aquastop system sensed water in or under the machine, which points to a leak, not a blocked drain. Cleaning the pump filter does not address E12.

Is Beko E12 the same as E27?

No. E27 is a separate newer-platform code that one competitor pillar merges into E12 without any manual stating a combined code. Treat E12 by its own row: aquastop water detection.

What should I do first when E12 appears?

Unplug, then check under and around the machine for water and mop it up. Then visually check hoses, plug back in, and try a short cycle. If water returns or a hose is visibly leaking, close the water valves and call service.
