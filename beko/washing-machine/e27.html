Beko Washing Machine Error Code E27

Quick answer

Error codeE27 — Beko Washing Machine
What it meansE27 means: check the water drain of the machine. The drain hose is not installed correctly — its position, height, or routing (kinking) is wrong. It is not the pump filter (that is E05) and not a leak (that is E12).
Most common causeDrain hose installed wrong: kinked or twisted hose, wrong drain height, or a loose/poorly seated hose end at the standpipe or drain. The manual's fix path is the 'Connecting the drain hose to the drain' section.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — checking the hose routing is the manual's own remedy, and it is all external: no tools and no panel removal.

See Connecting the drain hose to the drain section'

Applies to — read this first

E27 is a standalone code on the current BM/B user-manual platform (BM3WFSU, BM1WFSU, BM3WFU, BMW, BMGWU, B3WFU, WUE and B5WF families). On the older B13 service tables E27 had no standalone row — it was grouped with E07, E12 and E15 — which is why repair-site tables still lump it with sensor and leak codes. On your current manual, E27 means one thing only: check the drain hose installation. It is not a pump, sensor or leak code.

The ranking repair table lumps E27 into three grouped rows — E07/E27 (level sensor), E12/E27 (leak), E15/E27 (flowmeter) — so a reader cannot tell whether to check the drain hose, the pressure sensor, or the leak tray, and the leak advice it gives (tilt the machine to reset the aqua-stop) belongs to E12, not E27. What the machine does when E27 fires

The machine reports E27 and points you at the drain hose installation. Water may not drain properly, so the wash cannot complete — but this is a hose-routing problem, not a dead pump.

Common causes

  1. The drain hose is kinked or twisted along its length.
  2. The hose end is at the wrong height at the standpipe or drain (the manual's drain section sets the position).
  3. The hose is not seated properly in the standpipe or drain connection.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the machine off and pull it forward gently so you can see the drain hose along its full length.
  2. Straighten the hose: work out any kinks or twists, and make sure it is not pinched behind the machine.
  3. Check the hose height and position at the standpipe or drain against your manual's 'Connecting the drain hose to the drain' section — it must not lie on the floor and must sit correctly in the drain.
  4. Reseat the hose end firmly in the standpipe or drain.
  5. Run a drain/spin and watch the water leave freely. If the hose is correct and E27 persists, the fault is past the home checks.

E27 is an external plumbing check: the manuals name no step that needs the machine opened. If the hose routing is correct and E27 returns, call service rather than opening panels.

When to call a technician

  • E27 persists with the hose unkinked and correctly positioned.
  • Water is visibly leaking inside or under the machine — that is E12 territory (switch off, unplug, and call service), not a hose re-route.
  • Any burning smell or other fault codes alongside E27 — stop and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E27 is an external check, but switch the machine off before pulling it forward, keep hands dry around the outlet hose, and be ready for residual water in the hose. Never open the machine's panels or bypass any safety device.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E27 mean on a Beko washing machine?

E27 means: check the water drain of the machine — the drain hose is not installed correctly. The manuals point to the 'Connecting the drain hose to the drain' section (hose position, height, kinking). It is printed verbatim in 12 Beko user manuals across the BM3WFSU, BM1WFSU, BM3WFU, BMW, BMGWU, B3WFU, WUE and B5WF families.

Is E27 the same as E05 or E12?

No. E05 is a clogged pump filter, E12 is water inside or under the machine (unplug and clean), and E07 is the water level sensor. E27 is the drain hose installation — a separate row with a separate fix.

Is Beko E27 the same as Bosch E27?

No. Bosch's E27 is a door-lock/pressure-switch code on Bosch washers — a different brand's code with a different meaning. Do not follow Bosch E27 advice for a Beko machine.

Why do some tables group E27 with E07, E12 and E15?

Those tables follow the older B13 service platform, where E27 had no standalone row. On the current BM/B user-manual platform E27 is its own code: check the water drain. The old grouping is why competitors mislabel it.

Do I need to open the machine for E27?

No. The manual's remedy is the drain hose routing — position, height, kinking — which is all external. If the hose is correct and E27 persists, call service rather than opening panels.
