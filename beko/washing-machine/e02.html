Beko Washing Machine Error Code E02

Quick answer

Error codeE02 — Beko Washing Machine
What it meansE02 means the board has detected the heater as open circuit — the heater is not heating the water. Both Arçelik service manuals we checked define it identically ('* Heater open circuit error'), with the customer complaint 'Machine boiled the water once, but now it does not heat at all.' The manuals print the code as E2; newer displays show E02.
Most common causeThe heater is open circuit (or its circuit is broken), so the machine cannot heat the water.
Safe to check yourself?You can safely do one power-cycle reset and observe whether hot programs stay cold. Measuring the heater resistance or touching any connector is technician work.
Checked againstArçelik MLCD_BLDC-BL Fault Diagrams (Rev 01, 08.10.2009, prepared by Engin BAYATLI, Recep SERÇE) — manufacturer service document for the MLCD BLDC platform (Beko WMB series); Arçelik BX B7S BXBLED BL Washing Machine Service Manual No:1 (WTK92151W) — manufacturer service handbook with failure flow diagrams

Applies to — read this first

Naming first: the Arçelik service documents print this code as E2; current Beko displays show it as E02. Same code, same fault. Both Arçelik platforms we checked define E02 identically: a heater open-circuit fault — the board detects the heater as open circuit and the machine does not heat. E02 is the opposite fault to E03: E02 means the heater will not heat; E03 means the heater will not stop.

Protective note: with E1/E2/E3 "machine will make cold washing and end program without the heater having been activated."
Arçelik BX B7S BXBLED BL Washing Machine Service Manual No:1 (WTK92151W)E2 flow identical: "* Heater open circuit error"; "Machine boiled the water once, but now it does not heat at all"; KN5 heater socket + KN8 tacho socket; 25 Ohms heater check; 25560 Hertz water-level check; same cold-washing protective note.

Newer Beko front-load user manuals only list E5/E8/E17/E29 in the user troubleshooting table — E02 lives in the service documentation, which is why this page cites the service manuals directly. Do not confuse with E01 (the NTC temperature-sensor signal fault) — E01 is the sensor, E02 is the heater itself.

What the machine does when E02 fires

The manuals describe a protective response: with an E1/E2/E3 fault the machine does cold washing and ends the program before the heater is activated. In practice: hot programs run but the water stays cold, the cycle may take longer than usual, and E02 is displayed — the machine refuses to heat rather than heating blindly.

Common causes

  1. The heater element is open circuit (broken coil) — the manual's definition of E02.
  2. Heater wiring or terminals damaged, loose, or corroded.
  3. A short circuit between the first pins of the heater and tacho connectors (KN8/KN4 on the MLCD platform; KN5/KN8 on the BX B7S platform) — the manual's first electrical check, pointing at the control board.
  4. The water-level sensor misreading — the manual branches to a sensor-frequency check (around 25560 Hz) before condemning the heater.
  5. A faulty control board — the manual's last step after heater, connectors, and sensor check out.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the machine off, unplug it from the wall, wait about a minute, then plug it back in. A single reset clears a transient glitch — if E02 returns, the heater circuit fault is real.
  2. Run a hot program and check the result the way the manuals frame it: does the water stay cold and the cycle run long? Note exactly when E02 appears and copy the full model number from the rating label — a technician needs both.
  3. Stop there. The manuals' next steps — the heater resistance check (their pass value: 25 ohms), the connector short-circuit check, and the water-level sensor frequency check — all require opening the machine and working on mains-voltage parts. That is technician work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a connector touched, or a resistance measured, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E02 returns immediately after the power-cycle reset.
  • Hot programs consistently finish with cold water.
  • A technician's heater measurement is far from the manual's 25-ohm reference or shows open circuit.
  • Any burning smell, scorch marks, or tripped household electrics — switch off and unplug, then call a qualified technician.

Safety first

The heater circuit runs at mains voltage and the heater sits in water inside the machine. Never remove panels or touch heater terminals yourself. The manuals' own safety sections stand: if the product is faulty, it must be repaired by authorized service — risk of electric shock. If you smell burning or see scorch marks, switch off, unplug, and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E02 mean on a Beko washing machine?

E02 means the board has detected the heater as open circuit — the heater is not heating. Both Arçelik service manuals we checked define it this way, identically, on the WMB and WTK platforms. The customer complaint in the manuals: 'Machine boiled the water once, but now it does not heat at all.'

Some websites say Beko E02 is a water-supply problem. Is that right?

No. The two Arçelik service manuals define E2 strictly as a heater open-circuit fault. Water-supply faults live at E8/E4 in these manuals, never E2. Checking taps and hoses will not fix an open-circuit heater.

What is the difference between E01 and E02?

E01 is the NTC temperature-sensor signal fault (the board cannot read the water temperature); E02 is the heater itself being open circuit (the board cannot heat the water). The manuals treat them as separate faults with separate diagnostic flows.

Can I keep washing on cold cycles with E02 showing?

The manuals say the machine already does that itself: with an E1/E2/E3 fault it makes cold washing and ends the program before the heater is activated. Do not keep running failed cycles — arrange the heater-circuit repair.

Will a reset fix E02?

A single power-cycle reset clears a transient glitch. If E02 returns, the heater circuit genuinely needs testing — the manuals' heater check (25 ohms) is technician work.
