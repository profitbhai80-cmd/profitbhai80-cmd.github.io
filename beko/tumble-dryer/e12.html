Beko Tumble Dryer Error Code E12

Quick answer

Error codeE12 — Beko Tumble Dryer
What it meansE12 = Belt Broken — the drive belt has broken (or come off), so the drum cannot rotate. BX B7S / BLED heat-pump tumble dryer platform only. No second standalone dryer handbook was found publicly.

Safe to check yourself?Partly — you can check the symptom (motor runs, drum does not turn) and check the drum turns freely by hand, with the dryer unplugged. Belt, tensioner, idler or pulley replacement is a technician's job on most models.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Beko dryer E12 carries a family-count caveat this page states honestly and prominently: E12's meaning is verified in ONE genuine manufacturer document — the Beko/Arcelik service handbook below — which covers TWO named platforms (BX B7S and BLED) with separate service-test programs for each. That is the basis for the 2-family count: the code-to-meaning row is stated once in a table that applies to both named platforms. No second standalone dryer handbook was found publicly. The meaning itself ('Belt Broken') is not in doubt — it is verbatim from the handbook. Scope: E12 here is the BX B7S/BLED heat-pump dryer platform only. Do NOT merge E12 with washer E12 (water inside the machine) or with the BLDC motor codes E18–E27 (motor/inverter electronics — E12 is the mechanical belt).

What the machine does when E12 fires

The display shows E12 and the drum cannot rotate — the motor runs but the belt has broken or come off. In the service test, Step 11 rotates the motor clockwise ('Drr') and Step 16 rotates it counter-clockwise ('rLL').

Common causes

  1. The drive belt has broken or come off — the mechanical fault the handbook's row names.
  2. Belt, tensioner, idler or pulley wear — replacement is a technician's job on most models.
  3. Not a clogged filter: a clogged filter is E13, and folding it into E12 blurs the two codes (the leading dryer guide lists 'a clogged dryer filter screen causing resistance' as a belt-code cause — no handbook support).
  4. Not a motor/inverter fault: motor faults on BLDC models are E18–E27. A locked rotor (E25) can look like a belt fault but is a different code.
  5. Wrong-appliance reading — Beko washer E12 means there may be water inside the machine.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Symptom check: the motor runs but the drum does not turn (or the drum turns only by hand) — the belt has broken or come off.
  2. Unplug the dryer before any inspection. Check the drum rotates freely by hand and look for obstructions.
  3. Belt / tensioner / idler / pulley replacement is a technician's job on most models — do not open the machine to reach the belt yourself.
  4. Do NOT fold the filter code into E12: a clogged filter is E13, not E12.

The handbook gives NO E12-specific home procedure beyond the table row and the service-test rotation checks (Steps 11/16). Belt replacement is technician work on most models, and the handbook gives no belt part numbers or tension figures — this page invents none.

When to call a technician

  • The motor runs but the drum does not turn — the belt is the prime suspect and needs a technician.
  • The drum does not turn freely by hand, or there is a burning smell or damaged wiring — switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Other fault codes alongside E12 (for example a BLDC code E18–E27) — the fault may be motor/inverter electronics, not the belt.

Safety first

E12 is a mechanical fault in a mains-powered dryer: always unplug the dryer before any inspection, and never reach into the drum area or open the machine to reach the belt yourself. Belt replacement is technician work on most models.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E12 mean on a Beko tumble dryer?

Belt Broken: the drive belt has broken (or come off), so the drum cannot rotate.

How do I know it's the belt?

The motor runs but the drum does not turn. If the drum turns freely by hand but not under power, the belt is the prime suspect.

Is E12 a motor fault?

No. E12 is the mechanical belt. Motor / inverter faults on BLDC models are E18–E27. A locked rotor (E25) can look like a belt fault (drum won't turn) but is a different code.

Could a clogged filter cause E12?

No. A clogged filter is E13. A filter can contribute to overheating or timeouts, not to a broken belt.

Is dryer E12 the same as washer E12?

No. Washer E12 means there may be water inside the machine. Same token, different appliance, different meaning.
