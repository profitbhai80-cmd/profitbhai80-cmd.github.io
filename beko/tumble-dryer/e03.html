What the machine does when E03 fires

The display shows E03 and the dryer will not dry - it cannot detect a load in the drum. E03 is a LOAD state, not a component failure: a single item or an almost-dry load cannot be sensed.

Common causes

  1. No load detected - the load is too small or too dry for the moisture sensor to detect.
  2. Dirty moisture/humidity sensor strips inside the drum (near the lint filter) - the handbook's Steps 12/14 test is the sensor's short/open-circuit check.
  3. If E03 persists with a normal wet load, the humidity sensor, its cables/sockets, or the main board are the technician's checks.
  4. Wrong-appliance reading: washer E03 is a drain fault; cookupexperts' "motor overload" contradicts the handbook.

Safe checks, in order

  1. E03 is a LOAD state, not a component failure: put a sufficient amount of wet laundry in the drum and restart - a single item or an almost-dry load cannot be sensed.
  2. Clean the moisture/humidity sensor strips inside the drum (near the lint filter) - the handbook's Steps 12/14 test is the sensor's short/open-circuit check, and "ScE" is its failure display.
  3. If E03 persists with a normal wet load, stop - the humidity sensor, its cables/sockets, or the main board are the technician's checks (handbook Step 12 note).
  4. A technician runs the service-test program: Step 12 shorts the sensor by touching the sensor plate ("Sco" means OK, "ScE" means a sensor/cable fault); Step 14 checks the sensor open circuit.

The handbook gives a one-line definition plus the service-test steps (Steps 12/14) for E03. It gives NO motor or heater fix for E03 - do not invent one. The humidity-sensor test is technician work.

When to call a technician

  • E03 persists with a normal wet load in the drum - the humidity sensor and its circuit need a technician.
  • The service test shows "ScE" (sensor short-circuit error) or will not pass Step 14.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E03 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E03 is a load/sensor state in a mains-powered heat-pump dryer: never open the machine or probe the humidity-sensor wiring yourself. The service-test program (Steps 12/14) is technician work. Load the drum and clean only the user-accessible sensor strips, with the machine switched off.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E03 mean on a Beko tumble dryer?

Drum Empty: the dryer cannot detect a load in the drum.

Can E03 appear if there are clothes in the drum?

Yes, if the load is too small or too dry for the moisture sensor to detect. Add more wet laundry and restart.

Is E03 a motor fault?

No. Some lists say "motor overload" - the handbook says "Drum Empty". There is no motor meaning for dryer E03.

How do I test the moisture sensor?

In the service-test program the sensor is shorted by touching the sensor plate (Step 12): "Sco" means OK, "ScE" means a sensor/cable fault. That is a technician's test.

Is dryer E03 the same as washer E03?

No. Washer E03 is a drain fault. Same token, different appliance, different meaning.
