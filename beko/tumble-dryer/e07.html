What the machine does when E07 fires

The display shows E07 and the dryer stops - the door temperature sensor circuit is shorted. In the service test, Step 8 shows "d:SC" for this fault, and if an NTC or voltage error occurs the test is not allowed to pass to the next step.

Common causes

  1. Door NTC short circuit - a short in the door-NTC wiring or sensor.
  2. Not a motor fault, not a water-level sensor fault, and not a drain-pump fault: those are the three competing WASHER E07 meanings - none of them the dryer meaning.
  3. Do not confuse the door NTC with the compressor NTC (E09/E10/E11) - a different sensor with the same three fault types.
  4. A searcher with E07 who follows appliancemode's E06 section gets an open-circuit diagnosis for a short-circuit fault - the opposite fault on the same sensor.

Safe checks, in order

  1. E07 is a sensor-circuit fault (short circuit in the door-NTC wiring or sensor), not a user-serviceable cleaning job. No home fix is documented.
  2. A technician runs the service-test program: Step 8 shows "d:SC" for this fault, then checks the door-NTC harness, sockets, and sensor.
  3. Do NOT use appliancemode's E06-section resistance guidance as an E07 test (its 12 k-ohm baseline is unsourced; see the E06 page).

The handbook gives NO E07-specific home procedure and NO resistance values. E07 is a one-line definition plus one service-test step (Step 8, "d:SC"). Anything beyond that is a technician job.

When to call a technician

  • E07 appears at all - the door NTC circuit needs a technician with the service-test program.
  • The service test shows "d:SC" and will not pass Step 8.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E07 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E07 is a sensor-circuit fault in a mains-powered heat-pump dryer: never open the machine or probe the NTC wiring yourself. The service-test program is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E07 mean on a Beko tumble dryer?

Door NTC: Short Circuit. The door temperature sensor circuit is shorted.

What is the difference between E06 and E07?

Same sensor, opposite fault: E06 is an open circuit, E07 is a short circuit.

Can I fix E07 myself?

The handbook gives no home fix. A technician runs the service-test program (Step 8 shows "d:SC") and checks the sensor harness and sockets.

Why do other sites say E07 is a motor or drain fault?

Those are WASHER E07 meanings - and they disagree with each other (motor vs water-level sensor vs drain pump). Dryer E07 is the door NTC short circuit.

What is the difference between E07 and E08?

E07 is a short circuit in the door sensor; E08 is the same sensor overheating. Different fault modes, same sensor.
