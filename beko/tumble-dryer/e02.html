Beko Tumble Dryer Error Code E02

Quick answer

Error codeE02 — Beko Tumble Dryer
What it meansE02 = Tank Full - the condensation water tank is full. BX B7S / BLED heat-pump tumble dryer platform only. The meaning itself ('Tank Full') is not in doubt - it is verbatim from the handbook. Scope: E02 here is the BX B7S/BLED heat-pump dryer platform only. Do NOT merge E02 with dishwasher E:02 (water supply cut off), washer-dryer E02 (no inlet water), or washer E02 (heating fault).

What the machine does when E02 fires

The display shows E02 and the dryer stops - the condensation water tank is full. In the service test, Step 10 activates the pump and the Tank Full LED blinks if the overflow micro-switch socket is not connected.

Common causes

  1. The condensation water tank is full - empty it and resume.
  2. If E02 returns with an empty tank, the overflow micro switch and its cables are the check (handbook Step 10 note) - a stuck float or faulty switch can signal "full" with an empty tank.
  3. Wrong-appliance reading: dishwasher E02 means no water coming in; washer-dryer E02 means no inlet water - the opposite of dryer E02.
  4. Not a sensor error as defined: E02 is the tank-full state; the pump is a separate service-test step - do not replace the pump first.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Empty the condensation water tank (the user-manual procedure Beko itself publishes for the tank warning) and press Start/Pause to resume.
  2. If E02 returns with an empty tank, stop - the overflow micro switch and its cables are the check (handbook Step 10 note), a technician's job; do not present pump replacement as the first step.
  3. Do not use appliancemode's resistance figures (overflow switch infinite ohms / 0.1-1.0 ohms; drain pump 209-1395 ohms): unsourced, absent from the handbook.

The handbook gives a one-line definition plus one service-test step (Step 10) for E02. It gives NO resistance values and NO drain-pump test figures. Anything beyond emptying the tank is a technician job.

When to call a technician

  • E02 returns with an empty tank - the overflow micro switch and its cables need a technician (handbook Step 10).
  • The Tank Full LED blinks in the service test with the switch socket disconnected.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E02 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E02 is a tank-full state in a mains-powered heat-pump dryer: never open the machine or probe the overflow-switch wiring yourself. Empty only the user-accessible condensation tank, with the machine switched off, and leave the service-test program to a technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E02 mean on a Beko tumble dryer?

Tank Full: the condensation water tank is full.

I emptied the tank but E02 is still showing - why?

The handbook's check is the overflow micro switch and its cables (Step 10). A stuck float or faulty switch can signal "full" with an empty tank - a technician's check.

Is E02 a drain-pump fault?

Not as defined. E02 is the tank-full state; the pump is a separate service-test step. Do not replace the pump first.

Is dryer E02 the same as dishwasher E02?

No - the opposite. Dishwasher E02 means no water coming in; dryer E02 means the tank is full of water.

My dryer shows a tank symbol but not E02 - is that the same thing?

Related but not identical: user manuals show a water-tank warning symbol; E02 is the service-code display on BX B7S/BLED heat-pump models.
