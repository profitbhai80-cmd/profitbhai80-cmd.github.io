Beko Tumble Dryer Error Code E06

Quick answer

Error codeE06 — Beko Tumble Dryer
What it meansE06 = Door NTC: Open Circuit - the door temperature sensor circuit is open (a broken wire/connection or a failed sensor). BX B7S / BLED heat-pump tumble dryer platform only. FAMILY-COUNT CAVEAT, stated honestly: verified in ONE genuine Arcelik/Beko service handbook covering TWO named platforms (BX B7S and BLED) - the code-to-meaning row is stated once in a table that applies to both named platforms. No second standalone dryer handbook was found publicly.
Most common causeDoor NTC open circuit - a broken wire/connection or a failed door temperature sensor. Not an airflow fault: an open circuit is a wiring/sensor fault.
Safe to check yourself?No - the handbook gives no home fix for E06. A technician runs the service-test program (Step 8 shows "d:OC") and checks the door-NTC harness, sockets, and sensor.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Beko dryer E06 carries a family-count caveat this page states honestly and prominently: E06's meaning is verified in ONE genuine manufacturer document - the BX B7S / BLED Heat Pump Tumble Dryer Service Handbook (Beko 2013; doc 2977720092_BX_BLED-B7S_HP_Service_Handbook, Sept '17) - which covers TWO named platforms (BX B7S and BLED) with separate service-test programs for each. That is the basis for the 2-family count: the code-to-meaning row is stated once in a table that applies to both named platforms. No second standalone dryer handbook was found publicly, and the 2021 B5T4823RW service-test workshop manual is known only through a YouTube walkthrough - a video, not a citable manual document, and NOT counted here. The meaning itself ('Door NTC: Open Circuit') is not in doubt - it is verbatim from the handbook. Scope: E06 here is the BX B7S/BLED heat-pump dryer platform only. The door NTC is the temperature sensor near the door. Do NOT merge E06 with washer E06 (wash-water NTC per one source, motor per another - contested) or Electrolux dryer E06 (motor thermal protection).

What the machine does when E06 fires

The display shows E06 and the dryer stops - the door temperature sensor circuit is open. In the service test, Step 8 shows "d:OC" for this fault, and if an NTC or voltage error occurs the test is not allowed to pass to the next step.

Common causes

  1. Door NTC open circuit - a broken wire/connection or a failed door temperature sensor.
  2. Not an airflow fault: appliancemode lists "restricted airflow" as an E06 cause with no handbook support - an open circuit is a wiring/sensor fault.
  3. Wrong-appliance reading: washer E06 is an NTC fault per one source and a motor error per another (contested); Electrolux dryer E06 is motor thermal protection - neither is this dryer meaning.
  4. Do not confuse the door NTC with the compressor NTC (E09/E10/E11) - a different sensor with the same three fault types.

Safe checks, in order

  1. E06 is a sensor-circuit fault (open circuit = broken wire / connection or failed sensor), not a user-serviceable cleaning job. No home fix is documented.
  2. A technician runs the service-test program: Step 8 shows "d:OC" for this fault, then checks the door-NTC harness, sockets, and sensor.
  3. Do NOT use appliancemode's "door NTC approx. 12 k-ohm at 25 C" figure: unsourced, absent from the handbook (already flagged on the published E08 page).

The handbook gives NO E06-specific home procedure and NO resistance values. E06 is a one-line definition plus one service-test step (Step 8, "d:OC"). Anything beyond that is a technician job.

When to call a technician

  • E06 appears at all - the door NTC circuit needs a technician with the service-test program.
  • The service test shows "d:OC" and will not pass Step 8.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E06 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E06 is a sensor-circuit fault in a mains-powered heat-pump dryer: never open the machine or probe the NTC wiring yourself. The service-test program is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E06 mean on a Beko tumble dryer?

Door NTC: Open Circuit. The door temperature sensor circuit is open - a broken wire/connection or a failed sensor.

What is the door NTC?

The temperature sensor near the door that monitors drying temperature on BX B7S/BLED heat-pump models.

Can I fix E06 myself?

The handbook gives no home fix. A technician runs the service-test program (Step 8 shows "d:OC") and checks the sensor harness and sockets.

What resistance should the door NTC read?

The handbook does not publish a value. Do not trust the "12 k-ohm" figure on other sites - it is unsourced, and the washer NTC's 4,700 ohms is a different sensor.

What is the difference between E06, E07 and E08?

All three are the same door sensor in different fault modes: E06 open circuit, E07 short circuit, E08 overheat.
