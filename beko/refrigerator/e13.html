Beko Refrigerator Error Code E13

Quick answer

Error codeE13 — Beko Refrigerator
What it meansE13 = cyclic freezer fan error - the freezer fan speed is wrong or the fan has failed. On Cosmos SBS models (G91631NE-G91634NE) and FOUREVER 3-door models (G91629NWU). CAVEAT, stated honestly: both manuals are U4-platform documents with the same section structure and the same KN10 socket - two product families, one control platform. Same caveat class as the published Hisense refrigerator F2 page.
Most common causeThe freezer fan speed is wrong or the fan has failed. The handbooks name no sub-cause beyond the fan itself. "Ice buildup" as a cause is invented - neither handbook mentions it.
Safe to check yourself?No - the handbooks give no user reset for E13. The flowchart checks (KN10 pins 1-3, then the rear-compartment-wall obstruction check) are a service procedure - if you are not comfortable opening the control area or the freezer rear wall, call a service centre.
Checked againstArcelik BEKO COSMOS Service Handbook, section 13.8 (p.109) + Arcelik BEKO G91629NWU Service Manual, section 9.8 (p.42)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Beko refrigerator E13 carries a platform caveat this page states honestly and prominently: both documents are U4-platform documents with the same section 13 / section 9 structure and the same KN10 socket - two product families (SBS Cosmos vs FOUREVER 3-door wardrobe), one control platform. Same caveat class as the published Hisense refrigerator F2 page. E13 in this meaning exists only on the Cosmos/American U4 platform. There is no E13 on the bottom-mount warning table (the table stops at E11). Beko WM E13 is a BLAC motor communication fault (still on HOLD - no second manual defines it) and Gorenje F1 is a freezer fan fault under a different code letter - different codes, different systems. MANUAL ERROR, documented honestly: the G91629NWU section 9.8 HEADING is mislabelled in the manual ("E13 Speed Condenser Fan Error") while its body is the freezer-fan flowchart. The Cosmos handbook (both REV 01 section 13.8 and the REV 03 flowchart) names E13 as the freezer fan and E15 as the condenser fan, and the G91629NWU section 9.9 (E15) body is the condenser-fan flowchart - so the section 9.8 heading is a copy-paste error in that one document, and the meaning is settled by the Cosmos handbook plus the G91629NWU body text. Recorded here; not hidden.

What the machine does when E13 fires

The handbooks give no user-facing behaviour for E13 beyond the fault itself. The ukwhitegoods transcription adds: "Freezer fan will not run" - a transcription, not a manual quote.

Common causes

  1. Freezer fan speed error or failure - the handbooks' own fault definition.
  2. Disconnected cables at the control card DC Input-Output socket (KN10) pins 1, 2 and 3 - the flowchart's first check.
  3. Something preventing the rotation of the fan - the G91629NWU body text's own check (remove the rear compartment wall of the freezer and look).
  4. Wrong-code reading - E13 is NOT a defrost fault (E1 is the evaporator sensor, E4 is the defrost heater); Beko WM E13 (BLAC motor communication / water leak) and Gorenje F1 are different codes on different systems.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the cables connected to the control card DC Input-Output socket (KN10) pins 1, 2 and 3. If cables are disconnected, re-connect them, turn the refrigerator on and check whether the error is still displayed (Cosmos handbook flowchart, p.247).
  2. Remove the rear compartment wall of the freezer and check whether there is anything preventing the rotation of the fan. If there is any problem, fix it (G91629NWU manual, section 9.8 body).
  3. Do NOT use appliancemode's 12 VDC supply / 2.4 VDC feedback figures as manual values - neither handbook's visible text layer gives them. Do NOT publish "ice buildup" as a cause. Do NOT publish the JustAnswer AI summary's defrost-sensor/heater steps as E13 diagnosis.

The steps above are the handbooks' steps, printed exactly. The ukwhitegoods ASD V0 transcription adds: "Freezer fan speed error or failure. Freezer fan will not run. Check wiring. Replace fan. Check control module." - a transcription, not a manual, cited as such. All cable and rear-wall work is a service procedure.

When to call a technician

  • E13 is displayed at all - the flowchart checks are a service procedure; there is no homeowner fix.
  • E13 returns after the KN10 pins 1-3 cable check and the fan-rotation check - the fan or the control module needs a technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E13 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E13 is a fan fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the control area or the freezer rear wall or probe the fan wiring yourself. The KN10 socket and fan checks are service work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E13 mean on a Beko refrigerator?

The freezer fan has failed or is running at the wrong speed ("cyclic freezer fan error"). Verified in two Arcelik service manuals: the COSMOS handbook (section 13.8, models G91631NE-G91634NE) and the G91629NWU service manual (section 9.8). It is a fan-speed fault, not a sensor fault.

Is E13 a defrost fault?

No. One JustAnswer AI summary says E13 is a defrost sensor or heater problem - that is wrong, and it contradicts the thread's own expert. E13 is the freezer fan; the defrost codes on this platform are E1 (evaporator sensor) and E4 (defrost heater).

How do I check it?

Per the Cosmos handbook flowchart: check the cables on the control card DC Input-Output socket (KN10) pins 1, 2 and 3; if disconnected, re-connect them, turn the refrigerator on and check whether the error is still displayed. Per the G91629NWU manual: remove the rear compartment wall of the freezer and check whether anything is preventing the rotation of the fan; if there is a problem, fix it. This is a service procedure - if you are not comfortable opening the control area or the freezer rear wall, call a service centre.

Is E13 the same as the washing machine E13?

No. Beko WM E13 is a BLAC motor communication fault (and it is still on HOLD - no second manual defines it). Fridge E13 is the freezer fan. Same code string, different appliance, different fault. This page covers the refrigerator E13 only.

Why does one Beko manual call E13 a condenser fan error?

The G91629NWU service manual's section 9.8 heading says "Speed Condenser Fan Error" - but the body text under that heading is the freezer-fan flowchart (remove the freezer rear wall, check the fan rotation, replace the freezer fan). The Cosmos handbook names E13 as the freezer fan and E15 as the condenser fan. The heading is a copy-paste error in that one document; the meaning is the freezer fan.
