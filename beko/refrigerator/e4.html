Beko Refrigerator Error Code E4

Quick answer

Error codeE4 — Beko Refrigerator
What it meansE4 = freezer defrost heater error - the freezer defrost heater circuit has failed. On Cosmos SBS models (G91631NE-G91634NE) and FOUREVER 3-door models (G91629NWU). CAVEAT, stated honestly: both manuals are U4-platform documents with the same section structure and the same KN6/KN10 sockets - two product families, one control platform. Same caveat class as the published Hisense refrigerator F2 page.
Most common causeThe freezer defrost heater circuit has failed. The handbooks name no sub-cause beyond the heater circuit itself - E4 is the defrost HEATER error, not "defrost system" in general, and not the evaporator sensor (that is E1).
Safe to check yourself?No - the handbooks give no user reset for E4. The wattmeter service-test check is a service procedure - if you are not comfortable opening the control area or running a service test, call a service centre.
Checked againstArcelik BEKO COSMOS Service Handbook, section 13.5 (p.106) + Arcelik BEKO G91629NWU Service Manual, section 9.5 (p.39)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Beko refrigerator E4 carries a platform caveat this page states honestly and prominently: both documents are U4-platform documents with the same section 13 / section 9 structure and the same KN6/KN10 sockets - two product families (SBS Cosmos vs FOUREVER 3-door wardrobe), one control platform. Same caveat class as the published Hisense refrigerator F2 page. E4 in this meaning exists only on the Cosmos/American U4 platform. There is no E04 on Beko refrigerators: the bottom-mount warning table jumps from E03 to E06, and the Cosmos service codes have no E04 either. Beko WM E04/E4 is a heating fault and Gorenje F4 is a freezer fan fault - different codes, different systems.

What the machine does when E4 fires

The handbooks give no user-facing behaviour for E4 beyond the fault itself. Per the ukwhitegoods transcription of the same code: "Defrosting is not carried out at all" - frost builds up on the freezer evaporator and cooling degrades. That consequence line is a transcription, not a manual quote, so this page says so.

Common causes

  1. Freezer defrost heater circuit failed - the handbooks' own fault definition.
  2. Wrong-code reading - "defrost system" in general is broader than the handbook's precise "defrost HEATER error"; the evaporator sensor is E1, not E4; WM E04/E4 (heating) and Gorenje F4 (freezer fan) are different codes on different systems.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Plug the refrigerator into a wattmeter and operate the freezer defrost heater in service test; check whether the value read from the wattmeter conforms to the total power of the defrost heater + drain heater (Cosmos handbook flowchart, p.244).
  2. Service-test entry (Cosmos V3): Display+Vacation 2 seconds in the first 60 seconds after energising. The ASD V0 variant is Select+Set 3 seconds within 30 seconds of energising - do not conflate the two.
  3. Do NOT use appliancemode's resistance figures (defrost heater 350-450 Ohm; thermal fuse 0.1-1 Ohm; drain heater 2830-5970 Ohm; defrost sensor 72.5 kOhm at -15C) as manual values - no resistance values exist in either handbook's visible text. Do NOT publish the American page's "60 min x 3 defrosts" rule as a manual fact.

The steps above are the handbook flowchart's steps, printed exactly as far as the text layer goes. The ukwhitegoods ASD V0 transcription adds: "Defrost heater error. Defrosting is not carried out at all... Check defrost heater. Check control module." - a transcription, not a manual, cited as such. The wattmeter and service-test work is a service procedure.

When to call a technician

  • E4 is displayed at all - the wattmeter service-test check is a service procedure; there is no homeowner fix.
  • Frost builds up on the freezer evaporator and cooling degrades - the transcription's consequence of defrosting not being carried out at all.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside E4 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E4 is a heater-circuit fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the control area, the freezer evaporator cover, or probe the heater wiring yourself. The wattmeter and service-test checks are service work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E4 mean on a Beko refrigerator?

The freezer defrost heater has failed. Verified in two Arcelik service manuals: the COSMOS handbook (section 13.5, models G91631NE-G91634NE) and the G91629NWU service manual (section 9.5). It is the heater itself - not the whole defrost system, and not the evaporator sensor (that is E1).

What happens if the defrost heater fails?

Per the ukwhitegoods transcription of the same code: "Defrosting is not carried out at all" - frost builds up on the freezer evaporator and cooling degrades. That consequence line is a transcription, not a manual quote, so the page says so.

How do I check it?

Per the Cosmos handbook flowchart: plug the refrigerator into a wattmeter and operate the freezer defrost heater in service test; check whether the value read conforms to the total power of the defrost heater + drain heater. This is a service procedure - if you are not comfortable opening the control area or running a service test, call a service centre.

Is E4 the same as E04?

There is no E04 on Beko refrigerators: the bottom-mount warning table jumps from E03 to E06, and the Cosmos service codes have no E04 either. If your display shows "E04", check whether you are reading a washing machine or dishwasher display - WM E04 is a heating fault.

Is E4 a sensor fault?

No. E4 is the defrost heater. The sensor codes on this platform are E0 (freezer air), E1 (freezer evaporator), E2 (fridge evaporator) and E3 (fridge air). See the E1 page for the sensor code.
