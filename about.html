About Us

Appliance Error Codes & Troubleshooting is an independent guide site that explains what household appliance error codes mean, what commonly causes them, and which checks are safe to try at home — in plain language, and tied to the exact model family the code belongs to.

How our guides are made

Error codes are reused by manufacturers with different meanings on different models, so a guide is only published here after a three-step check:

  1. Manual check: the code's meaning is read in the manufacturer manual (or official service documentation) for at least one named model family — and where meanings differ between families, the page says so in a series table instead of picking one.
  2. Ranking check: we look at what currently answers the question online. If thin or copied pages are contradicting each other, that is a sign the manual needs to win — not another copy.
  3. Difference check: the published page must add something the ranking pages do not have — the series split, the named manual, the manual's own reset procedure, or an honest warning where a popular claim is wrong.

Codes still being researched are labelled “Guide in preparation” and publish no meaning at all. We would rather show an empty slot than a guess.

What this site is not

  • We are not affiliated with Hisense, Daewoo, Midea, Haier, Beko, Gree or any other manufacturer, and we are not an authorised service centre.
  • We do not sell parts, book repairs, or provide a repair service.
  • Our guides are general information, not a substitute for your model's official manual or a qualified technician.

How the site is funded

The site is kept free to read. In future it may carry clearly separated advertising from third-party ad networks in reserved spaces on some pages; advertising never changes what a guide says, and advertisers do not provide or approve our content.

Corrections

Manuals get revised and model families multiply. If you find an error, the contact page explains how to reach us — corrections with a manual reference are always acted on.
