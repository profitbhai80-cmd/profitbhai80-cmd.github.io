Gree Error Codes

Gree error codes, checked against Gree manuals. Coverage is starting with Gree mini-split air conditioners and grows one verified code at a time — a code is only published after its meaning is confirmed in the manual for the model family.

Gree Air Conditioner Error Codes

H3

Gree Air Conditioner Error H3

H3 = compressor overload protection on mini-split families (low charge, blocked capillary, compressor motor, loose OVC-COMP terminal; outdoor unit flashes 8×). On GMV VRF platforms H3 = fan drive module reset protection instead.

 C5

Gree Air Conditioner Error C5

C5 = jumper cap malfunction on the indoor control board (missing/wrong/damaged configuration jumper — common after a board swap). Power-cycle; board work is for a technician.

 E1

Gree Air Conditioner Error E1

E1 = compressor high pressure protection. Restart the unit, clear blocked airflow at the outdoor coil, clean filters; refrigerant charge is technician work.

 E2

Gree Air Conditioner Error E2

E2 = indoor anti-freeze protection. Clear the indoor unit's return airflow, clean the filters, and let the coil warm up; E2 is a protection status, not a broken part.

 E4

Gree Air Conditioner Error E4

E4 = compressor high discharge temperature protection (over 118°C). Switch off, let it cool, clean filters and clear outdoor airflow; valves, charge, and fan faults are technician work.

 E5

Gree Air Conditioner Error E5

E5 = AC over-current protection. Restart once (the manual's own step), clean the indoor filters, and check for supply problems; repeated trips need an electrician or technician.

 H1

Gree Air Conditioner Error H1

H1 = defrosting, not a fault. The unit pauses heating, clears frost from the outdoor coil, and resumes on its own. Only a stuck or constantly repeating H1 needs a technician.

 H6

Gree Air Conditioner Error H6

H6 = no feedback from the indoor fan motor. Power off, check nothing blocks the indoor fan, restart once (the manual's own step); motor, connector, or board faults need a technician.

 F1

Gree Air Conditioner Error F1

F1 (wall-split) = indoor ambient temperature sensor open/short circuit: cool indicator flashes once every 3s. Power-cycle; if it returns, the room sensor, its connection, or the mainboard needs a technician. U-Match/FCU owners: your F1 is the evaporator sensor, not this one.

 E3

Gree Air Conditioner Error E3

Gree AC E3 = compressor low-pressure protection — low refrigerant, closed valve, fan or blockage, or normal refrigerant-recovery mode.

 F3

Gree Air Conditioner Error F3

Gree AC F3 = outdoor ambient sensor open/short — the rare F-code where wall-split and U-Match agree.

 F2

Gree Air Conditioner Error F2

Gree AC F2 (wall-split) = indoor evaporator sensor open/short — flashes twice every 3s; U-Match owners: your F2 is the condenser sensor.

 F4

Gree Air Conditioner Error F4

Gree AC F4 (wall-split) = outdoor condenser sensor open/short; U-Match owners: your F4 is the discharge sensor.

 F5

Gree Air Conditioner Error F5

Gree AC F5 (wall-split) = compressor discharge sensor open/short — not E4, and not the wall controller (that is U-Match F5).

 F6

Gree Air Conditioner Error F6

Gree AC F6 (wall-split) = overload frequency limit — unit keeps running at reduced speed, not a sensor fault.

 F8

Gree Air Conditioner Error F8

Gree AC F8 (wall-split) = overcurrent frequency limit — low supply voltage or high pressure, unit keeps running slower.

 F9

Gree Air Conditioner Error F9

Gree AC F9 (wall-split) = high discharge temperature frequency limit — low refrigerant or expansion valve, unit keeps running slower.

 H4

Gree Air Conditioner Error H4

Gree AC H4 = system overload protection — tube temperature too high, compressor stops in cooling, whole unit stops in heating.

 FH

Gree Air Conditioner Error FH

FH = antifreezing frequency limit — keeps running slower.

 EE

Gree Air Conditioner Error EE

EE = the main control board's memory chip has failed — loading EEPROM malfunction / outdoor main-control memory-chi

 E6

Gree Air Conditioner Error E6

Gree AC E6: communication failure indoor↔outdoor — 4 factory manuals, blink counts, the official §9.2(3) fix.

 U1

Gree Air Conditioner Error U1

Gree AC U1: phase-current detection fault — 3 factory manuals, blink 13, U1-vs-U5-vs-E5 split.

 U5

Gree Air Conditioner Error U5

Gree AC U5: complete-unit current detection — justanswer AI correction, U5-vs-U1-vs-E6 split.

 U8

Gree Air Conditioner Error U8

Gree AC U8: zero-cross fault + mandatory evo series-split warning — the 3-vs-1 factory evidence table.

 PH

Gree Air Conditioner Error PH

Gree AC PH: DC bus-bar overvoltage — the 265VAC threshold, PH-vs-PL-vs-P3 split.

 P5

Gree Air Conditioner Error P5

Gree AC P5: overcurrent protection of phase current for compressor (blink 15) - compressor replacement is the last step.

 P6

Gree Air Conditioner Error P6

Gree AC P6: main board to driver board communication failure (blink 16) - can clear itself. Evo-era + U-Match only.

 P7

Gree Air Conditioner Error P7

Gree AC P7: module temperature sensor circuit malfunction (blink 18) - fix: replace outdoor control panel AP1.

 P8

Gree Air Conditioner Error P8

Gree AC P8: module high temperature protection (blink 19) - 20-min de-energize, thermal grease/radiator check, 80/95 C thresholds.

 H2

Gree Air Conditioner Error H2

Gree AC H2: static dedusting protection (blink 2) - a status display, not a fault. Turn off with the HEALTH button. Listed in R410A tables and the Soyal R32 table (§9.1 row 32).

 PA

Gree Air Conditioner Error PA

Gree AC PA: AC current protection, input side (U-Match/rooftop only) - heavy load, grid voltage, PFC module, drive-board sampling circuit; fix is replace the mainboard.

 PC

Gree Air Conditioner Error PC

Gree AC PC: current detection (current sense) circuit error (U-Match/rooftop only) - offset voltage over 12.5% of 1.65V in 1s; fix is replace the drive board.

 PL

Gree Air Conditioner Error PL

Gree AC PL: DC bus-bar voltage too low (blink 21) - L-N below 150VAC, capacitor-C check, replace AP1 last. Not the 185VAC figure other sites quote.

 PU

Gree Air Conditioner Error PU

Gree AC PU: charging malfunction of capacitor (blink 17) - 85% capacitor-voltage test, reactor check, 20-minute safety step. Not 'replace the capacitor'.

 U2

Gree Air Conditioner Error U2

Gree U2 means compressor protection against loss of phase on evo-era units, with no reset in the manuals.

 U3

Gree Air Conditioner Error U3

Gree U3 means the DC bus voltage dropped because the supply voltage is unstable, and it clears itself when voltage normalizes.

 U7

Gree Air Conditioner Error U7

Gree U7 means the four-way valve is abnormal in heating mode, with three manual causes and an auto-resume rule.

 U9

Gree Air Conditioner Error U9

Gree U9 means the outdoor unit's zero-crossing signal failed, and the manual fix is replacing the outdoor control panel AP1.

 H0

Gree Air Conditioner Error H0

Gree AC H0 means heating anti-high temperature drop frequency: the unit keeps running at reduced compressor frequency instead of stopping.

 H7

Gree Air Conditioner Error H7

Gree AC H7 means compressor desynchronizing (loss of step): the inverter drive has lost synchronism with the compressor motor.

 E0

Gree Air Conditioner Error E0

Gree AC E0 means pump failure on U-Match duct/cassette units only: the condensate water pump is at fault after 2 hours of unrecovered full-water protection.

 LD

Gree Air Conditioner Error LD

Gree LD means compressor loss of phase protection, verified in three Gree manuals.

 LE

Gree Air Conditioner Error LE

Gree LE means compressor locked rotor protection, verified in two Gree manuals.

 LA

Gree Air Conditioner Error LA

Gree LA means outdoor fan 2 error protection, on dual-outdoor-fan units only.

 Lc

Gree Air Conditioner Error Lc

Gree AC Lc: compressor startup failure (flash 11) — UVW wire, compressor, or drive board.

 L9

Gree Air Conditioner Error L9

Gree AC L9: power protection — fan stops, then fan and compressor restart after 3 minutes.

 LP

Gree Air Conditioner Error LP

Gree AC LP: indoor and outdoor units don't match — a matched pair is the only fix.

 HC

Gree Air Conditioner Error HC

Gree AC HC: PFC protection (flash 6) — grid, wiring/reactor, dirt, or main board; AP1 or reactor.

 HE

Gree Air Conditioner Error HE

Gree AC HE: compressor demagnetization protection (flash 14) — main board first, compressor second.

 EU

Gree Air Conditioner Error EU

Gree AC EU: IPM temperature frequency limit — unit keeps running slower; 20-minute de-energize.

 PF

Gree Air Conditioner Error PF

Gree AC PF (U-Match only): electric box sensor error, AD out of range for 5 seconds.

 PP

Gree Air Conditioner Error PP

Gree AC PP (U-Match only): input voltage error — flowchart checks 185-264VAC.

 Pd

Gree Air Conditioner Error Pd

Gree AC Pd (U-Match only): sensor connection protection — discharge sensor AD out of range 5s.

 JF

Gree Air Conditioner Error JF

Gree AC JF: Wi-Fi detection plate malfunction — app control lost, cooling still works.

 F0

Gree Air Conditioner Error F0

Gree AC F0: refrigerant leakage protection (wall-split) / indoor ambient sensor (U-Match).
