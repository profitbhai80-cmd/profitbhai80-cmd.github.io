Gree Air Conditioner Error Code U1

Quick answer

Error codeU1 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansU1 = compressor phase-current detection circuit malfunction. Identical across 2010 (Change 9/12, Change 18/24) → evo era → current Livo Gen4. Every service manual routes U1 to outdoor-board replacement — a technician-only fix. Keep U1 distinct: U1 = compressor phase-current detection, U5 = complete-unit current detection, E5 = overcurrent protection, E6 = communication fault. Do not import Mitsubishi U1 ("phase sequence issue" — a different brand's code that a multi-brand chart can mislead readers with) or any VRF meaning. Wall-split scope.
Most common causeThe compressor's phase-current detection circuit is faulty — every service manual's repair route is "Replace outdoor control panel AP1" (Change 9/12: "Replace outdoor main board").

What the machine does when U1 fires

The display shows U1 (indoor LED blinks 13 times on most generations). During cooling and drying: the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps operating. During heating: the complete unit stops.

Common causes

  1. The compressor phase-current detection circuit is faulty — every service manual's repair route is board replacement (technician work).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Restart the unit once (power cycle) — the generic R32-era owner-manual guidance: "Can be eliminated after restarting the unit. If not, please contact qualified professionals for service."
  2. If U1 returns, call a qualified technician — the manual's own fix is "Replace outdoor control panel AP1" (Change 18/24, Livo Gen4) / "Replace outdoor main board" (Change 9/12).

The manuals give NO homeowner procedure for U1 — the repair route in every service manual is board replacement (technician work). This page invents no DIY steps.

When to call a technician

  • U1 returns after a restart — the outdoor control panel/board needs replacement (the manuals' own fix).

Safety first

U1 is an outdoor-unit board fault: the outdoor unit is mains-powered and refrigerant-connected. Never open it, never probe terminals, and never replace a control board yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U1 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

U1 means a compressor phase-current detection circuit malfunction. It is verified verbatim in three factory manuals — the GREE evo owner's manual, the Change 18/24 service manual (§9.3 row 30), and the official Livo Gen4 service manual (§9.1 row 42) — plus the corroborating Change 9/12 manual.

What is the difference between U1, U5 and E5?

U1 = compressor phase-current detection circuit (compressor-specific). U5 = complete-unit current detection circuit (whole unit). E5 = overcurrent protection (a protection trip, not a detection-circuit fault). And E6 = communication fault. Four different codes — the manuals keep them separate.

Is the compressor itself dead?

Not necessarily: U1 flags the DETECTION circuit, and every manual's repair route is replacing the outdoor control panel — technician diagnosis decides whether the compressor is also implicated.

Can I fix U1 myself?

No. The manuals' only fix is replacing the outdoor control panel (AP1) / main board — technician-only, with no DIY steps in any manual.
