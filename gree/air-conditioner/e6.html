Gree Air Conditioner Error Code E6

Quick answer

Error codeE6 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansE6 = communication failure/malfunction between the indoor unit and the outdoor unit. The wording is identical across generations: 2010 (Change 9/12, Change 18/24) → 2014 (NEO) → evo era → current Livo Gen4. When it fires: during cooling the compressor stops while the indoor fan motor operates; during heating the complete unit stops. The 2010-era Change 9/12 repair note adds the single actionable check: is the outdoor connecting wire reliably connected? Wall-split scope — do not import Mitsubishi U1/U5-style wrong-brand traps or VRF meanings.
Most common causeThe indoor and outdoor units cannot communicate — the manuals' processing method is "Check if communication signal cable is connected reliably"; the 2010-era note asks "Is outdoor connecting wire reliably connected?"
Safe to check yourself?Homeowner-safe: restart the unit (power cycle). If E6 returns, do NOT open the outdoor unit yourself — communication-wire and control-board checks are technician work.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because E6 is Gree's communication fault — and the meaning is verified verbatim across FOUR factory generations: 2010 Change 9/12, 2010 Change 18/24, 2014 NEO, the evo R410A era, and the current official Livo Gen4 manual. Whatever Gree wall-split you own, E6 means the same thing: communication failure between the indoor unit and the outdoor unit. Scope note: Gree's U-Match duct/cassette platform also codes E6 as a communication error (greeac.co.nz U-Match R32 service manual, §3.3: "5 E6 Communication error") — same meaning, same platform family. Do NOT import Mitsubishi U1/U5-style wrong-brand traps or VRF meanings (E6 is not a VRF-specific code).

What the machine does when E6 fires

The display shows E6 (indoor LED blinks 6 times on most generations). During cooling: the compressor stops while the indoor fan motor keeps operating. During heating: the complete unit stops.

Common causes

  1. The communication signal cable is not reliably connected — the Livo Gen4 §9.2(3) processing method (technician step).
  2. The outdoor connecting wire is not reliably connected — the Change 9/12 repair note (technician step).
  3. Mis-wiring or a communication failure in the indoor↔outdoor link — the NEO manual's own wording.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Restart the unit (power cycle) — the owner-manual-era guidance: "Can be eliminated after restarting the unit. If not, please contact qualified professionals for service."
  2. If E6 returns, call a qualified technician — the manuals' own processing method is "Check if communication signal cable is connected reliably", which is a technician step. Do NOT open the outdoor unit yourself.

The manuals' actual fix (checking the signal cable / outdoor connecting wire) is electrical work — presented here as the technician's step, not homeowner DIY. The homeowner scope is the restart; everything deeper is service work.

When to call a technician

  • E6 returns after a restart — the communication cable and control boards need a technician (the manuals' own processing method).

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E6 involves the indoor↔outdoor communication link and mains-powered control boards: never open the indoor or outdoor unit, never probe terminals, and never re-wire anything yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E6 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

E6 means a communication failure/malfunction between the indoor unit and the outdoor unit. It is verified verbatim in four factory manuals — the GREE evo owner's manual, the Change 18/24 service manual (§9.3 row 5), the official Livo Gen4 service manual (§9.1 row 5, greecomfort.com), and the NEO installation manual — plus the corroborating Change 9/12 manual.

Is a restart enough for E6?

Sometimes: the owner-manual-era guidance says a restart can eliminate it. If E6 returns, the manuals' processing method is to check that the communication signal cable is reliably connected — a technician step.

What does the unit do when E6 fires?

During cooling: the compressor stops while the indoor fan motor keeps operating. During heating: the complete unit stops. (Change 18/24 and Livo Gen4 A/C status, verbatim.)

Is E6 a board fault or a wire fault?

The manuals' method checks the wire first ("Check if communication signal cable is connected reliably" / "Is outdoor connecting wire reliably connected?"). Board-level diagnosis follows only after the wiring is confirmed — technician work.

Does my 2012 Gree have the same E6?

Yes. The meaning is identical from the 2010 Change 9/12 generation through evo to current Livo Gen4 — the blink count (6) matches too.
