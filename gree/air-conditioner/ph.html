Gree Air Conditioner Error Code PH

Quick answer

Error codePH — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansPH = overvoltage protection for the DC bus-bar (PN) — the DC input / DC bus-bar voltage is too high. Identical across 2010 (Change 9/12, Change 18/24) → evo era → current Livo Gen4 (the Change 18/24 and Livo Gen4 rows are word-for-word identical). The factory threshold is 265VAC on the XT L–N wiring-board terminals — not arlington's uncited "264VAC". Keep PH distinct: PH = DC-bus OVERvoltage; PL = DC-bus UNDERvoltage ("DC input voltage is too low"); P3 = overcurrent. Wall-split scope (VRF defines PH as "High-voltage protection of compressor's drive DC bus bar" — same concept, different platform).
Most common causeThe supply voltage is too high (above 265VAC at the XT L–N terminals), or — if the AC input is normal — the circuit on control panel AP1 is faulty (the manuals' own two-branch diagnosis).
Safe to check yourself?Homeowner-safe: restart the unit; check whether the building has known high-voltage/supply issues or a stabilizer. If PH returns, call a qualified technician — the manuals' diagnostic sequence (measuring live XT terminals and the electrolytic capacitor C on AP1) is electrical work.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because PH is Gree's DC-bus overvoltage protection — and the meaning, the A/C status and the diagnostic sequence are verified verbatim across three factory generations: 2010 Change 18/24, the evo R410A era, and the current official Livo Gen4 manual (the Change 18/24 and Livo Gen4 rows are word-for-word identical). Do not confuse PH (DC-bus OVERvoltage) with PL (DC-bus UNDERvoltage — evo: "DC input voltage is too low") or P3 (overcurrent). VRF note: GMV VRF manuals define PH as "High-voltage protection of compressor's drive DC bus bar" — the same overvoltage concept on a different platform; this page is wall-split scope.

What the machine does when PH fires

The display shows PH (indoor LED blinks 11 times on most generations). During cooling and drying: the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps operating. During heating: the complete unit stops.

Common causes

  1. The supply voltage is higher than 265VAC at the XT L–N wiring-board terminals — the manuals' branch 1.
  2. If the AC input is normal, a malfunction in the circuit on control panel AP1 (electrolytic capacitor C / board) — the manuals' branch 2.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Restart the unit — the homeowner-safe first move.
  2. Check whether the building has known high-voltage or supply issues, or a stabilizer — the homeowner-safe context for the manuals' branch 1.
  3. If PH returns, call a qualified technician — the manuals' own diagnostic sequence (measure XT L–N voltage; if AC input is normal, measure electrolytic capacitor C on AP1; if normal, replace AP1) is electrical work on live terminals.

The manuals' diagnostic sequence involves measuring LIVE XT terminals and the electrolytic capacitor on AP1 — electrical work, presented here as the technician's steps, not homeowner DIY.

When to call a technician

  • PH returns after a restart — the supply voltage (branch 1) or the AP1 board circuit (branch 2) needs a technician per the manuals' own sequence.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

PH is a high-voltage fault: never measure mains voltage yourself, never open the indoor or outdoor unit, and never touch the control panel's capacitor — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does PH mean on a Gree air conditioner?

PH means overvoltage protection for the DC bus-bar (PN) — the DC input / DC bus-bar voltage is too high. It is verified verbatim in three factory manuals — the GREE evo owner's manual, the Change 18/24 service manual (§9.3 row 18), and the official Livo Gen4 service manual (§9.1 row 21) — plus the corroborating Change 9/12 manual.

What is the PH voltage threshold?

265VAC: the manuals say "if the voltage is higher than 265VAC" at position L and N on the XT wiring board. (arlington's "264VAC" is uncited — the factory manuals say 265VAC.)

What is the difference between PH, PL and P3?

PH = DC-bus OVERvoltage protection. PL = DC-bus UNDERvoltage (evo: "DC input voltage is too low"). P3 = overcurrent. Three different codes — this page is PH.

Is PH a supply problem or a board problem?

The manuals give a two-branch diagnosis: branch 1 — supply voltage above 265VAC; branch 2 — if the AC input is normal, the circuit on control panel AP1 is faulty. A technician works the branches in the manuals' order.

Can a stabilizer help with PH?

If the building supply runs high, the manuals' branch 1 is exactly that case ("turn on the unit after the supply voltage is increased to the normal range" is the manual's wording for waiting until voltage is normal). Stabilizer advice is electrician territory — this page does not prescribe one.
