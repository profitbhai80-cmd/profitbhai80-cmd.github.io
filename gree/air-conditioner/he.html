Gree Air Conditioner Error Code HE

Quick answer

Error codeHE — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansHE means compressor demagnetization protection: the unit trips to protect the compressor's permanent magnet from losing its magnetism. The indoor unit flashes 14 times. In cooling the compressor and outdoor fan stop; in heating they stop first and the indoor fan stops about 1 minute later.
Most common causeOutdoor main board damaged, or compressor damaged — in that order (the Mini manual lists the main board first).
Safe to check yourself?Only the checks on this page: switch the unit off and re-energize once. The main board and compressor are both technician parts.
Checked againstGREE Mini After-sale Service Manual (split type + floor standing; flash 14, 2 causes); GREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual, Malfunction Tab; Gree GWH09UB-K6DNA4A / GWH12UB-K3DNA4F Service Manuals §9.1; Gree XK19 wired-controller generic error table (corroboration only)

Applies to — read this first

HE is a real code on the families that name it — but it is not universal. It is verified on the evo-era, UB-series, Mini-after-sale and XK19-generic tables, and it is NOT in the Soyal §9.1 list (rows 1-53 read in full), not in the Livo Gen4/Livo+ §9.1 tables as recorded, and not in the U-Match R32/R410A tables. Do not present HE as a code every Gree wall-split can show.

The ranking pages get HE's meaning and then invent its fix. Aysonline gives one row — 'Compressor demagnetization fault — Replace compressor' — but no manual says that: the Mini manual's two causes are outdoor main board damaged FIRST and compressor damaged second, so 'replace compressor' presents the expensive, second-listed cause as the only fix. Luce gives one correct row with no source, no troubleshooting and no blink count. Airconditioningmanuals has an H-section page with no HE row at all. The same aysonline page carries errors elsewhere — its E8, H4, PU, PC and P0 rows are all wrong. The VRF HE meaning is unverified in the manuals checked: this page states that plainly instead of borrowing a VRF row.

What the machine does when HE fires

In cooling the compressor and outdoor fan stop. In heating the compressor and outdoor fan stop first, and the indoor fan stops about 1 minute later. The indoor unit flashes 14 times (Mini after-sale manual).

Common causes

  1. Outdoor main board damaged (Mini manual, cause 1 — listed first).
  2. Compressor damaged (Mini manual, cause 2).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off and re-energize once. If HE clears, the trip was transient.
  2. If HE returns, stop: the outdoor main board and the compressor are both technician parts, and the manual lists the main board first — a technician checks the main board before condemning the compressor.
  3. Do not order a compressor on the strength of one listicle row. 'Replace compressor' is not the manual's fix.

No manual gives an HE-specific owner reset, a resistance value, or a board-replacement procedure. No power-cycle duration exists in the manuals for HE — do not invent one.

When to call a technician

  • HE returns after one off-and-re-energize.
  • The compressor does not start, or the unit stops in heating every time.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or unusual noise from the outdoor unit — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

HE involves the outdoor main board and the compressor — mains-powered, high-voltage parts inside the outdoor unit. Never open the outdoor unit's covers or touch compressor wiring while investigating it.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does HE mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Compressor demagnetization protection: the unit trips to protect the compressor's permanent magnet from losing its magnetism. Verified in six Gree manuals. The indoor unit flashes 14 times.

What causes HE?

The Mini manual names two causes: the outdoor main board is damaged, or the compressor is damaged — in that order. Both are technician parts.

Do I need a new compressor if my Gree shows HE?

Not necessarily. One popular list says 'replace compressor', but the manual lists the outdoor main board first. A technician checks the main board before condemning the compressor.

Will HE clear itself?

No manual gives an auto-clear rule for HE. Switch the unit off and re-energize once; if HE returns, call a technician.

Is HE the same as H7?

No. H7 is compressor desynchronizing — the compressor starts but loses step while running. HE is demagnetization protection. Different faults, different diagnosis.
