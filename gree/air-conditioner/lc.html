Gree Air Conditioner Error Code Lc

Quick answer

Error codeLc — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansLc means compressor failure start-up: the compressor failed to start. The indoor unit flashes 11 times. In cooling and drying the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running; in heating the complete unit stops.
Most common causePoor contact of the compressor UVW wire, a broken compressor, or a broken compressor drive board — the U-Match manual's three causes.
Safe to check yourself?Only the checks on this page: switch the unit off and re-energize once. UVW wiring, compressor and drive-board checks are technician work.

Applies to — read this first

Lc is one of the best-documented Gree codes: eight manufacturer manuals agree on the same meaning, so there is no series split on wall-split or U-Match units. The trap is the letters, not the meaning: onlyerrorcodes.com describes Lc's fault under the H0 name (and vice versa), and on GMV VRF units LC means something entirely different — the indoor unit not matching the outdoor unit. Read the platform first, then the code.

The ranking pages get Lc directionally right and stop there: aysonline gives one row ('Device not starting up — power supply issue, faulty board') with no blink count, no UVW/compressor/drive-board causes and no VRF split; luce gives one row ('Startup failure') with no source and no troubleshooting; sidzcoolcare gives one bullet with zero detail. The same pages carry errors elsewhere — aysonline's E8, H4, PU, PC and P0 rows are all wrong, and luce lists C5 twice and calls P0 a drive-module problem (P0 is a test state). The U-Match manual also notes: check the error code on the nixie tube of the outdoor main control board — if PJ is displayed, it indicates inverter compressor startup failure. PJ is the outdoor-board form of the same startup failure, not a separate indoor code.

What the machine does when Lc fires

In cooling and drying operation the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running. In heating operation the complete unit stops. The indoor unit flashes 11 times (Mini after-sale manual).

Common causes

  1. Poor contact of the compressor UVW wire (U-Match R32 §3.4.23).
  2. Compressor is broken (U-Match R32 §3.4.23).
  3. Compressor drive board is broken (U-Match R32 §3.4.23).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code and the platform: Lc on a wall-split or U-Match unit is this page. On a GMV VRF unit LC means the indoor unit does not match the outdoor unit — a different fault entirely.
  2. Switch the unit off and re-energize once. If Lc clears, the startup succeeded on the second attempt.
  3. If Lc returns, stop: the UVW wiring, compressor and drive board are all technician checks inside the outdoor unit. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself.
  4. If the outdoor main control board's nixie tube shows PJ instead of Lc, it is the same startup failure in its outdoor-board form — tell the technician, it is not a separate indoor code.

No manual gives an owner-level compressor test, a multimeter step, or a DIY compressor/drive-board replacement for Lc. The manuals' only owner action is one off-and-re-energize; everything after that is a technician's job.

When to call a technician

  • Lc returns after one off-and-re-energize.
  • The compressor never starts, or the unit stops in heating every time it is switched on.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or unusual noise from the outdoor unit — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

Lc involves the compressor and its drive board — mains-powered, high-voltage parts inside the outdoor unit. Never open the outdoor unit's covers or touch compressor wiring while investigating it, and never attempt a compressor or drive-board replacement yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does Lc mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Compressor failure start-up: the compressor failed to start. Verified in eight Gree manuals (flash 11). In cooling the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running; in heating the complete unit stops.

What causes Lc?

The U-Match manual names three causes: poor contact of the compressor UVW wire, a broken compressor, or a broken compressor drive board. All three are technician checks — the compressor and drive board are not owner-serviceable parts.

Will Lc clear itself?

No manual gives an auto-clear rule for Lc. Switch the unit off and re-energize once; if Lc comes back, the UVW wiring, compressor, or drive board needs a technician.

Is Lc the same as H7?

No. Lc is the compressor failing to start at all; H7 is desynchronizing — the compressor starts but loses step while running. Different faults, different diagnosis.

Is Lc the same as VRF LC?

No. On GMV VRF units LC means the indoor unit does not match the outdoor unit. On wall-split and U-Match units Lc means compressor startup failure. Check the platform, not just the letters.
