Gree Air Conditioner Error Code P6

Quick answer

Error codeP6 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansP6 = communication failure/malfunction between the main board (main control) and the driver/drive board in the outdoor unit. Blink pattern: OFF 3S and blink 16 times (evo). The fault can clear itself (U-Match: "can be eliminated automatically"). SCOPE WARNING: P6 is listed in only ONE wall-split family (evo) - absent from Change 18/24, Livo Gen4, Livo+, GWH18QD, Amber 18-24, GWH09YC, and the 2010 Change 9/12 table.
Most common causeThe outdoor main control board does not receive data from the drive board - a harness/wiring fault between the main board and the drive board, or one of the boards is faulty.
Safe to check yourself?Wait first - the fault can clear itself. Then restart once. If P6 stays, the wiring between the main board and drive board, or one of the boards, needs checking by a technician.
Checked againstGREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual (Malfunction Tab) + Gree U-Match Series DC Inverter Service Manual (section 1.1 Table 1 row 35 + section 1.2)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because P6 has a mandatory scope split: it is verified in only ONE wall-split family (evo) plus the U-Match/multi commercial series. It is ABSENT from Change 18/24 section 9.3 (40-row table), Livo Gen4 section 9.1, Livo+ section 9.1, GWH18QD section 9.1, Amber 18-24, GWH09YC, and the 2010 Change 9/12 table. The page must say P6 is evo-era wall-split + U-Match/multi, and to check your own manual. There is NO meaning split between the two verified families - but VRF P6 ("Drive IPM module protection of compressor") is a DIFFERENT fault, and Versati Monobloc heat-pump P6 ("Drive module resetting") is a different appliance - do not merge either.

What the machine does when P6 fires

The display shows P6 and the outdoor main control board reports it has not received data from the drive board. Blink pattern: OFF 3 seconds, then blink 16 times (evo). The fault can clear itself once the main board receives data from the drive board again.

Common causes

  1. The outdoor main control board does not receive data from the drive board - the manuals' own fault definition.
  2. Harness/wiring fault between the main board and the drive board in the outdoor unit.
  3. One of the boards is faulty - main control board or drive board (technician diagnosis).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Wait first - the U-Match manual says this malfunction "can be eliminated automatically": the outdoor main control board clears it once it receives data from the drive board again.
  2. If P6 stays, restart the air conditioner once (generic step, presented as such - not a manual quote).
  3. If it still stays, the harness/wiring between the main board and the drive board, or one of the boards, is faulty - technician work. Do not invent multimeter or board-swap steps; no wall-split manual gives a P6 diagnosis procedure.

The steps above are the manuals' own guidance plus one generic restart. This page does not invent multimeter or board-swap steps - no wall-split manual gives a P6 diagnosis procedure.

When to call a technician

  • P6 stays after waiting and one restart - the wiring between the main board and drive board, or one of the boards, needs checking by a technician.
  • Your unit is not an evo-era wall-split or U-Match/multi - P6 may mean something else on your model; check your own manual.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside P6 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

P6 involves mains-powered electronics in the outdoor unit: never open the outdoor unit or touch board wiring yourself. The main-board-to-drive-board wiring and both boards are technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does P6 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Communication failure between the main board and the driver board in the outdoor unit. Verified in the evo owner's manual (Malfunction Tab) and the U-Match service manual (section 1.1 Table 1 row 35).

Will P6 clear by itself?

Often, yes. The U-Match manual says this malfunction "can be eliminated automatically" - the outdoor main control board clears it once it receives data from the drive board again. If it stays after a restart, it is a harness or board fault and needs a technician.

How do I reset a Gree P6 error?

Wait first (it may self-clear), then restart the air conditioner once. If P6 comes back, do not keep restarting - the wiring between the main board and drive board, or one of the boards, needs checking by a technician.

What is the blink pattern for P6?

OFF 3 seconds, then blink 16 times (evo Malfunction Tab).

Does every Gree AC use P6?

No - and this matters. P6 is listed in the evo-era wall-split manual and the U-Match manual, but NOT in Change 18/24, Livo Gen4, Livo+, GWH18QD, or Amber. Check your own model's manual. And do not confuse it with VRF P6 ("Drive IPM module protection of compressor") - a different fault.
