Gree Air Conditioner Error Code PP

Quick answer

Error codePP — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansOn U-Match units, PP means input voltage error (AC input voltage abnormality): the AC supply voltage to the outdoor unit is outside its normal range. The R410A manual's flowchart checks whether the input voltage is within 185-264VAC. PP is a U-Match-only code.
Most common causeSupply voltage outside the 185-264VAC range the manual checks, or the outdoor main board's voltage detection (U-Match R410A §2.2 flowchart).
Safe to check yourself?Do not measure mains voltage yourself. Switch the unit off and re-energize once; if PP returns, have a technician check the supply voltage and the outdoor main board.
Checked againstGree U-Match R32 Service Manual §3.3 row 39 (GUD125W/NhB-S, GUD140W/NhB-S); Gree U-Match R410A Service Manual §1.2 + §2.2 flowchart; Gree XK19 wired-controller generic error table (corroboration only)

Applies to — read this first

PP is a U-Match-only code — frame it that way, like PA/PC. It is NOT in the Soyal §9.1 list (rows 1-53 read in full), not in the Livo Gen4/Livo+ §9.1 tables as recorded, not in the evo/Change tables, and not in the Mini split-type table. Do not present PP as a universal wall-split code. The R32 manual gives the name only — no §3.4 troubleshooting section exists for PP in that manual — and the R410A manual gives the name plus the flowchart diagnostic. Together they are the two-model-family verification the gate requires.

No competitor page defines the U-Match PP at all. Sidzcoolcare gives one bullet — 'PP – Irregular AC Voltage Problem' — directionally right with zero detail (no 185-264VAC range, no manual citation, no VRF split, no fix); its H7, H6, E8 and F0 rows on the same page are all wrong. The GMV VRF manuals rank above it: VRF PP is 'AC current protection of inverter compressor' — a current fault, not a voltage fault. Luce and aysonline have no PP row at all. The R410A §2.2 flowchart's diagnostic question is: 'Is input voltage ranged from 185VAC to 264VAC?' — if yes, replace the mainboard; if no, cut off the power and notify the power company.

What the machine does when PP fires

The R32 manual lists PP by name only, with no §3.4 troubleshooting section and no behaviour row. The R410A manual's drive-malfunction flowchart is the diagnostic: it asks whether the input voltage is within 185-264VAC. No blink count exists for PP: U-Match codes display on the wired controller / outdoor main board / indoor receive light board, not as indoor lamp flashes. This page assigns none.

Common causes

  1. AC supply voltage to the outdoor unit outside the 185-264VAC range the R410A flowchart checks.
  2. Outdoor main board voltage detection at fault — the flowchart's 'yes' branch is to replace the mainboard.
  3. Unstable supply voltage from the grid or the building supply.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off and re-energize once. If PP clears, the supply dip was transient.
  2. Do not measure mains voltage yourself. If PP returns, call a technician to check whether the supply voltage is within 185-264VAC (the flowchart's range).
  3. If the supply voltage is in range and PP persists, the flowchart's next step is to replace the outdoor main board — technician work.

No manual gives a PP-specific owner reset, an owner-level voltage measurement, a supply-correction step, or a board-replacement step. Do not invent one, and do not use the VRF PP meaning (current protection) to diagnose a U-Match PP.

When to call a technician

  • PP returns after one off-and-re-energize.
  • Other units in the building show supply problems at the same time — the supply itself may be out of range.
  • Any burning smell, damaged wiring, or scorch marks near the outdoor unit — switch off and call a qualified technician immediately.

Safety first

PP is a mains-supply fault: never measure mains voltage yourself and never open the outdoor unit's covers or touch board terminals while investigating it. Mains-voltage work belongs to a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does PP mean on a Gree air conditioner?

On U-Match units, PP is the input voltage error: the AC supply voltage to the outdoor unit is outside its normal range. Verified in the U-Match R32 and R410A manuals.

What voltage should the unit see?

The U-Match R410A manual's flowchart checks whether the input voltage is within 185-264VAC. Do not measure mains voltage yourself — have a technician check it.

Will PP clear itself?

No manual gives an auto-clear rule for PP. Switch the unit off and re-energize once; if PP returns, the supply voltage or the outdoor main board's voltage detection needs a technician.

Is PP the same as the VRF PP?

No. On GMV VRF units PP is AC current protection of the inverter compressor — a current fault, not a voltage fault. This page is the U-Match PP only.

Does PP appear on wall-split Gree units?

No. PP is not in the Soyal, Livo Gen4, Livo+, evo, or Change wall-split tables. If a wall-split shows a code that looks like PP, check the code again — it may be a different code (or a U-Match indoor unit).
