Gree Air Conditioner Error Code LE

Quick answer

Error codeLE — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansLE = compressor locked rotor protection (compressor stalling) - the compressor rotor is locked/stalled and cannot turn. No blink count is verified for LE in any manual, and no manual gives a diagnosis for LE.
Most common causeNo manual gives a cause for LE. Neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table gives a blink count, an A/C status, or a diagnosis for LE - this page does not invent any of those.
Safe to check yourself?No - no manual gives a reset or a diagnosis for LE. Switch the unit off and call a technician. This is general guidance, not a manual step: a locked/stalling compressor rotor is a technician fault, and repeated restart attempts on a stalled compressor risk damage.
Checked againstGREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual, Malfunction Tab + Gree XK19 Wired Controller Manual, Table 4

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree LE is a THIN code: the meaning is verified in two named factory documents, but neither source gives a blink count, an A/C status, or a diagnosis for LE, and this page says so rather than inventing any of those. The second source is a generic wired-controller error table (applicable to several kinds of units), not a model-specific service manual. LE is present in the evo range and the XK19 table, but ABSENT from the model-specific service tables checked first-hand: Change 18/24 section 9.3 (rows 1-40, no LE), Livo Gen4 section 9.1 (rows 1-49, no LE), Livo+ section 9.1 (p.40, no LE), Soyal section 9.1 (rows 1-53, no LE), and the Mini After-sale Error Code List (split table, no LE row). LE does not exist on every Gree range - check your own manual.

GMV VRF - DIFFERENT fault, do not mergeOn GMV VRF, LE means "Rotation speed of EC DC water pump is abnormal" - a different fault on a different system.

LE vs Lc - do not mergeLc = startup failure (the compressor failed to start). LE = the compressor rotor is locked/stalled. Related but different codes; do not merge them.

The only manual-grounded Gree LE guide: verbatim rows from two named factory documents (evo Malfunction Tab, XK19 Table 4) with the "locked rotor = stalling" equivalence stated; the correction of the defrost explanation one site gives (LE is not a defrost/low-temperature code - defrost is H1); the honest "no blink count, no diagnosis in any manual" admission no competitor makes; the VRF LE split (water-pump rotation speed) and the Versati LE split (drive-main communication); the model-line split (present in evo/XK19, absent from Change/Livo Gen4/Livo+/Soyal/Mini). No ranking page gives the manual wording, the defrost correction, or either split.

What the machine does when LE fires

No A/C status is given for LE in either manual - neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table states what the unit does when LE shows. This page does not invent one.

Common causes

  1. No manual gives a cause for LE - neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table lists a cause, a blink count, an A/C status, or a diagnosis for LE.
  2. LE is NOT a defrost or low-temperature fault - defrost is H1, a status display. One ranking site claims LE is caused by "low temperature or defrosts factors" and clears when defrost completes; that is fabricated.
  3. LE is NOT a compressor-replacement instruction - one site's "Replace compressor" is generic advice, not a manual step. No manual prescribes compressor replacement for LE.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch the unit off and call a technician - a locked/stalling compressor rotor is a technician fault. This is general guidance, not a manual step, because no manual gives a reset or a diagnosis for LE.
  2. Do not keep restarting the unit - repeated restart attempts on a stalled compressor risk damage.
  3. Do not trust any site that quotes a blink count for LE without a manual source - neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table gives one.
  4. Do not use the defrost explanation for LE: LE has nothing to do with low temperature or defrost, and it does not clear itself when defrost completes.

Neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table gives any diagnostic step, cause, reset, or A/C status for LE. This page does not invent a blink count, a restart ritual, or a resistance test for LE.

When to call a technician

  • LE is displayed at all - a locked/stalling compressor rotor is a technician fault, so call a qualified technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside LE - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit is a Change, Livo Gen4, Livo+, Soyal, or Mini wall-split showing LE - LE is not listed on those ranges, so what you are seeing needs professional diagnosis of the real fault.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

LE is a locked/stalling compressor rotor: never open the outdoor unit or try to free the compressor yourself, and do not repeatedly restart a stalled compressor - repeated restart attempts risk damage. Switch the unit off and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does LE mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Compressor locked rotor protection - the compressor rotor is locked or stalling and cannot turn. Verified in two Gree documents (evo Malfunction Tab: "Compressor locked rotary protection"; XK19 Table 4: "Compressor stalling").

What is the blink pattern for LE?

None is verified - neither the evo manual nor the XK19 table gives a blink count for LE. Do not trust any site that quotes one without a manual source.

How do I reset a Gree LE error?

No manual gives a reset or a diagnosis for LE. Switch the unit off and call a technician - a locked/stalling compressor rotor is a technician fault. This is general guidance, not a manual step.

Is LE a defrost problem?

No. One ranking site claims LE is caused by "low temperature or defrosts factors" and clears when defrost completes - that is fabricated. Defrost is H1, a status display; LE is a locked compressor rotor.

Is LE the same on VRF or Versati units?

No. On GMV VRF, LE means the EC DC water pump rotation speed is abnormal. On Versati, LE means a drive-main-control communication error. On wall-split evo and XK19-controller units, LE means compressor locked rotor. Check your system type first.
