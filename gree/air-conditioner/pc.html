Gree Air Conditioner Error Code PC

Quick answer

Error codePC — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansPC = current detection (current sense) circuit error - after power charging, if the offset-voltage average exceeds 12.5% of 1.65V in 1s, the current detection (or current sensor) circuit is judged faulted and the system shuts down. U-Match (duct/cassette) and rooftop packaged units ONLY - PC is absent from every wall-split manual checked.
Most common causeThe current detection (or current sensor) sampling circuit element is abnormal, or the drive chip compressor current sampling AD terminal is badly welded or short-circuited (U-Match R32 service manual, section 3.4.32).
Safe to check yourself?No - no manual gives a user reset for PC. Restart the unit once; if PC comes back, it is a technician job (drive board / mainboard). There is no separate current sensor to replace - the sampling circuit is on the board.
Checked againstGree U-Match Series DC Inverter Service Manual, R410A (GC201602-I), section 1.2 + section 2.2 + Gree DC Inverter U-Match Series Service Manual, R32 (2019), section 3.3 row 34 + section 3.4.32 + Gree Mini After-sales Service Manual, U-Match appendix (p.35) + Gree ME33-42/G Owner's Manual

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree PC is a U-Match / rooftop packaged code ONLY - never a general wall-split code. PC is verified in two Gree U-Match service manuals (R410A and R32) plus the Mini U-Match appendix and the ME33-42/G adaptor manual (which states the adaptor is 'applicable for the Gree rooftop packaged unit, and U-Match unit'). It is ABSENT from every wall-split manual checked first-hand: Change 18/24 section 9.3, Viola GWH09/12RB section 9.1, Soyal GWH09/12/18AKC section 9.1, the G-TECH user manual, Livo Gen4/Livo+ section 9.1, the Mini split-type flash table, Flexx A-coil, and Xe71 Table 4. Note the wording chain inside the R32 manual: the section 3.3 table shorthand is 'Driver current error', but section 3.4.32 defines it as a current detection / current sensor CIRCUIT error - the R410A manual's 'Current sense circuit error' is the precise wording. PC is a detection-circuit fault, not a current-level fault.

What the machine does when PC fires

The ODU mainboard, the IDU wired controller and the IDU receiver light board display PC, and the system shuts down for protection. PC is not a flash-count code like the wall-split P5-P8: U-Match shows the code on the wired controller / dual-8 display.

Common causes

  1. Current detection (or current sensor) sampling circuit element abnormal - the R32 manual's first possible reason.
  2. Drive chip compressor current sampling AD terminal badly welded or short-circuited - the R32 manual's second possible reason.
  3. Wrong fix - there is NO separate current-sensor part to replace: the sampling circuit is on the drive board. 'Replace sensor or circuit' (one ranking site) is not a manual step.
  4. Wrong-platform reading - VRF PC is the same concept on a different platform; Versati row 50's 'Desynchronizing' is a likely OCR error, not a PC meaning.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Restart the unit once: switch it off, wait a few minutes, switch it back on. If PC clears, the trip may have been transient.
  2. If PC comes back, it is a technician job - the R32 manual's fix is 'Replace the drive board'; the R410A section 2.2 flowchart says 'Replace the mainboard' for the drive-malfunction group (P0/P7/PA/HC/PC). Record both wordings - do not harmonise them.
  3. Do not invent multimeter or sensor-replacement steps - no wall-split manual gives a PC diagnosis procedure, and the U-Match manuals give only the board fix.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy. This page does not invent a sensor-replacement step - no manual says there is a separate current sensor to replace.

When to call a technician

  • PC returns after one restart - the drive board (R32) or mainboard (R410A flowchart) needs a technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside PC - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit is a wall-split showing PC - PC is not a wall-split code, so what you are seeing needs professional diagnosis of the real fault.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

PC involves mains-powered drive electronics (drive board, current sampling circuit): never open the outdoor unit or probe the drive board yourself. Work only from the outside of the unit, and switch it off at the isolator before any visible check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does PC mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Current detection (current sense) circuit error - the drive board's current detection circuit is faulted. Verified in two Gree U-Match service manuals (R410A section 1.2, R32 section 3.3 row 34 + section 3.4.32).

Does every Gree AC use PC?

No - and this matters. PC is listed in the U-Match (duct/cassette) and rooftop packaged manuals, but NOT in Change 18/24, Viola, Soyal, G-TECH, Livo Gen4 or Livo+. If you have a wall-split unit, PC is not your code - check your own model's manual.

How do I fix a Gree PC error?

Restart the unit once. If PC comes back, it is a technician job - the manual's fix is to replace the drive board (R32 section 3.4.32; the R410A flowchart says replace the mainboard for the drive-malfunction group). There is no separate current sensor to replace; the sampling circuit is on the board.

Is PC the same as U1 or U5?

No. PC is the DRIVE's current detection circuit. U1 is the compressor PHASE current detection circuit, and U5 is the WHOLE-UNIT current detection circuit. Three different codes, three different diagnoses.

Does PC mean desynchronizing?

No. One heat-pump table (Versati II) appears to list 'Pc = Desynchronizing', but that is almost certainly an OCR misread - desynchronizing is P9 in the VRF tables, and the same Versati manual's own section 4.1.1 flowchart assigns PC to the current-sense circuit. Do not trust the row-50 reading.
