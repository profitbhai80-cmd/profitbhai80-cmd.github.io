Gree Air Conditioner Error Code U2

Quick answer

Error codeU2 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansU2 = compressor protection against loss of phase (phase-loss protection). The indoor unit blinks 12 times. U2 is an evo-era code: it is absent from every later service manual checked, and on later generations the same function is carried by Ld (compressor loss of phase protection).
Most common causeThe after-sale manual names three causes: the outdoor main board is damaged; the compressor is damaged; or the compressor connection wire is not connected well.
Safe to check yourself?No - the manuals give no reset procedure for U2. Note the code and the blink count, switch the unit off, and call a technician. All three causes are technician-level (outdoor board, compressor, compressor wiring).
Checked againstGREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual, Malfunction Tab + Gree Mini After-sale Service Manual, blink-count table (split type / floor standing section)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because U2 is an evo-era code. It is verified in two manufacturer documents with identical meaning, but it is ABSENT from every later service manual checked - Change 18/24 section 9.3 (full 40 rows), Viola section 9.1 (54 rows), Soyal section 9.1 (53 rows), Livo Gen4 section 9.1, Livo+, and U-Match R32/R410A/5-series. On later generations the same function is carried by Ld ("Compressor loss of phase protection"). A U2 display therefore means an evo-era (or older) unit. Scope warning: GMV VRF U2 = "Wrong setting of ODU's capacity code / jumper cap" - a different fault on a different platform. Rooftop GK-H U2 = "Inverter Indoor Fan Drive AC Input Zero-crossing Protection" - also a different fault. Neither belongs on this page except as this scope warning. Caveat, stated honestly: the Mini after-sale PDF's OCR drops the code-letter column for many rows, so the U2 match there rests on blink 12 plus the fault name (unique to U2 in that table - no other row carries "lost phase" / "loss of phase" at flash 12). The evo manual's U2 row is clean and unambiguous.

What the machine does when U2 fires

In cooling the compressor and outdoor fan stop. In heating the compressor and outdoor fan stop, then the indoor fan stops about 1 minute later.

Common causes

  1. Outdoor main board damaged - the after-sale manual's first cause.
  2. Compressor damaged - the after-sale manual's second cause.
  3. Compressor connection wire not connected well - the after-sale manual's third cause.
  4. Wrong-code reading - U2 is NOT a DC-bus voltage fault. That meaning belongs to U3; the f-static PDF's U2 row carries U3's meaning because its U-rows are shifted by one.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Note the code and the blink count (12 blinks) and switch the unit off.
  2. Call a technician - the causes are the outdoor board, the compressor, or its wiring, none of which is an owner fix.
  3. Do not present a generic reset as a manual remedy: the manuals give no breaker-reset, 5-10-minute, or power-cycle step for U2, and do not treat U2 as a DC-bus voltage fault (that is U3).

The manuals give NO reset procedure for U2. Do not invent one: no breaker-reset, 5-10-minute, or power-cycle step exists in either manual. All three causes are technician-level.

When to call a technician

  • U2 appears at all - the outdoor main board, the compressor, or the compressor connection wiring needs a technician to test.
  • Your unit is a later-generation model showing a phase-loss fault - check for Ld instead; U2 does not exist on Change 18/24, Viola, Soyal, Livo Gen4, Livo+ or U-Match.
  • Your unit is a GMV VRF or Rooftop GK-H unit showing U2 - that is a different fault on a different platform; use that platform's manual, not this page.

Safety first

U2 involves the outdoor main board, the compressor and mains-powered wiring: never open the outdoor unit or test the compressor wiring yourself. Note the code, switch the unit off, and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U2 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Compressor protection against loss of phase: the unit detected that the compressor lost a phase. Verified in the evo owner's manual (blink 12) and the Gree after-sale service manual (flash 12). In cooling the compressor and outdoor fan stop; in heating the indoor fan stops about a minute later too.

Why doesn't my newer Gree manual list U2?

U2 is an evo-era code. Later service manuals (Change 18/24, Viola, Soyal, Livo Gen4, U-Match) have no U2 row - the same function is carried by Ld ("Compressor loss of phase protection") on those generations. If your unit shows U2, it is an evo-era (or older) model.

What causes U2?

The after-sale manual names three causes: the outdoor main board is damaged, the compressor is damaged, or the compressor connection wire is not connected well. All three need a technician to test.

Is U2 the same as Ld?

Same concept (compressor loss of phase), different code on different generations. U2 is the evo-era code; Ld is the code later generations use. They are not interchangeable - check your model and manual.

Can I reset U2 myself?

The manuals give no reset procedure for U2, so the page cannot promise one will work. Switch the unit off and call a technician - the causes are the outdoor board, the compressor, or its wiring, none of which is an owner fix. (Note: some websites tell you U2 is a DC-bus voltage fault - that is U3's meaning, not U2's.)
