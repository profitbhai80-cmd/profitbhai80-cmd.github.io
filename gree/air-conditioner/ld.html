Gree Air Conditioner Error Code LD

Quick answer

Error codeLD — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansLD = compressor loss of phase protection (compressor phase loss/reversal) - one phase of the compressor supply is lost (or reversed). Blink pattern: OFF 3S and blink 12 times (Mini After-sale manual only - the evo and XK19 documents give no blink count).
Most common causeThe main board of the outdoor unit is damaged; the compressor is damaged; or the connection wire of the compressor is not connected well (Gree Mini After-sale Service Manual, Error Code List).
Safe to check yourself?No - there is no user reset for LD in any manual. The three manual causes are all technician checks. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself.
Checked againstGREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual, Malfunction Tab + Gree Mini After-sale Service Manual, Error Code List + Gree XK19 Wired Controller Manual, Table 4

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree LD has one meaning across three named factory documents, but it does not exist on every Gree range. LD is verified in the evo Owner's Manual Malfunction Tab, the Mini After-sale Service Manual Error Code List (split type and floor standing unit), and the XK19 wired-controller Table 4. It is ABSENT from the model-specific service tables checked first-hand: Change 18/24 section 9.3 (rows 1-40, no Ld), Livo Gen4 section 9.1 (rows 1-49, no Ld), Livo+ section 9.1 (p.40, no Ld), and Soyal section 9.1 (rows 1-53, no Ld). Check your own manual - LD does not exist on every Gree range.

No ranking page gives the blink count, the three causes, or the U2 distinction.

What the machine does when LD fires

In cooling, the compressor and outdoor fan stop operation. In heating, the compressor and outdoor fan stop operation at first, and about 1 minute later the indoor fan stops operation (Mini After-sale Service Manual).

Common causes

  1. The main board of the outdoor unit is damaged - the Mini manual's first cause.
  2. The compressor is damaged - the Mini manual's second cause.
  3. The connection wire of the compressor is not connected well - the Mini manual's third cause.
  4. Wrong fix copied from H5 - tightening terminals and "adjusting the compressor phase sequence" are H5 fixes, not LD steps. The Mini manual gives no phase-sequence adjustment step for LD.

Safe checks, in order

  1. There is no user reset for LD in any manual. Do not keep restarting the unit hoping it clears.
  2. Call a qualified technician - the manual's three causes (outdoor main board damaged, compressor damaged, compressor connection wire not connected well) are all technician checks.
  3. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself, and do not use the H5 fixes other sites copy for LD (tighten terminals, adjust the compressor phase sequence) - no manual gives those steps for LD.
  4. Check which code your display actually shows - LD and U2 are separate codes with the same blink count (12). Do not merge them.

No manual gives a user reset, a phase-sequence adjustment, or a diagnostic step for LD beyond the three causes above. The generic "check wiring and compressor, consult technician" advice on some sites is not a manual step.

When to call a technician

  • LD is displayed at all - the three manual causes are all technician checks, so call a qualified technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside LD - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit is a Change, Livo Gen4, Livo+, or Soyal wall-split showing LD - LD is not listed on those ranges, so what you are seeing needs professional diagnosis of the real fault.

Safety first

LD involves the compressor circuit and the outdoor unit's main board: never open the outdoor unit or probe the compressor wiring yourself. Work only from the outside of the unit, and switch it off at the isolator before any visible check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does LD mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Compressor loss of phase protection - one phase of the compressor supply is lost (or reversed). Verified in three Gree documents (evo Malfunction Tab, Mini After-sale Error Code List, XK19 wired-controller Table 4).

What is the blink pattern for LD?

OFF 3 seconds, then blink 12 times (Mini After-sale manual). In cooling, the compressor and outdoor fan stop; in heating, the compressor and outdoor fan stop first and the indoor fan stops about 1 minute later.

How do I reset a Gree LD error?

There is no user reset in any manual. The three manual causes are: the outdoor unit's main board is damaged, the compressor is damaged, or the compressor connection wire is not connected well - all technician checks. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself.

Is LD the same as U2?

No - although both are phase-loss codes with the same blink count (12) in the evo-era table. U2 is "Compressor protection against loss of phase"; LD is the drive-side "Compressor loss of phase protection" in the Mini/XK19 tables. The manuals list them as separate codes; check which code your display actually shows.

Is LD the same as Lc?

No. Lc is startup failure - the compressor failed to start at all. LD is a phase loss in the compressor circuit. Different codes, different causes, different fixes.
