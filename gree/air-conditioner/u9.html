Gree Air Conditioner Error Code U9

Quick answer

Error codeU9 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansU9 = zero-crossing malfunction of the outdoor unit (evo wording: "AC power zero voltage detect error"). The outdoor unit's zero-crossing signal failed. The indoor unit blinks 18 times (Viola). The manual fix is to replace the outdoor control panel AP1.
Most common causeThe outdoor unit's zero-crossing signal failed. The manuals name no component cause beyond the failed zero-crossing signal; their stated remedy is to replace the outdoor control panel AP1.
Safe to check yourself?No - the manuals give no reset procedure for U9 and no owner-level diagnostic step. Note the code and blink count, switch the unit off, and call a technician - the fix is an outdoor control board replacement.
Checked againstGree Change 18/24 Service Manual, section 9.3 row 39 + Gree Viola 09-12K Service Manual, section 9.1 row 48 + Gree Soyal Service Manual, section 9.1 row 47 + Gree Mini After-sale Service Manual + GREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual, Malfunction Tab (wording variant)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree itself words U9 two ways, and the page must carry BOTH wordings and say they are the same circuit: the evo owner's manual says "AC power zero voltage detect error", while the Change 18/24, Viola and Soyal service manuals say "Zero-crossing malfunction of outdoor unit". Both describe the outdoor unit's zero-crossing detection failing. U9 is absent from Livo Gen4 section 9.1 (no U9 row), Livo+, Change 9/12 section 9.3, and the U-Match tables (no U9 row). Scope-split, never merge: GMV VRF U9 = "Malfunction of pipeline for ODU" - a different fault. Rooftop GK-H U9 = "Current Detecting Error of Complete Unit" - a different fault (that is wall-split U5's concept). Distinguish from its neighbour: U8 = zero-cross detection circuit malfunction of the INDOOR unit (blink 17) - U8 is the indoor zero-cross circuit, U9 is the outdoor zero-cross circuit. Do not confuse the two. Caveat: Change 18/24 row 39 gives no clean indoor blink count (outdoor indicator pattern only); blink 18 comes from Viola.

What the machine does when U9 fires

During cooling operation the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running; during heating operation the complete unit stops.

Common causes

  1. Zero-crossing signal of the outdoor unit failed - the manuals' definition of the fault.
  2. Outdoor control panel AP1 fault - the manuals' stated remedy is "Replace outdoor control panel AP1" (Mini after-sale: "Replace the control board of outdoor unit").
  3. Wrong-code reading - U8 is the zero-cross detection circuit malfunction of the INDOOR unit (blink 17), not the outdoor unit. U9 is the outdoor zero-cross circuit.
  4. Wrong-platform reading - GMV VRF U9 (pipeline for ODU) and Rooftop GK-H U9 (current detecting error of complete unit) are different faults on different platforms.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Note the code and the blink count (18 blinks) and switch the unit off.
  2. Call a technician - the manual fix is "Replace outdoor control panel AP1", a technician job.
  3. Do not present a breaker reset or power cycle as a manual remedy: the manuals give NO reset procedure for U9 and no owner-level diagnostic step.

The manuals give NO reset procedure for U9 and no owner-level diagnostic step. The page's only owner action is: note the code and blink count, switch the unit off, and call a technician - the fix is an outdoor control board replacement.

When to call a technician

  • U9 appears at all - the outdoor control panel AP1 needs a technician; there is no owner fix.
  • U9 appears together with other outdoor-board symptoms - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit is a Livo Gen4, Livo+, Change 9/12 or U-Match unit showing U9 - U9 does not exist on those generations, so what you are seeing needs professional diagnosis of the real fault.

Safety first

U9 involves the outdoor control board and mains power: never open the outdoor unit or test the control board yourself. Note the code, switch the unit off, and call a qualified technician.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U9 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Zero-crossing malfunction of the outdoor unit: the outdoor unit's zero-crossing signal failed. Verified in three service manuals (blink 18). The evo owner's manual words the same fault as "AC power zero voltage detect error" - same circuit, different wording.

Why do websites give two different wordings for U9?

Because Gree itself words it two ways: the evo owner's manual says "AC power zero voltage detect error", while the Change 18/24, Viola, and Soyal service manuals say "Zero-crossing malfunction of outdoor unit". Both describe the outdoor unit's zero-crossing detection failing. Our page carries both so either wording matches your manual.

Is U9 the same as U8?

No. U8 is the zero-cross detection circuit malfunction of the INDOOR unit (blink 17, and evo alone says it is an exhaust-temperature sensor fault). U9 is the zero-crossing malfunction of the OUTDOOR unit (blink 18). Different circuits, different boards, different codes.

Can I reset U9 myself?

The manuals give no reset procedure for U9, so the page cannot promise one will work. The manual fix is "Replace outdoor control panel AP1" - a technician job. Switch the unit off and call a technician.

What happens when U9 appears?

In cooling the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running; in heating the complete unit stops. That behaviour is stated in the Change 18/24 and Viola service manuals.
