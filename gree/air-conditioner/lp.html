Gree Air Conditioner Error Code LP

Quick answer

Error codeLP — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansLP means the indoor unit and outdoor unit do not match: the paired units are the wrong model or capacity for each other. It is an installation/matching fault, not a component failure — no reset fixes it, and no sensor or board replacement fixes it either.
Most common causeWrong model or capacity pairing — the indoor and outdoor units are not a matched pair (NEO manual: 'Indoor and Outdoor Units Do Not Match (Model or Capacity)').
Safe to check yourself?No — there is no owner fix for LP. Do not replace a sensor or board; the only remedy the manuals give is fitting a matched indoor/outdoor pair.

Applies to — read this first

LP is a matching fault, and every manual that names it says the same thing: the indoor and outdoor units are the wrong model or capacity for each other. Seven manufacturer manuals agree, so there is no series split. LP appears at installation or after an indoor/outdoor unit swap — it is not a part that has failed, and no reset, sensor or board will fix it.

The ranking pages barely cover LP at all. An f-static PDF gives one circular line — 'Incompatible Indoor and Outdoor Units. Solution: Indoor and Outdoor Units are not compatible' — which restates the meaning as the fix and names no manual, no model/capacity distinction and no U-Match remedy. Pickhvac's GREE list has no LP row at all, and neither luce nor aysonline surfaced an LP row. The same f-static document also shifts its U-rows by one and gives PU the wrong fix, so its LP line should not be trusted on its own.

What the machine does when LP fires

The Mini after-sale manual says that in heating, the compressor, outdoor unit and indoor fan stop operation when LP shows. The fault is the pairing itself: the indoor and outdoor units cannot work together because their model or capacity does not match. No blink count is verified for LP — do not assign one.

Common causes

  1. Models of indoor unit and outdoor unit do not match with each other (U-Match R32 §3.4.24).
  2. Capacity of indoor unit and outdoor unit can't be matched (Mini after-sale manual).
  3. LP appears at installation or after an indoor or outdoor unit has been swapped for one from a different model or capacity.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Confirm the code is LP and note the indoor and outdoor model numbers from their rating labels.
  2. Do not power-cycle repeatedly and do not replace a sensor or board — no manual gives a reset or a part replacement for LP.
  3. Call the installer or a qualified technician: the manuals' only remedy is to turn off the unit and replace with a matched indoor or outdoor unit.

LP is not fixed by power-cycling or by replacing a sensor or board. The page says this plainly because the fix is a matched pair, not a part.

When to call a technician

  • LP shows on a newly installed unit or right after an indoor or outdoor unit was replaced.
  • The indoor and outdoor model numbers or capacities do not match — an installer needs to fit a matched pair.

Safety first

LP itself is a configuration fault, not a live electrical fault — but any work that swaps an indoor or outdoor unit involves refrigerant and mains power and belongs to a qualified technician. Never attempt to re-pipe or re-wire a mismatched pair yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does LP mean on a Gree air conditioner?

The indoor unit and outdoor unit do not match: the paired units are the wrong model or capacity for each other. Verified in seven Gree manuals.

Will turning the unit off and on clear LP?

No. No manual gives a reset for LP. It is a matching fault, not a glitch: the indoor and outdoor units are an unmatched pair.

How is LP fixed?

The U-Match manual's fix is the only one the manuals give: turn off the unit and replace with a matched indoor or outdoor unit. No sensor or board replacement fixes LP.

Is LP the same as E6?

No. E6 is a communication malfunction — matched units that cannot talk to each other. LP is units that do not match at all, even with good wiring.

Is LP the same as C5?

No. C5 is a jumper-cap malfunction on one control board. LP is the indoor/outdoor pair itself being mismatched (model or capacity).
