Gree Air Conditioner Error Code U8

Quick answer

Error codeU8 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansU8 = zero-crossing (zero-cross detection) circuit malfunction — a fault in the zero-crossing detection circuit on the mainboard (used for the PG indoor-fan motor / IDU fan motor circuit). This is the primary meaning: THREE factory families (including the current official Livo Gen4 manual) define it this way. MANDATORY SERIES-SPLIT WARNING: only the evo owner's manual defines U8 as "Disconnection of the exhaust temperature sensor" (1 family, no second manual found 2026-10-28→2026-11-03). If your unit is an evo-era model, check your own manual — and cross-check evo's F5 ("Exhaust sensor open or short circuit") against which sensor row your unit is reporting. Wall-split scope — do not import GMV VRF U8 meanings ("Malfunction of pipeline for IDU").
Most common causeThe zero-crossing detection circuit on the mainboard is abnormal (Change 18/24: "Control board is damaged"; Classic 7-12: "Malfunction of the zero-cross detection circuit of the mainboard"; Livo Gen4: "Power supply is abnormal" or "Detection circuit of indoor control mainboard is abnormal").
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own first move: power-cycle the unit (the Classic 7-12 flowchart says "Re-energize 1 minute after de-energization" — a slow capacitor discharge can cause a false U8). If U8 returns, the board needs replacement — technician work.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because U8 is Gree's most conflicted code: THREE factory families (including the current official manual) define U8 as zero-cross detection malfunction, while ONLY the evo owner's manual defines it as exhaust-temperature-sensor disconnection. The two serious competitor pages flatly contradict each other on U8 and neither acknowledges the conflict — this page presents both meanings with their model scopes. Lead with zero-cross; carry the mandatory evo warning. Wall-split scope — do not import GMV VRF U8 ("Malfunction of pipeline for IDU").

What the machine does when U8 fires

The display shows U8 (indoor LED blinks 17 times on most generations). Change 18/24: the remote/control panel still responds, "but the unit won't act". Livo Gen4: the complete unit stops.

Common causes

  1. The controller diagnosed incorrectly because of instant re-energization after de-energization while the capacitor discharges slowly — the Classic 7-12 manual's own false-positive case (fixed by re-energizing 1 minute after de-energization).
  2. Malfunction of the zero-cross detection circuit of the mainboard — the Classic 7-12 manual's own cause 2.
  3. The control board is damaged — the Change 18/24 manual's own reason.
  4. Power supply is abnormal, or the detection circuit of the indoor control mainboard is abnormal — the Livo Gen4 manual's own causes.

Safe checks, in order

  1. De-energize the unit, wait 1 minute, and re-energize — the Classic 7-12 flowchart's own first step ("Re-energize 1 minute after de-energization" → "The unit returns to normal").
  2. If U8 is still displayed, the manuals' own conclusion: "The zero-cross detection circuit of the mainboard is defined abnormal. Replace the mainboard." — technician work.
  3. Owner-manual-era guidance: "Can be eliminated after restarting the unit. If not, please contact qualified professionals for service."

The power-cycle here is a genuine manual step (not generic advice): the Classic 7-12 flowchart gives it as the first U8 branch. If U8 survives it, the manuals' own conclusion is board replacement — technician work.

When to call a technician

  • U8 returns after de-energizing, waiting 1 minute, and re-energizing — the zero-cross detection circuit of the mainboard is defined abnormal (the manuals' own conclusion): replace the mainboard.
  • evo-era model where the manual's U8 row reads "Disconnection of the exhaust temperature sensor" — then the sensor circuit, not zero-cross, is the suspect; match the manual.

Safety first

U8 is a mainboard-circuit fault: the indoor unit is mains-powered. Never open the indoor unit, never probe the mainboard, and never replace a board yourself — that is qualified-technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does U8 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Primary meaning: U8 = zero-crossing (zero-cross detection) circuit malfunction — a fault in the zero-crossing detection circuit on the mainboard (used for the PG indoor-fan motor circuit). It is verified verbatim in three factory manuals: Change 18/24 (§9.3 row 7 + §9.4(4)), Classic 7-12 (§8), and the official Livo Gen4 manual (§9.1 row 46 + §9.2(4)).

Why do two websites say different things about U8?

Because they each picked one meaning and hid the conflict: becoolrefrigeration.co.uk says zero-cross detection; luce.sg says exhaust-sensor disconnection. The factory evidence is 3-vs-1 for zero-cross — but ONLY the evo owner's manual says exhaust sensor, so evo-era owners must check their own manual.

My evo-era Gree shows U8 — which meaning applies?

Check your own manual. If it is an evo-era unit, the evo manual's U8 row reads "Disconnection of the exhaust temperature sensor". Also cross-check evo's F5 ("Exhaust sensor open or short circuit") to see which sensor row your unit is reporting.

Can I clear U8 myself?

The Classic 7-12 manual's own first step: de-energize, wait 1 minute, re-energize — a slow capacitor discharge can cause a false U8. If U8 returns, the manuals' own conclusion is board replacement — technician work.

What does the remote do on U8?

On the Change 18/24 generation: "Operation of remote controller or control panel is available, but the unit won't act." On Livo Gen4: the complete unit stops.
