Gree Air Conditioner Error Code LA

Quick answer

Error codeLA — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansLA = outdoor fan 2 error protection - on dual-outdoor-fan units only (L3 is fan 1, LA is fan 2). No blink count is verified for LA in any manual - do not assign L3's blink 21 to LA.
Most common causeThe shared L3/LA fan-row causes from the Mini manual: the condenser or air inlet/outlet is blocked; the fan is blocked or loosened; the fan motor or connection wire is damaged; or the main board of the outdoor unit is damaged.
Safe to check yourself?Partly - check the outdoor condenser and the air inlet/outlet for blockage, and check whether the outdoor fan is blocked or loosened. Those are the only user-checkable items in the manual's row. The fan motor/connection wire and the outdoor main board are technician checks.
Checked againstGree Mini After-sale Service Manual, Error Code List (L3 row note) + Gree XK19 Wired Controller Manual, Table 4

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree LA is a THIN code with a narrow scope: LA has no standalone row in any manual - it appears only as a parenthetical note inside the Mini manual's L3 row and as one line in the generic XK19 controller table. LA exists only on units with two outdoor fans: on dual-fan units L3 is fan 1 and LA is fan 2. It is ABSENT from the model-specific service tables checked first-hand: Change 18/24 section 9.3 (rows 1-40, no LA), Livo Gen4 section 9.1 (rows 1-49, no LA), Livo+ section 9.1 (p.40, no LA), Soyal section 9.1 (rows 1-53, no LA), and the evo Malfunction Tab (no LA row). Single-fan units use L3 only - check your own manual.

The only manual-grounded Gree LA guide: verbatim LA evidence from two named factory documents (Mini L3-row note "L3 indicates fan 1; LA indicates fan 2" + XK19 Table 4 "Outdoor fan 2 error protection"); the dual-fan-only scope (LA does not exist on single-fan units); the shared L3/LA fan-row causes and the A/C status (all loads stop except the indoor fan in cooling/drying; all loads stop in heating); the honest "no blink count verified for LA" admission - no competitor states it, and none of the big lists even carries LA; the VRF LA split (IDU series inconsistent, HBS network). No ranking page gives the fan-2 meaning, the causes, or the split.

What the machine does when LA fires

Cool/Dry: all loads stop operation except the indoor fan. Heat: all loads stop operation (Mini After-sale Service Manual, L3/LA row).

Common causes

  1. The condenser or air inlet/outlet is blocked - the Mini manual's first shared fan-row cause.
  2. The fan is blocked or loosened - the Mini manual's second shared fan-row cause.
  3. The fan motor or connection wire is damaged - the Mini manual's third shared fan-row cause (technician check).
  4. The main board of the outdoor unit is damaged - the Mini manual's fourth shared fan-row cause (technician check).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check the outdoor condenser and the air inlet/outlet for blockage, and clear any blockage you can reach safely from outside the unit.
  2. Check whether the outdoor fan is blocked or loosened - that is the other user-checkable item in the manual's row.
  3. If the fan motor or connection wire is damaged, or the outdoor main board is damaged, call a qualified technician - those are technician checks.
  4. Do not use L3's blink 21 (evo) for LA - no manual gives a blink count for LA. Do not invent a reset ritual or a resistance test for LA; none is published in any manual.

No diagnosis steps exist for LA as a separate code; the causes above are the shared L3/LA fan-row causes from the Mini manual, and the page says so. No blink count, reset ritual, or resistance test is published for LA.

When to call a technician

  • LA returns after the blockage checks - the fan motor, its connection wire, or the outdoor main board needs a technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside LA - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit has only one outdoor fan and shows LA - single-fan units do not list LA at all (they use L3), so what you are seeing needs professional diagnosis of the real fault.

Safety first

LA involves the outdoor fan and the outdoor unit's main board: never open the outdoor unit, reach into the fan, or probe the fan motor wiring yourself. Work only from the outside of the unit, and switch it off at the isolator before any visible check.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does LA mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Outdoor fan 2 error protection - on units with two outdoor fans, L3 is fan 1 and LA is fan 2. Verified in two Gree documents (Mini After-sale manual: "L3 indicates fan 1; LA indicates fan 2"; XK19 Table 4: "Outdoor fan 2 error protection").

Does LA apply to my Gree unit?

Only if your unit has two outdoor fans. Single-fan wall-split units (Change, evo, Livo Gen4, Livo+, Soyal) do not list LA at all - they use L3 for the outdoor fan.

What is the blink pattern for LA?

None is verified - no manual gives a blink count for LA. Do not use L3's blink 21 (evo) for LA; the manuals do not assign it.

What causes LA?

The manual's fan-row causes, shared with L3: the condenser or air inlet/outlet is blocked; the fan is blocked or loosened; the fan motor or connection wire is damaged; or the outdoor unit's main board is damaged. The first two are user-checkable; the last two are technician checks.

Is LA the same on VRF units?

No. On GMV VRF, LA means "For single control over multiple units, IDU series is inconsistent (HBS network)" - a completely different fault. On wall-split dual-fan units, LA means outdoor fan 2 error protection.
