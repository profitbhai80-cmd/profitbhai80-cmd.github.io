Gree Air Conditioner Error Code PU

Quick answer

Error codePU — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansPU = charging malfunction of capacitor (charging circuit error). The capacitor charging circuit has malfunctioned, so the unit stops the compressor (indoor fan keeps running in cooling/drying; the whole unit stops in heating). Blink pattern: OFF 3S and blink 17 times. Do NOT 'replace the capacitor' - no manual prescribes that; the manuals replace the control board.
Most common causeThe capacitor charging circuit has malfunctioned - checked in the manuals by the L-N supply voltage (210-240AC), the electrolytic-capacitor voltage against 85% of rated voltage, and the reactor (L) connection. No manual names a capacitor fault as the cause.
Safe to check yourself?No - no manual gives a user reset for PU. Turn the unit off and on once; if PU comes back, it needs a technician (capacitor-charging / control-board diagnosis, with live-voltage and stored-charge hazards).
Checked againstGree Change 18/24 Service Manual, section 9.3 row 32 + section 9.4(2) + Gree Mini After-sale Service Manual, Error Code List + section 9 + Gree Rooftop Packaged Service Manual, section 5.1

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Gree PU is one fault with one meaning across three manuals - but the fix other sites give for it is wrong. PU = charging malfunction of capacitor (charging circuit error), blink 17 in the two wall-split manuals, and the Rooftop Packaged manual agrees. The manuals' diagnosis checks the supply voltage, the electrolytic-capacitor voltage against 85% of rated voltage, and the reactor (L) connection - then replaces the CONTROL BOARD. No manual prescribes replacing the capacitor, and no manual says to 'tighten the PFC wire connection' (the manual's wire check is the reactor (L) connection).

What the machine does when PU fires

During cooling and drying operation the compressor stops while the indoor fan keeps running; during heating operation the complete unit stops. The display pattern is OFF 3 seconds, then blink 17 times.

Common causes

  1. Capacitor charging circuit malfunction - the manuals' own fault definition; the capacitor is not charging properly even if the supply is fine.
  2. Supply voltage outside 210-240AC at the L-N terminals of the wiring board - the Change 18/24 section 9.4(2) detection point.
  3. Reactor (L) connection fault - the reactor wire loosened, pulled out, or the reactor damaged (Change 18/24 section 9.4(2); Mini section 9).
  4. Control-board voltage detection circuit malfunction - if the electrolytic-capacitor voltage is higher than 85% of rated voltage, the control board's detection circuit is at fault (Mini section 9).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Turn the unit off and on once. There is no manual reset for PU - if PU comes back, it needs a technician.
  2. The manual's diagnosis (Change 18/24 section 9.4(2), Mini section 9) checks: the L-N voltage on the wiring board (210-240AC); the electrolytic-capacitor voltage with a DC voltmeter against 85% of rated voltage; the L-N voltage within +/-10% of rated voltage; and the reactor (L) connection (wire not loosened or pulled out, reactor not damaged).
  3. Safety step from the manual: 'Wait for 20min after turning off the power or use DC voltmeter to measure the voltage at both ends of capacity until it's lower than 20V' before touching the capacitor circuit - capacitors stay charged after power-off.
  4. Do NOT replace the capacitor (no manual prescribes it) and do NOT 'tighten the PFC wire connection' (the manual's wire check is the reactor (L) connection). The part the manuals replace, after the reactor and capacitor-voltage checks, is the outdoor control board.

The Mini manual's flowchart boxes are jumbled in the PDF text layer, so the checks above are given in check-point order, not a claimed strict sequence. All voltage and capacitor work is live-voltage / stored-charge technician work - never a homeowner step.

When to call a technician

  • PU returns after one restart - the capacitor-charging / control-board diagnosis needs a technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside PU - switch off and call a qualified technician.
  • Your unit is a VRF unit showing PU - VRF PU is a different fault ('AC input voltage of drive of inverter compressor'); use the VRF manual, not this page.

Safety first

PU involves live-voltage measurement and a capacitor that stays charged after power-off: never open the outdoor unit or touch the capacitor circuit yourself. The manual's own safety step is to wait 20 minutes after power-off, or until the capacitor voltage is below 20V, before touching anything - and that check is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does PU mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Charging malfunction of capacitor - the capacitor charging circuit has malfunctioned. Verified in three Gree manuals (Change 18/24 section 9.3, Mini After-sale Error Code List section 9, Rooftop Packaged section 5.1).

How do I reset a Gree PU error?

There is no manual reset for PU. Turn the unit off and on once; if PU comes back, it needs a technician - the manual's diagnosis checks the L-N supply voltage (210-240AC), the electrolytic-capacitor voltage against 85% of rated voltage, and the reactor (L) connection before the control board is replaced. Never open the unit yourself: capacitors stay charged after power-off (wait 20 minutes, or until the capacitor voltage is below 20V).

Does PU mean I need a new capacitor?

Not necessarily - and not what the manuals say. No manual prescribes replacing the capacitor: the diagnosis checks the supply voltage, the capacitor's voltage against 85% of rated, and the reactor connection, and the part the manuals replace is the outdoor control board.

What is the blink pattern for PU?

OFF 3 seconds, then blink 17 times (Change 18/24 section 9.3, Mini After-sale manual). In cooling/drying the compressor stops while the indoor fan runs; in heating the whole unit stops.

Is PU the same as PL?

No. PL = the DC bus voltage is too low (supply problem, blink 21, L-N below 150VAC). PU = the capacitor charging circuit itself has malfunctioned (blink 17) - the capacitor is not charging properly even if the supply is fine. And do not confuse wall-split PU with VRF PU ('AC input voltage of drive of inverter compressor') - a different fault; VRF's charging-loop code is PF.
