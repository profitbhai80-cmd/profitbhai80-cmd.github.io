Gree Air Conditioner Error Code P7

Quick answer

Error codeP7 — Gree Air Conditioner
What it meansP7 = malfunction of module temperature sensor circuit (the sensor circuit is open or short-circuited). The sensor CIRCUIT is faulty - not the module temperature itself. Blink pattern: OFF 3S and blink 18 times. The manuals' single fix: replace outdoor control panel AP1. P7 is NOT the same as P8.
Most common causeThe module temperature sensor circuit is open or short-circuited. The sensor circuit is on outdoor control panel AP1; there is no separate sensor-replacement step in any wall-split manual.
Safe to check yourself?No manual user reset for P7. Restart once; if P7 returns, it is technician work (outdoor control panel AP1 replacement). Do not open the outdoor unit yourself.
Checked againstGree Change 18/24 Service Manual (section 9.3 row 33) + Gree Livo Gen4 Service Manual (section 9.1 row 29) + Gree GWH18QD-K3DNA1G / GWH24QE-K3DNA1G Service Manual (section 9.1) + GREE evo Split AC Owner's Manual (Malfunction Tab)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because P7 is routinely conflated with P8: P7 = the sensor CIRCUIT is faulty (open/short); P8 = the module is actually too hot (module high temperature protection). Different faults, different fixes: P7 -> replace outdoor control panel AP1; P8 -> 20-min de-energize, thermal-grease/radiator check, then AP1. VRF wording ("Malfunction of drive temperature sensor of compressor", GMV manuals) is the same concept in different words; note it in one line.

What the machine does when P7 fires

The display shows P7 and the module temperature sensor circuit is reported open or short-circuited. Blink pattern: OFF 3 seconds, then blink 18 times. In cooling/drying the compressor stops while the indoor fan runs; in heating the whole unit stops.

Common causes

  1. Module temperature sensor circuit open or short-circuited - the manuals' own fault definition.
  2. The sensor circuit is on outdoor control panel AP1 - there is no separate sensor or IPM replacement step in any wall-split manual (aysonline's "replace sensor or IPM" fix is wrong).
  3. Wrong-code reading - temperature protection is P8, not P7; "OFC IC" (acguide/sidzcoolcare) is not a Gree term.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Restart the unit once; if P7 comes back, it will not clear on its own - the outdoor control panel AP1 needs replacing by a technician.
  2. The manuals give one fix, quoted verbatim: "Replace outdoor control panel AP1." The sensor circuit is on AP1, so there is no separate sensor to replace.
  3. Do not open the outdoor unit yourself and do not invent a sensor-replacement or IPM-replacement step - none exists in any wall-split manual.

The steps above are the manuals' own fix, printed exactly. This page does not invent a sensor or IPM replacement step - the manuals say replace outdoor control panel AP1.

When to call a technician

  • P7 returns after one restart - the outdoor control panel AP1 needs replacing by a technician.
  • Any burning smell, visibly damaged wiring, or other fault codes alongside P7 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

P7 involves mains-powered electronics in the outdoor unit: never open the outdoor unit or touch the control panel AP1 yourself. Replacing AP1 is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does P7 mean on a Gree air conditioner?

Malfunction of the module temperature sensor circuit - the circuit is open or short-circuited. Verified in four Gree manuals (Change 18/24 section 9.3, Livo Gen4 section 9.1, GWH18QD section 9.1, evo Malfunction Tab).

Is P7 the same as P8?

No. P7 = the temperature sensor CIRCUIT is faulty; P8 = the module is actually too hot (module high temperature protection). Different faults with different fixes - do not mix them up, as several ranking pages do.

How do I fix a Gree P7 error?

The manuals give one fix: replace the outdoor control panel AP1. The sensor circuit is on AP1, so there is no separate sensor to replace. This is technician work - do not open the outdoor unit yourself.

How do I reset a Gree P7 error?

Restart the unit once; if P7 comes back, it will not clear on its own - the outdoor control panel AP1 needs replacing by a technician.

What is the blink pattern for P7?

OFF 3 seconds, then blink 18 times (Change 18/24 section 9.3, evo Malfunction Tab). In cooling/drying the compressor stops while the indoor fan runs; in heating the whole unit stops.
