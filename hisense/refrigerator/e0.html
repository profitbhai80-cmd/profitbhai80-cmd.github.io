Hisense Refrigerator Error Code E0

Quick answer

Error codeE0 — Hisense Refrigerator
What it meansE0 = EITHER the sensor keys are not responding (RM469/RS711/RB470 models) OR the ambient/environment temperature sensor is defective (RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410/HRF266 models). Two genuinely different meanings - check your model number first. Do NOT import Hisense air-conditioner E0 (control-board fault) - different appliance.
Most common causeBranch A: the touch (sensor) keys stop responding. Branch B: the humidity/ambient temperature sensor is defective. Neither manual names a component-level cause beyond the fault itself.
Safe to check yourself?No - the manuals give zero homeowner steps for E0. Their full remedy: call a service centre for repair. The appliance keeps running in safe mode until repaired.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Hisense E0 has two different manual-verified meanings and the page cannot give you one without your model: on RM469, RS711 and RB470 models E0 (paired with E8) means the sensor keys are not responding; on RS5P535, LS110EA, RB5P410 and HRF266 models E0 means the ambient/environment temperature sensor is defective. Both branches run in safe mode until a technician repairs the appliance.

What the machine does when E0 fires

The display shows E0 and the appliance operates in safe mode on both branches - it automatically preserves or maintains the most suitable temperature for each compartment until a service technician repairs it.

Common causes

  1. Sensor keys not responding (branch A, RM469/RS711/RB470) - the manuals' own row; no sub-cause, test value, or inspection tree is given.
  2. Humidity and ambient temperature sensor defective (branch B, RS5P535/LS110EA) - the manuals' own row; no sub-cause is given.
  3. Ambient temperature sensor malfunctioning (branch B, RB5P410) / environment sensor malfunctions (branch B, HRF266) - the same ambient/environment sensor fault in different wordings.
  4. Wrong-appliance reading - following Hisense AC E0 advice (control-board fault, breaker power-cycle) for a refrigerator E0 misdiagnoses both branches.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Find your model number (inside the fridge, on the rating plate) and match it to the table above - branch A (RM469/RS711/RB470) or branch B (RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410/HRF266). The meaning depends on it.
  2. Call a service centre for repair - that is the manuals' full remedy on both branches. The appliance keeps running in safe mode until repaired, so book the repair rather than relying on safe mode.
  3. Do not present generic web advice ("unplug for 30 seconds", multimeter-testing sensor wiring) as a manual step - it is not in these manuals.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy, printed exactly. This page does not invent sensor-cleaning, multimeter, or power-cycle steps - none exist in these manuals for E0.

When to call a technician

  • E0 is displayed at all - the manuals' remedy is a service centre in every case; there is no homeowner fix.
  • Touch buttons do not respond (branch A) or the fridge holds safe-mode temperatures but never clears E0 (branch B) - both need a technician.
  • Any other fault code alongside E0 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

E0 is a sensor fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the back panel or probe the control board or sensor wiring yourself. The manuals' stated response is a service centre - safe mode is a holding pattern, not a fix.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E0 mean on a Hisense refrigerator?

It depends on your model: on RM469, RS711 and RB470 models E0 (with E8) means the sensor keys are not responding; on RS5P535, LS110EA, RB5P410 and HRF266 models E0 means the ambient/environment temperature sensor (humidity + ambient on RS5P535/LS110EA) is defective. Check your model number first.

My touch buttons don't respond and the display shows E0 - what do I do?

That is branch A: the sensor keys are not responding. The manuals say to call a service centre for repair; the appliance keeps running in safe mode until repaired. There is no manual-approved button combination or reset for this.

Will my fridge keep running with E0?

Yes, in safe mode on both branches: the appliance "will operate in safe mode" and "automatically preserve/maintain the most suitable temperature for each compartment" until a technician repairs it.

Is E0 the same as E8?

On the fan platform (RM469, RS711, RB470) yes - the manuals pair them in one row ("E0 or E8 ... sensor keys are not responding"). On HRF266 they are different: E0 = environment sensor, E8 = humidity sensor. See the E8 page.

My Hisense air conditioner shows E0 - is it the same fault?

No. AC E0 is a control-board (PCB) fault on a split system. Do not follow AC E0 advice (breaker power-cycle, board replacement) for a refrigerator E0.
