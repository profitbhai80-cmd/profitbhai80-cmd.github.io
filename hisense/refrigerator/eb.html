Hisense Refrigerator Error Code Eb

Quick answer

Error codeEb — Hisense Refrigerator
What it meansEb = the icemaker is defective / ice maker malfunctions. Verified identically in three Hisense documents. No safe-mode wording attaches to Eb in any manual (unlike E9) - the manuals just say call a service centre. Eb is the icemaker ITSELF; E9 (same platforms) is the icemaker TEMPERATURE SENSOR - never merge them.
Most common causeThe icemaker itself has failed. The manuals name no component-level sub-cause - only the fault and the remedy.
Safe to check yourself?No - the manuals give no homeowner reset for Eb. Their full remedy: call a service centre to fix the problem.
Checked againstHisense RS5P535NTFE user manual (p.149) + Hisense LS110EA Instructions for Use + Hisense HRF266N6CSE Service Manual (p.16)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Eb is easy to confuse with E9: on the same models, E9 means the icemaker temperature sensor is defective; Eb means the icemaker itself is defective. They are different faults on the same icemaker. Meanings are identical across all three verified documents, so there is no series split - one unified page. Field note, not a source: ars.repair service logs record Eb on HRS290P5FSE ("ERROR CODE displaying -> Eb - Ice maker Error ... found faulty icemaker. Installed new icemaker") - consistent with the manual meaning, but that model is not claimed as verified.

What the machine does when Eb fires

The display shows Eb and the icemaker will not work. The manuals give no safe-mode wording for Eb (unlike E9); the refrigerator section is not stated to be affected.

Common causes

  1. The icemaker is defective (RS5P535/LS110EA) / ice maker malfunctions (HRF266) - the manuals' own fault definition; no sub-cause is given.
  2. Wrong-code reading - following E9 sensor checks (water inlet valve, ice-maker temperature sensor) for an Eb fault targets the wrong part.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Call a service centre to fix the problem - that is the full remedy in all three documents.
  2. Do not invent a remedy: no "turn the ice function off and on" step, no filter/pressure checks - those appear in forum threads, not in the manuals.
  3. Whether Eb means repair or replacement is a technician's diagnosis after inspection; the ars.repair logs show technicians replacing the faulty icemaker on HRF266N6CSE/HRS290P5FSE, but that is field evidence, not a manual instruction.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy. This page does not invent icemaker-reset, water-valve, or filter steps - none exist in the manuals for Eb.

When to call a technician

  • Eb is displayed at all - the manuals' remedy is a service centre in every case; there is no homeowner fix.
  • The icemaker produces no ice while the fridge section still cools - the icemaker itself is defective; call a service centre.
  • Any other fault code alongside Eb - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

Eb is an icemaker fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the icemaker housing or probe its wiring yourself. The manuals' stated response is a service centre.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does Eb mean on a Hisense refrigerator?

The icemaker is defective (the ice maker itself malfunctions). Verified in the RS5P535 user manual, the LS110EA instructions for use, and the HRF266 service manual, with identical meaning.

Is Eb the same as E9?

No. On the same models, E9 means the icemaker *temperature sensor* is defective; Eb means the icemaker *itself* is defective. They are different faults - do not follow E9 sensor checks for an Eb fault.

Will the fridge keep cooling with Eb?

The manuals give no safe-mode wording for Eb (unlike E9). The refrigerator section is not stated to be affected, but the icemaker will not work; call a service centre to fix the problem.

Can I reset Eb by turning the ice maker off and on?

The manuals give no such step. Forum threads suggest toggling the ice function, but no Hisense manual prescribes it for Eb - do not present it as a manual remedy.

My Hisense fridge shows Eb - do I need a new icemaker?

The manuals say only "the icemaker is defective - call a service centre". Whether that means repair or replacement is a technician's diagnosis after inspection; the ars.repair logs show technicians replacing the faulty icemaker on HRF266N6CSE/HRS290P5FSE, but that is field evidence, not a manual instruction.
