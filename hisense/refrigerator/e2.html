Hisense Refrigerator Error Code E2

Quick answer

Error codeE2 — Hisense Refrigerator
What it meansE2 = temperature sensor error or fault; on RB5P410 models and in doc 962974en-a2, specifically the refrigerator evaporator sensor. On RM469/RS711/RB470 and doc 895744-a1 models E1 and E2 share one manual row. E2 is UNDEFINED on RS5P535, LS110EA and HRF266 - their tables skip E2 entirely. Do not follow air-conditioner E2 advice for a refrigerator E2.
Most common causeA temperature sensor has failed - on RB5P410 specifically the evaporator sensor of the refrigerator. The manuals name no sub-cause beyond the fault itself.
Safe to check yourself?No - the user manuals give no homeowner reset for E2. Their full remedy: call a service centre for repair. The appliance keeps running in safe mode until repaired.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Hisense E2 is worded two ways that agree rather than conflict: on the fan platform (RM469/RS711/RB470/doc 895744-a1) E1 and E2 share one "temperature sensor error or fault" row; on RB5P410 and in doc 962974en-a2, E2 is specified as the evaporator sensor of the refrigerator - an evaporator sensor IS a temperature sensor, so the generic meaning stays true and branch B just names which sensor. Update (2026-11-06): branch B is now verified in two Hisense documents, not one - doc 962974en-a2 gives the identical E2 meaning ("Evaporator sensor of the refrigerator is malfunctioning"). And E2 has a scope limit no competitor states: it is undefined on RS5P535, LS110EA and HRF266, whose tables skip E2 entirely.

What the machine does when E2 fires

The display shows E2 and the appliance operates in safe mode - it automatically preserves or maintains the most suitable temperature for each compartment until a service technician repairs it.

Common causes

  1. Temperature sensor error or fault (branch A) - the manuals' own row; no sub-cause is given.
  2. Evaporator sensor of the refrigerator defective/malfunctioning (branch B, RB5P410 + doc 962974en-a2) - the manuals' own row; the evaporator sensor is a temperature sensor, so this specifies branch A rather than contradicting it.
  3. Wrong-appliance reading - AC E2 (indoor-coil sensor / over-temperature protection, pipe 53-63 C) and washer E2 (water supply) are different appliances' codes.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Call a service centre for repair - that is the manuals' full remedy. Until the appliance is repaired by a service technician, it will operate in safe mode.
  2. Do not invent power-cycle or DIY sensor steps: the user manuals give no homeowner reset for E2.
  3. If your model is an RS5P535, LS110EA or HRF266 and it shows E2, treat it as undefined: photograph the display and contact Hisense support with your model and serial number rather than following another platform's E2 meaning.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy, printed exactly. This page does not invent power-cycle-then-DIY steps - none exist in the user manuals for E2.

When to call a technician

  • E2 is displayed at all - the manuals' remedy is a service centre in every case; there is no homeowner fix.
  • The fridge holds safe-mode temperatures but never clears E2 - the sensor needs replacing by a technician.
  • Any other fault code alongside E2 - switch off and call a qualified technician.

Safety first

E2 is a sensor fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the back panel or probe the control board or sensor wiring yourself. Sensor testing and replacement are technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E2 mean on a Hisense refrigerator?

A temperature sensor error or fault. On RM469, RS711, RB470 and doc 895744-a1 models E1 and E2 share one manual row; on RB5P410 models and in doc 962974en-a2, E2 is specifically the evaporator sensor of the refrigerator.

Is E2 the same as E1?

On the fan platform they share a row, so the display alone does not distinguish them. On the sensor platform they are separate codes with separate meanings - and E2 does not exist at all on RS5P535, LS110EA or HRF266 models.

Will my fridge keep running with E2?

Yes, in safe mode: the manuals say the appliance "will operate in safe mode" and "automatically preserve/maintain the most suitable temperature for each compartment" until a technician repairs it.

Can I reset E2 myself?

The user manuals give no homeowner reset for E2 - only "call a service centre for repair". Do not follow air-conditioner E2 advice (pipe temperatures, outdoor fan) - that is a different appliance.

My Hisense air conditioner shows E2 - is it the same fault?

No. AC E2 is an indoor-coil (evaporator) temperature sensor fault or over-temperature protection on a split system, with AC-specific behaviour. Refrigerator E2 is a different code table; use your fridge manual, not an AC guide.
