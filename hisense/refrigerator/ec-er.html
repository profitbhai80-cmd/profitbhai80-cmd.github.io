Hisense Refrigerator Error Code Ec / Er

Quick answer

Error codeEc / Er — Hisense Refrigerator
What it meansEc / Er = EITHER sensor keys not responding (RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410 user-manual models - the manuals lump Ec and Er in one row) OR, on HRF266 service-manual models, Ec = communication sending malfunction and Er = communication receiving malfunction. Check your model first. Do NOT present Ec/Er as a single "communication error" on user-manual models - their manuals say sensor keys.
Most common causeBranch A: the touch (sensor) keys stop responding. Branch B (HRF266): the communication sending (Ec) or receiving (Er) circuit malfunctions. The manuals name no sub-cause beyond the fault itself.
Safe to check yourself?No - the manuals give no homeowner reset for Ec/Er. Their full remedy: call a service centre for repair. The appliance runs in safe mode (branch A) until repaired.
Checked againstHisense RS5P535NTFE user manual (p.148) + Hisense LS110EA Instructions for Use + Hisense RB5P410SACCUK user manual (p.34) + Hisense HRF266N6CSE Service Manual (p.16)

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because Ec and Er are paired codes with a lumped-vs-split distinction: the user manuals (RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410) lump them in one row - "Ec or Er ... sensor keys are not responding"; the HRF266 service manual splits them - Ec = communication sending malfunctions, Er = communication receiving malfunctions. One page covers both codes because the user manuals treat them as one row. (Second corroboration from a prior round, not re-read today: Hisense RS-77WC1SQD/UPE-001 service manual, manualslib p.14 section 5.1: "Ec/Er communication" rows.)

What the machine does when Ec / Er fires

On branch-A models, the display shows Ec or Er, the touch keys do not respond, and the appliance operates in safe mode until a technician repairs it. On HRF266, the display shows Ec or Er for a communication sending/receiving malfunction; the service manual gives no safe-mode wording.

Common causes

  1. Sensor keys not responding / unresponsive (branch A, RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410) - the manuals' own row; no sub-cause is given.
  2. Communication sending malfunctions (Ec, HRF266) / communication receiving malfunctions (Er, HRF266) - service-manual shorthand only; no remedy text in the summary.
  3. Wrong-appliance reading - AC "EE" (EEPROM fault) advice does not apply to refrigerator Ec/Er.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Find your model number and match it to the table above - user-manual models (sensor keys) or HRF266 (communication sending/receiving).
  2. Call a service centre for repair - that is the manuals' full remedy on branch A. The appliance operates in safe mode until it is repaired by a service technician.
  3. Do not invent communication-board or wiring checks for owners - the service manual gives no remedy text for Ec/Er.

The steps above are the manuals' full remedy. This page does not invent reset, board, or wiring steps - none exist in these manuals for Ec/Er.

When to call a technician

  • Ec or Er is displayed at all - the manuals' remedy is a service centre in every case; there is no homeowner fix.
  • Touch buttons do not respond (branch A) - the sensor keys need a technician.
  • Any other fault code alongside Ec/Er - switch off and call a qualified technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Ec/Er is a sensor-keys or communication fault in a mains-powered refrigerator: never open the back panel or probe the control board or sensor wiring yourself. The manuals' stated response is a service centre.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What do Ec and Er mean on a Hisense refrigerator?

On RS5P535, LS110EA and RB5P410 models Ec or Er means the sensor keys are not responding. On HRF266 models they split: Ec = communication sending malfunction, Er = communication receiving malfunction. Check your model first.

Is Ec the same as Er?

On user-manual models yes - the manuals lump them in one row ("Ec or Er ... sensor keys are not responding"). On HRF266 they are different: Ec is sending, Er is receiving.

Will my fridge keep running with Ec or Er?

On branch-A models, yes, in safe mode until a technician repairs it. The HRF266 service manual gives no safe-mode wording for Ec/Er - book the repair.

Can I reset Ec or Er myself?

The manuals give no homeowner reset - only "call a service centre for repair". Do not follow air-conditioner "EE" advice (EEPROM fault, breaker power-cycle) - that is a different code on a different appliance.

My touch buttons don't work and the display shows Ec - is that the same as E0/E8?

On branch-A models, effectively yes: E0/E8 and Ec/Er are both "sensor keys not responding" rows in different manuals (E0/E8 on RM469/RS711/RB470; Ec/Er on RS5P535/LS110EA/RB5P410). The code letter differs by platform; the fault - unresponsive touch keys - is the same. See the E0 and E8 pages.
