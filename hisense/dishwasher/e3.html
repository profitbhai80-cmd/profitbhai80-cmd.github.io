Hisense Dishwasher Error Code E3

Quick answer

Error codeE3 — Hisense Dishwasher
What it meansDepends on the model family: the water is not reaching the required temperature (HSCE14FS), or a water drainage malfunction (H15DSS, HV60340UK).
Most common causeOn heating models: a failed heating element or temperature sensing fault. On drainage models: a blocked filter, kinked drain hose or blocked sink trap.
Safe to check yourself?Partly — drainage checks (filters, hose, sink trap) are safe to do yourself. Heating faults are not a DIY repair.
Checked againstHisense HSCE14FS user manual; Hisense H15DSS manual; Hisense HV60340UK manual

Applies to — read this first

Like E1, Hisense uses E3 for two unrelated faults. Identify your model family first — the checks are completely different.

Model familyWhat E3 means here
HSCE14FS familyThe water does not reach the required temperature — a heating problem.
H15DSS and HV60340UK familiesWater drainage malfunction — the machine cannot empty properly.

Find your model number on the rating label if you are not sure which family your machine belongs to — see how to find your model number.

Common causes

  1. Blocked dishwasher filters or a blocked/kinked drain hose (drainage models).
  2. A sink trap or waste connection blocked where the drain hose connects.
  3. A failing heating element, so the water never reaches temperature (heating models).
  4. A temperature sensor (NTC) fault, so the control cannot confirm heating (heating models).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off and unplug the dishwasher, and close the water tap.
  2. For drainage models: remove and rinse the filters in the floor of the tub — this is the single most common cause.
  3. Check the drain hose for kinks and check the sink trap/waste connection it drains into is not blocked (run the sink tap and watch it drain freely).
  4. For heating models: after a hot wash, carefully open the door at the end — dishes and water should be hot and there should be steam. Cold water with E3 points to the heater circuit.
  5. Restart a short cycle and see whether the code returns.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • E3 returns on drainage models after filters, hose and trap are confirmed clear — the drain pump may have failed.
  • On heating models, cold water plus E3 almost always means the heater or its sensor needs testing with a meter — technician work.
  • Any burning smell, tripped breaker, or visible leak at any point.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

Heating elements and their wiring are not user-serviceable. Switch off and unplug before any check, keep water away from electrical connections, and never bypass a temperature sensor or safety cut-out.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E3 mean on a Hisense dishwasher?

On HSCE14FS models it means the water is not reaching the required temperature. On H15DSS and HV60340UK models it means a water drainage malfunction. The two meanings need completely different checks.

Is E3 a drainage or a heating problem?

Both exist — it depends on your model family. If your machine leaves water in the bottom, treat it as drainage; if it drains fine but washes cold, treat it as heating.

Can I fix a heating E3 myself?

Beyond confirming the symptom, no. Testing and replacing a heater or sensor involves live-circuit components and belongs to a technician.

Will cleaning the filters clear E3?

On drainage models, very often yes — blocked filters are the most common cause. On heating models, filters will not help.
