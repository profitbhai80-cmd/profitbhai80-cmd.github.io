Hisense Washing Machine Error Code E4

Quick answer

Error codeE4 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansE4 means different things on different Hisense washer families — the series split no thin site explains. On Hisense top-load washers (HW-K60B, HWT3K8065B) E4 is a water-fill fault: the machine takes too long to fill. On front-load models using the E-code series (WFQY9014EVJM) E4 is a motor error. Front-loads using F-codes (WF3S8046BW) have no E4 at all.
Most common causeTop-load: the tap is closed, the inlet hose is restricted, water pressure is too low — or the drain hose is installed below 90 cm and water siphons out during the fill. Front-load E-series: a drive-motor fault.
Safe to check yourself?Top-load fill checks are safe (tap, hose, lid, pressure, drain-hose height). A front-load E4 motor error is technician work after the manual's one programme restart.
Checked againstHisense HWT3K8065B 3K 8kg Top Load User Guide; Hisense HW-K60B Fully Automatic User Manual; Hisense WFQY9014EVJM Front-Load User Manual; Hisense WF3S8046BW Front-Load User Manual

Applies to — read this first

Before reading any meaning, identify your machine: does it have a top lid or a front door, and does its display use E-codes or F-codes? The manuals split cleanly: top-loaders (HW-K60B, HWT3K8065B) define E4 as a fill fault; front-load E-series models (WFQY9014EVJM) define E4 as a motor error; front-load F-series models (WF3S8046BW) have no E4 — their inflow fault is F1 and their drainage fault is F3. Thin sites that claim E4 means "imbalance" or "identical codes on all models" contradict every manual we checked.

ManualExact wording for E4
Hisense HWT3K8065B 3K 8kg Top Load User Guide"E4 | Washing machine takes too long to fill." Solution: check the inlet hose installation, open and close the lid, check the tap is open and water pressure is not too low, then press Start/Pause. The same manual warns: a drain hose placed lower than 90 cm above the floor lets water siphon out during filling, causing E4.
Hisense HW-K60B Fully Automatic User Manual"E4 | Water supply error." Check the water tap is open, the supply hose is connected and water is flowing; check for frozen pipes.
Hisense WFQY9014EVJM Front-Load User Manual (E-code series)"E4 | Motor error." Remedy: restart the programme; if the error reoccurs, call a service technician.
Hisense WF3S8046BW Front-Load User Manual (F-code series)No E4 in this manual's code list. F1 = water inflow fault, F3 = drainage fault. If your front-load shows F1 or F3, read those — not this page.

Imbalance is NOT E4 on any family we checked: top-load manuals show imbalance as "Unb" / "Unbalance alarm"; the F-series uses its own codes. Pages claiming E4 = imbalance are inventing it.

What the machine does when E4 fires

On top-loaders the machine stalls during the fill phase — you may hear it trying to fill for a long time before E4 appears, and the wash never starts. On front-load E-series models the programme stops with a motor error and will not restart the cycle until the programme is restarted.

Common causes

  1. The water tap feeding the machine is closed or only partly open (top-load).
  2. The inlet hose is kinked, squashed, or its filter is blocked — or household water pressure is too low (top-load).
  3. The drain hose is installed lower than 90 cm above the floor — water siphons out as fast as it fills, and the fill never completes (the HWT3K8065B manual's own trigger).
  4. Frozen supply pipes in cold weather (HW-K60B manual).
  5. On front-load E-series models: a drive-motor fault — carbon brushes, tachometer, motor windings, or the control module.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Identify your family first: top lid or front door? E-codes or F-codes on the display? Follow the matching branch below — the fixes are completely different.
  2. Top-load branch: confirm the tap is fully open and water flows from it; straighten the inlet hose and rinse its filter; press Start/Pause to continue, as the HWT3K8065B manual instructs.
  3. Top-load branch: check the drain hose height — the manual requires it at least 90 cm above the floor. If it lies lower, refit it so water cannot siphon out during the fill.
  4. Top-load branch: open and close the lid firmly, then press Start/Pause — the manual's own reset sequence for E4.
  5. Front-load E-series branch: restart the programme once. If E4 reoccurs, stop — the manual says to call a service technician. Motor diagnosis (brushes, tachometer, windings, control module) is not user work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Never apply the top-load fill checks to a front-load motor error or vice versa — the whole point of the series split above is that the same display reading needs different handling. If a check needs a panel removed, a motor tested, or wiring touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • Top-load E4 persists with a known-good, fully open water supply, a straight inlet hose, and the drain hose above 90 cm.
  • Front-load E-series E4 reoccurs after the manual's programme restart — motor fault territory.
  • Burning smell, loud grinding, or the drum not turning at all on a front-load E4.
  • Any electrical fault signs (tripped breaker, scorch marks) — switch off at the wall and call a technician.

Safety first

Switch the machine off and unplug it before moving it or touching hoses; close the water tap first. On front-load E-series E4 the fault is the drive motor — never open the machine to inspect the motor, brushes, or wiring yourself. Water and electricity are a dangerous mix: never work on a machine connected to power with wet hands or a wet floor.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E4 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

It depends on the family: on Hisense top-load washers (HW-K60B, HWT3K8065B) E4 means the machine takes too long to fill — a water supply fault. On front-load models using the E-code series (WFQY9014EVJM) E4 is a motor error. Front-loads using F-codes have no E4 at all (inflow is F1 there).

How do I fix Hisense washer E4 on a top-load model?

Open the tap fully, straighten the inlet hose and rinse its filter, check water pressure, then open and close the lid and press Start/Pause — the HWT3K8065B manual's own steps. Also check the drain hose is at least 90 cm above the floor: below that, water siphons out during the fill and causes E4.

Some websites say Hisense E4 means unbalanced load. Is that right?

No. Hisense manuals show imbalance as "Unb" / "Unbalance alarm" on top-loaders — E4 is the fill fault (top-load) or the motor fault (front-load E-series). Pages claiming E4 = imbalance, or that the codes are "identical on all models," contradict the manuals.

My front-load Hisense shows E4. What should I do?

If your model uses E-codes, E4 is a motor error: restart the programme once, and if it reoccurs, call a service technician — that is the WFQY9014EVJM manual's own instruction. If your display uses F-codes (like the WF3S8046BW), E4 does not exist on your machine; check whether you misread F4 or another code.
