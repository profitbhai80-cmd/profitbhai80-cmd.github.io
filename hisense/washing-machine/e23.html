Hisense Washing Machine Error Code E23

Quick answer

Error codeE23 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansE23 = error while unlocking the door (front-load E-series): the door is closed but cannot be unlocked. Turn the machine off and back on; if it reoccurs, call a service technician (see the manual's chapter on opening the door manually).
Most common causeThe door is closed but the lock cannot release — the manuals describe the state, not a part.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the manual's sequence: off/on, then a technician if it reoccurs. The manual also points to its chapter on opening the door manually.
Checked againstHisense E-series troubleshooting table (Scribd copy of Hisense manual 820685, E23 row); Hisense HWGA9014 user's operation manual p.56

Applies to — read this first

E23 is the pair to E22 and the third of the door-code split: E2 = general door lock error; E22 = cannot LOCK; E23 = cannot UNLOCK. Two Hisense documents agree on the row: the door is closed but cannot be unlocked — turn the machine off, then turn it back on; if it reoccurs, call a service technician. The detail no competitor gives for E23 specifically: the manual points to its chapter on interruptions and changing the programme — opening the door manually — for getting the door open.

Competitors give no cannot-unlock-specific procedure: their door pages merge everything into one topic, and one repair site's E23 advice (string behind the door, replace the interlock) is not the manual's sequence. The manual's sequence is off/on, then a technician — with the manual door-opening chapter for access.

What the machine does when E23 fires

The cycle has ended (or stopped) but the door will not release: the lock stays engaged and the machine reports that the closed door cannot be unlocked. The machine is holding the lock for safety — water level, temperature or a lock fault can all keep it engaged.

Common causes

  1. The door is closed but cannot be unlocked (the manuals' wording).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Turn the machine off, then turn it back on — the manual's own first step.
  2. To open the door, follow the manual's chapter on interruptions and changing the programme (opening the door manually) for your exact model — do not improvise with string or tools.
  3. If E23 reoccurs, call a service technician — the manuals give no further user step.

Work through the steps in order and stop as soon as the door releases. Do not force the handle or improvise a release: forcing the door breaks latches and turns a fault into a more expensive one.

When to call a technician

  • E23 reoccurs after the off/on cycle.
  • The door will not release even via the manual's manual-opening chapter — call a service technician.

Safety first

A locked door can mean hot water or a hot drum inside: wait for the machine to cool, never force the door, and never bypass the lock. The manual's manual-opening chapter is the only sanctioned way in.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E23 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

On the front-load E-series, E23 is an error while unlocking the door: the door is closed but cannot be unlocked. Turn the machine off and back on; if it reoccurs, call a service technician.

Is Hisense E23 the same as E22?

No — the pair split: E22 = door closed but cannot be LOCKED (cycle will not start); E23 = door closed but cannot be UNLOCKED (door will not release). Different states, and E23 carries the manual door-opening pointer.

How do I open the door with Hisense E23?

Turn the machine off and back on first. To open the door, follow the manual's chapter on interruptions and changing the programme — 'opening the door manually' — for your exact model. Do not force the handle or improvise.

Is Hisense E23 the same as E2?

No. E2 = general door lock error. E23 = the specific cannot-unlock state. The three-way split (E2/E22/E23) keeps the recoveries straight.
