Hisense Washing Machine Error Code E3

Quick answer

Error codeE3 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansDepends on the machine type: a drainage error on top-load models (water not drained after about 15 minutes), or a water filling error in the front-load error table.
Most common causeTop-load: a blocked drain hose/pump or a kinked hose. Front-load table: inlet valve or its filter, or water pressure outside 0.05–0.8 MPa.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — for hose, filter and water-supply checks. Do not open the machine's body.
Checked againstHisense HW-K60B and HWT3K8065B (top-load) manuals; Hisense front-load error code table

Applies to — read this first

Warning: some websites list Hisense washing machine E3 as “door not closed”. Neither the top-load manuals nor the front-load table we checked says that — treat that claim as unverified and likely wrong for these models.

Model familyWhat E3 means here
Top-load HW-K60B / HWT3K8065BDrainage error — the water has not drained after about 15 minutes of draining.
Front-load (Hisense front-load error table)Water filling error — check the inlet valve and filter, and water pressure (rated range 0.05–0.8 MPa).

Find your model number on the rating label if you are not sure which family your machine belongs to — see how to find your model number.

Common causes

  1. A kinked, squashed or blocked drain hose (top-load drainage meaning).
  2. Coins, lint or debris blocking the pump/drain path.
  3. The water tap is off, the inlet hose filter is clogged, or pressure is too low (front-load filling meaning).
  4. The machine drains into a blocked standpipe or sink trap.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine before any check.
  2. Work out which fault you have: if the drum is full of water, it is the drainage fault; if the machine never filled, it is the filling fault.
  3. Drainage: straighten the drain hose, check it is not pushed too far down the standpipe, and check the standpipe/trap is clear.
  4. Drainage: if your model has a pump filter (usually behind a small flap at the front), drain the water into a tray first, then open the filter slowly and remove debris.
  5. Filling: confirm the tap is fully open and rinse the mesh filter inside the inlet hose connection.
  6. Run a rinse-and-spin (or a short wash) and watch a full drain/fill to confirm the code stays away.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • The drum still will not drain after hose, filter and trap are clear — the pump may have failed.
  • The machine fills very slowly even with good pressure and a clean inlet filter — the inlet valve may have failed.
  • You hear the pump straining or humming with no water movement.

Safety first

Unplug before opening the pump filter — expect a lot of water; have towels and a tray ready and keep the water away from the plug and socket. Never tip a full machine.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E3 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

On top-load HW-K60B/HWT3K8065B models it is a drainage error. In the front-load error table it is a water filling error. It depends on the machine type.

Is E3 a door problem?

Not according to the manuals we checked. Some websites claim “door not closed”, but the top-load manuals say drainage and the front-load table says filling. Trust your model's manual over a copied list.

Why is there water left in the drum with E3?

On top-load models that is exactly the fault: the machine tried to drain for about 15 minutes and the water level did not drop.

How do I clear E3?

Fix the drain or fill problem using the checks above, then run a fresh cycle. If the cause is fixed, the code will not return.
