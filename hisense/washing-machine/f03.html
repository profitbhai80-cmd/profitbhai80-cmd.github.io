Hisense Washing Machine Error Code F03

Quick answer

Error codeF03 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansDraining fault — the waste water is not being pumped out of the machine.
Most common causeA blocked pump filter, a kinked or frozen drain hose, or a blocked waste connection.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — for the filter, hose and waste-connection checks below.
Checked againstHisense WFXE6012-series manual; Hisense WFPV7012MT manual

Applies to — read this first

Both manuals name F03 as a draining fault. A second documented meaning — overflow / high water level — exists in some Hisense documentation. The tell-tale question is: is there water left in the drum? Standing water points to the draining fault; the machine overfilling or filling when it should not points to the overflow meaning and to a technician.

Find your model number on the rating label if you are not sure which family your machine belongs to — see how to find your model number.

Common causes

  1. The pump filter is blocked with coins, buttons, lint or hair grips — the classic cause.
  2. The drain hose is kinked, squashed behind the machine, or frozen in cold weather.
  3. The sink trap or standpipe the machine drains into is blocked.
  4. The drain pump itself is jammed or failing.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine.
  2. Locate the pump filter flap (front, low down on WFXE6012/WFPV7012MT families). Put a tray and towels down, use the drain tube if fitted, and let the water out slowly.
  3. Unscrew the filter, remove all debris, and check the pump impeller behind it turns freely with a finger or a pencil — never with the machine plugged in.
  4. Straighten the drain hose along its whole run; in winter, check for a frozen section near an outside wall.
  5. Check the sink trap/standpipe by pouring water down it — it should drain freely.
  6. Refit the filter fully, run a rinse-and-spin, and watch the drain complete.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. If a check needs a panel removed, a part tested, or a wire touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • F03 returns with a clean filter, a clear hose and a clear waste connection — the pump is the likely failure.
  • The machine overfills, or fills while switched off — stop using it and call a technician (this matches the overflow meaning).
  • Burning smell, or the breaker trips when the pump runs.

Safety first

Expect up to several litres of water from the filter — keep it away from sockets and the plug, and unplug before reaching anywhere near the pump impeller.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F03 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

In the WFXE6012-series and WFPV7012MT manuals, F03 is a draining fault: waste water is not being pumped out.

Is F03 always a drainage problem?

Some Hisense documentation also uses F03 for overflow/high water level. If the drum holds water that will not leave, it is drainage; if the machine takes in too much water, treat it as overflow and call a technician.

Where is the pump filter?

Behind the small flap at the front bottom of the machine on these families. Drain the water out first — opening it dry is a flood.

Can a frozen hose cause F03?

Yes, the manuals list a frozen drain hose among the causes. Thaw it gently with warm (not boiling) water around the hose.
