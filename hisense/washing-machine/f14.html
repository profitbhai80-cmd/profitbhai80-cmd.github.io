Hisense Washing Machine Error Code F14

Quick answer

Error codeF14 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansF14 = door unlock fault — the door cannot be unlocked after the cycle. Companion code: F13 = door LOCK fault (cannot lock at cycle start) — route readers between the two. SCOPE HONESTY: WFN9012's table does NOT list F14 (its §C covers F04–F07/F13/F23/UNB only) — F14 exists on the newer/larger-display families, and the page must not claim it is universal. eciftci's "F14 = EEPROM Error" flatly contradicts 8+ genuine manuals.
Most common causeThe door cannot be released at/after cycle end. The manuals' unlock conditions (WF5S1045BW): the door unlocks automatically when paused/ended only if (1) the water level is lower than the door-open level, (2) the drum temperature is below 50°C, (3) the motor speed is 0. If these are not met, there is no action — this is the plain-English explanation of an F14 that appears with water still in the drum or the load still hot.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own procedures: press Start/Pause (the machine will try to open the door; the alarm clears only when the machine unlocks it); or turn the nub to Off for 3 seconds, choose the programme and start again.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F14 is the door-UNLOCK fault — the door cannot be released at/after cycle end. Its companion is F13 = door LOCK fault (the door cannot be locked at cycle start) — the page routes readers between the two below. SCOPE HONESTY: F14 is absent from some older tables (e.g. WFN9012's table does not list F14 — its §C covers F04–F07/F13/F23/UNB only); it exists on the newer/larger-display families. Never assume — check your model's manual. And eciftci.com.tr's "F14 = EEPROM Error" contradicts all 8+ manuals.

What the machine does when F14 fires

The display shows F14 — the door cannot be released after the cycle; the alarm persists until the machine unlocks the door itself.

Common causes

  1. Water is still in the drum above the door-open level — the manuals unlock only when the water level is lower than the door-open level.
  2. The drum is still hot — the manuals unlock only when the drum temperature is below 50°C.
  3. The motor is still turning — the manuals unlock only when the motor speed is 0.

Safe checks, in order

  1. WFPV7012MT / WDQY-family (verbatim): "Press 'Start/Pause' button. The washing machine will try to open the door."
  2. "The alarm will be removed until the door is unlocked" — i.e. wait; the alarm persists until the machine succeeds.
  3. Alternative (verbatim): "turn the nub to Off for 3 seconds to turn off the machine. Choose the desired programmme to start the machine. The alarm will be removed."
  4. WF5S1045BB-family (verbatim): "Turn off the appliance, then turn it back on." → "If the error reoccurs, call a service technician."

The steps above are the manuals' own — the Start/Pause try-unlock or the 3-sec power-off + restart. No manual sanctions forcing the door, running a drain cycle, or using a manual release for F14 (that is a competitor addition).

When to call a technician

  • F14 reoccurs after the manual's procedures — the manuals' own instruction: call a service technician; the lock assembly needs a technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F14 involves the door-lock assembly and mains-powered electronics: never force the door open (no manual sanctions forcing — the alarm clears only when the machine unlocks the door itself), and never dismantle the lock yourself — that is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F14 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

F14 means a door unlock fault — the door cannot be unlocked after the cycle. It is verified verbatim in 8+ Hisense manuals (WF5S1045BB, WFPV7012MT, WDQY8014/9014/1014, WFQP8014EVM and others).

What is the difference between F13 and F14?

F13: cannot LOCK at cycle start. F14: cannot UNLOCK at cycle end. Different procedures — see the F13 page if your door will not LOCK.

Why won't the door open after the wash finished?

The manuals unlock only when water is below the door-open level, drum temp is below 50°C, and the motor is stopped. Water still in the drum or a hot load = no unlock. Press Start/Pause and let the machine try, or power off 3 sec and restart.

Can I force the door open?

No manual sanctions forcing; the alarm clears only when the machine unlocks the door itself.

My model isn't listed — does F14 apply?

F14 is absent from some older tables (e.g. WFN9012); check your model's manual. Never assume.
