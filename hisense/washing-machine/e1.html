Hisense Washing Machine Error Code E1

Quick answer

Error codeE1 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansE1 means different things on different Hisense washer families. On top-loaders (HWT3K8065B, HW-K60B) E1 means the lid is not closed — open and close the lid, then press Start/Pause. On front-load Gorenje-platform models (WFQY9014EVJM) E1 is a temperature sensor error (inlet water too cold). Newer F-code front-loads have no E1 at all.
Most common causeTop-load: the lid was not closed properly (or was opened under child lock). Front-load E-series: the inlet water is too cold / the temperature sensor circuit is faulty.
Safe to check yourself?Top-load lid checks are fully user-safe. A front-load E1 temperature-sensor error gets one programme retry, then a technician.
Checked againstHisense HWT3K8065B 3K 8kg Top Load User Guide; Hisense HW-K60B Fully Automatic User Manual; Hisense WFQY9014EVJM Front-Load User Manual

Applies to — read this first

E1 is a textbook Hisense series split — and the thin sites get it wrong on every family. Top-loaders (HWT3K8065B, HW-K60B): E1 = lid not closed, with the manual's own open-close-lid + Start/Pause reset. Front-load Gorenje-platform (WFQY9014EVJM): E1 = temperature sensor error, 'inlet water too cold'. Newer front-loads (WF5S1245Bx) use F-codes and have no E1. Identify your machine before reading further: top lid or front door? E-codes or F-codes?

The WF5S1245Bx front-load manual uses F01/F03/F13/F14/Unb — no E1 exists there. E1's E-style table belongs to top-loads and the older Gorenje-platform front-loads.

What the machine does when E1 fires

On top-loaders the machine refuses to start (or pauses) with E1 on the display until the lid is properly closed. Under child lock, opening the lid triggers the E1 alarm — and if the lid is not re-closed within 5 seconds, the machine drains automatically and stops the drain when the alarm clears. On front-load E-series models the programme stops with the temperature-sensor error.

Common causes

  1. The lid was not closed properly, or was opened mid-cycle (top-load).
  2. Child lock is engaged and the lid was opened (top-load — triggers the auto-drain nuance).
  3. The lid switch or latch is not registering the closed lid (top-load).
  4. The inlet water is too cold, or the temperature sensor circuit is faulty (front-load E-series).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Identify your family: top lid or front door? E-codes or F-codes on the display?
  2. Top-load: open the lid fully and close it firmly until it latches, then press Start/Pause — the manual's own reset.
  3. Top-load: if child lock is on, switch it off first, then close the lid and press Start/Pause.
  4. Front-load E-series: repeat the washing programme once, as the manual instructs. If E1 reoccurs, call a service technician — sensor diagnosis is not user work.

Work through the checks in order and stop as soon as the code clears. Do not apply the lid fix to a front-load temperature-sensor error or vice versa. If a check needs a panel removed, a sensor tested, or wiring touched, it is no longer a safe home check — skip to the technician section.

When to call a technician

  • Top-load E1 with a firmly closed, latched lid (lid switch failure).
  • Front-load E1 reoccurring after the manual's programme repeat (temperature sensor fault).
  • Any burning smell or scorch marks near the lid switch area.

Safety first

Switch the machine off before inspecting the lid or latch area. Never defeat or bypass the lid switch — it exists to stop the drum while you can reach it. Sensor and switch electrical tests are service-staff work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E1 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

On top-loaders (HWT3K8065B, HW-K60B) E1 means the lid is not closed — open and close the lid, then press Start/Pause. On front-load Gorenje-platform models (WFQY9014EVJM) E1 is a temperature sensor error (inlet water too cold).

Why does my Hisense washer drain by itself with E1?

That is the child-lock nuance in the HWT3K8065B manual: if the lid is opened under child lock and not re-closed within 5 seconds, the machine drains automatically. Close the lid and press Start/Pause.

Some sites say Hisense E1 is a water supply problem. Is that right?

No — no Hisense washing-machine manual gives E1 a water-supply meaning on any family. Top-load E1 = lid not closed; front-load E1 = temperature sensor. The water-supply claim is fabricated.

My Hisense front-loader uses F-codes. Where is E1?

It doesn't exist there — newer front-loads (e.g. WF5S1245Bx) use F01/F03/F13/F14/Unb instead. E1 belongs to top-loads and the older Gorenje-platform front-load E-table.
