Hisense Washing Machine Error Code F01

Quick answer

Error codeF01 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansF01 = water inflow fault — the machine is not fed with water, or the water supply is insufficient. The manuals' own 4-point check: faucet fully open with enough water pressure; inlet pipe not knotted; filtering net of the inlet pipe not blocked; water pipe not frozen. Then press Start/Pause; service if it reoccurs. Display variant: on WF3S8046BW / WF3S8043BW models the same fault is displayed as F1 — not a different code, not lid-lock (no Hisense front-load manual defines F1 as lid-lock). F02 is NOT a second fill code — the only genuine F02 is the "F02/F24" overflow alternate display (see the F24 page).
Most common causeThe water tap is not fully open, household water pressure is low, the inlet hose is knotted/pinched, the inlet filter net is blocked, or the supply pipe is frozen — the manuals' own 4-point check.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own 4-point check (tap, pressure, hose, filter, frozen pipe), then press Start/Pause after the fault is removed.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F01 exists on Hisense front-load F-series models only — and the identical meaning and 4-point check hold across 10+ named manuals. Display-variant note: the WF3S8046BW / WF3S8043BW family (single-digit display) prints this same fault as F1 — same fault, shorter display. Do NOT merge F01/F1 with top-load E1 or front-load E-series E1 (different platforms, different manuals). And F02 is not a second water-supply code: the only genuine F02 is WFPV7012MT's "F02/F24" alternate display for OVERFLOW — the opposite fault.

What the machine does when F01 fires

The display shows F01 (or F1 on WF3S8046BW / WF3S8043BW) — the machine is not being fed with water, or the supply is insufficient.

Common causes

  1. The faucet is not fully open or has too little water pressure — the manual's check 1.
  2. The water inlet pipe is knotted (kinked/pinched) — the manual's check 2.
  3. The filtering net of the water inlet pipe is blocked — the manual's check 3.
  4. The water pipe is frozen — the manual's check 4.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Faucet fully open and has enough water pressure — the WFN9012 manual's own check 1.
  2. Water inlet pipe is knotted or not — straighten kinks (check 2).
  3. Filtering net of the water inlet pipe is blocked or not — rinse the inlet hose mesh filter (check 3; "refer to maintenance and repair").
  4. Water pipe is frozen or not (check 4).
  5. Press "Start/Pause" key after the fault is removed — the manual's own step 5.
  6. If the fault occurs again, please contact with after-sale service — the manual's own step 6.

The steps above are the WFN9012 manual's own procedure a., in order — nothing added, nothing invented.

When to call a technician

  • F01 returns after the full 4-point check with a good supply — the inlet valve or level sensing needs a technician ("If fault occurs again, please contact with after-sale service").

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F01 involves water and mains-powered electronics together: switch the machine off before touching the inlet hose or its filter net, and never dismantle the inlet valve yourself — that is service work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F01 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

F01 means a water inflow fault — the machine is not fed with water, or the water supply is insufficient. It is verified verbatim in 10+ Hisense manuals (WFN9012, WF5S1045BB, WFPV7012MT, WDQR1014EVAJM, WFH 8014 and others).

My display shows F1, not F01 — is that different?

On WF3S8046BW / WF3S8043BW models (single-digit display), no: F1 is the same water inflow fault with the identical 4 checks. It is NOT lid-lock — no Hisense front-load manual defines F1 as lid-lock.

Is F02 another water-supply code?

No. The only genuine F02 is the "F02/F24" alternate display for OVERFLOW on WFPV7012MT/HWFM8012-family machines — the opposite fault. removeandreplace's "F01 or F02 = Water Supply Fault" misroutes F02-display (overflow) owners.

What order do I check things in?

The manual's own order: tap fully open + enough pressure → inlet pipe not knotted → inlet filter net not blocked → pipe not frozen → press Start/Pause → service if it reoccurs.
