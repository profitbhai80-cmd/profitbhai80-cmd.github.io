Hisense Washing Machine Error Code E11

Quick answer

Error codeE11 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansE11 = water overflow (front-load E-series): water in the drum exceeds a certain level, the programme is interrupted and water is pumped out. Two branches: if the machine is still adding water while the error shows, close the water tap and call a service technician; otherwise restart the programme, and if it reoccurs, call a service technician.
Most common causeToo much water in the drum — the manuals describe the overflow event, not a part. The safety-critical branch is continued filling while the error shows.
Safe to check yourself?Yes, for the manual's branches: restart the programme, or close the water tap if the machine keeps adding water. If it reoccurs, call a service technician.
Checked againstHisense E-series troubleshooting table (Scribd copy of Hisense manual 820685, E11 two-branch row); Hisense HWGA9014 user's operation manual p.56; ASKO W4086C platform manual; Gorenje partners platform manual

Applies to — read this first

E11's row is written as two branches, and this page is structured around them because the second branch is the safety-critical one: (1) water exceeds a level, programme interrupted, water pumped out — restart the programme, else technician; (2) water exceeds a level WHILE the machine continues to add water — close the water tap and call a service technician. Four documents agree on both branches. One repair site drops the tap-close branch entirely; this page does not.

Hisense E-series troubleshooting table (Scribd copy of Hisense manual 820685)E11: water overflow. If there is water in the drum that exceeds a certain level, and an error is reported on the display unit, the washing program will be interrupted and water will be pumped out of the washing machine. Restart the programme. If the error reoccurs, call a service technician. If there is water in the drum that exceeds a certain level, and an error is reported on the display unit, while the washing machine continues to add water, close the water tap and call a service technician.

The competitor gap is one repair site's E11 page, which keeps only the pumped-out branch and drops the still-adding-water branch (close the tap + technician) — the safety-critical half of the row. This page keeps both branches the manuals print.

What the machine does when E11 fires

The programme is interrupted and the machine pumps water out of the drum. If the machine is still adding water while E11 shows, it is not managing the level — that is the branch where the manual says to close the tap.

Common causes

  1. Water in the drum has exceeded the permitted level (the manuals' wording; no part is named).

Safe checks, in order

  1. Check whether the machine is still adding water while E11 shows: if it is, close the water tap immediately and call a service technician.
  2. If the machine is not adding water, let it pump out, then restart the programme.
  3. If E11 reoccurs, call a service technician — the manuals give no further user step.

Work through the steps in order and stop as soon as the situation is safe. The still-adding-water branch always wins: close the tap first, diagnose later.

When to call a technician

  • The machine keeps adding water while E11 shows — close the tap and call a service technician.
  • E11 reoccurs on fresh programmes after a restart.

Safety first

Overflow plus continued filling is the dangerous combination: close the water tap, unplug if water is escaping the machine, and never open the machine's panels to reach the inlet valve or level system yourself.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does E11 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

On the front-load E-series, E11 is water overflow: the programme is interrupted and water is pumped out. Two branches: restart the programme — but if the machine keeps adding water while the error shows, close the water tap and call a service technician.

My Hisense keeps filling with E11 showing — what do I do?

Close the water tap and call a service technician. That is the manual's own second branch, and it is the safety-critical one: the machine is not controlling its fill.

Is Hisense E11 the same as E10?

No. E10 = the water level SENSOR is faulty. E11 = water OVERFLOW — too much water is actually in the drum. Sensor fault vs overflow event.

Is Hisense E11 the same as E9?

No. E11 = overflow in the DRUM (pumped out; close the tap if still filling). E9 = water at the BOTTOM of the machine (Total Aqua Stop; off/on, then technician). Different water, different action.
