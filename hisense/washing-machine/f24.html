Hisense Washing Machine Error Code F24

Quick answer

Error codeF24 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansF24 = water level reaches overflow level — an overfill fault (the manuals' list-table wording is "Overflow or too much foam" — detergent overdose is a named cause in the manuals' own wording). Alternate display: WFPV7012MT and HWFM8012 print this row as "F02/F24" — on those models the same fault displays as F02, which is NOT a water-supply code (this corrects removeandreplace.com's "F01 or F02 = Water Supply Fault" for F02-display owners, who are currently sent the wrong way). Is F24 the same as F03? No — F03 = drainage fault (water won't pump OUT); F24 = overflow (too much water / foam).
Most common causeToo much water in the tub — or too much foam (detergent overdose), per the manuals' own "overflow or too much foam" wording.
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own procedure: press Start/Pause and feed water; select and start the required programme; the escalation branch ("if the appliance continues to pump water into the tub, close the water tap and call a service technician") is the manual's own.

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F24 is the OVERFLOW fault — and the overflow meaning belongs here, not on the F03 page (the published F03 page's "second documented meaning — overflow" aside has been revisited and removed; overflow is F24/F02). Alternate display: WFPV7012MT and HWFM8012 print the overflow row as "F02/F24" — on those families the same fault displays as F02. F02 is the same overflow fault, not a water-supply code. Do not merge F24 with F03 (drainage — water won't pump OUT) or with invented AquaStop/base-leak/E09 framings (no manual mentions them for F24).

What the machine does when F24 fires

The display shows F24 (or F02 on WFPV7012MT/HWFM8012-family models) — the water level has reached the overflow level.

Common causes

  1. Too much water in the tub — the overflow level has been reached (the manual's own condition).
  2. Too much foam — detergent overdose, named in the manuals' own list-table wording ("overflow or too much foam").

Safe checks, in order

  1. WFN9012-family (verbatim): "Remove it by pressing 'Start/Pause' key and feed water."
  2. "Select and start required program."
  3. "If fault occurs again, disconnect power supply and contact with after-sale service."
  4. WF5S1045BB / WF3S8046BW-family (verbatim): "Stop the programme by pressing the [start/pause] button." → "Restart the programme." → "If the appliance continues to pump water into the tub, close the water tap and call a service technician." ← the overfilling-inlet-valve case; the manuals' own escalation, printed exactly.

The steps above are the manuals' own two wordings — the WFN9012-family procedure and the WF5S/WF3S-family procedure with its escalation branch. Nothing added, nothing invented.

When to call a technician

  • The appliance continues to pump water into the tub after restart — the manuals' own escalation: close the water tap and call a service technician (inlet valve not closing — technician work, not DIY).

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F24 is an overfill fault: water and mains-powered electronics together. If the machine keeps filling, close the tap and switch it off — do not leave it filling unattended; the repair is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F24 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

F24 means the water level has reached the overflow level — an overfill fault (the manuals also list "too much foam" as a cause). It is verified verbatim in 10+ Hisense manuals (WFN9012, WF5S1045BB, WFPV7012MT, WF3S8046BW and others).

My washer shows F02, not F24. Is that the same fault?

On WFPV7012MT / HWFM8012-family machines, yes: the manual prints the overflow row as "F02/F24". It is the overflow fault, NOT a water-supply fault (despite what some websites say).

What causes the overflow — too much detergent?

The manuals' list-table wording is "overflow or too much foam": detergent overdose is a named cause. Run the Start/Pause-feed-water-restart procedure; reduce detergent next wash.

The machine keeps pumping water into the tub even after restart.

The manuals' own escalation: close the water tap and call a service technician (inlet valve not closing — technician work, not DIY).

Is F24 the same as F03?

No. F03 = drainage fault (water won't pump OUT). F24 = overflow (too much water / foam). Different faults, different procedures. (The published F03 page's "second documented meaning — overflow" aside has been revisited and removed — the overflow meaning belongs to F24/F02.)
