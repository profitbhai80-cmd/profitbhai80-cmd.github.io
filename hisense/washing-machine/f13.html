Hisense Washing Machine Error Code F13

Quick answer

Error codeF13 — Hisense Washing Machine
What it meansF13 = door lock fault — the door cannot be locked when the programme tries to start ("Door lock is abnormal" / "Error while locking the door — Door is closed, but it cannot be locked"). Companion code: F14 = door UNLOCK fault (cannot release the door at/after cycle end) — route readers between the two. F13 is not E12/E22/E23 (front-load E-series door codes on a different platform — codigodeerror's "F13 / E12" equivalence is uncited).
Most common causeThe door lock is abnormal — the door is closed but cannot be locked. The manuals' own procedures are the 20-sec PTC procedure (WFN9012 family) or the switch-off/on + restart programme (WF5S/WF3S family).
Safe to check yourself?Yes — the manuals' own procedures: run the programme 20 sec, unlock the door (PTC) to check whether the door is closed, open/close it again, then Start/Pause (WFN9012 family); or switch off, switch back on and restart the programme (WF5S/WF3S family).

Applies to — read this first

Read this first, because F13 is the door-LOCK fault — the door cannot be locked when the programme tries to start. Its companion is F14 = door UNLOCK fault (the door cannot be released at/after cycle end) — the page routes readers between the two below. F13 is not E12/E22/E23 (front-load E-series door codes on a different platform — codigodeerror's "F13 / E12" equivalence is uncited). Procedure wording varies slightly by family (WFN9012-family: 20-sec PTC procedure; WF5S/WF3S-family: switch off/on + restart programme) — both wordings are given below, both from manuals.

What the machine does when F13 fires

The display shows F13 — the programme tried to start but the door could not be locked.

Common causes

  1. The door lock is abnormal — the door is closed but cannot be locked (the manuals' own wording).

Safe checks, in order

  1. WFN9012-family (verbatim): "Start program for 20 sec and unlock the washer door (PTC) to check whether the door is closed."
  2. "Open or close it again."
  3. "After fault is removed, press 'Start/Pause' key."
  4. "If fault occurs again, disconnect power supply and contact with after-sale service."
  5. WF5S1045BB / WF3S8046BW-family (verbatim): "Switch off the washing machine." → "Switch it back on and restart the washing programme." → "If the error reoccurs, call a service technician."

Both procedures are the manuals' own — the WFN9012-family 20-sec PTC procedure and the WF5S/WF3S-family switch-off/on + restart. Use whichever matches your manual's wording.

When to call a technician

  • F13 keeps coming back after the manual's procedure — the manuals' own instruction: disconnect power and contact after-sale service; the lock assembly needs a technician.

More guidance: when an appliance error requires professional service.

Safety first

F13 involves the door-lock assembly and mains-powered electronics: never force the door (the manuals never sanction forcing), and never dismantle the lock yourself — that is technician work.

Never open an appliance that is connected to power, never bypass a safety device, and never attempt gas, refrigerant or high-voltage work without the proper qualifications. If anything feels beyond a basic external check, stop and call a qualified technician.

Frequently asked questions

What does F13 mean on a Hisense washing machine?

F13 means a door lock fault — the door cannot be locked when the programme tries to start. It is verified verbatim in 10+ Hisense manuals (WFN9012, WF5S1045BB, WFPV7012MT, WF3S8046BW and others).

What is the difference between F13 and F14?

F13: the door cannot be LOCKED at cycle start. F14: the door cannot be UNLOCKED at cycle end. Different ends of the cycle, different manual procedures — see the F14 page if your door will not OPEN.

What does the 20-second PTC check do?

Plain-English: the door lock is a thermal (PTC) device; the manual's procedure runs the programme briefly then releases the lock so you can check the door is truly closed, then re-close it firmly.

The door looks closed but F13 keeps coming back.

Per the manuals: if the procedure doesn't clear it, disconnect power and call after-sale service — the lock assembly needs a technician.

Can I force the door?

No: the manuals never sanction forcing; F13 means the lock didn't engage, and forcing risks the latch.
